This is the weekly summary of what changed on the M365.FM website between 2 and 8 November 2026. It lists only changes to the site itself: features, design and fixes.

## Easier to find your way

- **One menu and one sidebar** now work the same way in every language and on all content pages. See the [updates page](https://www.m365.fm/updates/) for every weekly summary.
- **The start page exists in every language**, the language is chosen from your browser setting, and old addresses forward in one step instead of several.
- **Internal links no longer take a detour:** old addresses are replaced by the final address when the site is built, which is also better for search engines.
- **A top 10 of the previous week** is now published every week, with an English page at [/top/](https://www.m365.fm/top/).

## Faster on the phone

- The audio player loads only after you tap it, there are fewer animations, and an analytics script was removed.
- Google AdSense is gone. Free ad spaces now show M365.FM's own services and clearly marked sponsor banners; the [sponsor page](https://www.m365.fm/sponsor/) explains the packages and how paid content is labelled.
- Community scripts carry a version number, so new features appear immediately instead of waiting for old copies to expire.

## Profiles, experts and companies

- **Expert Connect** is organised by roles and explains how it works, and open requests and matching are included. The [expert page](https://www.m365.fm/for/experts/) shows what a profile can do.
- **Industry and job role** are now topics you can choose in your profile.
- **Companies and their entities come as a pair.** A company can take over its entity with a company email address, and employees of a community company appear automatically under people.
- **Entities carry sourced facts** about companies, products and people. [iFAKT](https://www.m365.fm/entity/ifakt/) is the first company filled in this way, with Polaris and its modules.
- **Several sign-ins per profile**, and accounts can be merged.
- **MVP tracker:** a public profile view and an export for the MVP portal. GitHub activity is no longer shown publicly anywhere.
- **Forms** share one design across entities, profiles, Expert Connect and administration.

## Content

- **Transcripts as files:** every episode now has a TXT and a VTT file.
- **The updates page** shows only these weekly summaries.
- **English blog posts are translated into 14 languages** automatically by the translation team.

## Fixes

- The site build was repaired several times this week: the size limit, duplicate sitemap entries, forwarded addresses in the sitemap and the structured data of the company directory.
- The media kit email now carries a valid PDF attachment.

See you next Friday with the summary for week 46.
