PL-300 is the exam for the Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate certification. This article outlines the Microsoft Learn study guide. It lists skills only and contains no exam questions. Exam logistics such as duration, number of questions and fee are not on the study guide page.

## Key facts

- Skills measured as of April 20, 2026.
- Passing score 700 or greater.
- Most questions cover generally available features, and some may cover commonly used preview features.
- Localized exams are updated about eight weeks after the English version.

## Prepare the data (25 to 30 percent)

- **Get or connect to data:** connect to sources or a shared semantic model, change data source settings including credentials and privacy levels, choose between DirectLake, DirectQuery and Import, create and modify parameters.
- **Profile and clean:** evaluate statistics and column properties, resolve inconsistencies, nulls and quality issues, resolve import errors.
- **Transform and load:** choose column types, group and aggregate, pivot, unpivot and transpose, convert semi-structured data, build fact and dimension tables, use reference or duplicate queries, merge and append, create keys, configure loading.

## Model the data (25 to 30 percent)

- **Design a data model:** table and column properties, role-playing dimensions, cardinality and cross-filter direction, a common date table, calculated columns and tables.
- **DAX calculations:** single aggregation measures, CALCULATE, time intelligence, basic statistical functions, semi-additive measures, quick measures, calculation groups.
- **Optimize performance:** remove unnecessary rows and columns, find poor measures, relationships and visuals with Performance Analyzer and DAX query view, reduce granularity.

## Visualize and analyze the data (25 to 30 percent)

- **Create reports:** choose and format visuals, narrative visuals with Copilot, themes, conditional formatting, slicing and filtering, Copilot to create or suggest a report page, paginated report decisions, visual calculations by using DAX.
- **Usability and storytelling:** bookmarks, custom tooltips, interactions, navigation, sorting, sync slicers, Selection pane, drillthrough, export settings, mobile design, personalization, accessibility, automatic page refresh.
- **Patterns and trends:** the Analyze feature, grouping, binning and clustering, AI visuals, reference lines, error bars and forecasting, outliers and anomalies, Copilot to summarize the semantic model.

## Manage and secure Power BI (15 to 20 percent)

- **Workspaces and assets:** create and configure workspaces and apps, publish items, dashboards, distribution methods, subscriptions and data alerts, promote or certify content, identify when a gateway is required, scheduled refresh.
- **Secure and govern:** workspace roles, item-level access, semantic model access, row-level security roles and group membership, sensitivity labels.

## Change log

Three areas are marked Minor, and the guide does not describe the changes. Compare the outline with your own prior notes if you studied the earlier objectives.

## Preparation

Use the newer Practice Assessment on AI Skills Navigator. The study guide lists training paths, Power BI documentation, Microsoft Q&A, community forums and exam prep videos, and an exam sandbox. Accommodations can be requested.

## Study order suggestion

This is a suggestion, not Microsoft guidance. Weight your time by the percentages, and practice Power Query and DAX, because the audience profile names both.

## Audience profile

The guide describes a candidate who delivers actionable insights from available data by applying domain expertise, provides business value through easy-to-understand visualizations and enables self-service analytics. The candidate works with business stakeholders on requirements and with analytics and data engineers on data, uses Power BI to prepare, model, visualize and analyze, and manage and secure data, and is proficient in Power Query and DAX.

## Renewal and logistics

Associate certifications expire annually. Renewal is a free online assessment on Microsoft Learn, and connecting your certification profile to Learn lets you schedule and renew exams and share certificates. If your language is not offered you can request an extra 30 minutes.

## Practice assessments in detail

Learn describes two options. The new Practice Assessment runs on AI Skills Navigator and more closely matches the exam experience. The legacy assessment on Microsoft Learn is no longer updated, and Microsoft strongly recommends the new experience. The guide also links an exam sandbox so you can see the exam environment before booking.

## Where the weight sits

Three of the four domains each carry 25 to 30 percent, so at least three quarters of the exam sits in data preparation, modeling and visualization. Manage and secure Power BI carries 15 to 20 percent, which still includes row-level security and sensitivity labels, so do not skip it.

## Not covered

The guide gives no retirement note, scoring scale, number of questions, duration or fee, and no weighting of individual subtopics.
