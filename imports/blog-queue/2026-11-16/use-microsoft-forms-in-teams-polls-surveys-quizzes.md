Microsoft Forms works in Teams in three ways: as a Forms tab in a channel for shared forms, through the Polls app for quick polls in chats, channels and meetings, and as a link you share from forms.office.com for surveys and quizzes. Which one fits depends on whether you want a long-lived form, a quick vote or a graded quiz.

## Option 1: A shared form in a channel

Microsoft Support describes this flow.

1. In Teams, select **Apps**, search for **Forms** and select **Add** if the app is not installed. Pick the channel.
2. Go to the channel and select **Add a tab** (plus icon).
3. Under **Tabs for your team**, select **Forms**.
4. Choose **Create a shared form that your team can edit**, enter a name and select **Save**.

**Post to the channel about this tab** is checked by default, so uncheck it if you do not want to announce the tab yet. The form opens in edit mode. To use a form that already exists, choose **Add an existing form**, search for it and pick one of three modes: **Collect responses** (people fill it), **Show results** (the team sees results) or **Collaborate** (the team edits it). A channel can also receive a daily summary of responses: select **More options** next to the channel, **Connectors**, then **Configure** beside Forms and pick the form.

One limit is documented. The setting **Allow respondents to edit their responses** is unavailable for forms created in Teams. The support page says to create a group form instead.

## Option 2: A quick poll in chat or a channel

1. Select **Actions and apps** beside the compose box, then **Polls**.
2. Enter a question and at least two options. Use **Add option** for more.
3. Turn on **Multiple selections** if people may choose more than one answer.
4. Optionally turn on **Record names of respondents**. Only the creator sees names and individual responses.
5. Optionally select **Share aggregated results with respondents**.
6. Select **Preview**, then **Send**.

Respondents select **Start now**, choose an answer and select **Submit Vote**. To see results, the creator selects **More** on the poll card, which opens the Responses overview in Microsoft Forms. From there you can open the results in Excel, check individual results or delete results. Microsoft's page does not state a maximum number of options for this kind of poll.

## Option 3: Polls in a Teams meeting

Add the Polls tab before the meeting: open the meeting in **Calendar**, select **Chat with participants**, then **Add a tab**, search for **Polls**, select **Add** and **Save**. Select **Create New Poll** and choose a type: **Multiple Choice Poll**, **Multiple Choice Quiz**, **Word Cloud Poll**, **Rating Poll** or **Ranking Poll**. Microsoft Support lists limits of up to 12 options for multiple choice, a rating scale up to 5 and ranking from first to fifth.

Save a draft or launch it later with **Launch** in the Polls tab or the Polls pane. Attendees see a pop-up, choose an answer and select **Submit**. Results appear live, and you can close, export, delete or reopen a poll. Microsoft Support says only meeting owners and presenters can create, manage and launch polls, and polls are not available to members of other organizations in a multi-tenant organization. Multiple Choice Poll and Quiz are not available in GCC High or DoD. The same page also mentions attendees creating ad-hoc polls, which conflicts with the owner and presenter rule and is not resolved there.

## Create a quiz or survey

Quizzes and surveys are built in Microsoft Forms at forms.office.com, then shared to Teams. For a quiz, Microsoft Support lists these steps: select **New Quiz**, enter a name, choose **Quick start with** and a question type, type the question and answers, mark correct answers with the **Correct Answer** check mark and enter **Points**. The **Message** icon adds feedback to an answer. Question types include Choice, Text, Rating, Date, Ranking, Likert, File upload and Net Promoter Score. Use **Preview** to test it. Quizzes save automatically. Using Copilot in Forms requires a Microsoft 365 account and a Copilot license.

## Share to Teams and choose who can respond

Open the form and select **Collect responses**. The options are:

- **Anyone can respond:** anyone inside or outside your organization who has the link can respond.
- **Only people in my organization can respond:** all members of your organization can respond, but others cannot, even with the link.
- **Specific people in my organization can respond:** only pre-selected members of your organization can access and respond. You enter the names in the **To:** box.

You can send an invitation to a Teams group or channel, copy a link, download a QR code or embed the form. Microsoft Support lists limits: invitations to up to 500 individual recipients, and a Teams group of up to 200 members. The **Record name** and **One response per person** settings are available only when access is limited to your organization.

## Common problems

- **Forms tab not offered:** Microsoft Support says the Forms app must be added first. Search for it under **Apps** and select **Add**. The pages read for this article do not describe admin policies for Forms or Polls.
- **Cannot let respondents edit answers:** use a group form, as noted above.
- **Poll not visible to people from another organization:** Microsoft says members of other organizations in a multi-tenant organization cannot see or respond to live meeting polls.
