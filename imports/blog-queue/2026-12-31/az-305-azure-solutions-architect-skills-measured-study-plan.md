AZ-305, Designing Microsoft Azure Infrastructure Solutions, measures four domains: identity, governance and monitoring; data storage; business continuity; and infrastructure. This guide reproduces the outline from the Microsoft Learn study guide, which states the skills measured as of April 17, 2026. It contains no exam questions.

## Exam facts from the study guide

- A score of 700 or greater is required to pass.
- No formal prerequisite exam or certification is named.
- Candidates should have advanced experience and knowledge of IT operations, including networking, virtualization, identity, security, business continuity, disaster recovery, data platforms and governance, and experience with Azure administration, Azure development and DevOps processes.
- The audience profile describes an Azure solutions architect who designs cloud and hybrid solutions covering compute, network, storage, monitoring and security, and aligns designs with the Azure Well-Architected Framework and the Cloud Adoption Framework for Azure.
- The English exam is updated first. Localized versions follow about eight weeks later. If your language is not available, you can request an additional 30 minutes.

## Domain weights

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---|
| Design identity, governance, and monitoring solutions | 25 to 30 percent |
| Design data storage solutions | 20 to 25 percent |
| Design business continuity solutions | 15 to 20 percent |
| Design infrastructure solutions | 30 to 35 percent |

The page gives only domain-level weights. It gives no weights for individual subtopics.

## Design identity, governance, and monitoring solutions

- **Logging and monitoring:** recommend a logging solution, a solution for routing logs, and a monitoring solution.
- **Authentication and authorization:** recommend an authentication solution, an identity management solution, a solution for authorizing access to Azure resources and to on-premises resources, and a solution to manage secrets, certificates and keys.
- **Governance:** recommend a structure for management groups, subscriptions and resource groups with a tagging strategy, a solution for managing compliance, and a solution for identity governance.

## Design data storage solutions

- **Relational data:** recommend a storage solution, a database service tier and compute tier, a scalability solution and a data protection solution.
- **Semi-structured and unstructured data:** recommend solutions for storing each, a storage solution that balances features, performance and cost, and a solution for protection and durability.
- **Data integration:** recommend a solution for data integration and for data analysis.

## Design business continuity solutions

- **Backup and disaster recovery:** recommend a recovery solution for Azure and hybrid workloads that meets recovery objectives, and backup and recovery solutions for compute, databases and unstructured data.
- **High availability:** recommend a high availability solution for compute, relational data, and semi-structured and unstructured data.

## Design infrastructure solutions

- **Compute:** specify components based on workload requirements, and recommend virtual machine-based, container-based and serverless solutions, and a solution for batch processing.
- **Application architecture:** recommend a messaging architecture, an event-driven architecture, API integration, a caching solution, application configuration management and automated deployment.
- **Migrations:** evaluate a migration solution that uses the Cloud Adoption Framework, evaluate on-premises servers, data and applications, and recommend solutions for migrating workloads to IaaS and PaaS, databases and unstructured data.
- **Network:** recommend connectivity to the internet and to on-premises networks, solutions to optimize network performance and security, and a load-balancing and routing solution.

## Renewal and practice assessment

Associate, expert and specialty certifications expire annually, and you can renew by passing a free online assessment on Microsoft Learn. The page also links to a free practice assessment.

## Suggestion: a study plan

This section is a practical suggestion and not a Microsoft statement. The order follows the weights.

1. Start with infrastructure (30 to 35 percent): compute options, messaging and event-driven design, migrations and networking.
2. Move to identity, governance and monitoring (25 to 30 percent).
3. Cover data storage (20 to 25 percent), then business continuity (15 to 20 percent).
4. For each bullet, write a one-paragraph recommendation and the reason for it, because the verbs on the outline are recommend, evaluate and specify.
5. Finish with the practice assessment.
