MB-800 is the exam for the Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Business Central Functional Consultant Associate certification. This article summarizes the skills-measured outline in the Microsoft Learn study guide, as of June 30, 2026, plus the exam page facts and a study order. It covers the outline only and has no exam questions.

## The candidate profile

Learn describes someone who implements Business Central: they configure the core application, create companies, migrate data and work with implementation teams. The modules listed are financial management, sales, purchasing, inventory and fixed assets. Candidates have applied experience meeting business requirements, configure role centers (insights, cues, action tiles, reports and charts), expand functionality with extensions, and configure and use Copilot capabilities in Business Central, including built-in agents. They understand integration with Microsoft 365, Microsoft Power Platform and Microsoft Dynamics 365.

## Format facts

| Item | From the Learn pages |
| --- | --- |
| Level | Intermediate |
| Time | 100 minutes |
| Delivery | Proctored through Pearson Vue, possible interactive components |
| Languages | English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Arabic (Saudi Arabia) |
| Passing score | 700 or greater |
| Retake | After 24 hours following the first attempt, later waits vary |
| Renewal | Every 12 months by a free online assessment |
| Practice | Practice assessment on AI Skills Navigator, and an exam sandbox |

The study guide says the bullets under each skill illustrate how it is assessed and related topics may be covered, and that most questions cover generally available features.

## The four areas

| Area | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Set up Business Central | 20-25% |
| Configure financials | 30-35% |
| Configure sales and purchasing | 10-15% |
| Perform Business Central operations | 30-35% |

### Set up Business Central (20-25%)

- **Company:** create a company with Assisted Setup, configuration worksheets and configuration packages, journal opening balances, and data migration.
- **Security:** user profiles, new users, permissions and permission sets, security filters, security groups, and security auditing.
- **Core functionality:** reports and report layouts, job queues, number series, and enabling Copilot and agent capabilities.
- **Dimensions:** dimensions and values, global and shortcut dimensions, default dimensions for master data and account types, blocked combinations and default dimension priorities.
- **Workflows:** native workflows, a notification system, and users for approval and workflow user groups.

### Configure financials (30-35%)

- **Financial management:** general ledger setup, accounting periods, payment terms, deferrals, currencies with exchange rates, payment methods.
- **Chart of accounts:** G/L accounts, account categories and subcategories, financial reporting, G/L allocations.
- **Posting groups:** specific and general posting groups, the general posting setup, inventory posting setup, multiple posting groups.
- **Journals and bank accounts:** bank accounts, journal templates with number series, batches, recurring journal templates.
- **Payables:** vendor accounts, Purchases & Payables Setup, payment journals, and how vendors, vendor ledger entries, detailed entries and G/L entries relate.
- **Receivables:** the same for customers, plus cash receipt journals and payment registration.
- **Fixed assets:** settings, depreciation books, classes and subclasses, main assets and components, depreciation methods.

### Configure sales and purchasing (10-15%)

- **Inventory:** setup, item categories, attributes, units of measure, variants, locations, how items, item ledger entries, value entries and G/L entries relate, costing methods, stockkeeping units.
- **Master data:** customer settings such as shipping agents and addresses, vendor settings such as order address and lead time.
- **Pricing and discounts:** purchase prices and line and invoice discounts, sales prices and discounts.

### Perform Business Central operations (30-35%)

- **Basic tasks:** designing versus customizing versus personalizing pages, filters, finding documents and entries, inspecting pages, Edit in Excel, data analysis mode.
- **Purchases and sales:** quotes, orders, receipts and shipments, reversals, invoices, recurring lines, blanket orders, deferrals, item availability.
- **Financial documents:** invoices and credit memos, combining shipments or receipts, corrections, releasing and reopening, prepayment invoices.
- **Journals and payments:** payment and cash receipt journals, payment registration, applying and unapplying entries, reversals, bank reconciliation, recurring journals, allocations, exchange rate adjustment, the Dimension Correction Tool, revaluation.
- **Fixed assets and inventory:** acquisitions, depreciation, disposals, inventory receipts and shipments, transfers, cost adjustment, counting, reclassification.

## What changed on June 30, 2026

The study guide's change log shows:

- Set up Business Central decreased in percentage, and Perform Business Central operations increased. Configure financials did not change.
- Major changes: set up core functionality, perform basic tasks, and process journals and payments.
- The group describing Business Central integrations with other products was removed from the skills list.
- Process inventory transactions is new.

If your study material predates June 2026, compare it with this outline.

## A study order (my advice)

This is my suggestion, not a Microsoft plan:

1. Begin with financials and posting groups. Many operations questions depend on understanding how entries flow to the general ledger.
2. Practice the document flows in a sandbox company: quote, order, receipt or shipment, invoice, correction. Do each once with deferrals and once with a reversal.
3. Learn dimensions early, since they touch setup, journals and corrections.
4. Spend time on the changed areas: Copilot and agents setup, page customization versus personalization, and the new inventory transactions group.
5. Finish with the practice assessment and the exam sandbox, to get used to the interface.
