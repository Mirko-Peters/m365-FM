AB-100 is the exam for the Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert certification. This article summarizes the skills measured outline from the Microsoft Learn study guide dated November 16, 2026, and the exam page. It contains no exam questions.

## Exam facts

- Passing score: 700 or greater.
- The exam page lists the retirement date as none, and the study guide has no beta or retirement notice.
- The exam page says updates to AB-100 are coming on November 16, 2026, for the English version. Localized versions follow about eight weeks later.
- If the exam is not offered in your language, you can request an additional 30 minutes.
- The study guide says most questions cover generally available features, and the exam may include preview features if they are commonly used.
- Price and duration are not stated. The page says only that the price is based on the country or region where the exam is proctored.

## Domains

| Domain | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Plan AI-powered business solutions | 35 to 40% |
| Design AI-powered business solutions | 30 to 35% |
| Deploy AI-powered business solutions | 20 to 25% |

## Plan AI-powered business solutions (35 to 40%)

- **Analyze requirements:** assess the use of agents in task automation, data analytics and decision-making. Review data for grounding, including accuracy, relevance, timeliness, cleanliness and availability. Organize business solution data for other AI systems.
- **Design the overall AI strategy:** apply the AI adoption process from the Cloud Adoption Framework for Azure, design multi-agent solutions with Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio and Microsoft Foundry, define rules and constraints, decide when to build custom agents or extend Microsoft 365 Copilot, decide when custom AI models are needed, provide prompt library and prompt engineering guidelines and include the elements of the Microsoft AI Center of Excellence.
- **Evaluate costs and benefits:** select ROI criteria including total cost of ownership, create an ROI analysis, decide whether to build, buy or extend AI components and implement a model router.

## Design AI-powered business solutions (30 to 35%)

- **Design AI and agents:** business terms and customizations for Copilot in Dynamics 365 apps, connectors for Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, agents for Dynamics 365 Contact Center, task, autonomous and prompt and response agents, Foundry Tools, topics and fallback in Copilot Studio, agents and agent flows, prompt actions, and the Power Platform Well-Architected Framework.
- **Design extensibility:** custom models in Microsoft Foundry, agents in Microsoft 365 Copilot, agent extensibility in Copilot Studio including Model Context Protocol, computer use, reasoning and voice mode behaviors, and agents in Microsoft 365 including Teams and SharePoint.
- **Orchestrate prebuilt agents and apps:** AI features in Dynamics 365 for finance and supply chain and for customer experience and service, Microsoft 365 agents for business scenarios, Copilot for Sales and Copilot for Service, Power Platform AI features, and knowledge sources for finance and operations agent chats and in-app help.

## Deploy AI-powered business solutions (20 to 25%)

- **Analyze, monitor and tune:** monitoring process and tools for agents, backlog and user feedback, AI-based tools for tuning, agent performance metrics and telemetry.
- **Manage testing:** process and metrics to test agents, validation criteria for custom models, Copilot prompt best practices, end-to-end test scenarios across Dynamics 365 apps and a test case strategy using Copilot.
- **Design the ALM process:** for data used in AI models and agents, Copilot Studio agents, connectors and actions, Microsoft Foundry Agent Service, custom AI models and AI in Dynamics 365 apps.
- **Design responsible AI, security, governance, risk and compliance:** security and governance for agents, model security, vulnerabilities including prompt manipulation, responsible AI principles, data residency and movement compliance, access controls on grounding data and model tuning, and audit trails for models and data.

## Certification requirement

In addition to passing AB-100, candidates must earn at least one listed associate-level certification. The page lists 15 options, with examples such as PL-200, PL-400, AI-103 and AB-620.

## Preparation resources named

A training course, Architecting agentic AI business solutions, which the page says is not a test-preparation course. A practice assessment on AI Skills Navigator, which requires sign-in. An exam sandbox.

## A study order

This is a suggestion based on the weights, not a Microsoft recommendation. Start with the plan domain because it carries the largest weight, then design, then deploy. The study guide says bullets show how skills are assessed and related topics may also appear.

## Caution about the exam page

Some text on the exam page does not match this exam, such as a line about information protection and data loss prevention tasks and a reference to a DevOps Engineer Expert certification. Check the study guide before relying on those lines.
