MB-330 is the exam for the Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Functional Consultant Associate certification. Microsoft Learn describes the candidate as someone who designs solutions and configures Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management to meet customer business requirements. This article summarizes the skills-measured outline of the study guide. It does not contain exam questions.

## Check the exam status first

Both the certification page and the study guide carry a warning: this exam will retire on February 1, 2027, at 11:59 PM Central Standard Time. The warning links to a Microsoft Skills Hub blog post. My advice: if you plan to sit the exam, schedule it early enough that a retake window still fits before the retirement date.

## The five areas

The current outline is dated October 21, 2026.

| Area | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Implement product information management | 25-30% |
| Implement inventory and asset management | 20-25% |
| Implement and manage supply chain processes | 15-20% |
| Implement warehouse management and transportation management | 20-25% |
| Implement master planning | 10-15% |

### Product information management (25-30%)

This is the largest area and has four groups:

- **Create and manage products:** products and variants, product templates, bills of materials and versions, category hierarchies, product lifecycle states and attributes.
- **Prerequisites for products:** inventory dimensions (storage, tracking and product dimension groups), item model groups, reservation hierarchies and unit sequence groups.
- **Additional product details:** bar codes and GTIN codes, unit conversions, default order settings and product filter codes for warehouse operations.
- **Inventory pricing and costing:** costing versions for standard and planned costs, price groups, standard-cost items and trade agreements for prices and discounts.

### Inventory and asset management (20-25%)

- **Inventory activities:** journals of many types, transfer orders, inventory closing and adjustment, blocking, batch disposition codes and reports including ABC classification.
- **Quality:** nonconformances, quality associations, the quality control process, quality orders and quarantine orders.
- **Asset management:** work orders, assets and asset types, capacity, preventive and reactive maintenance, asset loans and lifecycle models.
- **Implementing asset management:** maintenance plans, functional locations, maintenance requests, work order consumption and scheduling.

### Supply chain processes (15-20%)

- **Procurement and sourcing:** vendors, requisitions, requests for quotation, purchase orders, purchase agreements, vendor rebates, consignment inventory, delivery schedules, procurement categories, vendor collaboration, returns, product receipt and workflows.
- **Landed cost:** parameters and voyage statuses, voyages with purchase or transfer orders, cost estimations, cost type codes, auto-costs and tracking inbound voyages.
- **Common sales and marketing features:** customers, sales agreements, quotations, orders, returns, commissions, intercompany orders, customer rebates, available-to-promise and capable-to-promise, and product bundles.

### Warehouse and transportation management (20-25%)

- **Configure warehouse management:** sites, warehouses, locations, zones, work policies, location directives, wave, load and work templates, labels, packing dimensions, inbound and outbound processes and inventory status.
- **Perform warehouse processes:** replenishment, load planning workbench, movements through warehouse work, cycle counting, the mobile app for orders, containerization and cross docking.
- **Transportation management:** carriers, loads and shipments, freight bills and invoices, route plans, route and rate engines, dock appointment scheduling and freight reconciliation.
- **Warehouse Management mobile app:** menus and menu items, display settings, detours, installation and configuration and warehouse workers.

### Master planning (10-15%)

- **Configure master planning:** coverage groups and item coverage, parameters and plans, positive and negative days, action messages, delay and time fences, and period templates.
- **Manage master plans:** run planning and analyze results, process planned purchase and transfer orders, the Supply Schedule form, safety margins and safety stock journals.

## What changed in the October 2026 outline

The study guide's change log compares the version before October 21, 2026 with the current one. It lists the five area headings as no change and marks the audience profile as a minor change, together with these groups: manage and process inventory activities, implement landed cost, implement common sales and marketing features, perform warehouse management processes and manage master plans. The other groups are listed as no change. If your study material predates October 2026, compare it to the current outline.

## A study order (my advice)

This is my suggestion, not a Microsoft plan:

1. Start with product information management. It has the biggest share, and inventory dimensions and item model groups feed nearly everything else.
2. Practice warehouse configuration in a test environment. The setup chain of locations, directives and templates is easier to learn by building it.
3. Spend time on the Warehouse Management mobile app group, which is a group of its own in the outline.
4. Use the practice assessment on AI Skills Navigator near the end to find weak areas.
