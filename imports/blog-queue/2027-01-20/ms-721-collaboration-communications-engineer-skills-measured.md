MS-721 is the exam for Microsoft 365 Certified: Collaboration Communications Systems Engineer Associate. It tests whether you can plan, deploy, configure, maintain and troubleshoot Teams meetings, Teams Phone and Teams Rooms and devices. The study guide for skills measured as of April 28, 2026 gives four areas, and Teams Phone has the largest share at 30-35%. This article summarizes the certification page and the study guide. It lists the outline only and has no exam questions.

## Who the exam is for

Learn describes candidates who are responsible for planning, deploying, configuring, maintaining and troubleshooting collaboration communication systems. The listed workloads are Microsoft Teams Phone, Teams meetings, webinars and town halls, Teams Premium, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Teams Rooms, other certified and supported devices, and flexible workplaces.

Candidates should understand networking, telecommunications, audio and video and meeting room technologies, and identity and access management. Learn names these management tools: the Microsoft Teams admin center, the Microsoft Teams Rooms Pro Management portal, PowerShell and the Microsoft Call Quality Dashboard. The role works with Teams administrators, identity and access administrators, Microsoft 365 administrators and owners of other workloads such as facilities managers, network engineers, security engineers, device manufacturers, telephony providers and certified solution providers.

## Skills measured at a glance

| Area | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Plan and design collaboration communications systems | 20-25% |
| Configure and manage Teams meetings, webinars, and town halls | 15-20% |
| Configure and manage Teams Phone | 30-35% |
| Configure and manage Teams Rooms and devices | 20-25% |

A small inconsistency to know about: the study guide uses the heading Implement and configure Teams Phone for the detailed section, and Configure and manage Teams Phone in the summary list. They describe the same area.

## Area 1: Plan and design (20-25%)

- **Teams meetings:** planning meetings, webinars and town halls, meeting policies and settings, PSTN audio conferencing, when to use Teams Premium including Microsoft 365 Copilot, meeting types for business requirements, policy assignment methods and policy packages.
- **Teams Phone and PSTN connectivity:** licensing for users, shared devices and resource accounts, PSTN connectivity requirements, Teams Phone Mobile, Operator Connect, SMS with Calling Plan numbers, billing methods including pay-as-you-go, Shared Calling, number planning and porting, call flows for auto attendants and call queues, dynamic emergency calling, devices, Survivable Branch Appliances, Direct Routing and Session Border Controllers, compliance recording, certified contact center solutions and the Queues app.
- **Teams-certified devices:** room configurations including bring your own device, Teams Rooms Basic versus Pro, Windows versus Android platforms, interoperability such as Cloud Video Interop, Direct Guest Join and SIP Guest Join, resource account requirements, and Intune and AOSP enrollment requirements.
- **Network preparation:** the Teams Network Assessment Tool, eCDN, the Network planner, architecture, QoS policies, media bit rate, network roaming policy, network topology, tenant data upload for the Call Quality Dashboard and reporting labels for Call Analytics.

## Area 2: Meetings, webinars and town halls (15-20%)

- **Meetings:** meeting policies and settings, avatars, apps, polls, Q&A, breakout rooms, templates, controlled-content policies, Cloud Video Interop policies, recording and transcription to support Copilot and Teams Premium, and virtual appointments.
- **Audio Conferencing:** the default bridge, bridge numbers including toll-free numbers, assigning numbers to users and the numbers shown in invitations.
- **Webinars:** roles, who can schedule, policies, in-org versus public webinars, advanced features and attendee interaction.
- **Town halls:** settings, Microsoft eCDN, advanced features and town halls with Teams, Viva Engage and Microsoft Stream.

## Area 3: Teams Phone (30-35%)

- **Policies:** dial plans, calling, call park, caller ID, IP phone, mobility, voicemail, call hold, outbound restrictions, inbound blocking, unassigned number routing, compliance recording and Shared Calling.
- **Auto attendants and call queues:** resource accounts and licenses, phone numbers, routing methods and call priorities, business hours and holidays, music on hold, caller ID, voice applications policies, callback and the Queues app.
- **Emergency calling:** locations, routing and calling policies, networks and locations, validating addresses and troubleshooting dynamic emergency addresses.
- **Licenses, numbers and policies:** Teams Phone Standard, Calling Plan, numbers via the admin center and PowerShell, policy packages and group policy.
- **Calling features:** Cloud Voicemail, group call pickup, forwarding, simultaneous ring, delegation, Audio Conferencing and private lines.
- **Direct Routing:** SBC connectivity, PSTN gateway, usage records, voice routes and routing policies, validation, Location-Based Routing, Local Media Optimization, translation rules, SBA policies and troubleshooting in the admin center.

## Area 4: Teams Rooms and devices (20-25%)

- **Manage and maintain:** device settings, local network settings, updates, Conditional Access MFA exceptions for resource accounts, Teams Rooms Pro Management portal, Intune requirements, common area, user and conference phones, device tags, SIP Gateway, health monitoring and troubleshooting of authentication, updates, remote provisioning and proximity join.
- **Android devices:** IP phone policies, configuration profiles, hotline, remote deployment, custom displays and Intune requirements for AOSP and MDEP devices.
- **Teams Rooms on Windows:** domain group policy exclusions, custom displays, XML configuration and peripherals.
- **Optional features:** HDMI ingest, content camera, casting, proximity join, room remote, Direct Guest Join, hot desking and AI-enabled voice and video for shared space devices.
- **BYOD spaces and bookable desks:** room types, inventory, device assignment, bookable desks and monitoring.

## Exam logistics

| Item | What Learn says |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 100 minutes |
| Format | Proctored, with possible interactive components |
| Languages | English, Chinese (Simplified), French, German, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish |
| Retake | After 24 hours for the first retake, longer waits for later retakes |
| Passing score | 700 or greater |
| Renewal | Every 12 months, free online assessment |

The page recommends registering with a personal Microsoft account, because exam records tied to a work or school account can be lost if you leave the organization. No price is listed, since it depends on the country or region where the exam is proctored. Localized exams are updated about eight weeks after English, and Learn says you can request an additional 30 minutes if the exam is not offered in your language.

## A preparation plan built on the weights

This plan is my advice, not a Microsoft recommendation.

1. **Teams Phone first.** It carries up to 35%. Build a lab or use a trial tenant to create policies, a resource account, an auto attendant and a call queue.
2. **Plan and design.** Practice choosing between Calling Plan, Operator Connect and Direct Routing for a given scenario.
3. **Devices.** Learn the differences between Teams Rooms on Windows and Android and the Intune side.
4. **Meetings.** Work through meeting, webinar and town hall policies.
5. **Network.** Read the QoS and Call Quality Dashboard topics.
6. **Interface.** Try the Exam Sandbox so the question formats are no surprise.

## Typical mistakes (my advice)

- Treating the exam as only about meetings. Phone and devices together carry more than half.
- Skipping the planning topics because they sound theoretical.
- Skipping PowerShell. The audience profile names it as a management tool.
