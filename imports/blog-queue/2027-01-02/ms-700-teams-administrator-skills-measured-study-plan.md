MS-700, Managing Microsoft Teams, measures how well a Teams administrator can plan, implement, manage and maintain Teams. The current skills measured on Microsoft Learn are dated October 27, 2026 and split into four areas. The largest is configuring and managing the Teams environment at 40-45%. This article walks through the outline and turns it into a study plan. It covers the outline only and has no exam questions.

## Who the exam is for

Microsoft Learn describes the candidate as a Teams administrator who plans, implements, manages and maintains internal and external collaboration and communication solutions with Teams. You are responsible for teams, channels, chat, apps, events and calling. You work with owners of identity, licensing, information management, user adoption, security, compliance, devices and networking. Learn expects a working knowledge of how Teams integrates with Microsoft 365 services.

## Skills measured at a glance

| Area | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Configure and manage a Teams environment | 40-45% |
| Manage teams, channels, chats, and apps | 20-25% |
| Manage meetings and calling | 15-20% |
| Monitor, report on, and troubleshoot Teams | 15-20% |

Learn adds two notes. The bullets under each skill illustrate how it is assessed, and related topics may be covered. Most questions cover generally available features, but commonly used preview features can appear.

## Area 1: Configure and manage a Teams environment (40-45%)

This area has five groups of skills:

- **Plan network settings:** bandwidth for voice, video, meetings and events, Network planner, ports and protocols, and the Teams Network Assessment Tool and Microsoft 365 network connectivity test tool.
- **Security and compliance:** licensing requirements, alert policies, Teams administrator roles, Defender for Office 365 threat policies, retention, sensitivity labels, DLP, Conditional Access, Information Barriers, Communication Compliance and Insider Risk Management.
- **Governance:** lifecycle management licensing, where Teams stores content, update policies, policy packages, group creation settings, expiration and naming policies, archiving, restoring deleted groups, access reviews, and PowerShell and Microsoft Graph operations.
- **External collaboration:** External access, guest access, SharePoint and OneDrive sharing, Microsoft Entra external collaboration settings, removing guests, shared channels, B2B direct connect and multitenant organizations.
- **Clients and devices:** licensing for Teams Phone and devices, configuration profiles, Teams Rooms accounts, firmware, device tags, remote sign-in and Teams for VDI.

## Area 2: Manage teams, channels, chats, and apps (20-25%)

- **Create and manage teams:** Teams Advisor, creating teams with the admin center, client, PowerShell or Graph, templates, membership, privacy and sensitivity, and frontline teams.
- **Channels and chats:** standard, private and shared channel types, channel settings and policies, and messaging policies.
- **Apps:** org-wide app settings, app setup and permission policies, consent and blocking, extensibility options, app store purchasing and appearance, and uploading apps.

## Area 3: Manage meetings and calling (15-20%)

- **Meetings and events:** choosing between Appointments, webinars, town halls and meetings, meeting settings including Microsoft 365 Copilot, templates, meeting and customization policies, and event settings.
- **Teams Phone:** phone numbers for users, services and conferencing bridges, voice settings and policies, voicemail, auto attendants, call queues and calling policies.

## Area 4: Monitor, report on, and troubleshoot Teams (15-20%)

- **Monitor and report:** voice and meeting quality, alert rules, usage reports, team creation and deletion, guest access, the network connectivity test tool and feedback policies.
- **Troubleshoot:** client-side logs, clearing the client cache, self-help diagnostics, installation and update issues, client health in the admin center, sign-in, Copilot and AI experiences, and meeting join problems.

## What changed on October 27, 2026

The change log lists minor changes to Plan network settings for Teams, Create and manage teams, Manage apps for Teams and Troubleshoot audio, video, and client issues. The external collaboration row has no change label in the table. The audience profile and the top-level areas are marked no change. If you studied from an older outline, re-read these four groups first.

## A study plan built on the weights

1. **Weeks 1-3: environment (40-45%).** Spend the most time here. Work in a test tenant with governance policies, guest settings and external access. Learn which admin center controls which setting.
2. **Week 4: teams, channels and apps (20-25%).** Create a team from a template, apply a messaging policy, and configure an app permission policy.
3. **Week 5: meetings and calling (15-20%).** Set up a meeting policy, a webinar or town hall, an auto attendant and a call queue.
4. **Week 6: monitoring and troubleshooting (15-20%).** Open the Call Quality dashboard, collect client logs, and use self-help diagnostics.
5. **Final days:** take the free practice assessment and try the exam sandbox.

These week counts are a suggestion, not a Microsoft recommendation.
