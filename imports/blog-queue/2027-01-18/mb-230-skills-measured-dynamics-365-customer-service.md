Exam MB-230, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service Functional Consultant, is active. The exam page shows a passing score of 700 and lists Retirement date none. According to the study guide, the skills measured as of March 11, 2026 are weighted as follows: manage cases in Customer Service 51 to 55 percent, configure representative experience and routing 25 to 30 percent, and extend Customer Service 15 to 20 percent. This article reproduces that outline and says what to take from it. It does not contain exam questions.

## Status of the exam

From the exam page:

- Passing score: 700.
- Languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified).
- Retirement date: none.
- Part of the requirements for Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Customer Service Functional Consultant Associate.
- The English version was updated on October 3, 2025. Localized versions are updated about eight weeks later, though the page says there may be delays.

The exam page also contains an older-style summary sentence that names tasks such as managing entitlements and SLAs, scheduling, multi-session experiences and connected customer service. The study guide's current outline is organized differently, so I follow the study guide below. The study guide says it includes two versions of the skills measured depending on when you take the exam. The text I read gave the version as of March 11, 2026, so check the guide for the version that applies on your exam date.

## Who the exam is for

Microsoft's audience profile describes a functional consultant with customer service expertise, responsible for solutions focused on customer service, quality, reliability, efficiency and customer satisfaction. The role designs and implements service management processes and automation with solution architects and developers, and coordinates with customer engagement administrators on case management, Dynamics 365 Copilot in Service, collaboration, knowledge management and customer feedback. Strong applied knowledge of cases, knowledge management, queues, SLAs, Customer Service applications, Customer Voice and routing is expected.

## Manage cases in Customer Service (51 to 55 percent)

**Define and configure foundational Customer Service components**
- Implement and monitor record creation and update rules
- Configure and modify the case resolution experience
- Configure and manage security roles
- Configure the timeline control, including records, card forms and highlights

**Create and administer knowledge management**
- Configure internal knowledge search capabilities
- Configure tables for knowledge management
- Manage translated knowledge articles
- Integrate and search external knowledge sources
- Configure AI suggestions and Copilot-led knowledge article creation

**Manage collaboration**
- Configure Microsoft Teams chat
- Configure Join a Teams call for specific users
- Configure suggested contacts in Microsoft Teams, including AI and rules-based suggestions
- Configure the Teams collaboration experience

**Manage agents and Copilot in Service features**
- Configure Ask a Question, including filtering content
- Configure case, timeline and conversation summaries
- Configure Draft a Response
- Configure the Case Management Agent
- Integrate agents with Customer Service, including plug-ins and knowledge sources

**Create and manage SLAs for cases**
- Configure SLA settings
- Implement actions by using Power Automate
- Apply SLAs
- Create and manage SLA items, including key performance indicators (KPIs), warning actions, success actions and applicability
- Configure a timer control on a form
- Create SLA KPIs

## Configure representative experience and routing (25 to 30 percent)

**Configure routing**
- Configure workstreams
- Configure user settings
- Manage capacity profiles
- Configure work classification and assignments, including rules
- Configure basic routing rulesets
- Configure skills-based routing and skill finder models
- Configure queues
- Configure routing for records

**Configure productivity tools**
- Configure scripts, including slugs
- Create macros

**Configure the Copilot Service workspace**
- Configure session templates
- Create experience profiles
- Configure the Inbox, including creating custom views
- Configure application tab templates

## Extend Customer Service (15 to 20 percent)

**Configure the data model and UI components**
- Create and configure forms
- Create and configure views
- Configure model-driven app components
- Create and configure columns
- Configure alerts and in-app notifications
- Create or modify a table
- Configure relationships
- Configure Dataverse search
- Create and manage email templates

**Capture customer feedback by using Customer Voice**
- Trigger distribution of a survey
- Personalize a survey
- Populate survey results against cases and conversations

## Notes from Microsoft on the outline

The study guide says the bullets after each skill illustrate how the skill is assessed and that related topics may be covered. It also says most questions cover features that are generally available, and the exam may contain questions on preview features if they are commonly used.

The change log in the guide compares the outline before and after March 11, 2026. It lists the audience profile as a minor change, shows no percentage change for the representative experience and routing group, and lists minor changes to the routing and Copilot Service workspace objectives.

## How to use the outline

This section is my advice, not a Microsoft statement.

1. **Spend time where the weight is.** Over half the outline is about cases, so make sure you can build the case flow end to end in a test environment.
2. **Treat knowledge management as its own block.** It has five objectives, including AI suggestions and external sources.
3. **Practice routing hands-on.** Workstreams, capacity profiles, queues and skills-based routing are easier to remember after you have configured them in a sandbox.
4. **Do not skip the smaller group.** Extend Customer Service is the smallest group, yet it contains basics such as forms, views, columns and relationships that you use everywhere else.
5. **Tick the list.** Copy the objectives into a checklist and rate yourself on each as not seen, read about, or done in a test environment.
6. **Use Microsoft's own prep tools.** The study guide links a free practice assessment and the exam sandbox. This article does not cover questions.
7. **Recheck before booking.** The page says the exam changes periodically, so read the outline on the day you schedule.
