Microsoft's post "What's new in Microsoft Copilot: October 2026" groups the month's changes into user capabilities and IT admin capabilities. Most items arrive in stages: Frontier or Insiders first, then a worldwide release in November or later. The post says its dates are tentative and subject to change. It was published on 30 October 2026 and updated on 13 November 2026. This article sorts the items by who is affected and states the stage Microsoft gives for each. It does not cover items the post does not list.

## How to read the stages

- **Frontier** means early access through the [Microsoft Frontier program](https://www.m365.fm/entity/frontier-program/). Microsoft Learn says an admin controls which users get Frontier features and that they are preview, subject to change and possibly outside standard support.
- **Insiders** refers to preview builds of the Office apps.
- **Worldwide** means broad availability.
- **Public preview** means a preview that is open for use but not final.

## Copilot app

| Item | What Microsoft says | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Home navigation | Brings Chat and Cowork into one place with a shared navigation pane | Frontier in October, worldwide in November |
| Projects | Groups chats, tasks, instructions and files for ongoing work, with a central Outputs view | Cowork in October, Copilot Chat in November |
| Autopilot | A cloud-based personal agent with its own account, mailbox and files, governed through Agent 365 | Frontier in November |
| Skills in Copilot Chat | Extends Cowork skills to Chat, with skills created in natural language and shared skills under Agents and Plugins | November |

[Cowork](https://www.m365.fm/entity/copilot-cowork/) also gets role-based plugins for Sales, Service, Education, Legal and Finance, OneNote and Planner capabilities that write back with user approval, a task catalog and the option to reference earlier chats and tasks.

## Copilot Workspaces

The post says Workspaces evolved from [Copilot Notebooks](https://www.m365.fm/entity/copilot-notebooks/). Existing notebooks become workspaces. Listed changes are shared workspaces with live cursors (Frontier in October), Office file creation and editing (Frontier in October, worldwide in January), updated Audio Overview with topic-based episodes, sensitivity label inheritance and a Critique and Debate format (Frontier in October, worldwide in November), and more reference file types: JPG, PNG, CSV and TSV.

## Microsoft 365 apps

**Excel.** Accounting skills for reconciliations, journal entries and financial analysis, with reversible changes (Frontier in October, worldwide in November). A clean data skill that finds duplicates, inconsistent formatting and wrong data types and recommends fixes that you approve (October). An interactive canvas, an auto-updating report of visuals, metrics and insights (Insiders in October, worldwide in November).

**PowerPoint.** A model update for better layouts and visual hierarchy, simpler Brand Kit setup, publishing and unpublishing official Brand Kits, a create-interactive-slide feature, a template assessment skill and a record-a-skill feature that saves repeated tasks as reusable skills. All are listed for October.

**Word.** A Legal plugin with more than 20 legal skills, document-specific formatting and design, and Markdown support including .md and .docx conversion on the web. All are listed for October.

**Across Word, Excel and PowerPoint.** Microsoft lists a refreshed design that matches the Copilot app, and Copilot editing for Word, Excel and PowerPoint in GCC.

## OneDrive, SharePoint, Edge and Teams

- **OneDrive.** A Prompt Gallery with prompts in Create, Analyze, Find and Collaborate categories, opened from the Copilot button.
- **SharePoint.** A knowledge base that builds shared knowledge from emails, messages, meetings and SharePoint content, in Frontier in October.
- **Edge.** A simplified Copilot experience with an updated icon and a streamlined side pane.
- **Teams.** Intelligent recap without saving a transcript, which needs a Copilot license for the organizer to turn it on or off (public preview in May, worldwide in October). For the Queues app and Teams Phone, intelligent call recaps and Copilot in shared call history, both listed for September.
- **Connectors.** Life sciences connectors through Microsoft Marketplace, with no date given.

## IT admin capabilities

- **Insights Agent** answers natural-language questions about agent usage and adoption. Insights Agent V2 is an early-access preview in Frontier that shows thumbs ratings and comments to agent owners after an admin enables feedback sharing.
- **Workforce Insights Agent** gives admins a workspace to set organizational definitions and validate how AI reasons over that context. It rolls out to Frontier in November.
- **Purview retention for Copilot memory.** Data Lifecycle Management policies can preserve saved memories.
- **Purview DLP for Cowork.** One policy across Copilot chat and Cowork can block references to sensitivity-labeled content, block prompts with supported sensitive data and stop sensitive prompts from going to Bing. Public preview in October.

## What to do with this list

1. **Sort by stage.** Separate items that are already worldwide from items still in Frontier or Insiders. Only the first group is safe to promise to all users.
2. **Check the Frontier setting.** Learn gives the admin path: Copilot, Settings, View all, search for Frontier, then Copilot Frontier. The options are No access, All users, or Specific users and groups.
3. **Review governance.** Purview retention for memory and the Cowork DLP policy matter before you widen use of agents.
4. **Verify licenses.** The post gives licensing only for the Teams recap item. Check each product page for the rest.
5. **Recheck dates.** Microsoft says the dates are tentative, and the post was updated after its first publication.

## What this post does not say

The post does not list prices, and it does not state license requirements for most items. It gives no date for the life sciences connectors. I did not find a Microsoft source in this research that counts the October updates as nine items, so this article follows the post's own grouping.
