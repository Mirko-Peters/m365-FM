## FAQ

**What is the difference between Dataverse and a regular database?**

Dataverse stores facts plus meaning, while a regular database mainly stores facts. In the episode, Mirko Peters calls it a business semantics engine: tables, columns and relationships carry ownership, security boundaries, metadata and lifecycle behavior that travel with the data across model-driven apps, canvas apps, Power Automate, Power Pages and AI agents.

**When should you move from SharePoint lists to Dataverse?**

Move when the data stops being a simple list and becomes operational truth with relationships, lifecycle and policy. The episode names four breaking points for SharePoint lists: relational complexity, integrity gaps such as orphaned records, scale thresholds and delegation limits, and governance ambiguity, because row ownership is not a built-in structural primitive there.

**Why does the Dataverse data model matter for Copilot and AI agents?**

AI can only use the meaning the model makes explicit. Copilot does not invent meaning, it consumes it from table and column names, descriptions, relationships, ownership and security. Precise models allow simple prompts, while vague ones force long ones. Agents also need structured tables for runs, decisions, exceptions and outcomes to behave consistently over time.
