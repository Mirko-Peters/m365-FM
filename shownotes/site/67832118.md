## FAQ

**What is the difference between standard, private and shared channels in Microsoft Teams?**

Standard channels are visible to every member of the parent team, and their files sit in a folder of the team's SharePoint site. Private channels are limited to a selected subset of team members with separate storage. Shared channels let selected internal and external people into one channel without joining the whole team.

**When should I use a shared channel instead of adding guests to a team?**

Use a shared channel when external partners, vendors or clients need one conversation space but not your whole team. With B2B direct connect, configured in Entra ID cross-tenant access settings, they keep their own identity and tenant, so you avoid creating guest accounts that linger in your directory after the work ends.

**How do I keep Teams channels from oversharing sensitive files?**

Pick the channel type at project kickoff based on who must see the content, and test it with a sample file. Restrict who can create channels, apply sensitivity labels so protection follows files, use Conditional Access and DLP for external users, and schedule regular access reviews to remove people who no longer need access.
