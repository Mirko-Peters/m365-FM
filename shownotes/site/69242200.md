## FAQ

**What is the difference between cloud adoption, migration and strategy?**

Adoption is exposure, migration is movement and strategy is choice. Adoption proves the organization can use the platform, migration proves workloads moved, but only strategy turns business outcomes into enforced trade-offs. The episode argues that blurring these words lets enterprises declare success after a lift and shift without changing decision rights, funding or accountability.

**Why does moving workloads to Azure often fail to speed up delivery?**

Because the on-prem operating model moves along with the workloads. Provisioning gets faster, but the same review boards, change windows and ticket queues still gate production. The episode calls this the execution cliff: cloud becomes expensive hosting until decision rights are redefined and platform teams ship guardrails as self-service paved roads.

**What does a mature Azure landing zone actually include?**

It includes enforced default paths, not just a reference deployment. Management groups carry inherited policy, subscriptions act as boundaries for cost, access and blast radius, and Azure Policy uses deny, modify and deploy-if-not-exists effects. Subscription vending delivers tags, diagnostics and connectivity up front, and the landing zone is versioned and owned like a living product.
