## FAQ

**Is Copilot App Builder the same as Power Apps canvas apps?**

No. App Builder borrows the canvas app workflow of defining a schema, painting the UI and binding controls, but it is a personal automation lane. It defaults to SharePoint lists, inherits the creator's permissions and largely bypasses ALM pipelines, so it lacks Dataverse, role-based security and the auditing that enterprise apps need.

**When should an App Builder app move to Dataverse?**

Move it as soon as it crosses thresholds you published on day one. Warning signals include more than one list with cross-list joins, approvals needing audit trails, external APIs, customer or finance data, consistent historical reporting, or a second team asking to use it. According to the episode, hitting any two means you should promote it.

**How do you stop Copilot from leaking confidential data?**

Enable Copilot-specific DLP with sensitivity labels and block processing of confidential and highly confidential content unless the agent and destination are allowed. Back this with quarterly access reviews, cleanup of open links, Restricted SharePoint Search during rollout waves, admin approval for email and external connectors, and monitoring of the Purview audit stream for Copilot activity.
