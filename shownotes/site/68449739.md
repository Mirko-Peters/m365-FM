## FAQ

**Why is dumping CSV files into OneLake a problem?**

It leaves Microsoft Fabric unable to reason over your data. CSV snapshots without semantic models or relationships are isolated files with no shared meaning, so even Copilot cannot tell what revenue or a region code means in your schema. The warehouse becomes a static archive of past states instead of a system that interprets what is happening.

**How do you set up a Fabric data agent?**

Start by building semantic models that declare relationships, then define a data agent and point it at your Fabric data. Give it organization-specific instructions about what terms like risk or revenue mean, which sources are authoritative and what needs human approval. Finally, connect it to Copilot Studio so employees can ask questions in natural language.

**How does governance work for data agents in Microsoft Fabric?**

Governance limits what agents can see and do while keeping their output traceable. Purview controls who can see what, data loss prevention stops confidential data from leaking, and Fabric's built-in governance carries lineage, classification and access rules with each dataset. Decisions needing judgment escalate to humans, and lineage lets you trace any conclusion back to its source.
