## FAQ

**Can Power Pages work without Dataverse?**

No, Power Pages depends completely on Dataverse. The site's web pages, files and the contacts who log in are all stored there. Dataverse can reach external data through virtual tables and connectors, and there are links to Power BI and SharePoint, but the best fit is data that already lives in Dataverse.

**How is Power Pages licensed?**

Power Pages is licensed by monthly users, bought in packs for authenticated and for anonymous visitors. Each person who logs in counts once for that month. A pay-as-you-go option also exists at roughly double the price. Microsoft does not block extra users, but an under-licensed site runs on a smaller server and becomes slow.

**How do external users log in and get permissions in Power Pages?**

External users sign in as Dataverse contacts using providers such as Microsoft Entra External ID, Google, LinkedIn or Facebook. Access is controlled through web roles and table permissions rather than Dataverse security roles, so visibility depends on how the logged-in contact relates to records, for example cases linked to their parent account.
