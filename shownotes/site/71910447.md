## FAQ

**When do microservices make sense for a team?**

Microservices make sense when a concrete problem requires physical separation today. The episode names uneven scaling, compliance isolation, large teams with stable ownership and domains settled enough for durable contracts. For teams under roughly 20 to 30 engineers, it says coordination cost often outweighs the scaling benefit, so vague reasons like future proofing are too weak.

**What is a modular monolith?**

A modular monolith is one deployable unit with clear internal boundaries split by business capability, such as orders, pricing, billing and inventory. It keeps the speed of local calls, one deployment path and local transactions. Because the capability boundaries already exist in the code, a module can still be extracted into a service later if needed.

**Are distributed transactions supported in modern .NET?**

Yes, but only on Windows and only when explicitly enabled. Starting with .NET 7, you must set implicit distributed transactions to true, or you get an exception. The episode warns that local transactions can be promoted unexpectedly when an extra connection appears and recommends local transactions with outbox and event patterns instead.
