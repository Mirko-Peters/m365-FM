## FAQ

**What do I need to make external phone calls from Microsoft Teams?**

You need a Teams Phone license plus a connection to the public telephone network. Internal calls between Teams users are free, but reaching customer mobiles or landlines requires one of three options: Microsoft Calling Plans, Operator Connect through your existing carrier, or Direct Routing with your own session border controller.

**What is the difference between Calling Plans, Operator Connect and Direct Routing?**

Calling Plans buy numbers and minutes directly from Microsoft, Operator Connect lets your existing carrier plug straight into Teams, and Direct Routing uses your own session border controller. Calling Plans are simplest but only available in certain countries, while Direct Routing offers the most control but needs technical expertise to set up.

**Is Teams Phone cheaper than a traditional PBX?**

Often yes, but the license price alone is misleading. The episode warns that real costs can be two to three times the headline license once calling minutes, international calls and partner support are added. You also save on upfront hardware, but should budget for a reliable internet connection and get a full quote.
