This episode is for admins and security owners who want real protection, walking through discovery, classification, policy design and monitoring as one layered DLP strategy.

## Key takeaways

## FAQ

**Where should I start when setting up DLP in Microsoft 365?**

Start with discovery, not rules. Content Explorer shows where sensitive information types are stored across SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and Exchange, and Activity Explorer shows how those items are shared or sent. Building this map first lets you target policies at real data flows instead of guessing which workloads hold risky content.

**Why is labeling every file as sensitive a bad idea?**

Over-classification hides the files that really matter and frustrates users. When every document gets the same heavy restrictions, alerts lose meaning and people look for workarounds such as personal email or consumer storage. The episode recommends built-in and custom sensitive information types, with auto labeling for high-risk data and manual labels where judgment helps.

**How do I reduce DLP false positives?**

Review policy matches and audit logs to see exactly which items triggered and why, then tune the conditions instead of disabling the rule. Combining conditions, such as sensitive content plus an external recipient, cuts noise further. In one example, internal finance reports were mistaken for risky data until the match conditions were adjusted.
