## FAQ

**How do you automate GRC reports with Power Automate?**

You build a flow in three steps: connect, standardize and aggregate. Prebuilt connectors link the SharePoint evidence library, the Excel risk register and the Dataverse incident table. The flow then normalizes dates, severity scales and category labels, and pushes everything into one SharePoint list that triggers draft reports in Word or Power BI.

**Why are manual GRC reports risky for compliance teams?**

Manual GRC reports are risky because every retyped number or emailed draft adds a chance of inconsistency, and the data is often outdated by the time leadership or auditors see it. Incidents logged after the snapshot stay invisible, and skilled analysts spend their hours reconciling formats instead of analyzing risks or advising the business.

**What can you do with GRC automation beyond generating reports?**

Beyond reports, the same flows can produce tailored versions for executives and auditors from one data set, escalate high-severity Dataverse incidents automatically through Teams or email, and feed live Power BI dashboards. With continuously standardized data, repeated small incidents can reveal an eroding control early, and audits start from an always-ready evidence set.
