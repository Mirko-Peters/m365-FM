## FAQ

**Why do Viva Connections dashboards often fail to get used?**

They usually fail because content is generic and not tied to how teams actually work. Out of the box dashboards often show a few SharePoint pages, news and static links, without audience targeting. Employees already reach their files and chats through Teams or Outlook, so a dashboard with nothing personally useful is quickly ignored.

**What are Adaptive Card Extensions in Viva Connections?**

Adaptive Card Extensions, or ACEs, are cards built with the SharePoint Framework that show live data on the Viva Connections dashboard, including data from external systems such as ticketing tools. They work best as entry points into business processes, such as opening ticket details or triggering approvals, rather than as static feeds.

**What should you consider when building SPFx web parts for Viva Connections?**

Focus web parts on workflows and data people need almost daily, such as ticket status, approvals or KPI summaries. Plan the development environment carefully, scope permissions so sensitive data reaches only the right audience, limit API calls that slow load times, and test on mobile, where layouts and performance often differ from desktop.
