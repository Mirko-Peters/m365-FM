## FAQ

**How can a SOC tell whether a Copilot alert is a real data leak?**

By correlating signals instead of reading the alert alone. Purview DSPM for AI adds the file's sensitivity label history and the requesting identity, while Defender XDR shows endpoint actions such as syncs or uploads. Lined up, a label downgrade, Copilot access and a file landing in a personal account become a timeline that reveals intent.

**Can Microsoft Purview stop Copilot from processing confidential files?**

Yes, with label-based DLP policies. Purview DLP lets you write a policy so that content labeled highly confidential or legal only is off limits to Copilot, no matter how the prompt is worded. Because Copilot respects Purview sensitivity labels, the label becomes part of the defense, and tampering with it becomes an actionable trigger.

**How do you keep forensic evidence of Copilot prompts?**

You have to turn on auditing and retention for AI interactions, because prompt records are not captured automatically everywhere. Once configured, Purview records the prompt wording, the related sensitivity label and the retention rules, and eDiscovery can export both the source document and the Copilot chat, giving legal and compliance teams a reliable timeline.
