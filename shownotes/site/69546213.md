## FAQ

**What does ViDA change for intra-EU B2B invoicing?**

ViDA moves intra-EU B2B VAT from periodic returns to transaction-level controls. Suppliers must issue structured e-invoices within 10 days and report invoice data digitally in near real time, while purchasers report their side as well. Summary invoices remain possible only within the same month and a short issuing window afterwards.

**Why is an e-invoicing connector not enough for ViDA compliance?**

A connector only handles transport, while ViDA inspects the data and process behind every invoice. If master data, VAT IDs, tax codes or credit note links are wrong in Dynamics 365 Finance, the payload fails before it leaves. You also need immutable payloads, acknowledgement capture, an owned exception queue and evidence packs.

**Does the expanded One Stop Shop reduce VAT cash flow risk?**

No, OSS reduces administration but not treasury risk. Output VAT is reported through OSS, yet local input VAT cannot be deducted in the OSS return and must be recovered through a separate, slower refund route. Companies with significant local costs may therefore keep some local registrations and need daily VAT cash forecasting.
