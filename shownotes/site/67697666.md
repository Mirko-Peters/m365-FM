## FAQ

**What is the difference between agentic AI and AI autocomplete?**

Autocomplete predicts the next line and forgets everything once the prediction is made, while an AI agent keeps context, memory and goals across the project. Agents can coordinate in specialised roles such as design, review and testing, and adapt when conditions change, for example when a dependency version or a cost constraint shifts.

**Can AI agents manage Azure infrastructure without human oversight?**

No, agents reduce manual provisioning and drift correction but work inside guardrails humans define. Teams set approved regions, cost ceilings and compliance boundaries, and the agent acts as an enforcer rather than an unconstrained operator. The episode recommends starting with a non-critical workload or a single service such as storage or logging before expanding.

**Do AI testing agents fix bugs on their own?**

No, testing agents do not rewrite production code or patch flaws themselves. They surface reproducible scenarios with diagnostic detail, such as race conditions during scaling events or failures from unusual inputs, and may suggest a fix. Developers or QA engineers review proposed tests before promoting them, and the repair decision stays with humans.
