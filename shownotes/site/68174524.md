## FAQ

**Is there a perfect prompt for Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

No, there is no perfect one-shot prompt. Overloaded requests with many goals produce bland, compromised output. Microsoft's guidance is to state the goal, give relevant context, set expectations and name sources, then refine through follow-ups, for example summarizing a meeting first and reformatting it as bullet points for a specific team afterwards.

**How much context should I give Copilot in a prompt?**

Give Copilot only the context that defines your environment, not the whole handbook. A few company values, key vocabulary terms or the skeleton of a template you always use are enough to shift drafts from generic filler to your house style. Pointing Copilot to the right documents grounds it further.

**Do newer models like GPT-5 make prompting skills less important?**

No, newer models make prompting discipline more important. They retain context longer and follow tone shifts better, but vague or contradictory prompts now yield polished, convincing output that is still irrelevant. Verification matters more, and you can ask Copilot to point out which emails, meeting notes or documents support each section.
