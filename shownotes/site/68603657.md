## FAQ

**Why does Microsoft 365 Copilot give generic answers about company policies?**

Out of the box, Copilot is grounded in Microsoft Graph and public knowledge, which favours breadth over your organisation's depth. It can explain DLP in theory but cannot apply your exception list, regional escalation SOPs or legal memos unless a specialist agent brings that content and logic into the conversation.

**What is a custom engine agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

A custom engine agent is an agent you build with your own orchestration, such as Semantic Kernel or LangChain, connected to your data and rules. It combines retrieval, for example Azure AI Search, actions against internal APIs, and guardrails like tenant controls, data scopes and responsible AI filters, and answers with citations.

**How do I make my custom agent show up in Copilot Chat?**

Update the app manifest schema to version 1.22, add the Copilot agents capability and declare custom engine agents if you bring your own orchestration. Add conversation starters and actions, package the app, and deploy it through the Teams admin center or app catalog to a pilot group, then verify with real scenarios.
