## FAQ

**Can Microsoft Entra ID replace third-party identity tools like Okta and Duo?**

Yes, for most E5 tenants Entra ID P2 already covers what these tools are bought for. Conditional Access, risk-based policies, MFA and Privileged Identity Management are native. The episode estimates a 5,000-user organization spends over $1 million a year on a separate identity stack, plus integration and reconciliation overhead that consolidation removes.

**Why do so many Microsoft 365 Copilot deployments underperform?**

Most Copilot deployments underperform because they sit on top of architectural debt. Chaotic SharePoint sites with duplicate files, overshared permissions and inconsistent DLP policies lead Copilot to return contradictory or sensitive answers, and users lose trust. Organizations that see returns classify data, enforce consistent policies and measure outcomes like cycle time rather than logins.

**How do you stop Conditional Access exceptions from piling up?**

You stop exception build-up with quarterly policy reviews plus automated drift detection. Every exception needs a documented justification and a review date, and is removed once that reason no longer applies. Automated compliance scanning and Sentinel alerts on policy changes flag deviations, so forgotten legacy authentication exceptions do not quietly remain in place.
