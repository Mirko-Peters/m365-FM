## FAQ

**What are containers in Power Apps and why should makers use them?**

Containers are layout controls that position child elements through relationships instead of fixed X and Y formulas. Horizontal containers place children side by side, vertical containers stack them, and nested together they adapt to desktop, tablet and phone. Properties such as flexible width, flexible height, padding, gap and alignment replace manual pixel positioning.

**How does a Power Apps component library keep apps on brand?**

A component library centralizes reusable headers, buttons, navigation bars and dialogs that every app in the tenant can use. When the library changes, for example a new logo, font or color, apps using those components inherit the update. The episode recommends prefixed names such as cmp and exposing only brand-approved properties to makers.

**What common mistakes should makers avoid when building with containers?**

Avoid over-nesting, because wrapping every control in its own container slows rendering and makes apps harder to edit. Name containers clearly, such as CNT header or CNT main, check the horizontal or vertical direction icon in the tree view, and pick one container to absorb extra space instead of making everything flexible.
