## FAQ

**What are the building blocks of Teams meeting extensibility?**

Teams meeting extensibility rests on three pieces: custom meeting apps, in-meeting side panels and Microsoft Graph meeting lifecycle events. Each has its own development model, permissions and deployment considerations, and each works on its own. The episode argues the real value comes from wiring them together so the meeting behaves like one responsive system.

**How do you subscribe to Teams meeting lifecycle events with Microsoft Graph?**

You create a Microsoft Graph subscription to the online meeting resource and list the events you need, such as started, ended or participant changes. This requires subscription and read or write permissions, a webhook endpoint that Graph can call, and handling of validation and security tokens so spoofed calls are not processed.

**How can a side panel open automatically in a Teams meeting?**

A side panel can open on demand through an app command, such as a custom button in the meeting UI, or automatically through a meeting event. Launch behavior is configured in the app manifest and wired through the Teams SDK. A started event can, for example, open the panel with CRM data already loaded.
