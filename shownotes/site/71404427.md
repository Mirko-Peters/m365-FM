## FAQ

**Does Microsoft 365 Copilot create new security risks for permissions?**

No, Copilot does not create new access; it removes the effort needed to use access people already have. Content that was overshared but hard to find, such as HR drafts or finance workbooks, becomes reachable through one natural language prompt. The episode argues the real failure sits in drifting permissions, not in the AI itself.

**What should be in place before scaling a Copilot rollout?**

A minimum viable governance baseline should come first. The episode lists sensitivity labels with operational meaning, access reviews to stop permission drift, an oversharing scan of the locations Copilot draws from, audit turned on to see interactions and accessed resources, and prompt-level controls so risky grounding paths do not slip through by default.

**How long does it take for Purview DLP policies to apply to Copilot?**

Microsoft documents propagation delays of often 24 hours for most users and up to 48 hours in some cases, depending on workloads, devices, connectivity and sync state. The episode recommends testing first, verifying sync status and checking which workloads evaluate the policy before relying on it, because policy intent is not the same as policy reality.
