## FAQ

**What are the three new Microsoft solution areas in FY26?**

AI Business Solutions, Cloud and AI Platforms, and Security. AI Business Solutions combines modern work and business applications, Cloud and AI Platforms merges the three Azure areas of data and AI, digital and app innovation, and infrastructure, while Security remains standalone with tighter validation such as third-party audits.

**Which designation should a partner earn first under the FY26 changes?**

The one where your Partner Capability Score is already closest to 70 points. Your first designation sets the anniversary date for later ones, so anchoring deliberately simplifies renewals. The episode also suggests clustering qualification across areas or starting from the advanced specialization you want and working backward.

**Why do partner admin link and CPOR matter for partner points?**

They make your work visible to Microsoft's scoring. Partner admin link belongs on every Azure subscription where you deliver managed services, and CPOR claims on every Microsoft 365 deployment. The episode says CPOR earns double the designation points of CSP transaction credit alone, which can shorten the path to a designation.
