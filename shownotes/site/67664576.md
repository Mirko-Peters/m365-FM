## FAQ

**What happens when you create a team in Microsoft Teams?**

Creating a team typically provisions a Microsoft 365 group first, followed by linked resources: a SharePoint site, membership updates in Entra ID, an Exchange mailbox and calendar, and sometimes services like Planner. Exact behavior depends on tenant configuration. That is why a small Teams change can surface as an issue in another service.

**Why do private channels create extra SharePoint sites?**

Private channels get their own storage, so SharePoint creates new sites for them automatically. A project lead who adds a few private channels may not intend to create sites, but admins later find them in their view. This is by design, and it means governance and storage policies can multiply without anyone planning for it.

**How can admins keep the Microsoft 365 groups behind Teams under control?**

Run three regular checks: make sure every group has at least one active owner, review membership to catch lingering external users, and confirm expiry or lifecycle policies exist. Use the admin center and audit logs to find ownerless groups. External accounts left in a group get access everywhere that group reaches, not just one team.
