## FAQ

**Why does my Power App not show all records from a SharePoint list?**

Your query is probably not delegable, so Power Apps only processes a limited slice of records locally. Functions such as Search, Len, Mid or combined conditions can't run on SharePoint's side, so the app returns an incomplete result without errors. The yellow delegation warnings in the formula bar are the sign to take seriously.

**When is a SharePoint list good enough as a data source for Power Apps?**

A SharePoint list is fine for small prototypes used by one department with under a thousand records. Once the app spans departments, holds compliance-sensitive data, needs relational data or drives more than a few flows, the episode recommends moving to Dataverse. It calls lists fine for prototypes but dangerous for production.

**How is Dataverse security different from SharePoint list permissions?**

Dataverse secures data by table, row and field with role-based access control, while SharePoint permissions work at site, list or item level. Many unique item permissions in SharePoint add overhead and slow queries. Dataverse can hide a salary field from finance while letting HR edit it, and it audits who changed what and when.
