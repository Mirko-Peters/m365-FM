## FAQ

**What is the difference between Dynamics 365 agents, Copilot Studio agents and Copilot Cowork?**

They serve different jobs. Built-in Dynamics 365 agents, such as the supplier communication agent, are fairly static and do only the task they are named for. Copilot Studio agents analyze business data and return detailed insights. Copilot Cowork works across Microsoft 365, setting up meetings and preparing documents, presentations and emails for users.

**How should finance teams govern AI in Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations?**

Start with an environment strategy that defines which environments and technologies are used and how security is managed. Billur also stresses giving agents the right context, knowledge sources and skills, splitting work across agent workflows instead of one agent, and continuously monitoring for data leakage, user security issues and regulatory violations.

**Will AI replace jobs in finance and accounting?**

Billur sees AI as an evolution rather than a job killer: the type and way of work will change, as it did with calculators, computers and ERP systems. Humans still make the decisions because they bring emotional intelligence and know their customers and vendors, while AI offers reports and recommendations that make work easier.
