## FAQ

**Why are ARM templates hard to maintain?**

ARM templates are hard to maintain because they are verbose, brittle JSON files. Even simple deployments sprawl into hundreds of lines, error messages rarely point to the actual fault, and a missing bracket or comma can stop a deployment. Teams then fix things manually in the Azure portal, and the template falls out of sync.

**What is configuration drift in Azure?**

Configuration drift is the growing gap between what your template defines and what actually runs in Azure. It comes from quick portal edits and undocumented fixes that never get written back into code. Over time this breaks troubleshooting, creates untracked security exposures and means restoring from templates no longer rebuilds the same environment.

**Does Bicep replace ARM templates?**

Bicep replaces writing ARM JSON by hand, but it compiles into ARM behind the scenes, so deployments work the same way. It offers a shorter, readable syntax, reusable modules and fewer punctuation errors. Azure Verified Modules and private modules add shared building blocks, and the episode suggests starting by converting one frequently used template.
