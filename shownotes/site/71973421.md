## FAQ

**Why do CRM projects fail?**

According to Thomas Sandsør, CRM projects fail when the organization does not invest in the change and thinks it is simply buying a product it can use the following week. CRM is a transition to people using technology, so change management and early management engagement matter more than features or buttons.

**How do you drive CRM adoption among salespeople?**

Give salespeople visible value and recognition. Thomas posts a win card with a GIF in the Teams sales chat whenever a deal closes, which motivates people to enter their data. He also expects AI agents that suggest a next best action to turn CRM from an after-the-fact reporting tool into a real assistant.

**Will AI agents replace CRM consultants and traditional CRM workflows?**

Thomas does not think so. He expects users to interact more with agents and less with model-driven apps, but consultants will still design the data model, explain why data is captured and enforce clean data input. He also argues that AI cannot build the trust that face-to-face contact brings to closing deals.
