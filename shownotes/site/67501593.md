## FAQ

**Why does SharePoint fail at knowledge management?**

SharePoint fails when organizations treat it as a storage cabinet rather than a designed knowledge system. Without structure, ownership and lifecycle rules, duplicate versions spread across Teams, OneDrive and email, outdated files never leave, and search results feel random. Employees then stop trusting it and build shadow repositories, even though the platform itself offers the needed tools.

**What makes a SharePoint intranet work as a single source of truth?**

Three anchors make it work: navigation that mirrors how teams talk about their work, lifecycle practices that keep content fresh, and metadata that makes search precise. Documents are created, reviewed, archived and eventually deleted, while tags such as department, topic or approval status let search show only the approved, current version.

**How does SharePoint content quality affect Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Copilot can only be as reliable as the content it draws from. If SharePoint is full of duplicates, outdated files and missing metadata, Copilot may surface an old policy or the wrong draft. With lifecycle rules, clear ownership and metadata discipline in place, it can point to the approved document version quickly.
