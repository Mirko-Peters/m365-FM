## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft Fabric capacity costs spike without any new deployment?**

Spikes usually come from query shapes, not platform glitches. Interactive bursts of unbounded or non-sargable queries, often generated with Copilot, run alongside semantic model refreshes on one shared capacity meter. Correlating execution plans, scanned versus returned rows and capacity metrics by time window reveals which queries scanned whole tables to return a handful of rows.

**Are Fabric workspace roles enough to secure data?**

No, workspace roles are a collaboration model that answers who can work in a workspace, not who can see a table or column. The episode recommends schema-based database roles in the warehouse with deny by default, views and procedures as the only access paths, and service principals scoped to the minimum objects they need.

**How do you stop schema drift between a Fabric lakehouse and warehouse?**

Treat the lakehouse as an intake zone and the warehouse as the contract zone, then gate the seam between them. Validation gates check schema, types, required fields, keys and duplicates before loading. Failing rows go to quarantine tables with metadata, and the violation count is published as a visible metric instead of silently coercing data.
