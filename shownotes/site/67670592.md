## FAQ

**Is disabling Power Platform a good way to reduce risk?**

No, disabling Power Platform usually relocates risk rather than reducing it. Business demand for reports, approvals and small apps continues, so employees turn to personal spreadsheets, consumer cloud storage or free automation services. Data then sits outside retention, DLP, auditing and Conditional Access, where IT cannot see or protect it.

**Why does removing Power Platform licenses backfire?**

Removing licenses lowers usage numbers and costs but does not detach Power Platform from Microsoft 365. Users still meet prompts in Teams, SharePoint and Outlook they cannot use, and that friction drives workarounds. A practical first step is checking which Teams or SharePoint actions still show Power Platform prompts after license changes.

**What does the CoE Starter Kit do for Power Platform governance?**

The CoE Starter Kit gives admins visibility into apps, flows, environments, makers and connectors across the tenant. With that inventory, IT can classify apps, retire dormant ones, enforce secure connectors and spot duplicate solutions, such as several departments building expense trackers, then offer a shared template instead of blocking usage.
