## Key takeaways

- Governance only works when it is embedded in the flow of work, enforced by the platform and measurable through the share of sensitive data that is correctly labeled and protected.

## FAQ

**What is the best metric for Microsoft 365 governance?**

The episode recommends one executive metric: the percentage of sensitive data that is correctly labeled and protected. It shows whether the environment can recognize and defend high-risk content without relying on human memory. Supporting indicators are time to revoke access, the share of privileged roles under PIM and external sharing exposure in high-risk areas.

**How can a sensitive file be overshared in under 10 minutes?**

It happens through ordinary collaboration, not an attack. In the episode's example, an unlabeled financial planning file is shared in SharePoint, dropped into a Teams chat, forwarded and finally shared through an external link. Without automatic classification, mandatory protection or real-time interruption of risky sharing, the environment simply keeps saying yes.

**Why should admin rights in Microsoft 365 be temporary?**

Standing admin rights let a single compromised or over-privileged account weaken labels, DLP and sharing settings for the whole tenant. The episode recommends moving privileged roles under Entra Privileged Identity Management with just-in-time activation, approval for high-impact roles, expiry and an audit trail showing who activated what, when and for how long.
