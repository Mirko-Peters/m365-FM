## FAQ

**Why does Mirko Peters say consistency alone is not enough?**

Consistency keeps a system running, but it does not decide where the work travels or who acts on it. Mirko published daily hoping it would lead to a job, yet output without distribution, positioning and execution stayed unrewarded labor. He argues the winning stack was consistency combined with distribution, positioning, real-world execution and relationships.

**Why did script writing matter more than another certification?**

Script writing fixed the real bottleneck, which was making his business value easy to understand. Another certificate would only have added more proof of knowledge. Writing for spoken delivery exposed weak logic, forced clearer sequencing of ideas and taught him to frame technology as organizational consequence, improving his thinking, communication and positioning at the same time.

**Is shadow IT a user discipline problem?**

Usually not; Mirko describes shadow IT as a design outcome. People bypass the official path when it is too slow, unclear or painful, for example when Power Platform requests take weeks or SharePoint structures are confusing. Tighter lockdowns rarely help. Making the governed path more usable, with clearer ownership, better defaults and faster workflows, reduces bypass behavior.
