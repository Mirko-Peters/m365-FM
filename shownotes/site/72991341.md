## FAQ

**What is an Azure landing zone?**

An Azure landing zone is a pre-configured environment, not a product you deploy with one button. Networking, identity, monitoring and policies are in place before workloads arrive. The subscription is the unit of isolation, and the landing zone wraps it with central decisions about allowed regions, network connections, mandatory tags and forbidden actions.

**What is the difference between platform and application landing zones?**

Platform landing zones hold shared services, while application landing zones run workloads. Platform zones are few, long-lived subscriptions for identity, management and connectivity, owned by the platform team. Application zones are many and disposable, inherit policies from their management group and peer into the hub network instead of owning firewalls or gateways.

**How should I start building an Azure landing zone?**

Start with a minimum viable landing zone instead of the full enterprise-scale hierarchy. That means one management group hierarchy, a few core policies, a hub with one spoke and central logging to a Log Analytics workspace. Onboard a real workload, learn from the friction, and plan IP ranges and management groups before deploying.
