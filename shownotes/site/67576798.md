## FAQ

**Why is my Power Apps SharePoint form slow in production?**

Production data exposes costs that testing with a few records hides. Each lookup drop-down makes its own call, and conditional formulas recalculate whenever users change a field, because Power Apps does not optimize them automatically. Test with production-scale lists, keep formulas light and move heavy operations into Power Automate flows or SharePoint settings.

**Why does a Power Apps form fail to save even though validation passed?**

SharePoint still enforces its own column rules regardless of the logic in Power Apps. If a column is required in SharePoint but hidden or allowed empty in the custom form, the save fails with no meaningful error. Align both layers: Power Apps for instant feedback, SharePoint column settings for data integrity.

**When should I keep the native SharePoint form instead of Power Apps?**

Keep the native form when you only want cosmetic changes such as rearranging fields or a cleaner look. Native forms rarely break, render consistently across devices and need no extra maintenance. Power Apps is worth its added complexity when business logic requires it, such as conditional sections, cascading drop-downs or pre-filled defaults.
