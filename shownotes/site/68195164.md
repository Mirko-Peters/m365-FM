## FAQ

**Does Microsoft train its AI models on our Copilot prompts and data?**

No. According to the episode, prompts, responses and Microsoft Graph data used by Copilot are not fed back into Microsoft's foundation models, and this is formalized in the Product Terms and the Data Protection Addendum. Prompts and responses are stored inside the Microsoft 365 service boundary, where retention and deletion can be managed with Purview.

**Can a Copilot license give users access to files they could not open before?**

No. A license only enables Copilot features in apps like Word, Excel and Outlook. Copilot surfaces only data a user can already access through Microsoft Graph. The risk lies in sloppy permissions: if sensitive files are shared with everyone, Copilot can surface them, so RBAC and permission cleanup matter before rollout.

**What should I check in my tenant before enabling Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Start with three checks: run a Purview DSPM for AI assessment and apply its one-click fixes, assign licenses by group with RBAC scoping, and enable Purview audit, retention and DLP to block highly sensitive data. Before that, have legal confirm Product Terms and DPA coverage and map residency commitments such as the EU Data Boundary.
