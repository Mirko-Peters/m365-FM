## FAQ

**When should I move a SharePoint list to Dataverse?**

Move when your data needs relationships across tables, record- or field-level security with compliance-grade auditing, or must scale to run a business-critical process long term. If the answer to all three is no, a list is probably fine. Lists suit prototypes and short-term trackers, while Dataverse suits governed production apps several teams depend on.

**What hidden costs come with Dataverse?**

Licensing and storage are the two main cost drivers, followed by time and skills. Trials are temporary and not every license covers production use, so validate with procurement first. Estimate storage and record counts six months ahead, identify which premium connectors or features you need, and budget for training in schema design and governance.

**Why do Power Automate flows break when a list moves to Dataverse?**

Flows often break because they rely on SharePoint list item IDs, which don't align with Dataverse record identifiers, so the data moves but the flows fail. Security also shifts from SharePoint permissions to role-based models. Inventory flows and dependencies, rebuild and test flows against Dataverse IDs, pilot with a small group and keep a rollback plan.
