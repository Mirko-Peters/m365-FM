## FAQ

**Do Copilot Notebook summaries inherit sensitivity labels from their source documents?**

No. According to the episode, a notebook summary does not inherit the sensitivity label, retention rule or metadata of the files it draws from. A summary built from HR records, finance sheets and slide decks is born unlabeled, even though restricted information lives inside its sentences, so it can be forwarded without any alert.

**Why can't Microsoft Purview and DLP see content generated in Copilot Notebooks?**

Because both tools are built around files, while a notebook is a conversation layer. Purview scans files, not contexts, and DLP triggers when a labeled file is downloaded or emailed, not when AI paraphrases its content. Audit logs show a session, not which sources the AI fused into its answer.

**How can admins bring Copilot Notebook output back under governance?**

Treat AI output as first-class data. The episode recommends default sensitivity labeling that inherits the highest label of the sources, monitoring notebook activity through Purview audit logs alongside DLP alerts, restricted sharing with a named owner, derived-data policies, and expiration dates of thirty, sixty or ninety days with version history.
