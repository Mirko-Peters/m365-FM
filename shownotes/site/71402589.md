## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft 365 notifications hurt productivity?**

They fragment attention and push people into reaction mode, which slows decisions and stretches cycle times. The episode explains that a single glance at a banner or red badge already breaks concentration, and rebuilding deep focus can take about 23 minutes. When signals become untrustworthy, teams also compensate with more meetings and recurring syncs.

**What is an attention baseline in Microsoft 365?**

It is a governed, tenant-wide notification standard treated like a security baseline. Instead of leaving settings to each user, IT and leaders define defaults: Teams set to mentions and replies, Outlook desktop pop-ups disabled, quiet hours aligned across apps, Viva focus blocks protected, SharePoint alerts pruned and mobile badges removed, with a defined path for real escalation.

**What results did the global services firm see after changing notification defaults?**

Within 90 days, notification volume dropped by nearly 45 percent and meeting hours fell by 18 percent. Each user gained about 2.1 hours of focus time per week, and project SLA performance improved. The firm of about 8,000 users said the key change was leadership removing the expectation of instant responses, not only the technical settings.
