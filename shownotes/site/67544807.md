## FAQ

**How can IT teams keep up with Microsoft 365 Message center updates?**

They can stop trying to read everything and triage instead. Sort updates into security, compliance, user-experience and preview categories, use Message center tags as a first filter, and check whether a change applies to your licenses, regions and workloads. A scheduled weekly review session replaces constant, scattered checking of the feed.

**What is a simple decision tree for prioritizing Microsoft 365 changes?**

First ask whether the update has compliance or security consequences; if yes, act immediately. If not, ask whether it could disrupt a business-critical workflow or key integration; if yes, test it and plan the rollout. If both answers are no, move it to a monitored bucket until it actually matters.

**Who besides IT needs to know about Microsoft 365 updates?**

Anyone whose workflows change, not just the technical owners. Legal and data protection officers care about retention changes, finance about licensing costs, HR about employee data visibility, and in German organizations the works council when monitoring or audit logs change. Briefing these stakeholders before rollout avoids conflict and stalled projects.
