## FAQ

**Why does low-code create governance risk at scale?**

Low-code does not remove complexity, it moves it from the build surface into runtime configuration, connectors, identities and permissions. At small scale that feels like progress. As flows multiply, builders leave and exceptions pile up, nobody can explain the logic end to end, so audits, incidents and change requests turn into reconstruction work.

**When should a low-code workflow move to Fabric notebooks?**

A workflow should graduate when it becomes mission-critical: it touches financial reporting, regulated or customer-impacting data, runs unattended on a schedule the business relies on, feeds other automations, or needs on-call support. Another trigger is when nobody except the original builder can explain it end to end with evidence.

**What is in the 30-60-90 day plan for low-code governance?**

In the first 30 days, inventory the low-code estate that touches core data and tag what is business critical. By day 60, define graduation criteria and move those assets into governed execution with clear ownership. By day 90, measure failures, rework, diagnosis time and audit prep hours before and after graduation.
