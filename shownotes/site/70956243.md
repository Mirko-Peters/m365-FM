## FAQ

**Why does Copilot adoption stall after a successful pilot?**

Adoption usually stalls because the pilot moves from interface excitement into governance reality, often between week six and week twelve. Users hit conflicting SharePoint files, permissions based on history rather than responsibility, and answers grounded in documents nobody trusts. Trust softens, people verify more and return to old habits for important work.

**What does a single source of truth mean for an AI-first organization?**

It means one trusted decision reality, not one giant storage location. Information can live across SharePoint, Teams and Excel, but the organization must define which source governs which decision. Designated official libraries, meaningful naming, labels and named owners help separate draft content from decision-grade content, so AI retrieval becomes less noisy.

**How should leaders measure AI readiness in the first 90 days?**

Leaders should track four signals instead of logins and licenses. Decision velocity measures time from input to decision, decision clarity checks whether decisions trace to an owner and trusted source, rework shows how often outputs are rebuilt, and the AI trust signal shows how often people must verify outputs before using them.
