## FAQ

**What is the multi-cloud network tax?**

It is the combined cost of moving data between clouds. It includes egress fees whenever data leaves a provider, cross-connect port charges at the point of presence, a latency penalty from physical distance, plus duplicated gateways, monitoring, DNS setups and engineer time. Redundant circuits add monthly minimums even when they carry no traffic.

**How do ExpressRoute, Direct Connect and Cloud Interconnect connect clouds?**

They all terminate at carrier-neutral points of presence, where cross-connect cables link one provider's port to another's. You pay each party separately: Microsoft's port, Amazon's or Google's port, and the facility for the connection between them. There is no tunnel merging networks, just physical fiber with its own latency and fees.

**How can I reduce multi-cloud networking costs?**

Pick a primary cloud where your data lives and treat other clouds as short-term extensions. Consume results through APIs instead of copying data sets between providers, keeping compute close to storage. If you must connect clouds, choose regions in the same metro area and connect through the same carrier-neutral facility to cut latency and cost.
