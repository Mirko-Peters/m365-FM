## FAQ

**Why are Microsoft Teams private channels not private by default?**

Private channels create their own separate SharePoint sites, so they add a new permission surface rather than true privacy. In the episode's first incident, guests whose project had ended still had OneDrive sync pointed at a private channel library, and without guest expiration or access reviews they kept receiving fresh copies of sensitive files.

**How do I stop users from pasting PII into Teams chats?**

Create a Microsoft Purview DLP policy scoped to Teams chat and channel messages. Use a high-confidence rule that blocks sensitive information types such as Social Security or credit card numbers and allows override only with a written justification, plus a medium-confidence rule that warns with a policy tip and alerts compliance.

**How can I make Teams guest access expire automatically?**

Use Microsoft Entra ID Governance. Run access reviews on Microsoft 365 groups with guests, reviewed by group owners, and auto-remove anyone not confirmed. Grant guests access through entitlement management access packages with fixed, time-bound assignments of 60 or 90 days, where extensions require approval and access is removed automatically on expiry.
