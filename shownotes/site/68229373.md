## FAQ

**How do I send an internal newsletter only to the right employees in Microsoft 365?**

Use Exchange dynamic distribution groups that filter users by directory attributes such as department, office or license type. Membership updates automatically as people join, move or leave. Keep static distribution lists for curated audiences like pilot teams, avoid nesting dynamic and static groups, and use clear prefixes to tell them apart.

**Should an internal newsletter be sent from a personal mailbox or a shared mailbox?**

Send it from a shared mailbox so the newsletter has an institutional identity that survives when an individual leaves or changes roles. Grant Send As for unified branding or Send on Behalf for visible authorship, add folders for drafts, published issues and replies, apply retention, and avoid automatic replies to prevent mail loops.

**How can I measure whether employees read an Outlook newsletter?**

Combine Exchange message trace for delivery with delivery reports and mailbox audit logs for opens, then load the data into Power BI. The episode suggests four KPIs: open rate, click-through rate, reply quality and delivery success. Simple A/B tests on subject lines with two temporary distribution lists add further insight.
