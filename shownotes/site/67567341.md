## FAQ

**What is Microsoft Places used for?**

Microsoft Places coordinates hybrid work by showing who plans to be on site, which events are scheduled and which desks or rooms are free. Instead of simply booking a seat, people can see when teammates are usually in the office and pick the day where coming in is actually useful, all from tools like Outlook and Teams.

**How is Microsoft Places different from a desk booking tool?**

Desk booking tools only answer whether a seat is free, while Places adds context for deciding whether to come in at all. It combines presence trends, upcoming events such as a town hall and space availability, so the choice of an office day is informed beforehand rather than recorded after people have already decided.

**Does Microsoft Places track individual employees?**

No, according to the episode Places is designed to report aggregated, anonymized usage rather than individual logs. Insights come from normal activities like room bookings, desk reservations and event attendance, so managers see trends such as which days are busy or which areas are underused, without monitoring when a specific person arrived or left.
