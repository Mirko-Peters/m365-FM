## FAQ

**Why is translation not enough in multilingual meetings?**

Translation captures words, but high-context communication carries meaning in tone, timing and what goes unsaid. A phrase like revisiting something next quarter can be a neutral note to one person and a polite refusal to another. Clean captions and transcripts therefore do not guarantee that everyone leaves with the same understanding of decisions.

**Can Teams Premium detect hidden disagreement in meetings?**

No, Teams Premium does not read faces or decode hidden cultural meaning on its own. It offers live translation, interpreter and intelligent recap with AI notes, tasks, chapters and speaker-attributed moments. Those give a stronger record of the meeting, which helps people spot weak commitments, missing owners and repeated deferral language during a later review.

**What is an intent review after a meeting?**

An intent review is a short check right after a high-stakes meeting to test whether real commitment happened. A meeting owner, a recap reviewer and an action item validator look for vague ownership, soft resistance and missing dates, then send a brief follow-up that asks who approves and who owns the next step.
