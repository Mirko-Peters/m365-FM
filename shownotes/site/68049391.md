## FAQ

**How does sentiment analysis route customers in Dynamics 365 Contact Center?**

It analyzes tone, phrasing, pacing and word choice in voice and chat conversations, then moves frustrated or high-value customers up the queue to the agents best equipped to defuse them. Supervisors can tune the routing rules, for example weighting high-value accounts or churn threats, so it is configuration rather than a black box.

**What do the case management, customer intent and knowledge management agents do in Dynamics 365?**

They take over repetitive service work. The case management agent creates and updates tickets, the customer intent agent builds an intent library from historical conversations, and the knowledge management agent drafts articles and flags duplicates. The episode recommends starting in assist mode, where humans confirm each update, then increasing autonomy as trust grows.

**How does Copilot stop customers from having to repeat themselves to support agents?**

Copilot gives agents structured conversation summaries with customer details, product, history and sentiment before they respond. Ask a question answers natural-language queries from internal knowledge and, if configured, up to five trusted external domains, and Copilot drafts chat or email replies with context. Agents should still review drafts before sending them.
