## FAQ

**What is Copilot Pages in Microsoft 365?**

Copilot Pages is an editable, shareable canvas inside Copilot Chat that turns a temporary chat answer into a page you own. You move a response onto the page, then refine it by chatting with Copilot or editing it yourself. The page stays available until you delete it and appears in the Copilot library.

**What is the difference between Copilot Pages, Microsoft Loop and Copilot Notebooks?**

Pages are for one finished artifact you hand to someone, Loop is for a living workspace a team keeps returning to, and Notebooks are for research projects where Copilot reasons across many files and chats. They are closely connected: a Copilot page is the same kind of file as a Loop page.

**Can external guests open a shared Copilot page?**

No, guests and external people cannot open a shared page straight from the link at the time of recording. Inside your organization, sharing works like any other Microsoft 365 file, with edit or read access and real-time co-authoring, and a page inherits the sensitivity label of the most sensitive source document.
