## FAQ

**Why are complex passwords not enough to protect Microsoft 365?**

Complex passwords do not stop password spray attacks, which try common patterns across many accounts until one works. The episode describes a tenant where a single compromised account sent thousands of phishing messages for days. Enforcing MFA through Conditional Access adds the verification layer that password-only defenses lack, with little friction for users.

**What does blocking legacy authentication achieve?**

Blocking legacy authentication closes a back door, because older protocols do not support MFA and let attackers sidestep modern defenses. Combined with requiring Conditional Access for all users, not just executives, it forms the baseline the episode calls the 80 percent fix. Modern clients like Outlook, Teams and OneDrive already handle these challenges.

**How do you stop old guest accounts from becoming a security risk?**

Use expiration policies so guest access ends automatically unless someone renews it. Forgotten vendor or contractor accounts offer attackers quiet persistence and can bypass identity protections. Group or team based expiration lets team owners keep inviting partners without tickets, while unused accounts are cleaned up and access only lasts as long as the project needs it.
