## FAQ

**What does the Power Apps for Figma plugin do?**

Power Apps for Figma lets makers design layouts, controls and components in Figma and move them into Power Apps canvas apps. It can also import designs from Power Apps. By following prefixes that represent Power Apps controls, teams can build a whole design system in Figma and reuse it across projects in their company.

**What is the difference between the free and pro version of Power Apps for Figma?**

The pro version adds direct copy and paste of selected Figma frames or controls into Power Apps. According to Lukas, the free version covers roughly 40 percent of the features, including importing designs and creating layouts. Pro also adds extra icon sets, extended properties, style resizing and translation into more than 100 languages.

**How should developers prompt AI agents when vibe coding?**

Keep each prompt strict and limited to one thing you want to finish or update. Lukas found that when a prompt asks for several changes, AI agents such as GitHub Copilot tend to rewrite code that already worked. He also connects his projects to GitHub so every change is in history and can be rolled back.
