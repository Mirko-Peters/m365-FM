## FAQ

**Can a SharePoint list really hold 30 million items for a Power App?**

SharePoint can store that many items, but Power Apps cannot work with them comfortably. The episode says the 30 million figure describes capacity, not usable performance. Apps slow down noticeably beyond a few thousand records, delegation is limited, and even indexed columns only help to around 25,000 records according to the episode's reading of Microsoft's documentation.

**Are SharePoint lookup columns real database relationships?**

No, SharePoint lookup columns are lightweight pointers that store an ID and some display text. They do not enforce referential integrity, prevent duplicates or cascade deletes, so deleting a parent item can leave orphaned references. Each lookup also triggers extra calls in Power Apps, which adds latency to forms and nested galleries.

**Why do item-level permissions slow down SharePoint-based Power Apps?**

Each item with broken inheritance gets its own access control entry that SharePoint must evaluate when loading data. With hundreds or thousands of such items, galleries slow down because permissions are checked item by item. Dataverse and SQL instead apply role-based rules at table, row or column level when the query runs.
