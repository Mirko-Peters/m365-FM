## FAQ

**What is the infrastructure illusion in Microsoft 365?**

It is the gap between the infrastructure in the diagram and the one people actually use. Mirko shows sales closing deals in email, hiring managers tracking candidates in spreadsheets and controllers reconciling month-end in Excel. Policies, audits and security are built for the designed system, while the real work happens where governance cannot see it.

**Why does Copilot expose so much sensitive information?**

Copilot works with whatever the user can already access, so it inherits overshared permissions and unclassified files. It then synthesizes scattered emails, notes and spreadsheets into summaries that make private, informal information explicit and shareable. Mirko argues this is an infrastructure problem that Copilot reveals, not a Copilot problem, and restricting the tool misses the cause.

**How do I map how work really happens in my organization?**

Start by following real workflows, not by adding controls. Pick a recent deal, a hire and a month-end close, ask the people involved what actually happened, and document every tool, workaround and policy deviation. Then name each gap, ask why it exists and decide whether to change the design or the business.
