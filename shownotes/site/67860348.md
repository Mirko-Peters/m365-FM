## FAQ

**Why does Microsoft 365 Copilot give outdated or wrong answers?**

Copilot works with whatever data your tenant holds, so expired contracts, mislabeled files, duplicate spreadsheets and abandoned SharePoint folders produce confident but wrong summaries. The fix is to start small: clean one folder you can fix in about two days, label it, remove old drafts, and test Copilot on that slice first.

**How do you get employees to actually want Copilot?**

Show visible wins that save people real time, and let them spread through colleagues. Organizations in the episode used business champions rather than IT staff, ran tight pilots such as six analysts over three weeks with daily feedback, and let word of mouth in chats and meetings build demand.

**How should Copilot training be run?**

Train with real, messy files instead of polished canned demos, and say up front that Copilot will sometimes get things wrong. Keep sessions to fifteen to thirty minutes, provide a sandbox with noisy data for safe practice, and hand out a cheat sheet on how to fact-check Copilot's output.
