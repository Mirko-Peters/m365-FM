## FAQ

**What are the first governance steps for an ungoverned Power Platform tenant?**

Lock down tenant settings first, then apply data loss prevention policies. Craig White starts by deciding who can create environments and whether cross-tenant connections are allowed. Next, DLP policies control which APIs and connectors apps, flows and agents can call. In a new tenant this has no user impact; established tenants need more careful analysis.

**Is shadow IT in Power Platform a bad thing?**

Not necessarily; Craig White sees it as a way to find motivated makers. Because Power Platform is on by default, it cannot be fully stopped. People who taught themselves from articles or videos can become champions in the community and reveal business areas with challenges IT was not aware of, so the goal is to harness it.

**Can organizations still rely on the CoE Starter Kit now that Microsoft stopped adding updates?**

Yes, because it is built only on supported Power Platform components. Craig White points out that it uses model-driven apps, Power Automate flows and the Power Platform admin APIs, with no custom code involved. Organizations can keep extending it, but should see the underlying platform, not the toolkit, as their real foundation.
