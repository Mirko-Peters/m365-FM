## FAQ

**Does Copilot create new permission risks in Microsoft 365?**

Mostly no. Copilot works inside the access model you already allowed, so broad inherited permissions, stale groups and overshared sites become broad discoverability. Content that used to stay buried behind search friction can now surface in answers. Copilot also exposes information chaos, because duplicate and outdated files weaken the quality of its responses.

**What is time to control lag in a Microsoft 365 tenant?**

It is the time between creating a workspace and that workspace having an owner, classification, life cycle path and access review expectation. The episode puts it often between 30 and 90 days. That matters because the first weeks are the most active, so risk is highest exactly when governance is weakest.

**What does the 14-day governance reality check involve?**

It has three steps. A baseline exposure scan shows who can access what and where external sharing stays open. An uncontrolled workspace audit finds teams, sites and groups without owners, classification or recent membership review. A policy gap snapshot compares documented labeling and retention coverage with what is actually enforced in the tenant.
