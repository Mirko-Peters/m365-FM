## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Entra just a renamed Azure AD?**

No, Microsoft Entra is a suite rather than a rename. Entra ID continues Azure AD for authentication, Conditional Access and user lifecycle. Entra Permissions Management adds least-privilege visibility across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, and Entra Verified ID provides decentralized, verifiable credentials. Together they target multi-cloud, hybrid work where identity is the constant.

**What is access creep and how does Entra prevent it?**

Access creep is the slow build-up of permissions as people change roles, join projects or cover for colleagues without old rights being removed. Entra identity governance counters it with lifecycle workflows for joiners, movers and leavers, entitlement management access packages, recurring access reviews by managers, and separation-of-duties checks that flag risky permission combinations.

**How does Microsoft Entra detect risky sign-ins?**

Entra uses machine learning on real-time signals instead of fixed rules. It weighs device health, location, time and unusual behavior, and draws on Microsoft's global authentication telemetry. Patterns such as impossible travel or password spray attempts are rated as risky, which can trigger multifactor reauthentication or block the attempt entirely.
