## FAQ

**How do I enable the AI data entry agent in Power Apps?**

You enable it per environment in the Power Platform admin center, under the environment's product feature settings, by switching on the AI form fill toggle in the AI capabilities section. Smart paste and file upload then appear in model-driven app forms without redesign, and you can test in a sandbox before production.

**Does AI form fill in Power Apps bypass validation rules or security roles?**

No, it works inside the existing rules. Every suggested value respects column data types and validation logic, and the agent runs under Dataverse security roles, so users can only fill records they could already edit. Suggestions show their source text, users accept or reject them, and accepted values appear in audit logs as normal edits.

**What are the limitations of the Power Apps AI data entry agent?**

It needs a Dataverse-backed model-driven app under a premium license and consumes Copilot and AI Builder capacity. The episode also notes it is strongest with business English, struggles with mixed languages or specialized jargon, and only maps to tables and columns that already exist, so a mature schema has to come first.
