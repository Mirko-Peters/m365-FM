## FAQ

**When should I use Dataverse instead of SharePoint lists for Power Apps?**

Use Dataverse once a process needs relationships, role-based or field-level security, server-side logic, audit history or reliable delegation across large data sets. SharePoint lists suit small, flat, single-team tracking. The episode warns that migrating later is a full redesign, so the decision should consider the process you expect to run, not entry price.

**What is the delegation problem with Power Apps on SharePoint?**

When a query cannot be delegated, Power Apps evaluates only a local subset of records, typically 500 or up to 2,000 if the setting is changed. The app still looks normal but answers from incomplete data, so users act on wrong results with confidence. Dataverse supports delegation for standard filter, sort, search and lookup operations.

**Is Dataverse too expensive compared to Excel and SharePoint?**

Its license and capacity costs are more visible, but Mirko Peters argues entry price is not system cost. Cheap tools shift spending into labor: reconciliation, duplicate records, permission workarounds, cleanup and eventual migration. For processes that will grow across teams, Dataverse can be the more economical choice even when the license line is higher.
