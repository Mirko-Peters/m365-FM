## FAQ

**Why are Conditional Access and Purview audit not enough to secure AI agents?**

They act at the wrong moments. Conditional Access decides at token time whether an agent identity may get a token, and Purview captures transcripts and activities after the fact. Most harm happens when an agent executes a specific tool call, such as a delete or share, so each high-risk call needs a deterministic policy gate first.

**What is a per-tool-call policy gate for AI agents?**

It is a decision service that evaluates every high-risk action before a tool runs. The agent submits a structured request with actor, intent, scope, data class, venue and operation ID; the engine returns allow, deny or transform and persists that outcome next to the action. Tools accept only these decisions, never free-form commands.

**How should AI agent identities be segmented in Microsoft 365?**

Split each agent into separate identities for read, write and egress. Discovery runs under a read-only principal, write actions under a principal with minimal resource-scoped permissions and short-lived credentials, and external communication under an egress-aware identity with no access to internal data. A compromise in one lane then cannot cross into the others.
