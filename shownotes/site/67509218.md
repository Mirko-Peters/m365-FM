## FAQ

**What does GPT-5 change in Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

GPT-5 moves Copilot from following instructions toward understanding intent. According to the episode, it keeps track of tone, audience and purpose across files and apps instead of starting fresh with each command. That means fewer rounds of re-prompting, drafts that sound more like your own writing, and output that is usable on the first attempt.

**How does Copilot with GPT-5 help with email overload in Outlook?**

It goes beyond summarizing threads by recognizing which messages you are likely to act on. The episode describes learning from what you reply to, ignore and forward, drafting routine approvals in your usual tone, separating escalations from simple thank-you replies, and condensing long threads into a brief before your next meeting, so triage takes far less effort.

**Can Copilot turn Excel data into a PowerPoint presentation?**

Yes, and the episode argues GPT-5 makes the result far less generic. Earlier versions produced accurate but bland charts and slides. With GPT-5, Copilot is described as building a slide sequence that tells a story, covering performance against the last quarter, regional trends and implications, with talking points placed beside the charts.
