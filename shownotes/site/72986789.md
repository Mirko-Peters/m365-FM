## FAQ

**How many Microsoft solutions partner designations should a partner pursue?**

Usually two, aligned to your core revenue. The episode advises starting from the revenue stream you want in 18 months, looking at which workloads drive your margins, and going deep in those areas instead of spreading Partner Capability Score thin across all six solution areas. Expand only when you have capacity.

**How does Microsoft co-sell actually work for partners?**

Co-sell is a two-way motion, not Microsoft handing out leads. Partners register their own deals in Partner Center, keep a specific profile, provide short seller-ready assets such as one-pagers, case studies and ROI models, and tell a better-together story that shows how their solution helps Microsoft sellers drive cloud consumption.

**What does solutions partner with certified software require for marketplace?**

It requires marketplace commercial performance over a trailing 12 months. According to the episode, partners must meet one threshold: four million dollars in marketplace billed sales, 12 net new customers each spending over 10,000 dollars, or 30 transactions with eight unique customers, each at least 100 dollars.
