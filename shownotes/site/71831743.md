## FAQ

**Why do dashboards stop being used after launch?**

Dashboards fade because they rely on someone remembering to open them at the right moment. People are already busy with many tools, meetings and approvals, so checking analytics becomes an optional chore. The weakness is the pull-based operating model, not the chart design, and redesigning pages does not fix it.

**How do you avoid alert fatigue with Power Automate notifications?**

Avoid alert fatigue by giving every alert context, an owner and a response path instead of firing on raw thresholds. Each notification should say what changed, why now, who owns it and what happens next. Anomaly detection with dynamic baselines can cut false positives, and duplicate or ignored alerts should be suppressed or reviewed.

**When should notification flows move from Power Automate to Azure Functions or Service Bus?**

Move to Azure Functions or Service Bus when you need high-volume scheduling or burst handling across thousands of notifications. For moderate volume that stays inside Microsoft 365, Power Automate plus Dataverse can carry a lot. The episode advises keeping the design simple and separating only when throughput or reliability requires it.
