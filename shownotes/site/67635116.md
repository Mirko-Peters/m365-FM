## FAQ

**What is a good Copilot prompt for summarizing my inbox?**

Try asking Copilot to summarize your new emails into two lines with today's top priorities. Mirko uses this prompt to get a quick recap of decisions needed and updates to note, then works the important threads first. Copilot can also sort mail into what needs action now, what to skim later and what to ignore.

**Which Copilot prompt works best after a Teams meeting?**

Ask Copilot to list the five key decisions, five action items with owners and outstanding risks from the meeting. This turns loose conversation into a working checklist in under a minute, according to the episode, and replaces the hour often spent rewriting notes and messaging colleagues to confirm who agreed to what.

**How can Copilot in Excel help analyze messy data?**

Copilot can clean and organize raw data and then interpret it, not just build charts. A suggested prompt is to analyze churn data for the last three quarters, surface unusual patterns and suggest three possible reasons with context. That skips hours of formatting and pivot tables and reduces manual formula errors.
