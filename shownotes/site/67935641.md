## FAQ

**What is Digital Twin Builder in Microsoft Fabric?**

Digital Twin Builder is a low-code tool in Fabric Real-Time Intelligence, in preview at the time of recording, for modeling real-world assets and processes. It lands source data in OneLake in its native formats and layers an ontology on top, so sensor, ERP and HR feeds map to shared definitions instead of fragile ETL pipelines.

**What are namespaces, types and instances in the semantic canvas?**

They are the three levels of the ontology you build on the semantic canvas. Namespaces are top categories such as factory or fleet, types sit inside them such as pump or conveyor, and instances are the real things you track, such as one specific pump. This keeps definitions consistent across teams and new data sources.

**Does Digital Twin Builder fix messy source data?**

No, it is low code, not no work. The builder does not repair broken signals or free-text fields, so you still need clean sources, subject matter experts to define the vocabulary and mapping discipline. The episode recommends a small mapping contract and a steward per namespace to avoid conflicting field names later.
