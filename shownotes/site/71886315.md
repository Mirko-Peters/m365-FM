## FAQ

**What is the difference between Entra External ID and Entra Verified ID?**

Entra External ID is the orchestration and policy layer for customer and partner access journeys, while Entra Verified ID is the portable proof layer for issuing and checking verifiable credentials. External ID manages how external users get in and stay governed; Verified ID defines what they can prove without central data collection.

**What does claim-centric identity mean?**

It means verifying the specific proof a party needs to present, instead of creating and storing an account in every app. Rather than asking whether a user has an account, the organization asks what this party must prove right now, such as partner certification or employment status, and checks that proof against policy.

**How should organizations start rolling out portable identity?**

Start with one external journey that already costs money, time or control, such as partner onboarding or customer registration. Consolidate identity orchestration into one managed layer, reduce friction with passkeys and just-in-time migration, then add verifiable credentials where reusable proof removes repeat work, and finally widen reviews, expiration and revocation.
