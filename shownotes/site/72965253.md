## FAQ

**What is the difference between a canvas app and a model-driven app in Power Apps?**

The difference is where you start. Canvas apps begin with a blank screen where you design every control and connect data later, from almost any source. Model-driven apps begin with a Dataverse data model, and the forms, views and navigation are generated automatically, along with security roles, auditing and business process flows.

**Do model-driven apps need a premium Power Apps license?**

Yes, model-driven apps always require premium licensing because they run on Dataverse. Canvas apps using only standard connectors such as SharePoint, Excel or Outlook are often covered by Microsoft 365 plans. Once any app reads or writes Dataverse, premium licensing applies, so the cost is then the same for both app types.

**Can you combine canvas apps and model-driven apps in one solution?**

Yes, and the episode recommends it. A common pattern uses a model-driven app as the system of record for data, processes and security, embeds canvas apps in forms for specialized screens, and adds standalone canvas apps for mobile field work on the same Dataverse tables, with Power Automate flows connecting them.
