## FAQ

**Why can't the Teams admin center fix a user who cannot sign in to Teams?**

Because Teams does not decide who gets a token, Microsoft Entra does. Conditional Access evaluates MFA, device compliance, location and risk before Teams loads, and the Teams admin center has no view of that evaluation. The Entra sign-in log shows which policy applied and why the attempt failed, so troubleshooting should start there.

**Does blocking an app in the Teams admin center remove its access to tenant data?**

No, it mainly hides the app in the Teams client. The consent grant, the enterprise application and its service principal remain in Entra, and tokens can keep working until they are revoked. The episode recommends treating app permissions like privileged access and checking who consented, which scopes were granted and how to revoke them quickly.

**Why does a newly assigned Teams policy not take effect right away?**

Because the user's client keeps working with tokens issued under the earlier decision context. The Teams admin center shows that a policy is assigned, but behavior changes only when tokens refresh or the user reauthenticates. Repeatedly toggling settings in the meantime adds confusion, because the eventual change gets attributed to the wrong setting.
