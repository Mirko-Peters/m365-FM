## FAQ

**Is Azure Backup immutable by default?**

No. Azure Backup is not immutable on default settings. Immutability comes from three protections: soft delete forces a delay before deletion, multi-user authorization requires a second person for destructive changes, and vault lock makes the configuration irreversible, so retention cannot be lowered and soft-deleted points cannot be purged, even by an owner.

**In what order should I enable soft delete, multi-user authorization and vault lock?**

Enable soft delete first and prove it by deleting and restoring a test item. Then enforce multi-user authorization on deletion, disabling protection and retention reduction. Apply vault lock last, only after testing recovery and alerts, because it is irreversible and you will no longer be able to lower retention to save cost.

**How can someone destroy Azure backups without deleting them?**

They can lower retention below the age of the oldest recovery point and simply wait, so the points expire on their own. They can also change a policy so protection goes from daily to never, or disable protection temporarily and never re-enable it. Alerts on retention policy changes help catch these quiet attacks.
