This episode is for Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 architects, admins and compliance owners who want a document management design that holds. You learn how to bind Dynamics records to governed SharePoint libraries with content types, Purview labels, event-based retention, DLP and Copilot.

## Key takeaways

- Dynamics 365 should hold the events and SharePoint the evidence, linked through a document location created by rule for each record instead of attachments and copies.
- Content types and required metadata such as lifecycle state, sensitivity and retention category give documents identity, so the approved version can always be found and proven.

## FAQ

**How should Dynamics 365 and SharePoint share responsibility for documents?**

Dynamics 365 should hold the events and SharePoint the evidence. Each opportunity, project or case gets a document location created by rule in one SharePoint site per capability, and the URL is written into the record, so users open governed links with content types and metadata instead of attachments or copies.

**When should retention start for contracts, projects and case evidence?**

Retention should start at a business event, not at upload. In the episode's model, contracts are kept seven years after expiration, project artifacts five years after completion in Dynamics 365, and case evidence ten years from resolution. Legal holds pause the clocks, and disposition is reviewed and recorded rather than happening silently.

**How many sensitivity labels should an organization start with?**

Start with a small ladder of four labels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Create labels per risk rather than per team, with a few Confidential variants such as finance, legal contract and project commercials, plus a Restricted evidence label that blocks print and download. Add labels only when a new jurisdiction demands it.
