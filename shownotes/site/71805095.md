## FAQ

**Why are custom connectors in Power Automate a governance risk?**

Custom connectors are usually owned by individual makers, so they can carry insecure API definitions, excessive permissions and open authentication that admins never see. The CoE Starter Kit shows who owns a connector but not what data it moves, and when the owner leaves, mission-critical integrations tied to their identity can stop working.

**When is Logic Apps Standard cheaper than a consumption plan?**

According to the episode, once workflows run more than about six million actions per month, a consumption plan costs more than Logic Apps Standard. Standard uses fixed hosting, such as a WS1 plan, and custom connectors run as built-in extensions with no per-call fee, so costs stay predictable instead of growing with every execution.

**Which automations should stay in Power Automate and which move to Logic Apps Standard?**

Keep flows in Power Automate when they serve one team's productivity, such as team notifications, mobile approvals and simple Microsoft 365 triggers. Move an automation to Logic Apps Standard once it touches a virtual network, cross-departmental data or a legacy API, because it then needs VNet integration, infrastructure as code and transaction-level auditing.
