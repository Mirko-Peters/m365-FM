## FAQ

**How do you know if an AI project needs enterprise-scale infrastructure?**

Ask five questions about scalability, hardware, data intensity, algorithmic complexity and integration. If the answer is yes across all five, the project is not just another workload. It needs its own architecture, specialized accelerators like GPUs or TPUs, continuous data pipelines and disciplined operations, not a spot on standard servers beside email and ERP.

**Why do AI pilots fail when they move to production?**

Pilots fail because they were sheltered by curated data, hand-picked instances and a few engineers nursing them along. Production brings data silos, improvised permissions that break under audit, hardware shortfalls, compliance trip wires and a shortage of specialists. Without MLOps automation for testing, deployment and monitoring, every handoff and update becomes a gamble.

**What is an AI factory in enterprise AI?**

An AI factory is a central orchestration layer that coordinates DataOps, MLOps and GenAIOps instead of letting teams improvise separately. It combines centralized data, reusable templates, role-based access control, private networking and scheduled GPUs, so every pipeline deploys the same way and pilots move into production in a governed, repeatable way.
