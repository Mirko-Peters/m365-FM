## FAQ

**Why do static compliance checklists fail?**

Static checklists fail because regulations keep changing while the list stays frozen. Teams keep ticking the same boxes and believe they are covered, even though the requirements have moved. Automating such a list in Power Automate only bakes in the rigidity, so every new rule forces costly rebuilds of hardcoded flows.

**How often should Power Automate compliance checks run?**

Compliance checks should run on a rhythm that matches the risk: weekly for broad reviews, daily for higher-risk checks and quarterly for tasks tied to board reporting. Running every hour causes alert fatigue, while checking only twice a year lets risks build up. Staggering schedules also protects tenant performance in larger environments.

**How do you keep compliance flows from sprawling across departments?**

Govern them like any other enterprise system. Use a shared naming prefix with department and process names, write all results to one central compliance log, publish approved templates, and restrict who can create compliance flows. A pattern library of tested building blocks lets departments reuse designs instead of cloning and modifying flows on their own.
