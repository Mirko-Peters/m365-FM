## FAQ

**How long does Microsoft 365 Copilot take to pay for itself?**

In Forrester's composite model, payback landed in roughly ten months, with a three-year risk-adjusted ROI of about 116 percent. The episode stresses that these are modeled averages from a study commissioned by Microsoft, so rollout pace, training and governance shape your own result.

**How many hours does Copilot save per user each month?**

Forrester modeled about nine hours saved per Copilot user per month, as a risk-adjusted average rather than a promise. Sophisticated power users sometimes save twenty hours, while hesitant users reclaim only a handful at first. The operational model also assumes that only half of the recovered hours are put back into productive work.

**What costs besides the license should I budget for Copilot?**

Beyond the list price of thirty dollars per user per month at the time of recording, budget for professional services, IT staff to implement and manage the system, integration, compliance checks and especially training. The episode calls training one of the largest cost buckets, because users need structured instruction and time to explore the tool.
