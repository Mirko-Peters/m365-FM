## FAQ

**Do Power Apps Generative Pages require a premium license?**

Yes, in practice every user of a generated page needs a Power Apps premium license. Generative Pages build a React page inside a model-driven app on Dataverse, and Dataverse is not part of standard Microsoft 365 rights. The app is classified as premium by its technical dependencies, not by what the maker intended.

**Can SharePoint virtual tables avoid Dataverse licensing costs in Power Apps?**

No, a virtual table is itself a Dataverse artifact, so it does not avoid premium licensing. Dataverse still handles the metadata, permissions and create, read, update and delete operations, even though the rows stay in SharePoint. The episode recommends using virtual tables only for read-only dashboards and adds that they slow performance through double authorization.

**How much does Power Apps premium licensing cost for a department of 50 users?**

At the prices quoted in the episode, about 2,000 dollars per month, or 24,000 dollars a year. That assumes the per user plan at roughly 40 dollars per user per month, which covers unlimited premium apps. The per app plan starts at about 10 dollars per user per app, so costs multiply with every additional app.
