## FAQ

**Why do Power Apps on SharePoint lists only show 500 or 2,000 records?**

Because SharePoint cannot delegate many queries, Power Apps pulls data to the client and evaluates it locally, capped at 500 records by default or 2,000 with the setting raised. Records beyond that window are silently missing, so filters, searches and counts return wrong results. The blue delegation banner is the warning sign.

**When should a Power App move from SharePoint lists to Dataverse?**

Move when a list will exceed 100,000 items, users need compound filters or multi-column search, records have more than one or two lookups, offline is required, or security must be row- or field-level with auditing. Persistent blue delegation banners in core flows and errors near the 5,000-item mark are further red flags.

**Is Dataverse worth the premium license compared to SharePoint?**

The episode argues yes once an app is data-centric. SharePoint is bundled with Microsoft 365, but its workarounds cost people hours: Power Automate flows that simulate joins, denormalized columns, calculated columns faking rollups and manual auditing. Dataverse licensing is a predictable line item, while those workarounds become a recurring cost the episode compares to an annuity.
