## FAQ

**What is the difference between showback and chargeback?**

Showback attributes cost without impact, while chargeback moves the cost into a team's budget. Showback is telemetry: reports and dashboards that change no incentives. Chargeback makes people feel the spend but can push teams to look cheap, argue allocations and hide in shared services. Neither is accountability, which needs owned decisions, enforced constraints and an audit trail.

**How should Azure budget alerts be set up so teams actually respond?**

Treat each budget as an escalation contract tied to a named owner, not a shared mailbox. Use staged thresholds, for example notify at 50 and 75 percent, require a response at 90 and escalate at 100. Each alert should open a ticket that demands one of four responses: acknowledge, mitigate, request an exception or reforecast.

**How can organizations reduce Microsoft 365 license shelfware?**

Make license ownership and reclamation automatic instead of relying on quarterly reviews. Product owners should attest that users need specific licenses, with automatic reclaim after a grace period. Wire leaver events to license harvesting, assign licenses by role, put temporary premium SKUs into an expiring exception lane, and track unused licenses and time to reclaim.
