## FAQ

**Why isn't Copilot Cowork improving my team's productivity?**

Because most managers supervise it like a human intern instead of redesigning the workflows around it. They spend minutes crafting a prompt and even longer fixing the output, which cancels the time saved. Without clean grounding data and clear intent, the agentic system simply scales existing chaos and produces almost-good work that clogs review cycles.

**What does it mean for a manager to become an architect of AI systems?**

It means designing the environment that makes good output inevitable rather than managing the output itself. An architect diagnoses where the data flow broke instead of blaming people, cleans grounding data in SharePoint, versions prompts, maps capability gaps and places deliberate check-and-approve gates where the machine stops and a human takes over.

**Which metrics show whether a Copilot rollout is actually scaling?**

Track cycle time, rework rate, decision latency and incident rate instead of hours saved or prompts sent. Cycle time runs from request to verified output, rework rate shows how often humans correct AI output, decision latency measures waiting for approvals, and incident rate covers hallucinated facts and compliance breaches. Review them in a weekly system review.
