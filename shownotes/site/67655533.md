## FAQ

**How do I set up a Minecraft scavenger hunt for team building?**

Follow six steps: choose or generate a fresh world, set a clear play boundary, hide target items or chests, define win conditions, pick local or managed hosting and send invites, then time-box the round. No coding, mods or advanced server setup is needed, and it takes roughly thirty to sixty minutes.

**Why does traditional team building often fail?**

It usually lacks purpose, so people sense they are checking a box and disengage. The episode argues these events miss progression, feedback loops and authentic engagement. Games like Minecraft provide clear goals, immediate feedback and visible progress, so teammates actually rely on each other instead of pretending to trust one another.

**What should a debrief after a Minecraft capture the flag round ask?**

Ask three short questions right after each round: who called out roles and how was that received, where communication broke down under pressure, and what one change the team will try next round. Keeping it brief reinforces lessons while fresh and keeps momentum going into the next game.
