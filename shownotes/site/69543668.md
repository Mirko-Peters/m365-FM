## FAQ

**What is the difference between Microsoft 365 and ServiceNow in enterprise workflows?**

Microsoft 365 is the engagement plane and ServiceNow is the execution plane. Teams, Outlook and Copilot capture intent, collaboration and context where people already work. ServiceNow owns the authoritative workflow state, routing, approvals and evidence. The episode argues the two should be stitched together with explicit controls rather than one platform replacing the other.

**How do Microsoft 365 Copilot and Now Assist work together?**

They work as two assistants with separate jurisdictions. Copilot understands meetings, mail, chats and files in Microsoft 365 and turns messy intent into structured requests. Now Assist understands ServiceNow records, workflows, knowledge and case history. The recommended pattern is a handoff: Copilot captures the request, then ServiceNow takes over when the next step changes workflow state.

**Should AI agents be allowed to write to ServiceNow directly?**

Not without governance. The episode recommends starting with read-only integration such as Microsoft Graph connectors that index ServiceNow content, then moving to supervised write actions that land as ServiceNow workflow transitions tied to identity, with least privilege and rollback paths. Agentic execution comes last, only for actions proven low risk, reversible and observable.
