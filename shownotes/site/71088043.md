## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts stall after a few weeks?**

Rollouts often stall around weeks 6 to 12 because real work exposes weak permissions, duplicate workspaces and missing labels. Early demos build confidence, but inconsistent answers and permission surprises lower trust, usage drops and leaders start asking for ROI proof. The root cause is governance treated as one-time configuration instead of ongoing operation.

**What does it mean to call Microsoft 365 an enterprise operating system?**

It means Microsoft 365 already allocates access, coordinates resources, provides shared work surfaces and enforces constraints, just like an operating system. Identity controls who can act, data shapes what the platform knows, Teams and SharePoint carry collaboration, Purview defines compliance boundaries and Power Automate and agents form an automation plane that moves actions across the tenant.

**What is the personal, collaborative and enterprise zone model?**

It is a way to match governance to business risk instead of applying one rule everywhere. The personal zone holds drafts and experiments with baseline security, the collaborative zone needs named owners, naming and lifecycle standards, and the enterprise zone carries critical and regulated content with strict ownership, metadata and regular access reviews.
