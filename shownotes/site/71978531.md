## FAQ

**How long does it take to make Copilot a habit?**

Carina de Vries plans around 90 days, because building a daily routine takes about 28 days and turning it into a habit takes roughly 60 more. That is why she recommends introducing a few critical features first rather than piling up many at once, since each new habit needs its own time to settle.

**What weekly routine helps users adopt Copilot?**

Share the best Copilot tip on Monday, hold a Q&A session on Wednesday and let users share their best prompts on Friday. This keeps people working with Copilot three days a week. The project team can review shared prompts, pick the strongest ones and build a prompt library for the whole organization.

**Do organizations need full governance in place before starting Copilot adoption?**

No, Carina argues waiting for complete governance is unnecessary. Organizations should set up a minimal set of tenant settings that keep data in the tenant, then start the rollout. Further governance, such as agreeing which tasks teams use Copilot for, can be developed alongside the adoption program rather than blocking it.
