## FAQ

**How do you connect Dynamics 365 to Microsoft Fabric?**

You connect them through Dataverse, without middleware or third-party tools. You need a Power BI workspace enabled for Fabric and admin rights in Power Platform, then select the Dataverse tables you need, define the Fabric destination and schedule ingestion. Data Factory or Dataflow Gen2 then shape the data for reporting.

**Why is exporting Dynamics 365 data to Excel a problem?**

Excel exports become standalone snapshots that are stale almost immediately. Different people apply different filters, so totals stop matching and meetings turn into debates about which version is right. The root cause is that operational and analytical systems are not wired together, not a lack of skill or motivation.

**How should embedded Power BI reports in Dynamics 365 be designed?**

Design separate, focused reports for each major role instead of one generic dashboard. Sales needs pipeline and deals at risk, operations needs backlog and shipment delays, finance needs invoice aging. Row-level security filters each report to the user's territory or unit, and placing it inside forms keeps insight where decisions happen.
