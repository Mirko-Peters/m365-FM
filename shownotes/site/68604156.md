## FAQ

**What causes SharePoint sprawl?**

SharePoint sprawl comes from uncontrolled site creation, no lifecycle for retiring sites, vague ownership, zero retention and weak naming conventions. The episode defines sprawl as duplicated files, abandoned sites, orphaned permissions and stale sharing links, and explains how it adds search noise, raises compliance risk and leads Copilot to synthesize answers from duplicate or outdated content.

**Can I enforce SharePoint site lifecycle with E3, or do I need E5?**

Yes, E3 works: gate site creation with a request form, provision from templates and run Power Automate checks every 90 days that send attestation emails and escalate to managers. E5 with SharePoint Advanced Management automates this with inactive site policies that email owners, switch idle sites to read-only and archive them to Microsoft 365 Archive.

**What is the difference between a retention policy and a retention label?**

A retention policy targets locations, such as keeping everything in a site for five years, while a retention label targets individual items, such as a specific contract kept seven years as a record. When both apply, the label wins, so the episode recommends labels for critical content and policies as the broad baseline underneath.
