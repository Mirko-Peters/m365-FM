## FAQ

**Why do users prefer submitting IT tickets in Microsoft Teams instead of a portal?**

Because it does not interrupt their work. Instead of finding a portal URL, logging in and filling a long form, users press a button and complete a short adaptive card in the chat they already use. It feels like sending a message, while Jira Service Management still receives a structured ticket with mapped fields and routing.

**What do admins need to configure for Jira Service Management in Teams?**

Admins need careful field mapping, consent decisions and secure authentication. Teams forms must map to the fields Jira expects so tickets do not arrive incomplete. On the Microsoft 365 side, consent in Azure AD (now Microsoft Entra ID) and delegated or application permissions must be decided, plus scoped Jira API tokens, single sign-on and Conditional Access.

**Can IT agents manage Jira tickets without leaving Microsoft Teams?**

Yes, agents can work tickets directly from adaptive cards in Teams. Notifications from the Jira bot show the description, priority and requester, with buttons to comment, change status or assign the ticket. Notifications can be grouped into dedicated channels, and screenshots shared in the conversation become part of the Jira ticket automatically.
