## FAQ

**What does Copilot do in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

It scores, prioritizes and recommends while the seller keeps control. Copilot ranks leads by likelihood to convert, grades opportunities with relationship health estimates and suggests next steps from call and email history. It also prepares pre-call summaries, writes post-call recaps and drafts follow-up emails that the rep edits and approves before sending.

**How is predictive forecasting in Dynamics 365 Sales different from spreadsheets?**

It recalculates deal probabilities from live pipeline signals instead of manual inputs. When email cadence drops or meetings stall, the forecast view updates without anyone editing cells. Managers see risk on dashboards mid-quarter and can coach reps in time, rather than discovering problems at the quarter review, with Power BI available for deeper drill-downs.

**How does LinkedIn Sales Navigator work with Dynamics 365 Sales?**

It brings social signals such as job changes, promotions and mutual connections into the CRM record. Sales Navigator can be added on or licensed through the Relationship Sales bundle with Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise. Sellers then notice when a champion changes roles and can reach the successor before a deal stalls.
