## FAQ

**Why do Azure bills grow even after a successful cloud migration?**

Bills grow because provisioning is frictionless while deprovisioning needs governance. The episode points to orphaned disks and snapshots, zombie VMs from failed pilots, dev and test environments running around the clock, and workloads left on pay-as-you-go because nobody classified them. Without policies that enforce cleanup, elasticity becomes a cost accelerant instead of a saving.

**Which FinOps metrics should cloud teams track?**

The episode recommends six metrics instead of total spend: reserved instance and savings plan coverage, ideally 65 to 75 percent; cost per workload; idle resource percentage; cost variance against forecast; license utilization; and the share of shadow workloads outside governed environments. Together they reveal whether the environment is actually becoming more efficient over time.

**How should organizations govern Copilot costs before a broad rollout?**

Start with data hygiene, then a controlled pilot. In the episode, one organization cleaned up SharePoint, made sensitivity labels mandatory, limited Copilot to managed devices through Conditional Access, piloted with 400 power users, and enforced hard token limits per user and application. A FinTech team cut monthly Azure OpenAI spend from $340,000 to $68,000.
