## FAQ

**What is a power architect in a Microsoft 365 transformation?**

A power architect is the person or cross-functional group responsible for designing how power flows through the organization. The role covers decision flow, data ownership, access distribution, interaction patterns and escalation paths. Unlike enterprise architects, product owners, CoE leads or governance teams, it owns the fit between authority, accountability, access and how decisions move.

**Why do high Copilot and Teams adoption numbers not prove that a transformation is working?**

Adoption metrics measure contact with tools, not whether authority moved or decisions got faster. An organization can show rising Teams usage, more published flows and broad Copilot enablement while key decisions still run through private manager conversations and overloaded approvers. Pair usage data with decision latency, cycle time, rework, exception volume and shadow workarounds.

**Where should leaders start when redesigning how decisions flow in their organization?**

Start with one value stream, not an enterprise-wide program. Pick a bounded workflow such as contract reviews or customer onboarding, then map decisions, dependencies, ownership and access as they happen in practice. Redesign approvals, roles and escalation before adding tooling, instrument business metrics, and only expand once the pattern has reduced real operational drag.
