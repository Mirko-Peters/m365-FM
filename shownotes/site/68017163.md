## FAQ

**Why do Cloud Adoption Framework strategies fail?**

They usually stay too high level. Strategy documents full of buzzwords like agility or innovation do not name workloads, Azure services or measurable outcomes, so admins cannot build against them and finance cannot fund them. The episode recommends tying each motivation to one workload, one Azure service, one metric and one review cadence.

**What does a CFO need to see in an Azure migration business case?**

Three things: the real upfront cost, a break-even timeline and a named owner for cost overruns. Those numbers should come from an Azure Migrate assessment and the TCO calculator, factoring in levers like reserved instances, savings plans and Azure Hybrid Benefit, then translated into outcomes such as stable budgets or fewer service desk hours.

**What is a cloud center of excellence and why does CAF need one?**

It is a cross-team group where security, networking, operations, DevOps and finance decide together how strategy becomes action. The episode argues migrations fail over ownership, not technology, so this group should name an identity and access owner, a billing owner and a patching owner before any Azure subscription is signed.
