## FAQ

**How does Copilot in Dynamics 365 Field Service create work orders from voice notes?**

The technician records a short spoken update after the job, and Copilot turns it into structured work order fields plus a readable service narrative. Because it knows the job, customer, asset, parts allocation and service history from the CRM, it maps phrases like a replaced pump to the correct part number instead of storing raw text.

**Can Copilot help field technicians troubleshoot a repair on site?**

Yes, Copilot surfaces guidance in context while the technician is still at the equipment. Based on the work order, asset history and the logged issue, it pulls past service records with similar symptoms, the parts used and notes from earlier technicians, drawing on internal repair logs, manuals and IoT sensor feeds rather than the open web.

**How does Copilot improve parts planning in field service?**

Copilot starts matching parts while the technician is still describing the fault, instead of waiting for a finished diagnosis. It checks which component that model uses, whether it is in inventory and where the nearest stock sits, so a part can be delivered during the job. Repeated requests across jobs also give procurement early warnings.
