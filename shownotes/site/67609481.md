## FAQ

**Can Copilot replace a Power BI developer?**

No, the episode concludes Copilot complements developers rather than replacing them. Copilot was much faster at producing visuals and simple measures, but in complex models it guessed relationships and time intelligence wrongly. The developer supplied business context, validation and dashboard storytelling, so the recommended approach is Copilot for drafts and humans for checking and refining.

**Where does Copilot make mistakes in Power BI data models?**

Copilot struggles where business rules are not written into the schema. It joined tables by matching column names, created a many-to-many relationship that inflated profit margins, ignored customer status fields and built a year-over-year measure on the order date instead of the calendar table. The results looked plausible but were wrong.

**Is a Copilot-generated Power BI dashboard ready for executives?**

Usually not without changes. Copilot quickly fills a canvas with charts by region, time and category, but the layout feels generic and gives every metric similar weight. The developer ordered visuals by what decision makers need first, used plain business titles and connected metrics like discounts to profit, which made the dashboard tell a story.
