## FAQ

**What is the difference between Defender XDR and separate security tools?**

Defender XDR correlates email, identity, endpoint and cloud app signals into a single incident with one timeline, while siloed tools each see only one part. With separate consoles, analysts must stitch sender, user, token, device and app together by hand, which slows response and lets low-severity alerts hide an active intrusion.

**Why isn't quarantining a phishing email enough?**

Because the attack has often already moved from the mailbox to identity. If the user consented to a malicious OAuth app or handed over a token, the attacker keeps reading mail through Graph. Real containment means revoking sessions and tokens, removing the consent, rolling back mailbox rules and isolating the device if needed.

**How long does Defender XDR take to pay back?**

The episode puts typical payback in a 12 to 18 month window. Savings come from fewer analyst hours spent correlating across consoles, less integration plumbing and training, fewer duplicate licenses for organizations already paying for Microsoft 365 and Azure, and faster response that shrinks the blast radius of incidents.
