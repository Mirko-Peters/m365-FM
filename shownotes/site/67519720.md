## FAQ

**Why does Microsoft 365 adoption not equal digital transformation?**

Adoption metrics only show that people log in, not that they collaborate or decide differently. Licenses, rising storage and daily Teams usage can coexist with scattered conversations and unchanged habits. Real transformation shows up in depth of collaboration, new workflows and better decisions, which requires technology, mindset and relationships to move forward together.

**How can an intranet built on SharePoint get employees to actually use it?**

Build it around how people actually work and test it with staff rather than assuming what they need. In the episode's example, employees wanted open conversations instead of static department pages. Simpler navigation, clear content ownership, surfacing updates in simpler feeds tied to Viva, and continuous feedback rounds turned an unused portal into a trusted space.

**What role does leadership play in Microsoft 365 adoption?**

Leadership attitude can accelerate adoption or stall it completely. When managers consistently support the change, explain why it matters and use the tools themselves, employees follow with less resistance. Where managers bypass the tools or only demand compliance, training does not stick, and Mirko expects the same pattern to repeat with AI in Microsoft 365.
