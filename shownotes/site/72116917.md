## FAQ

**When should you use OneNote versus Loop in Microsoft 365?**

Use OneNote for personal notes and Loop for shared content that must stay current. Karinne Diamond Bessette keeps her own notes in OneNote but embeds Loop components linked to Planner tasks so takeaways stay visible. For team work she uses Loop pages pinned in Teams or pasted into email as one source of truth, though Loop cannot be shared externally.

**What are Copilot Notebooks used for?**

Copilot Notebooks gather related content in one place so Copilot can ground its answers on it. Karinne added calls, emails, group messages and Loop components about a company event, then asked for a summary and what her role required. The result listed sources, the most recent information and who to ask, taking about 20 minutes instead of hours.

**How does outdated content affect Copilot answers?**

Redundant, obsolete and trivial content muddies AI answers, because an old but better-written document can outrank newer, more correct information. Karinne recommends good document hygiene: expiry reviews, such as reminders for files older than two years, and checking regulations first, since compliance failures cost money. Only then should teams clean up content for relevance.
