## FAQ

**Why are AI meeting recaps risky for decision-making?**

Because a recap is an interpretation, not a record, and it quickly becomes the organization's memory. It can state confidently what the room never decided, or omit the dissent, risk caveat or minority view that mattered. The episode's norm is that a recap is input, not truth, and must point to decisions, owners and assumptions.

**What is intentional friction in AI-assisted work?**

Intentional friction means deliberate checkpoints reserved for high-impact work where humans must do the hard thinking. The episode names three: a human-written problem statement before any AI draft becomes the working artifact, at least two plausible alternatives with reasons for rejection, and a human reasoning trace listing assumptions and what would change the decision.

**How should leaders use Viva Insights in a Copilot rollout?**

Leaders should use Viva Insights as feedback to redesign work, not as a tool to police individuals. Its signals show patterns like shrinking focus time, after-hours creep or meeting overload, but cannot explain causes. The episode warns that high Copilot usage can mask strain, so signals should prompt inquiry rather than serve as verdicts.
