## FAQ

**What is a control plane in Microsoft 365?**

A control plane is the layer that decides how other systems behave, translating intent into policy. In this episode Entra ID becomes its backbone: a user's role or guest status is set once in identity, and SharePoint, Teams, Purview, Defender and Copilot agents inherit that context instead of each team defining its own baseline.

**How can I estimate license waste in a Microsoft 365 E5 tenant?**

Start by adding up unused premium capability, inactive licenses, Copilot and agent costs, and governance labor. The episode's model for 5,000 E5 seats assumes 20 to 30 percent of advanced features sit idle and 10 to 15 percent of licenses belong to dormant accounts, arriving at roughly $1.3 million in annual leakage.

**What does a 90-day Microsoft 365 recovery plan look like?**

A 90-day recovery runs in three phases: audit, automate governance, then build the control plane. The first 30 days inventory inactive licenses, orphaned apps and excessive permissions. By day 60, lifecycle workflows, entitlement management, sensitivity labels and DLP are automated. By day 90, Entra ID drives authorization decisions across the tenant.
