## FAQ

**What is the medallion architecture in Microsoft Fabric?**

The medallion architecture splits data into three layers with strict contracts: bronze, silver and gold. Bronze in the lakehouse files area holds raw, immutable, append-only data. Silver holds validated Delta tables with enforced types, deduplication and tests. Gold holds curated star schemas exposed through Direct Lake semantic models, where business logic and DAX measures live.

**How should I structure Fabric workspaces for an enterprise?**

Use separate workspace sets for platform, domains and shared analytics, each with dev, test and prod. The platform owns bronze and silver, domains build gold models on shortcuts to platform silver tables instead of copies, and only shared analytics hosts certified semantic models. Workspace roles are bound to Entra groups, never to individual users.

**How do I get started with Microsoft Fabric in the first week?**

Follow a seven-day plan that builds one end-to-end path. Set up capacity, Entra groups and workspaces first, then one ingestion pipeline with validation tests, one gold table with a Direct Lake semantic model, Purview scans and labels, monitoring alerts with a deliberate failure drill, and finally a domain workspace using shortcuts and RLS.
