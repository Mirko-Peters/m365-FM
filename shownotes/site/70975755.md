## FAQ

**Why does tighter leadership control slow down AI value?**

Control stops scaling because every extra approval creates a queue. AI lets teams produce more analysis and options, but if decisions still route through one leader, that output just reaches the same bottleneck faster. Teams start optimizing for approval instead of outcomes, initiative drops, and the leader becomes a single point of failure.

**How should leaders respond to Power Platform sprawl?**

Leaders should treat sprawl as a signal rather than lock everything down. Unmanaged apps and flows show where the official operating model is too slow or rigid. Instead of freezing builds, the episode recommends tiered autonomy: guardrails, shared patterns, visible ownership, reusable components and clear environment strategies, with review reserved for higher-risk automations.

**What is the seven-day leadership reset?**

It is a one-week experiment on a single recurring decision that waits too long on leadership. Map how it really flows, remove one unnecessary approval, name one owner, and check they have the data and access to decide. Track decision latency or rework, then review the results with the people inside the flow.
