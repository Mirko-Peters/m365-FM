## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Syntex the same as SharePoint Premium?**

Yes, Syntex was not discontinued but folded into SharePoint Premium. Its document processing, content assembly, OCR and taxonomy tagging now sit on one platform together with SharePoint Advanced Management. Content processing services remain pay-as-you-go, while some newer experiences, such as certain content apps, are licensed per seat.

**How does SharePoint Premium make Microsoft 365 Copilot more useful?**

It gives Copilot structured content to work with. AI models classify documents, extract metadata such as vendor, amount and due date, and OCR old PDFs so they become searchable. Without that structure, a prompt like finding invoices above a certain amount returns random files; with it, Copilot can filter, summarize and send results reliably.

**How does SharePoint Premium help prevent oversharing?**

It adds governance tools that find and fix overexposed content. Data access governance reports flag wide-open sites and mismatched sensitivity labels, site access reviews ask owners to validate permissions regularly, and restricted access control limits a site to approved security groups. AI-driven policy recommendations also suggest fixes based on well-governed sites.
