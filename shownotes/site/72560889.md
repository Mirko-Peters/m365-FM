## FAQ

**What is harvest now, decrypt later and why does it matter for Microsoft 365 data?**

It is an attack where encrypted traffic is captured today and stored until quantum computers can break the RSA or ECC protecting it. For Microsoft 365, that means emails, SharePoint documents and Teams conversations with long-term value could be exposed retroactively, so data that must stay confidential for years needs post-quantum protection planned now.

**What is crypto agility and how does it fit into governance?**

Crypto agility is the ability to swap cryptographic algorithms without redesigning applications. The episode treats it as a governance primitive: key management tracks which algorithms each key uses, sensitivity labels with long confidentiality horizons trigger post-quantum requirements, procurement demands it from vendors and architecture reviews reject designs that cannot change algorithms.

**What should Microsoft 365 and Azure teams do now to prepare for quantum computing?**

Start with a cryptography inventory showing where RSA and ECC are used and how keys are managed. Then classify data by confidentiality horizon, build a crypto agility roadmap, align it with Microsoft's published timelines, form a cross-functional quantum governance working group, run a governed pilot, invest in skills and add post-quantum requirements to vendor assessments.
