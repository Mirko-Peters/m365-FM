## FAQ

**What do autonomous agents in Dynamics 365 customer service do with incoming email?**

They turn email into structured cases. Agents read headers, threads and attachments, extract entities like order IDs and serial numbers, score sentiment and urgency, and bind the sender to a contact where possible. They then create the case with required fields, detect duplicates, route it through unified routing and let Copilot draft a reply for human review.

**When should an AI agent escalate a customer service case to a human?**

Escalation should happen when defined signals trip: confidence below the threshold, strongly negative sentiment, a VIP flag or safety terms. The case then goes to a person with a compact summary, compressed timeline, labeled attachments and highlighted policy cues, and experts are pinged in Teams with that context, so nobody has to dig through threads first.

**What needs to be in place before automating email-to-case in Dynamics 365?**

Controls must come before speed. That means a written intent taxonomy with confidence thresholds, dedicated mailboxes per intent family, required CRM fields for customer, asset, entitlement and SLA, and governance such as audit logs, PII redaction, DLP and role-based access. Without clean categories, product lists and entitlements, the agent only accelerates existing mistakes.
