## FAQ

**Does an Azure budget stop spending when it is exceeded?**

No, an Azure budget is a signal, not a spending limit, and it will not shut down workloads. The episode recommends budgets at subscription scope with early thresholds around 50 and 70 percent, routed through action groups, webhooks or Logic Apps into tickets or channels with a named owner instead of a shared mailbox.

**How do you enforce resource tagging for cost allocation in Azure?**

Make tags a deployment precondition with Azure Policy. Use deny for missing or invalid tags on production workloads and modify to add or inherit baseline tags from the subscription or resource group. Keep the taxonomy to six to eight tags with controlled values, because tag values are case sensitive and free text drifts.

**Why are subscriptions the main cost governance boundary in Azure?**

A subscription is where RBAC scope, policy scope and a measurable financial blast radius meet, which resource groups cannot reliably provide. The episode proposes a vending model: no subscription without a named owner, a budget with early thresholds, allowed SKUs and regions for its purpose, and an escalation workflow that actually routes.
