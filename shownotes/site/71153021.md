## Key takeaways

- Separate technical custody by IT from service ownership and business sovereignty over data, and give every service and data domain a named, accountable owner.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between technical custody and business sovereignty?**

Technical custody means IT keeps Microsoft 365 available, secure and correctly configured. Business sovereignty means the business owns the meaning, quality, access logic and lifecycle of its information. IT can host data but should not classify it for a department, so both layers, plus compliance as a guardrail layer, need separate named owners.

**How do you prevent orphaned teams in Microsoft Teams?**

Design ownership into provisioning and lifecycle instead of relying on memory. The episode recommends a no owner, no workspace rule, at least two real owners for production collaboration spaces, review prompts sent to named people rather than shared mailboxes, and owner reassignment triggered automatically by HR joiner, mover and leaver events.

**Which metrics show whether Microsoft 365 ownership actually works?**

The episode suggests five metrics: ownership coverage, policy adherence, cycle time, Copilot usefulness and auditability. Together they show how much of the estate has a named accountable owner, whether behavior follows the rules, how fast compliant requests are handled, how trustworthy AI results are and whether ownership decisions can be evidenced over time.
