## FAQ

**Why do RAG and multimodal apps hallucinate on real enterprise data?**

They hallucinate mostly because the data is not prepared, indexed or monitored, not because the model lacks intelligence. Real inputs are blurry scans, old PDFs and phone photos. The episode recommends hybrid keyword plus vector search with semantic reranking in Azure AI Search, plus ongoing evaluator runs for groundedness, relevance and coherence.

**How do you know whether an AI agent built in Azure AI Foundry is actually useful?**

Check whether it reduces human hours, whether you capture metrics like groundedness, fluency and coherence, and whether telemetry catches drift before users complain. Azure AI Foundry's observability features and built-in evaluators are the documented way to measure this. Without them, you are guessing whether the agent is accurate or just sounds confident.

**What safeguards do autonomous agents need before going into production?**

Autonomous agents need minimal identity and access rights, logging with a rollback path for every serious action, and content and behavior filters such as Azure AI Content Safety. The episode also stresses human-in-the-loop options, stage testing and approval workflows, noting that autonomous agents in Copilot Studio were still in preview at recording time.
