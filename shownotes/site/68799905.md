## FAQ

**What is hybrid QAOA on Azure Quantum?**

Hybrid QAOA runs a parameterized quantum circuit and lets a classical optimizer adjust its angles in a loop. The circuit combines a cost Hamiltonian that penalizes conflicts with a mixer that keeps exploring. On Azure Quantum you develop on simulators, sample real QPUs briefly, and Python orchestrates the iterations until improvement plateaus.

**Which business problems suit quantum optimization today?**

Combinatorial problems where constraints conflict, such as logistics routing modeled as Max-Cut and workforce scheduling, suit hybrid QAOA best. The episode walks through a regional logistics network and a 500-person healthcare roster. It warns against expecting speedups on toy problems that a simple greedy heuristic already solves, and against claims about breaking RSA.

**How should you interpret results from a quantum circuit?**

Read the full histogram of measured bit strings, not a single winning sample. Quantum circuits are statistical experiments, so you need enough shots, then decode bit strings into hubs or shifts and reapply hard constraints. A robust answer shows consensus across the top cluster; a flat histogram means the encoding or circuit depth is wrong.
