## FAQ

**Where is the Copilot web access setting and should I turn it off?**

Look for the web access setting or a related plugin toggle in the Search and Intelligence center or in Copilot policies; the wording varies by tenant. The episode does not prescribe on or off. Make a conscious choice, test what Copilot returns from public sites, and tell users what changes if you block it.

**How should Microsoft 365 Copilot licenses be assigned?**

Treat them as a flexible pool rather than permanent ownership. Pilot with smaller groups, review license and usage reports monthly or quarterly, reclaim inactive seats and give them to people already showing adoption, such as analysts with repetitive documentation or staff drafting lots of email, instead of handing seats out by hierarchy.

**Does DLP work the same for Copilot in Edge and in Word?**

Not necessarily, so validate it. Some admins report that DLP and compliance rules apply unevenly between Office-hosted Copilot and the Edge sidebar. The suggested test: open a sensitive file in Word and ask for a summary, repeat it in Edge, then record whether outputs differ and whether DLP blocks both.
