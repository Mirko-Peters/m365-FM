## FAQ

**When should you use Power Automate workflows instead of events?**

Use workflows for local task execution, such as sending a notification or updating a single record. The episode argues that once logic spans multiple systems, domains or owners, it should move to business events and APIs, because long master flows hide dependencies, multiply calls and make ownership and timing hard to reason about.

**What are Dataverse business events and why do they matter?**

Dataverse business events fire only after an operation completes successfully, so they represent confirmed business facts rather than in-flight guesses. Combined with custom APIs and event catalogs, they let teams publish named moments such as an incident detected, while several small subscribers react in parallel with clear ownership and testable handlers.

**How do you move from mega-flows to event-driven orchestration without a rewrite?**

Start with one decision path where latency causes real cost, such as incident escalation or payment confirmation. Map the actual chain of flows, child flows, retries and waits, define the business moments, then publish one event with a few focused reactions. Let old and new models coexist and retire the legacy chain only once replacements are stable.
