## FAQ

**When is Microsoft 365 Copilot enough without building a RAG solution?**

Copilot is enough when the task lives in Outlook, Teams, SharePoint or OneDrive and the result is a draft, summary, rewrite or quick list. It respects your permissions and needs no custom pipeline, but it cannot index systems outside Microsoft 365, enforce citations or let you tune chunk sizes.

**How does retrieval-augmented generation work in Azure?**

Documents are ingested, split into chunks, tagged with metadata such as owner, version and date, and turned into embeddings stored in Azure AI Search. At query time the retriever finds the closest chunks, injects them into the prompt with rules to cite sources and say it does not know, and the model answers only from them.

**Is RAG better than fine-tuning a model on company policies?**

For policy and SOP content, the episode argues yes. Policies change often, and with RAG you fix the source and re-index instead of retraining the model. The engine stays the same while the data changes, and mandatory citations make every answer traceable and retrieval quality measurable through ongoing evaluation.
