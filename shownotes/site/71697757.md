## FAQ

**Why can a green executive dashboard be misleading?**

Because it tracks activity, not progress. Logins, session counts and training completion are easy to measure, but they do not show whether people trust a tool or get value from it. High activity can even signal friction, for example when someone spends three hours on a task that should take ten minutes.

**How can leaders use sentiment analysis in Microsoft 365 without surveilling employees?**

By aggregating and anonymizing the data and looking at patterns, not people. Mirko Peters describes using Microsoft Graph as a governance layer: the AI extracts trends and removes personal identifiers before any report reaches a human. The guiding question becomes what is broken in a team's workflow, not who is complaining.

**What are the five executive signals that replace the 40-chart deck?**

The decision confidence index, the behavior shift indicator, the escalation risk signal, the internal mobility and agility score, and the strategic alignment pulse. Together they condense Microsoft 365 collaboration data into a one-page leadership pulse report showing certainty, real workflow change, emerging friction, readiness to adapt and whether the strategy is actually believed.
