## FAQ

**What is a sovereign Microsoft 365 tenant?**

A sovereign tenant is one that is explicitly designed rather than incrementally configured, so it behaves predictably. You can say who has access to what and why, model what a policy change will affect before saving it, and detect and fix configuration drift within minutes through continuous reconciliation instead of quarterly audits.

**Why should Microsoft 365 governance start with identity and PIM?**

Identity comes first because every other layer depends on knowing who makes changes and who agents act for. The first 90 days inventory privileged roles, put every role above baseline under PIM with time-bound access, block legacy authentication, require MFA for all users and set up a role governance committee.

**How do you stop configuration drift in a Microsoft 365 tenant?**

You stop drift by making code, for example Microsoft365DSC, the only source of truth for your configuration. Every change is version controlled, peer reviewed and tested before production, reconciliation checks compare actual and desired state regularly, and low-risk deviations are fixed automatically while high-risk ones wait for a human approval.
