## FAQ

**What is prompt injection in AI assistants?**

Prompt injection is an attack where carefully crafted text slips an instruction into a request so the AI bypasses its safety rules. Models treat well-formed instructions as valid and cannot judge intent, so a scheduling assistant could leak a private file. Layered filters, red teaming and human review reduce the risk.

**Which AI systems count as high risk under the EU AI Act?**

The EU AI Act treats uses such as recruitment tools, credit scoring and workplace monitoring as high risk. Systems are sorted into minimal, limited, high and unacceptable tiers. High-risk systems need transparency, detailed audit logs and human oversight, so a Power Platform app that ranks CVs could fall into that category.

**Who should sit on an AI governance board?**

An AI governance board should be cross-functional, with technology leads, data governance owners, legal or compliance advisors, a business representative and at least one independent reviewer. Together they run bias and fairness audits, check data provenance, track drift metrics and maintain an AI use case inventory with risk tiers, because no single function can anticipate every consequence.
