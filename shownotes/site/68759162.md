## Key takeaways

- Intune only measures device health; it protects the tenant when compliance, Conditional Access and privileged access work together, so build layered CA policies in report-only mode first and keep exclusions surgical, owned and expiring.
- Adopt Microsoft's security baselines for Windows, Defender and Edge, align compliance policies to them and convert standing admin roles to eligible PIM assignments with [MFA](https://www.m365.fm/entity/mfa/?utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=shownotes&utm_campaign=intune-security-misconfigurations-why-your-intune-deployment-is-a-security-risk), justification and compliant-device activation.
- Protect BYOD with app protection policies and approved client apps, block legacy authentication, and roll out every change through pilot, early adopter and broad rings backed by a weekly Graph or PowerShell audit.

## FAQ

**What are the most common Intune security misconfigurations?**

The episode names five: weak Conditional Access policies, missing or divergent security baselines, PIM gaps that leave standing admin access, unmanaged BYOD devices without compliance or app protection, and reckless update and policy rollouts without rings. Each one widens the path attackers use to reach identities, mail and files.

**How should I roll out Conditional Access policies without locking users out?**

Start in report-only mode for a pilot group, watch insights and reporting for blocked sign-ins and legacy protocols for a week or two, then enforce in rings: pilot, early adopter, broad. Keep two break-glass accounts excluded and document every other exclusion with an owner, a reason and an expiry date.

**How do I protect corporate data on personal BYOD devices with Intune?**

Use app protection policies when full enrollment is not feasible. Require approved client apps such as Outlook, OneDrive and Teams through Conditional Access, block saving to personal storage, require a PIN, and wipe app data on sign-out. Block legacy authentication outright so old mail clients cannot skip MFA.
