## FAQ

**Why is identity the new security perimeter in Microsoft 365?**

Because attackers now log in with stolen credentials instead of breaking through firewalls. Cloud apps, remote work and personal devices dissolved the old network boundary, and under the shared responsibility model your configuration, policies and user access are your job. That makes Microsoft Entra ID, not the firewall, the gate that decides who gets in.

**Is MFA enough without Conditional Access?**

No, MFA protects against stolen passwords but does not judge context. Fatigue attacks can trick users into approving a fraudulent prompt. Conditional Access adds checks on device compliance, user risk and location, and can require another factor or block a sign-in when something looks wrong, such as a login from an unusual region at an odd hour.

**What does Privileged Identity Management do for admin accounts?**

PIM removes standing admin rights by making roles just-in-time. An admin requests activation, the role is active for a defined period and then expires automatically. Approval workflows, access reviews and audit trails add further control. The episode notes that PIM and Identity Protection require Microsoft Entra ID P2 licensing.
