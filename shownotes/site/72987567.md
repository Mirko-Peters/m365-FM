## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft Marketplace submissions get rejected?**

Most rejections come from five administrative errors, not product flaws. Microsoft's documentation says they cause 80% of review rejections: missing terms of use link, missing privacy policy link, no testing instructions for reviewers, missing service or account disclosures, and undisclosed additional purchases. Each is preventable with Microsoft's free pre-submission checklist.

**How long does it take to get listed on Microsoft Marketplace?**

Plan for 12 to 24 weeks minimum. Microsoft's estimates are eight to 20 weeks for AppSource and 12 to 24 weeks for Azure Marketplace, but only for a perfect first submission. Each rejection cycle adds two to four weeks, technical integration can take six to 10 weeks, and legal review adds more.

**Should I list my solution on AppSource or Azure Marketplace?**

Choose the storefront where your buyer shops, not the one that is easier to publish on. AppSource serves line-of-business buyers of business apps such as Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Teams integrations. Azure Marketplace serves developers, cloud architects and procurement teams. Multi-year pricing plans move an offer off AppSource to Azure Marketplace only.
