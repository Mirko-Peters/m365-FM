## FAQ

**Why does Copilot not show a recap after my Teams meeting?**

Usually because transcription was not turned on. If the admin policy is set to on with transcript required, Copilot only works when the meeting is transcribed, and without a transcript there is nothing to recap after the call ends. Policy changes can also take hours to sync across Teams clients.

**Can Copilot Chat see files I do not have permission to open?**

No. Copilot Chat is bound by Microsoft Graph and semantic indexing, so it only surfaces emails, chats, documents and meetings you can already access. Purview sensitivity labels can stop confidential content from being summarized, while SharePoint Advanced Management and Restricted SharePoint Search reduce oversharing and limit which sites are indexed.

**How do Copilot Studio agents show up in Microsoft Teams?**

They have to be published at least once first. Makers can share them under the built with Power Platform section, or submit them for admin approval so they appear as built for your org. Administrators then decide visibility and can pin agents to the app bar using setup policies.
