## FAQ

**How many channels should a Microsoft Teams team have?**

There is no magic number; channels should mirror how work actually moves. The episode argues that well-performing teams frame channels around workflows, roles and recurring processes. One team may work well with six channels, another may need twelve, as long as every channel has a clearly defined purpose people understand.

**Why do people stop using Teams and go back to email?**

Mostly because unclear structure creates friction, not because they dislike the tool. When nobody knows where to post, updates land in the wrong channel, files get duplicated and search fills with noise. People then quietly drift back to email or private chats, as one project team with a channel per topic did.

**What communication rules should a Teams channel have?**

Write down where each type of update goes, expected response times per channel and how urgent issues escalate. The episode builds this on three principles: clarity about where messages belong, consistency so habits stick, and accountability so members remind each other when norms slip. Without agreed rules, even a good layout fails.
