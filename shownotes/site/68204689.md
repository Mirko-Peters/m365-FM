## FAQ

**Do Copilot Studio agents have their own permissions?**

No. Copilot Studio agents mostly run on delegated permissions, requesting an access token on behalf of the signed-in user. Anything that user can see, the agent can retrieve and summarize. Connector consent granted once in an environment is also inherited by other agents hosted there, which widens their reach.

**Does a tenant-wide DLP policy automatically protect every Copilot Studio environment?**

Not reliably. The episode explains that Power Platform DLP policies are scoped to environments, and agents often sit in development environments nobody hardened. Each environment hosting agents needs a deliberately assigned policy with business, non-business and blocked connector groups, or new environments become potential leak paths.

**How can I test whether a Copilot Studio agent leaks sensitive data?**

Red-team it with a catalog of forbidden prompts, such as requests for executive salaries or all customer records, and track whether it refuses. Then watch connector analytics in the Power Platform admin center, send audit events to Microsoft Purview or Microsoft Sentinel, and reclassify connectors every quarter.
