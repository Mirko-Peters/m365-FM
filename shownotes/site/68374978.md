## FAQ

**When should I use the Researcher agent instead of Copilot with GPT-5?**

Use the Researcher agent whenever someone may later have to prove where a statement came from. The episode names five mandatory cases: governance documentation, financial or regulatory reporting, knowledge articles, Entra security and identity audits, and executive market analysis. Copilot with GPT-5 remains fine for emails, summaries and brainstorming, where speed matters more than provenance.

**Why is GPT-5 output in Copilot not enough for an audit?**

Because it is optimized for fluency, not provenance. GPT-5 predicts a plausible answer and may reference SharePoint or Teams content, but it gives no retrieval log showing which sources shaped its reasoning. The Researcher agent asks clarifying questions, fetches and cross-checks sources and records citations, producing a record an auditor can retrace, even if it takes minutes.

**How does a hybrid Copilot and Researcher agent workflow work?**

Copilot drafts first, the Researcher agent verifies, and Copilot polishes. GPT-5 builds the outline quickly, every statistic and key claim then goes to the agent to be traced to sources, and the cited results return to Copilot for tone and summary. Unverified drafts stay labeled provisional and are kept out of dashboards and regulated reports.
