## FAQ

**What is the difference between Power BI and Microsoft Fabric?**

Power BI is the reporting layer, while Microsoft Fabric is the larger platform it now sits on. Fabric combines OneLake storage, pipelines, warehouses, lakehouses, real-time analytics and AI under one roof. Instead of reporting at the end of someone else's pipeline, BI professionals can shape data upstream inside the same environment.

**How can I get a Microsoft Fabric tenant to test with?**

You can register a developer or trial tenant yourself, often on the same day, without a long IT request. It gives you a sandbox outside production. The episode warns not to confuse a Power BI Premium Per User trial with a Fabric trial, and to check Microsoft's current documentation for trial terms.

**What does OneLake change for Power BI reports?**

OneLake gives every Fabric component one shared storage layer, so teams query the same data object instead of separate extracts. That reduces duplicate copies and mismatched numbers between departments. With Direct Lake, reports can connect straight to OneLake and may need less scheduled refresh, though the episode advises testing this with your own data.
