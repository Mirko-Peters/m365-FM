## FAQ

**Why does strict Zero Trust often hurt productivity in Microsoft 365?**

Strict Zero Trust turns every trust decision into prompts, policies and denials that employees must fight just to work. Users then prop doors open with workarounds, such as personal email or unapproved file sharing. The episode argues that this undermines the security it was meant to create, because the workaround paths come without visibility, logging or protection.

**How should Conditional Access policies be designed to avoid blocking legitimate work?**

Conditional Access policies should follow how people actually work rather than fear of what might happen. Instead of a blanket block on sign-ins outside the corporate IP range, design exceptions for trusted partners who need external access. Instead of an MFA prompt on every sign-in, use risk-based conditions that only escalate when the system sees unusual activity.

**How does Privileged Identity Management help admins without permanent Global Administrator rights?**

With Privileged Identity Management, admins request elevated rights when they need them, and the permissions expire automatically after the task. Attackers who compromise an account find no standing admin keys, admins can still act quickly during an outage, and compliance teams get complete logs showing who requested what, when and why, which supports audits.
