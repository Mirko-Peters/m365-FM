## FAQ

**Why don't Entra sign-in reports prove Copilot adoption?**

Because they only show who opened an app, not whether anyone used Copilot inside it. Microsoft Entra ID records identity and sign-in events such as who signed in, from where and on which device. A chart of thousands of Word users just means people opened Word. Prompt and action data in Copilot insights telemetry is what shows real usage.

**Which apps see the most Microsoft 365 Copilot usage?**

In many tenants, communication apps like Outlook, Teams and Word see heavier Copilot usage than PowerPoint or Excel. Email triage, chat replies and quick edits happen constantly, so small repetitive tasks drive sticky adoption. The episode recommends validating this against your own tenant's reports and focusing training on the top apps by prompt count.

**How do you tell real Copilot adoption from novelty use?**

Look for prompt categories that recur over time. Define your top five categories, such as rewriting, shortening, tone adjustments and summarization, and track them over three months. Categories that keep appearing across 30, 60 and 90 days represent genuine work, while one-off joke prompts that spike early and vanish should be filtered out before leadership sees the numbers.
