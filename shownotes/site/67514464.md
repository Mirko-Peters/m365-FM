## FAQ

**Where did my teams and channels go in the new Microsoft Teams?**

They are still there, reached through the Teams icon in the left-hand menu. When you select it, the sidebar switches context and shows your teams, expandable channels, pinned conversations and hidden groups. Nothing was deleted or archived automatically; the redesign only changed the entry point, so reaching a channel now takes two or three clicks.

**Why did Microsoft remove the Teams tab from the sidebar?**

Microsoft changed navigation to scale the app and reflect how people work, with chat as the most frequent activity. Folding teams and channels closer to chat lets you move between conversations and channels with less context switching, and it prepares the client for tighter integration with apps, Copilot and Loop components across Microsoft 365.

**How can I work faster in the new Teams layout?**

Pin your priority teams and channels so they sit one click away. The episode also recommends pinning tabs such as a Planner board or OneNote file in the channel, tuning notifications so only key teams send banner alerts, and learning keyboard shortcuts, which act as anchors back to important areas instead of mouse-driven searching.
