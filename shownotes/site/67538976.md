## FAQ

**Why are Microsoft 365 Copilot answers vague or incomplete?**

Usually because of the data, not the AI model. Cluttered SharePoint libraries with duplicate final versions, missing metadata and broken permissions leave Copilot guessing between conflicting files or blind to whole data sets. It amplifies the quality of what it finds, so cleaning libraries, fixing access and enforcing metadata makes answers sharper.

**How do SharePoint permissions affect what Copilot can see?**

Copilot can only use data the user has access to, so broken permissions create blind spots. Outdated group memberships, inconsistent sharing policies and stale accounts can hide reports from the people doing the work. Applying role-based access consistently, cleaning up groups and regularly reviewing who has access lets Copilot draw on the full picture.

**How does Power Automate work together with Copilot?**

Copilot interprets the request and Power Automate executes the follow-up. For example, after a meeting Copilot can produce a summary while a flow creates Planner tasks for action items, posts reminders into Teams channels and updates Dynamics. This turns static answers into consistent, trackable actions instead of relying on individual habits.
