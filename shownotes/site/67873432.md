## FAQ

**Are model-driven apps better than canvas apps?**

For secure production apps on Dataverse, the episode argues they often are, because business logic lives in Dataverse rather than in screen formulas. In one rollout described, a canvas app needed constant patching as the schema evolved, while a parallel model-driven build kept pace. Canvas still offers more visual freedom and quicker drag-and-drop results out of the box.

**How does security work in model-driven apps?**

Security is defined once in Dataverse through security roles and respected everywhere in the app. Roles can restrict tables, records and individual fields, and they tie to Microsoft Entra ID groups, so adding or removing a user from a group changes access automatically. Test new roles with a pilot user before rolling them out to production.

**How can I make a model-driven app look less dated?**

Restructure the default form into logical sections and tabs, build role-specific views such as a manager summary and an analyst detail view, then apply theming. Changing the header color and primary buttons, adding your company logo and trimming navigation to relevant tables makes the app feel intentional without destabilizing the underlying Dataverse data model.
