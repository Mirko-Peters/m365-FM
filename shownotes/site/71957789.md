## FAQ

**Why do AI projects fail without change management?**

Dan Barber argues that an AI strategy without change management is on a route to failure. Traditional projects often minimized it, but AI programs cannot afford to. Teams first need to understand the business, its processes and valuable use cases; otherwise they deploy AI that adds little value and can leave them at more risk than before.

**How can AI help with imposter syndrome?**

Dan uses AI as an ally against imposter syndrome rather than one more thing he must master. It helps him learn, plan and stop procrastinating, for example by producing a starting draft that he then rewrites and makes his own. That removes the blank page, which used to push work to the last minute and build stress.

**How can tech professionals filter information overload?**

Dan sorts incoming information into four boxes based on whether he wants to know it and needs to know it. Need-and-want comes first, then need-only, then want-only, where he relies on experts in his network. He realized much of what he consumed belonged in the fourth box, neither needed nor wanted, and stopped reading it.
