## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Intune only for managing devices?**

No. Intune started as a way to push settings to devices, but it now works across identity, apps and data. Combined with Azure AD (now Entra ID) and Conditional Access, it shapes which apps users can open and under what conditions, so access follows the person and the health of the device.

**How does Intune protect company data on personal phones?**

Through Mobile Application Management, which protects data inside apps instead of controlling the whole device. Intune can encrypt app data and block copy and paste between managed and unmanaged apps, so a document opened in Word cannot be pasted into WhatsApp or personal Gmail, while the employee keeps using the phone freely.

**How do Intune and Microsoft Purview help with audits?**

Intune continuously generates compliance posture data, such as patch status, active app protections and Conditional Access decisions, and Purview collects and standardizes it for reporting. When auditors ask, teams can pull reports showing policies were consistently enforced instead of reconstructing evidence from log files and screenshots after the fact.
