## FAQ

**How long does Microsoft Defender XDR keep logs and alerts?**

According to the episode, standard Defender retention is often capped at thirty days for some signals and up to ninety days for others. That is a problem when attackers stay quiet for months, because the evidence of how they first got in has already rolled off before anyone starts investigating.

**Is Microsoft Defender enough for compliance audits?**

Often not on its own. Frameworks like GDPR, ISO 27001 and HIPAA expect logs kept for six months, a year or longer, plus proof that incidents can be reconstructed. Even advanced auditing in Microsoft 365 adds retention in silos, without the central correlation and long-term storage that compliance officers expect from a SIEM like Sentinel.

**Does connecting Defender to Microsoft Sentinel double my storage costs?**

No. The episode explains that Defender data streams into Sentinel through data connectors without duplication, so you pay only for what Sentinel ingests and retains. To keep the bill lean, filter with analytics rules and start with high-value streams such as identity, privileged access and endpoint alerts instead of every log line.
