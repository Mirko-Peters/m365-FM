## FAQ

**Why are forgotten guest accounts in Microsoft 365 a security risk?**

Forgotten guests are risky because they already carry trust inside your tenant. A stale account is linked to a legitimate identity and often has approved access to Teams or SharePoint. If that external or personal account is compromised, an attacker can walk in as a known collaborator without breaking through defenses, and detection becomes harder.

**How do stale guest accounts affect ISO 27001 and GDPR compliance?**

They break the least-privilege principle that these frameworks rely on. ISO 27001 expects defined processes for access rights from creation to termination, and GDPR ties personal data exposure to accountability. Auditors often find that organizations cannot show offboarding of external collaborators, and that missing documentation becomes the audit finding itself.

**How can admins automate guest lifecycle management in Microsoft 365?**

Admins can give guest access an expiration date at invitation and require owners to recertify it to keep it. Access reviews in Microsoft Entra ID prompt resource owners to validate guests regularly, and Conditional Access can require MFA or block risky sign-ins. This replaces one-time cleanups with a continuous, auditable process.
