## FAQ

**Why doesn't Microsoft 365 Copilot boost productivity right after rollout?**

Copilot rarely delivers instant gains because organizations roll it out without adoption planning, defined tasks or trusted data. After an initial burst of curiosity, staff ask what it is for and whether answers are correct, then return to old routines. Trimming minutes from individual tasks does not scale without clear processes and a shared understanding of value.

**How should you choose the first Copilot use case?**

Pick a task that is frequent, effortful or costly when errors slip through, ideally meeting at least two of those criteria. Examples from the episode include recurring regulatory reports, monthly finance packages and first-level IT support tickets. Measure how long the task takes before Copilot, then again afterwards, to show a concrete before and after.

**How do you measure Copilot ROI?**

Measure process outcomes, not usage. Counting button clicks or prompts only proves curiosity. Better metrics include report completion rates once Copilot is embedded, average time to approval, and first response time in ticket queues before and after integration. These show actual time and risk reduction, which is what matters in board discussions.
