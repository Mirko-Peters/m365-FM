## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft 365 Copilot pilots stall in organizations with messy permissions?**

Copilot pilots stall because the tool works only on effective access, so it mirrors whatever permission design already exists. Users with broad, historical access get sharp answers, accountable leaders with restricted context get thin ones, and some people see content that feels inappropriate. The pilot then feels political, and trust and adoption drop.

**How can I find hidden gatekeepers in my Microsoft 365 environment without buying new tools?**

Start with friction, not the org chart. Pick a few important decisions and trace who initiates them, who gets consulted and who actually unblocks movement. Then compare accountability with effective permissions, see where Teams conversations converge, and check who really stewards critical SharePoint content. The same names keep reappearing where work moves or stalls.

**What is decision latency and how should I measure it?**

Decision latency is the time between a request becoming real and a decision becoming usable, not merely approved in theory. Pick three to five recurring decisions, define clear start and end points, then break the path down to see where requests waited, looped back or needed someone outside the official process to move.
