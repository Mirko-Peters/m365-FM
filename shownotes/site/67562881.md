## FAQ

**When should I use Microsoft To Do instead of Planner?**

Use To Do for your own priorities and Planner for anything several people work on. To Do is good for personal lists, flagged Outlook emails and reminders, but when team tasks live only in private To Do lists, nobody else sees progress, work gets duplicated and deadlines slip.

**When is Microsoft Lists better than Planner for tracking tasks?**

Lists fits when work needs more than who, what and when. Recurring audits, approvals or compliance reviews need columns like status, reviewer, category and documentation links, custom views and automated notifications. Planner boards get stretched with buckets and labels for this, while Lists behaves like a lightweight database built for structured work.

**What role does Microsoft Loop play in task management?**

Loop works best as a capture layer during collaboration, not as another task silo. In a Teams meeting or chat, a Loop component lets everyone record action items in real time, keeping the context of the discussion, so tasks reach To Do, Planner or Lists instead of getting lost in chat history.
