## FAQ

**How long does Power Automate keep flow run history?**

Not forever: run history is time-limited by default, and the retention window depends on your plan and license. To see yours, open a flow's run history and find the oldest available run. Once runs roll off they are gone, which is why the episode calls run history a debugging aid, not an archive.

**What should I log for every approval in Power Automate?**

Log at least a request ID, the stage name, the action taken, the approver's object ID, a timestamp and any comments. Write these as discrete fields, not one big text note, to a durable store. That lets you query who approved what and when, and also record reminders and escalations as evidence they actually fired.

**How do I stop approval flows from breaking when an approver leaves?**

Resolve approvers dynamically instead of hard-coding email addresses. Look them up from a Microsoft Entra ID group, a SharePoint list or Dataverse, and store role membership or object IDs. When someone leaves, you swap the group or list member and the flow keeps running, rather than approvals failing silently or vanishing.
