## FAQ

**Why can Microsoft 365 Copilot leak HR or legal content into summaries?**

Copilot retrieves through Microsoft Graph with the caller's effective permissions, so anything a user can read is in scope. If broadly shared SharePoint libraries hold weakly labeled HR or legal files, a Teams summary can include them. The fix is default-deny scopes, connector allow lists and sensitivity labels enforced with deny or redaction.

**What is the reason, plan, gate, execute pattern for Copilot?**

It separates thinking from doing. Reason is read-only and produces a cited, structured recommendation. Plan turns it into a machine-readable artifact with steps, preconditions and rollback. Gate checks shape, citations and rollback and routes approvals by risk tier. Execute runs only the approved plan under a dedicated, least-privileged agent identity.

**How do you stop Copilot from inventing company policy?**

Use authority gating: build a registry of canonical sources per domain with owners, labels, versions and stable anchors, and enforce 'no citation, no answer' in an evaluator. Policy-shaped answers without citations that resolve to the registry are refused with a reason, and users get bounded options such as searching the right policy library.
