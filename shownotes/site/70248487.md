## FAQ

**Why is Microsoft considered ahead in enterprise AI despite ChatGPT's popularity?**

Enterprise AI is decided in identity, data, infrastructure and workflow, not consumer visibility. The episode argues Microsoft controls all four layers through Entra ID, Microsoft 365, Fabric and Azure, so AI lands where employees already work and data already lives, while consumer tools must win new procurement, integration and training for every sale.

**Why does Copilot adoption look slow if Microsoft is winning?**

Copilot adoption looks slow because enterprises move from pilots to departments to full rollouts over months and years. The episode contrasts low preference in free-choice surveys with much higher adoption once Copilot becomes the sanctioned tool, arguing that organizational standards, not personal taste, decide which AI tools employees use at work.

**What is data gravity and why does it matter for AI platforms?**

Data gravity means enterprise data stays where it was created, in SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and Dynamics 365, protected by existing identity and compliance controls. Moving it is costly and risky, so organizations prefer AI that works on data in place. That favors Microsoft over models with bigger context windows that cannot reach the data.
