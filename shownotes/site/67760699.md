## FAQ

**Why do cloud migrations to Azure never really end?**

Because the platform keeps changing after go-live. Azure regularly introduces new services, retires old ones and updates compliance tools, and pricing shifts as well. Each change reopens questions about architecture, cost and compliance, so treating migration as a project with an end date creates false expectations and turns ongoing change into reactive firefighting.

**How do I make cloud monitoring dashboards lead to real action?**

Tie at least one metric directly to a specific action and an owner who can carry it out. For example, if a virtual machine shows low utilization every night, schedule automation to shut it down outside business hours and track the spending difference over a month. Monitoring plus authority plus follow-through produces improvement.

**What is an accountability dead zone in cloud operations?**

It is an area where one team sees a problem but cannot fix it, while the team able to fix it feels no direct responsibility. To find one, ask whether the owning team can approve the changes, has the budget to act promptly and has the technical access. A no to any question reveals a dead zone.
