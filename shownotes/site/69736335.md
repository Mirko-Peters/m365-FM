## FAQ

**Why do organizations with MFA still suffer identity breaches?**

Because MFA protects authentication, while most damage comes from authorization. Attackers can steal a session token after the MFA challenge and then act with whatever entitlements that identity has accumulated. The episode argues that privilege creep, stale access, over-permissioned service principals and permanent exceptions matter more than the login itself.

**What does continuous access evaluation change in Microsoft Entra?**

Continuous access evaluation lets Entra tell participating applications that a session is no longer acceptable, so they enforce revocation in near real time instead of waiting for token expiry. Disabling an account or raising its risk level can then cut an attacker's session short. It only works where apps honor the signal and exceptions are governed.

**How does connecting Microsoft Defender to ServiceNow reduce MTTR?**

It removes manual handoffs between detection and containment. Defender correlates signals into an incident, which automatically becomes a ServiceNow security incident with context attached. Pre-approved, reversible actions such as session revocation or blocking risky sign-ins run immediately, while humans approve high blast radius decisions and ServiceNow tracks recovery tasks and timestamps.
