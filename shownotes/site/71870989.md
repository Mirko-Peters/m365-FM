## FAQ

**Why can't SPF, DKIM and DMARC stop deepfake business email compromise?**

They prove how a message was sent and handled, not whether the sender has authority for the request. A fraudulent payment request can pass every check if it comes from a compromised mailbox or an approved sending service, and many domains still run DMARC in monitoring mode without quarantining or rejecting anything.

**How does Microsoft Entra Verified ID help against fraudulent payment requests?**

It lets a workflow demand a signed, verifiable credential proving the requester holds the specific authority before value is released. The email can start the process but cannot finish it: without a valid credential, the wire transfer, banking change or executive reset does not go through, regardless of how convincing the message looks.

**What should a verifiable credential for approvals contain?**

It should express decision rights, not just employment. Mirko recommends separating the person's identity, their specific authority, and conditions such as a dollar threshold, department or time limit. Narrow credential types like a payment approval or vendor master data change authority are easier to govern, test and revoke quickly when roles change.
