## FAQ

**What does SharePoint Premium do for Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

SharePoint Premium prepares content for Copilot by extracting fields, classifying documents, tagging images and assembling content. Invoices, contracts and images become governed metadata instead of raw files, so Copilot can reason over labeled truth. The episode stresses locking exposure and applying labels first, then turning on the models and measuring the results.

**What is the difference between restricted access control and restricted content discovery?**

Restricted access control limits a site to members of a specified group regardless of existing sharing links, while restricted content discovery hides a site from Copilot until a user has opened files there. Discovery restriction does not replace proper security; it narrows exposure temporarily while you fix permissions and structure.

**How do I set confidence thresholds for SharePoint Premium invoice extraction?**

Set a global minimum confidence, such as 85 in the episode, and route anything below it to a needs-review status for a human to verify. Values above the threshold populate automatically. Consider stricter thresholds for totals and invoice numbers, and keep a human checkpoint before anything is posted to the ledger.
