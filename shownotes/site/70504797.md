## FAQ

**What makes a Microsoft 365 consulting service high-value?**

A high-value service combines five elements: a painful problem, a clear measurable outcome, a defined methodology, packaged deliverables and reduced risk. If even one element is missing, the service drifts toward commodity work and hourly rate comparisons. When all five are present, the episode argues, clients stop negotiating price and start asking when the work can begin.

**Why is hourly pricing a problem for Microsoft 365 consultants?**

Hourly pricing turns expertise into a cost the client tries to minimize. A governance engagement quoted as 150 hours at 120 dollars reads as an 18,000 dollar expense to negotiate down. It also misaligns incentives, because the faster and more efficient the consultant becomes, the less the client pays, while outcome pricing rewards that efficiency.

**How do you productize a Microsoft 365 consulting service?**

Start by documenting your existing methodology and separating the repeatable steps from the parts that vary per client. Then build templates for recurring deliverables such as governance charters and lifecycle policies. Finally, bundle them into a named program, like a Copilot readiness program, with a fixed scope, fixed timeline and fixed price that signals confidence.
