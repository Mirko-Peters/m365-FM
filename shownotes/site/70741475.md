## FAQ

**Why do technically perfect Microsoft 365 tenants fail?**

They are designed for capability on day one instead of durability over years. Mirko explains that nobody remembers why policies were written, the architect who understood the model leaves, and strict controls push users toward workarounds. The tenant keeps running but becomes unmaintainable, which is why he calls it a silent collapse by year three.

**What are the three governance zones for Microsoft 365?**

The zones are personal work, collaborative work and enterprise records, each with its own governance level. OneDrive and personal chats need almost no overhead. Teams channels and project sites need owner accountability, lifecycle rules and sharing policies. HR, finance and regulated content need mandatory sensitivity labels, enforced retention, access reviews and restricted external sharing.

**What should I check before scaling Copilot in my tenant?**

Check your governance debt first, starting with permissions sprawl and data classification. The episode recommends knowing how many global admins and stale guest accounts exist, whether every team and Power Automate flow has an owner, what share of files carry sensitivity labels, and how external sharing really works, because Copilot surfaces whatever users can already access.
