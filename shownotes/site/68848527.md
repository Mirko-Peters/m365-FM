## FAQ

**How many stages should a Dynamics 365 business process flow have?**

The episode recommends three stages at most, such as qualify, commit and deliver, each with two required fields. Exit criteria should be verbs, like next action selected or owner and due date set. The demo replaces six vague stages with this setup, so records cannot sit in limbo and handoffs happen on save.

**How do you improve Dynamics 365 adoption after go-live?**

Treat Dynamics 365 as a product with a steady rhythm rather than more training. A single product owner runs a weekly 30-minute triage on live records, ships small monthly releases that remove friction, and publishes release notes in the app. Leadership running pipeline reviews inside the system instead of slides drives data quality.

**What should a nearshore team handle in a Dynamics 365 project?**

The nearshore team should build small, well-defined backlog items while strategy, priorities and acceptance stay with the in-house product owner. Tickets need one-sentence acceptance criteria and must be testable in two minutes. Guardrails include one branch strategy, managed solutions downstream and a daily 15-minute sync between product owner and nearshore lead.
