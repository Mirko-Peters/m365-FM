## FAQ

**How does email to case work in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?**

Email to case turns incoming support emails into structured case records automatically. It extracts customer, product, priority and identifiers such as order numbers, attaches files and stitches threads together. The episode recommends enabling it for every support mailbox with server-side sync rather than relying on humans forwarding emails, and stamping cases before anything waits in an inbox.

**What does unified routing do with incoming cases?**

Unified routing assigns each case to the right queue and agent based on rules, skills, capacity and SLA urgency instead of personal preference. The episode suggests starting with three queues, real skill tags and capacity profiles. Routing diagnostics record intake, classification and assignment, so a misrouted case shows which rule sent it there.

**Can Copilot Studio send customer replies on its own?**

No, the setup in this episode keeps a human in control of every send. Copilot Studio is wired into the case form, drafts replies that cite knowledge base articles, and waits for an agent to review and send. Start with three prompts: first reply, ask for missing information, and close with steps taken.
