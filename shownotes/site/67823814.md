## FAQ

**How do I send an Adaptive Card to Teams with Power Automate?**

Use a three-step flow: a trigger, the card JSON and a post action. Pick a targeted trigger such as item created or due date approaching, paste the JSON from the Adaptive Card Designer with placeholders replaced by list column tokens, then use the post adaptive card in a chat or channel action.

**Why does my Power Automate flow succeed but no card appears in Teams?**

Most often the Teams connector is authenticated with an account that lacks rights to post in the target chat or channel. The run history still shows success, yet nothing arrives. Check the connection before testing, then pilot with a small group so problems surface before hundreds of users see the card.

**How many buttons should an Adaptive Card in Teams have?**

Keep it to one or two actions, such as approve or decline, or snooze or complete. The episode describes cards with many options being ignored because users froze. Show the due date, owner and status, and run a three-second test: if a newcomer cannot tell what to do, simplify.
