## FAQ

**What are the three components every AI agent needs?**

Memory, entitlements and tools, according to the Microsoft and BCG framework cited in the episode. Memory lets an agent carry context between tasks, entitlements define what it is allowed to access, and tools are the apps, APIs and connectors it uses to act. Each becomes a failure point when it is too broad or unmanaged.

**What is the principal-agent problem with AI agents?**

It is the gap between what a manager intends and what an agent actually optimizes for. Agents follow the literal instructions they receive rather than business goals, and they know more about their own actions than the manager can see. Yet the manager stays accountable for outcomes, which the episode calls a moral crumple zone.

**How can managers keep autonomous AI agents under control?**

Start by inventorying agents and tiering them by risk and autonomy. Then set escalation thresholds where a human must approve actions such as high-value transactions or outbound communications, and red-team critical agents. Back this with role-based access control, MFA for elevated actions, DLP, audit logs and regular reviews of what agents accessed and did.
