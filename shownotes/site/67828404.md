## FAQ

**Why does a Microsoft Purview retention policy increase storage?**

Retention keeps content even when users delete it, so preserved copies pile up in the background. SharePoint sites fill with retained versions, OneDrive holds deleted documents and Teams chats stretch back years. The episode recommends measuring storage during a small pilot and fixing scope and labeling before expanding a policy.

**Can you undo a retention label or policy in Microsoft Purview?**

Not always easily. Some label settings are designed to be effectively permanent, and reversing others can require reprocessing, reindexing or Microsoft Support. Check Microsoft documentation on immutability, document what a policy affects before rollout, keep a tested rollback path and apply strict settings only where policy or law demands it.

**What information architecture should be in place before rolling out Purview?**

Follow six steps: map where content lives, enforce predictable naming, require a few metadata fields such as department, document type and year, set high-level classification rules, clean up stale teams, sites and OneDrive accounts, and pilot in one business unit while involving legal, records management and security.
