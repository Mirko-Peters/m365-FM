## FAQ

**Why does lift and shift migration fail AI readiness?**

Lift and shift moves workloads quickly but carries over every old habit, including fragmented identity, inconsistent tagging, data silos and permissions replicated as is. AI accesses data contextually, so one badly scoped role can expose confidential material. The result, as the episode puts it, is an estate that is cloud ready but AI hostile.

**What does data readiness for AI actually mean?**

Data readiness means structure, lineage and governance. Data must be normalized and retrievable, you must know where it came from and how it was transformed, and there must be a consistent, automated way to authorize, audit and restrict usage. The episode's test is whether you can trace your top ten data sets within an hour.

**What is the three-step strategy to make an Azure cloud AI ready?**

The strategy is unify, fortify and automate. Unify the data estate with Microsoft Fabric, Microsoft Purview and Defender for Cloud after a ruthless inventory. Fortify through governance as code using Azure Policy, managed identities and conditional access. Automate intelligence feedback so monitoring detects anomalies and adjusts policies at machine speed, without waiting for human review.
