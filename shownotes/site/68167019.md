## FAQ

**Does Copilot Memory record everything I do?**

No. Copilot Memory only stores facts when you show clear intent, such as asking it to remember a tone preference or that you favor bullet points. Each addition triggers a memory updated badge. Ordinary prompts are not remembered, and Microsoft designed it to ignore sensitive categories like health conditions, political views and sexual orientation.

**What is the difference between Copilot Memory and Windows Recall?**

The difference is the consent model. Copilot Memory keeps only what you explicitly tell it to remember, while Windows Recall, exclusive to Copilot+ PCs, automatically takes regular encrypted screenshots that stay on the device. Because Recall collects data without asking each time, enterprises need to assess it separately in compliance and risk reviews.

**How do I see or delete what Copilot remembers about me?**

Ask Copilot "what do you know about me?" and it summarizes its current memories. To remove one, tell it to forget that item, or request a full wipe. You can switch memory off under Settings, Account, Privacy, Personalization and memory, and separately opt out of having your conversations used for model training.
