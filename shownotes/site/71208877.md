## FAQ

**Does governance slow down innovation?**

Not when it is mature, according to Mirko Peters. Immature governance slows organizations down because it relies on manual reviews, memory and heroic intervention. When templates, clear ownership and automated controls are designed up front, the governed path becomes the fastest one, and in the episode's case study, standard workspace delivery moved from days to hours.

**What is the four-part readiness test for Copilot and Power Platform?**

The test checks data, identity, control and outcome. Is the data structured and labeled? Are ownership and access clearly defined? Are controls automated rather than dependent on memory? Is there a measurable business result without hidden risk? If a rollout fails any of the four, it is not ready to scale, and the conversation takes about fifteen minutes.

**Why do Copilot rollouts stall after the pilot?**

They stall when experimentation has to become operational trust and existing governance debt surfaces. The episode places this between week six and week twelve. Copilot inherits existing permissions, so overshared sites, unlabeled content and unclear ownership become blockers. A phased rollout starting with low-risk, reviewed sites and ongoing remediation helps move from pilot to real value.
