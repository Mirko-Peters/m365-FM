## FAQ

**What is threat analytics in Microsoft Defender?**

Threat analytics is in-product research written by Microsoft security researchers that maps actor techniques to MITRE ATT&CK, lists indicators of compromise and gives recommendations. It also shows tenant-specific exposure and device or account evidence where Defender has seen similar behavior, which lets you connect each report to your own incidents and Secure Score controls.

**How do you turn a threat analytics report into action in one hour?**

Pick one active, relevant report, list three to five MITRE techniques, and build saved advanced hunting queries for each across the last 14 to 30 days. Then join hits to incidents, map every recommendation to a Secure Score action with an owner and SLA, check policy status, and rerun the queries to verify.

**How do you measure whether threat analytics work is paying off?**

Track two measures: time to detect, from the first indicator Defender sees to an analyst acting on an incident with context, and named attack paths closed by technique, control and date. The episode uses incident timelines, Secure Score history and report exposure status as dashboards, reviewed daily, weekly and monthly.
