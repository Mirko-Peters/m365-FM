## FAQ

**Can Dynamics 365 Sales be used for membership management without custom code?**

Yes, by remodeling instead of rebuilding. Accounts become organizations, contacts become members, and a new membership table links them with term dates, status, plan and auto-renew. Because Sales runs on Dataverse, you keep timelines, security roles, Outlook and Teams integration, SharePoint documents and Power Automate without writing a bespoke membership application.

**Should I create a separate member table in Dataverse?**

No, keep identity in the contact table and only rename its label to member. A duplicate person table breaks Outlook tracking, timelines and integrations and creates reconciliation work. Put the variability into bridge tables instead: memberships link members to organizations, assignments link members to committee positions, and participations link organizations to partner programs.

**How do you automate membership renewals in Dynamics 365?**

Make renewal a stage in the business process flow and trigger automation a set number of days before the term ends. Power Automate generates the renewal record, creates the next payment intent, notifies the member and schedules follow-up. When payment arrives the membership returns to active; otherwise it moves to grace, then lapsed, then archive.
