## FAQ

**Why do traditional Microsoft 365 training workshops fail?**

Workshops fail because people learn features out of context and forget them before they need them. The forgetting curve means details like where to click fade quickly without practice. When an employee finally needs a feature, such as blurring a background in a Teams call, the classroom lesson is gone and help desks get the question.

**What is learning in the flow of work in Microsoft 365?**

It means learning happens inside the apps at the moment a task requires it, not in a separate session. Microsoft 365 delivers this through in-product tips, guided walkthroughs, Microsoft Search answers and short micro lessons. Users apply the guidance immediately, which helps it stick, and they never have to leave the app to look something up.

**How does Viva Learning support Microsoft 365 adoption?**

Viva Learning brings training content directly into Teams, so employees do not need another portal. It combines Microsoft content, LinkedIn Learning modules and an organization's own training. Managers can assign short learning cards that appear alongside normal conversations, so a team can pick up a new feature, such as webinars, in a few minutes.
