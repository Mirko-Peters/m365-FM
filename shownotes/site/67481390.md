## FAQ

**What do I need before Copilot shows up in Microsoft Teams?**

You need a Microsoft 365 E3 or E5 plan plus the separate Copilot add-on license assigned to users. The episode also stresses an up-to-date tenant and Teams clients, correct Copilot permissions in the Teams admin center, meeting transcription turned on, and compliance policies that do not block the features Copilot relies on.

**Why does Copilot in Teams give incomplete meeting summaries?**

Most often because meeting transcription is off. Copilot builds its summaries, action items and insights from transcript data, so without transcription the results are inconsistent or missing. Policies that block transcription for compliance reasons have the same effect, which is why admins should check those settings before launch.

**Can Copilot in Teams make decisions for a team?**

No. Copilot supports decisions but does not replace them. A summary that reports agreement on a deadline does not prove anyone accepted responsibility, and nuance may never reach the transcript. People still need to verify commitments and make the final call, using Copilot to gather structured information faster.
