Many Microsoft 365 governance programs spend years turning red into amber without ever reaching green. This episode is for IT, security and compliance leads who rely on Purview scores, readiness reviews and Power BI dashboards to prove control. It follows five cycles of reviews, budgets, audits and true-ups to show how metrics create false control, and what simpler, enforceable governance looks like.

Key takeaways

  • Maturity scores, readiness indices and heat maps measure motion and documentation, not reduced exposure, so they can keep governance programs running without changing outcomes.
  • Exceptions in Conditional Access, Teams policies and PIM, report-only policies and audit-only DLP quietly become the real control model unless they carry an end date and a review loop.
  • Simple, human-readable rules backed by small automations - expiring stale guests, claiming ownerless groups, reviewing policy changes - can deliver more control than ever-growing licensing and workshops.
