Your Zero Trust controls can say "allowed" while 12,000 files quietly leave SharePoint. This episode shows security and compliance teams how to correlate Entra sign-in risk, the unified audit log, Purview policy edits and Copilot interaction logs into one defensible timeline, with concrete KQL detection recipes, alerts and playbooks.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Zero Trust without evidence
  • 00:46 – Entra sign-in risk: verify the verifier
  • 06:13 – Unified audit log and lateral movement
  • 13:39 – Purview retention and policy tampering
  • 18:51 – Copilot interaction logs
  • 24:54 – Case: a quiet data exfiltration
  • 31:20 – Operationalizing Zero Trust evidence

Key takeaways

  • Repeated medium-risk Entra sign-ins that pass MFA are a stronger exfiltration lead than a single high-risk spike, so escalate by count and diversity.
  • Data staging shows up as correlated patterns in the unified audit log, such as a new sync client plus widened sharing links and novel file reads in one session.
  • Purview retention edits and Copilot interaction logs must be correlated with identity and audit events, exported beyond native retention and protected by dual control.
