One cloned security role with organization-level read can expose executive data to every vendor in a Power Apps portal. This episode shows Power Platform admins and makers how Dataverse users, teams, security roles and business units really work, how guest leaks happen and how to lock down and monitor external access.

Key takeaways

  • Dataverse combines security role privileges additively, so a single organization-level or parent-child scope given to a guest overrides every narrower restriction.
  • Isolate external users in their own child business unit, build guest roles from scratch with user-level scope, use teams and access teams for record sharing and field-level security for sensitive columns.
  • Keep the model safe with regular reviews in the Power Platform admin center, Purview DLP policies, Dataverse auditing, Entra ID group-based cleanup and role checks before every deployment.

Worth quoting

“You can't soft fence external users, you have to architect their isolation through business units and minimize their privileges to what they demonstrably need.” – Mirko Peters (05:20)

“The mistake that creates leaks isn't ignorance, it's false confidence. People assume data verse forgives imprecision. It doesn't. It obediently enforces whatever combination of roles you define.” – Mirko Peters (05:37)
