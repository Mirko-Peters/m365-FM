Fabric admins and data engineers whose pipelines look secure until an audit starts will learn where the default settings leave gaps. The episode shows how managed identities, Azure Key Vault and deliberate RBAC close those gaps without slowing teams down.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden risks lurking in Fabric pipelines
  • 05:36 – Managed identities: killing the password problem
  • 10:31 – Locking down secrets with Azure Key Vault
  • 15:16 – RBAC: who really has access to your data?
  • 20:45 – Security as a business enabler

Key takeaways

  • Default inherited workspace permissions and hardcoded secrets are the biggest silent risks in Microsoft Fabric pipelines.
  • Managed identities remove passwords from pipelines, and Azure Key Vault centralises the remaining secrets with logged, identity-scoped access.
  • Intentional, regularly reviewed role-based access control keeps pipeline edit and data access aligned with least privilege and makes audits provable.

Worth quoting

“The biggest vulnerability is the access you never bothered to check. Every setting you left untouched becomes a potential doorway.” – Mirko Peters (05:10)

“The idea is simple but powerful. Give each Fabric pipeline its own unique identity in Azure active directory.” – Mirko Peters (07:33)
