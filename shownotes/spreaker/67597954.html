Your CRM, ERP and databases hold the answers, but combining them still takes exports, spreadsheets and weeks of waiting. This episode is for business and data leaders who want one AI chatbot that answers cross-system questions instantly. You will learn how Fabric connectors and Copilot with Fabric data agents unify your data and turn it into real-time, predictive insight.

Key takeaways

  • Scattered CRM, ERP and database silos cost time and trust because every system insists on its own version of the truth.
  • Fabric connectors bring systems like Dynamics 365, SAP, Salesforce and SQL into one hub that Copilot with Fabric data agents can query in plain language.
  • Once data is unified, the same setup moves teams from backward-looking KPIs to predictive insights they can act on in real time.
