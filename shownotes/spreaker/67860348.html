Messy, outdated data turns Copilot into a generator of confident nonsense, and users stop trusting it after the first bad answer. This episode is for IT and adoption leads planning or rescuing a Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout. Get ten practical actions, from cleaning your worst folder to champions, real-data training and a rollback plan.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Data goblins and Copilot
  • 00:40 – Why deployments fail before day one
  • 03:45 – How organizations got people to want Copilot
  • 06:40 – Frameworks that don't sound like sales pitches
  • 10:15 – Training without the eye rolls
  • 13:50 – What happens when you skip the hard part
  • 17:10 – The ten actions that actually work

Key takeaways

  • Copilot only reflects the data it is grounded on, so cleaning a small, well-labelled slice of content before rollout prevents the first bad answers that destroy trust.
  • Adoption grows from visible wins, business champions and word of mouth, supported by simple frameworks such as ADKAR rather than heavy change-management decks.
  • Train with real, messy files in short sessions and sandboxes, set honest expectations, and keep a rollback plan for a failed launch.
