Great Copilot answers vanish the moment you close the chat. This episode explains Copilot Pages for Microsoft 365 users who want to keep, refine and share AI output, how it differs from Loop and Copilot Notebooks, and the limits nobody mentions.

In this episode

  • 02:02 – Where Copilot Pages came from
  • 03:36 – How it works
  • 05:09 – Everyday use cases
  • 06:49 – Sharing and collaboration
  • 08:08 – Pages vs. Loop vs. Notebooks
  • 09:51 – Azure and the platform underneath
  • 11:06 – Mistakes and honest limits

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Pages turns a temporary Copilot chat answer into a persistent, editable page you own and can refine side by side with Copilot.
  • Pages are shared like any Microsoft 365 file, support real-time co-authoring, inherit sensitivity labels and are stored in SharePoint Embedded, but cannot yet be opened by external guests.
  • Treat every page as a first draft: verify AI output, keep pages lean, and remember that results are only as good as the content you feed Copilot.
