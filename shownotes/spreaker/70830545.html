Your org chart says who should decide, but your Microsoft 365 permissions decide who actually can. This episode is for leaders, governance owners and AI rollout teams who keep hitting slow decisions, hidden gatekeepers and stalled Copilot pilots. Learn how to trace real power through access, conversations and content, and how to realign control with responsibility.

  • 09:10 – Why organizations drift from the org chart
  • 17:26 – Transformation exposes the real power map
  • 21:11 – Signals: latency, Entra, Teams, SharePoint, workflows
  • 41:24 – Shadow IT, shadow AI and Copilot amplification
  • 51:36 – Over-permissioned vs. locked-down organizations
  • 63:00 – Mapping real power and action steps

Key takeaways

  • Real power in a Microsoft 365 organization sits where access, context and trust converge, not where the org chart places authority.
  • Decision latency, effective Entra permissions, Teams centrality and SharePoint ownership are measurable signals that reveal hidden gatekeepers.
  • Copilot does not fix misaligned control; it amplifies it, so access and accountability must be reviewed together before scaling AI.
