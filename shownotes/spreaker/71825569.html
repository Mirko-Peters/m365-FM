Need to share Dataverse or Dynamics 365 data with customers, partners or members outside your organization? Power Pages expert Nicholas Hayduk explains how the platform works, how licensing and external authentication behave, and where projects go wrong. Ideal for Power Platform makers, architects and partners planning their first portal.

  • 18:05 – EC Learn, ownership and customization
  • 31:54 – Wishlist and hot takes

Key takeaways

  • Power Pages is the Power Platform tool for external users and depends entirely on Dataverse for data, content and identities.
  • Licensing is based on monthly active (authenticated or anonymous) users, and under-licensing shows up as poor performance rather than blocked logins.
  • Projects fail when expectations come from marketing or from designers who do not know the platform, which leads to unplanned custom code.

Worth quoting
