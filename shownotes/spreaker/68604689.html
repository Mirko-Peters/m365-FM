SharePoint agents don't hallucinate leaks; overscoped sources and permissive inheritance hand them sensitive data at machine speed. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and SharePoint admins rolling out agents. Learn four control planes, from scoping and permissions to approvals and Purview DLP, that cage agents without killing their usefulness.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why SharePoint agents leak data
  • 00:34 – How agents actually see your data
  • 04:22 – Control plane 1: scope knowledge sources
  • 08:45 – Control plane 2: break inheritance and label
  • 13:29 – Control plane 3: approvals, licensing and data policies
  • 17:18 – Control plane 4: Purview DLP that stops agents

Key takeaways

  • Agents see whatever their identity can access through permissions; retrieval filters and grounding only steer relevance, they are not security boundaries.
  • Scope knowledge sources to single libraries, break inheritance on sensitive libraries and replace broad groups with role-based security groups.
  • Bind sensitivity labels to Purview DLP policies that block agent processing, and gate agent creation with approvals, licensing controls and data policies.

Worth quoting

“Most people confuse retrieval filters with security boundaries. They are not. Retrieval says search here first. Permissions say you may enter.” – Mirko Peters (01:09)

“The thing most people miss is simple. Grounding does not override rights. Grounding filters relevance. It doesn't shrink the agent's legal authority.” – Mirko Peters (01:32)
