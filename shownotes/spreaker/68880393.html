Contracts get lost, versions get overwritten and audits fail because documents live in folders, inboxes and private channels instead of next to the records they prove. This episode is for Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 architects, admins and compliance owners who want a document management design that holds. You learn how to bind Dynamics records to governed SharePoint libraries with content types, Purview labels, event-based retention, DLP and Copilot.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 should hold the events and SharePoint the evidence, linked through a document location created by rule for each record instead of attachments and copies.
  • Content types and required metadata such as lifecycle state, sensitivity and retention category give documents identity, so the approved version can always be found and proven.
  • Purview sensitivity labels, event-based retention and DLP enforce rights and endings automatically, and Copilot becomes a reliable witness only once this structure exists.

Worth quoting
