Push Microsoft 365 security to the maximum and users find workarounds; give them total freedom and the next breach is only a click away. This episode helps admins and security leads design portals, Conditional Access and PIM so the CISO, the GDPR officer and everyday users can all live with the result.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why both extremes fail
  • 04:08 – The hidden impact of admin portals
  • 08:20 – Conditional Access: when security collides with workflow
  • 12:05 – PIM without the pain
  • 16:10 – The system the CISO, GDPR officer and user can agree on
  • 20:07 – Balance as an ongoing practice

Key takeaways

  • Neither all-in Zero Trust nor unlimited user freedom works, because security, compliance and usability are interconnected.
  • Conditional Access rules should follow how people actually work, using targeted exceptions and risk-based conditions instead of blanket blocks that drive shadow IT.
  • Privileged Identity Management replaces standing global admin rights with time-bound, logged elevation that satisfies security, compliance and admins alike.

Worth quoting

“Extreme security breaks people, the extreme freedom breaks systems. The sustainable approach is finding that middle path where policies protect without paralyzing and where productivity thrives without opening major attack surfaces.” – Mirko Peters (03:40)

“When users feel the legitimate path is blocked, they don't always stop working. They just find a new path, and it usually comes without visibility, logging, or protection.” – Mirko Peters (10:52)
