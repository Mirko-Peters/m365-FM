Internal updates posted in Teams often vanish in chat noise. This episode is for communications, HR and IT teams who want a reliable internal newsletter without new software. It shows how to build one with Outlook, Exchange dynamic groups, a shared mailbox, templates, Power Automate and Power BI.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why internal updates get lost
  • 00:54 – Define target audiences with dynamic groups
  • 03:23 – Sender identity: shared mailbox and permissions
  • 06:25 – Design the newsletter template
  • 10:56 – Automate the editorial workflow
  • 14:30 – Measure and improve
  • 17:53 – Governance and scaling

Key takeaways

  • Use Exchange dynamic distribution groups built on directory attributes for self-maintaining audiences and static distribution lists only for curated groups.
  • Send every edition from a shared mailbox with a reusable Outlook template, and run submissions and approvals through Microsoft Forms, Planner and Power Automate.
  • Measure delivery with message trace and delivery reports, visualize KPIs in Power BI, and govern the newsletter with clear roles, retention and version control.

Worth quoting

“When everyone receives everything, relevance dies and attention follows. You don't need a thousand readers, you need the right hundred.” – Mirko Peters (01:05)

“A proper internal newsletter needs an institutional identity, something people recognize the moment they see it in their inbox.” – Mirko Peters (03:51)
