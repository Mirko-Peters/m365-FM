Without CI/CD, a medallion architecture in Fabric Warehouse quickly turns into an expensive data swamp. This episode shows data engineers how to version, test and promote bronze, silver and gold layers, and how GitHub, Azure DevOps and Fabric share the work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why medallion needs CI/CD
  • 00:32 – Bronze without rollback: the CSV graveyard
  • 03:55 – Silver layer: where governance dies quietly
  • 06:56 – Gold layer: analytics at 3 AM
  • 10:01 – GitHub, Azure DevOps or Fabric: who's in charge
  • 13:47 – Avoiding the deployment nightmare

Key takeaways

  • Treat every medallion layer, from bronze ingestion to gold analytics, as code in source control with branching and reviews.
  • Never promote a change without automated schema and data quality tests, approval gates and a one-step rollback to the last good build.
  • Use the tools together: GitHub for collaboration, Fabric deployment features for packaging, and Azure DevOps for governance and approvals.

Worth quoting

“Bronze pipelines are not temporary throwaway scripts. They live longer than you think, and if they're not branchable, reviewable, and version controlled, they'll eventually blow up.” – Mirko Peters (02:02)

“If bronze chaos is messy but obvious, silver chaos is clean but invisible, and invisible failures are worse because leadership doesn't care that your layer looked tidy.” – Mirko Peters (06:30)
