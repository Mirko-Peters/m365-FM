Most Azure migrations hit their cutover dates and still leave the business harder to run, with identity drift, broken audit trails and rising costs. This episode is for CIOs, platform teams and architects in regulated enterprises planning a large migration. You learn how to sequence platform, migration factory and modernization so governance holds while workloads move.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Migration as an IT project: the foundational mistake
  • 07:25 – Azure is the control plane, not the destination
  • 13:55 – Landing zones as an organizational contract
  • 17:54 – Management groups, shared services and hub-and-spoke
  • 32:29 – Workload prioritization and tabletop triage
  • 48:24 – Phased modernization: foundation, scale, modernize

Key takeaways

  • Enterprise Azure migrations fail systemically, not technically: treat them as an operating model change and build the landing zone contract for identity, policy, network and logging before the first workload moves.
  • Use an environment-first management group hierarchy, shared services subscriptions and hub-and-spoke networking, and treat prioritization as portfolio management across rehost, replatform, refactor and retire.
  • Run a migration factory with repeatable validation gates, earn modernization through platform maturity, and turn the CCOE into a platform product team that offers paved roads instead of approvals.
