Your Copilot rollout looks like progress, yet your architecture feels less stable every quarter. This episode is for Microsoft 365, Power Platform and Azure leaders who are scaling agents and want to understand why behavior drifts even when nothing was changed. You learn the mean-time-to-explain metric, three real-world entropy scenarios and an agent-first design that keeps execution legible and owned.

Key takeaways

  • Rolling out Copilot and agents is an operating model shift from deterministic SaaS to probabilistic orchestration, not a feature deployment.
  • Mean time to explain — how fast you can reconstruct why an agent did something — is the risk metric that decides whether agents can scale.
  • Agent-first architecture keeps reasoning probabilistic but forces execution through deterministic, owned boundaries: agent identity, tool contracts, telemetry, lifecycle and kill switches.
