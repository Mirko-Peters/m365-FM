Your architecture diagram says work flows through the CRM, SharePoint and Teams, but your people close deals in email, track candidates in spreadsheets and reconcile the books in Excel. This episode is for IT, compliance and business leaders preparing Microsoft 365 and Copilot for real-world use. Learn the four signals of your real infrastructure and how to map it honestly before you govern, secure or automate it.

Key takeaways

  • Every organization runs two infrastructures: the designed one in the diagram and the real one built from email, spreadsheets, inherited permissions and workarounds created under pressure.
  • Copilot operates on the real infrastructure and inherits every overshared permission and unclassified file, so exposure through AI is an infrastructure problem rather than a Copilot problem.
  • Before adding controls or AI, follow three real workflows, name the gaps, ask why they exist and decide whether to change the design or the business.
