Many leaders respond to AI with more approvals and oversight, and then wonder why Copilot and Power Platform deliver so little value. This episode shows executives and Microsoft 365 owners why control stops scaling and how to redesign decision systems, rights and feedback loops so teams can act inside clear guardrails.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why leadership control is failing
  • 00:53 – Why control worked before AI
  • 11:45 – The leader who became the bottleneck
  • 20:03 – Four shifts: context, decision systems, alignment, feedback
  • 36:55 – What leaders should measure instead
  • 44:26 – Enterprise scenarios: Copilot, Power Platform, hybrid teams
  • 54:49 – Leadership capabilities: systems thinking to feedback loops
  • 71:04 – Seven-day leadership reset and business payoff

Key takeaways

  • AI lowers the cost of analysis, so leadership control that once reduced risk now turns into latency and a single point of failure.
  • Leaders should replace personal approvals with explicit context, decision classes, escalation thresholds and aligned decision, data and intervention rights.
  • Measure decision latency, dependency rate, ownership clarity, override patterns and rework instead of activity or license usage.

Worth quoting

“AI does not remove bottlenecks by itself. It exposes them. Once exposed, those bottlenecks are often leadership shaped.” – Mirko Peters (11:04)
