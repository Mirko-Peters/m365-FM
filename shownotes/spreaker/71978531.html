Many Copilot rollouts stall because organizations focus on licenses and features instead of people. MVP Carina de Vries shares her 90-day approach for adoption leads and IT managers: how to define happy users, build Copilot habits with a weekly cadence and embed new ways of working after the project ends.

  • 18:44 – What makes a happy user

  • 31:05 – Wonder, simplify and embed
  • 38:19 – Leadership, habits and governance
  • 44:27 – Success stories and hot takes

Key takeaways

  • Copilot adoption is about building new behavior, not about the technology itself.
  • A weekly rhythm of a Monday tip, a Wednesday Q&A and Friday knowledge sharing helps users turn Copilot into a habit.
  • Start with a few critical features, support middle management and plan for the embedding phase after the rollout.

Worth quoting

“I try to keep and repeating this sentence, co pilot is not about technology, co pilot is about building new behavior” – Carina de Vries (24:28)
