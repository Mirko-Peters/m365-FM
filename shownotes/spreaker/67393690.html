If your team still exports Dynamics 365 data to Excel to build charts, the numbers are stale before the meeting starts. This episode is for Dynamics 365, Power BI and data platform professionals who want live analytics inside the apps people already use. You will learn how to connect Dynamics 365 to Microsoft Fabric and embed role-specific Power BI reports for real-time decisions.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – When reporting feels like Groundhog Day
  • 04:32 – The missing link between data and action
  • 08:37 – Wiring Dynamics 365 to Fabric: the practical playbook
  • 12:37 – Designing embedded reports your team will actually use
  • 16:44 – From reactive to proactive: the immediate payoff
  • 21:05 – Next steps: pilot one embedded report

Key takeaways

  • Exporting Dynamics 365 data to Excel creates stale, conflicting snapshots because operational and analytical systems are not wired together.
  • Pointing Microsoft Fabric at Dataverse, shaping the data with Data Factory or Dataflow Gen2 and securing it with role-based access creates a live analytical copy without middleware.
  • Role-specific Power BI reports embedded in Dynamics 365 forms cut insight latency from days to minutes and drive adoption because they sit inside the daily workflow.
