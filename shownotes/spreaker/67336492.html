Your Graph-powered app passes every test, then hits a wall of admin approval prompts in production. This episode is for developers, makers and admins who build or approve apps on Microsoft Graph. You will learn how delegated and application permissions differ, why some need admin consent, and how least privilege keeps rollouts smooth.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Graph API access breaks in production
  • 05:15 – Delegated vs application permissions
  • 10:05 – Consent confusion: why some permissions demand admins
  • 15:25 – Building secure, smooth consent strategies
  • 21:01 – What it all boils down to

Key takeaways

  • Apps that work in a permissive dev tenant often fail in production because enterprise consent policies restrict which Graph permissions users can grant.
  • Delegated permissions act within a signed-in user's rights, while application permissions let an app act on its own across the organization and therefore require admin consent.
  • Requesting only the narrowest scopes, piloting with trusted users and preparing admin approval early keeps Graph-based rollouts on track.

Worth quoting

“Delegated permissions come into play when an actual user is signed in and using your app. You're piggybacking off their identity, working in the bubble of what they're already allowed to see and do.” – Mirko Peters (05:55)

“If compromised, a single poorly planned app could read sensitive emails, HR data or customer info across the tenant without anyone noticing. That explains why the admin roadblock exists. It's a fail safe, not an obstacle.” – Mirko Peters (14:19)
