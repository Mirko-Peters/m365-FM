Power Apps built on Excel and SharePoint lists launch fast, then quietly fail on duplicates, delegation limits and access workarounds. This episode is for Power Platform makers, architects and IT leaders deciding on a data foundation, and it explains why Dataverse is an operating model decision that keeps apps, automation and Copilot trustworthy at scale.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden cost pattern most teams miss
  • 04:30 – Dataverse is about behavior, not storage
  • 11:52 – Flat vs relational data and delegation limits
  • 19:28 – Data quality, cycle time and auditability
  • 30:46 – Governance, security, ownership and environments
  • 46:25 – ALM, model-driven and canvas apps
  • 55:56 – Scenarios: shadow apps and AI grounding
  • 71:25 – Cost, four leadership decisions and adoption

Key takeaways

  • Excel and SharePoint lists are cheap to start but shift cost into coordination, duplication and rework once a process grows across teams.
  • Dataverse enforces relationships, data types, security roles and audit history, giving apps, automation and AI one governed source of truth.
  • Before scaling, leaders must decide data model ownership, environment strategy, access model and ALM, then adopt Dataverse where structural pain is already visible.
