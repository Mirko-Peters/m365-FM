Everyone in your organization shares responsibility for Microsoft 365, so in practice nobody owns orphaned teams, stale external sharing or unlabeled content. This episode is for IT leaders, compliance owners and executives who want a tenant they can actually govern. Learn how to separate technical custody from business sovereignty and build a service ownership model that makes Copilot and Power Platform trustworthy.

Key takeaways

  • Shared responsibility works at the cloud boundary, but inside a Microsoft 365 tenant it creates an ownership vacuum that shows up as orphaned teams, never-ending external sharing and retention without ownership.
  • Separate technical custody by IT from service ownership and business sovereignty over data, and give every service and data domain a named, accountable owner.
  • Design ownership into the environment with at least two owners, HR-triggered reassignment, Entra delegation, Purview labels and DLP, and measure ownership coverage before scaling Copilot.
