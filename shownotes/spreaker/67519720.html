Your Microsoft 365 dashboards show rising adoption, yet collaboration still feels like email with emojis. This episode is for IT, change and business leaders who want to understand why rollouts stall culturally and how to align technology, mindset and relationships so the investment actually transforms how people work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The trap of "we already rolled out M365"
  • 04:43 – Why a meta perspective changes everything
  • 09:03 – SharePoint, Viva and the aha moments
  • 13:25 – The lasting effects of the Teams rollout
  • 17:44 – The human factor, relationships and the AI future

Key takeaways

  • Deployment is only the starting line: active licenses and rising usage numbers do not prove that collaboration or decision making has changed.
  • Treating Microsoft 365 as a long-horizon journey aligns IT stability, leadership vision and user needs instead of letting them pull in different directions.
  • Leadership attitudes, trust and continuous feedback decide whether SharePoint, Viva and Teams become cultural infrastructure, and the same pattern will repeat with AI.

Worth quoting

“Equating deployment with transformation feels natural because the measurable side of a rollout is easy to track. The messy human side is harder to capture and often gets ignored.” – Mirko Peters (00:56)

“Usage data only tells you that someone clicked the login button. It doesn't show if they found value or if they quietly went back to old habits.” – Mirko Peters (02:25)
