Many Microsoft partners enroll in the AI Cloud Partner Program, collect badges and still stall out. This episode is for partner leaders at MSPs, SIs and ISVs, and it lays out four pillars, designations, co-sell, marketplace and an operational engine, plus a 90-day plan to turn program participation into real revenue growth.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Program membership vs strategy
  • 00:52 – Why most partner strategies fail
  • 02:43 – Pillar 1: designations that pay off
  • 05:00 – Pillar 2: co-sell that works
  • 07:36 – Making co-sell operational
  • 09:58 – Pillar 3: marketplace as revenue engine
  • 12:58 – Pillar 4: the operational engine
  • 15:24 – The 90-day action plan

Key takeaways

  • Start from the revenue stream you want in 18 months and pick two designations that match it, rather than chasing points across all solution areas.
  • Co-sell is a two-way, operational motion that needs a better-together story, seller-ready assets, an up-to-date Partner Center profile and active deal registration.
  • A transactable, MACC-eligible marketplace offer plus an internal operational engine for PCS, certifications and incentives turns program participation into revenue.

Worth quoting

“Being in the program and winning with it are two different things. One is compliance, the other is strategy.” – Mirko Peters (00:08)

“Pick two designations that align to your core revenue. Own them deeply. Build real capability. Get the certifications. Hit the usage metrics. And ignore the rest until you have capacity to expand.” – Mirko Peters (04:46)
