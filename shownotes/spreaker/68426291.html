Copilot in Microsoft Fabric confidently produces wrong numbers when your data model is inconsistent. This episode is for data engineers and BI teams who want to trace those hallucinations back to medallion layers, missing semantics and weak governance. You learn how to rebuild the model and instructions so Copilot answers become accurate and explainable.

  • 11:51 – Data source and agent instructions
  • 17:51 – The systemic cure
  • 22:30 – Teaching Copilot to tell the truth

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Fabric does not validate facts; it mirrors the quality, gaps and inconsistencies of your schema and metadata.
  • Clean medallion layers and a well-defined semantic model with consistent measures, synonyms and calculation groups are the foundation for accurate AI answers.
  • Centralized data source and agent instructions, example queries, certified datasets and regular truth audits keep Copilot reliable over time.

Worth quoting
