Employees forget most of what they learn in Microsoft 365 workshops within days, and help desks pay the price. This episode shows IT and adoption leads how in-product guidance, Microsoft Search and Viva Learning deliver micro lessons right in the flow of work. Learn where to redirect training budgets so skills actually stick.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why training still fails
  • 04:16 – What modern learning really means
  • 08:32 – Built-in learning in Microsoft 365
  • 12:39 – What adoption looks like
  • 16:57 – Why old training is dead
  • 21:15 – Rethinking training investments

Key takeaways

  • Classroom workshops fail because knowledge learned out of context fades quickly along the forgetting curve.
  • Microsoft 365 already embeds learning through Microsoft Search, in-product tips and Viva Learning inside Teams.
  • Because embedded guidance updates with the product, it keeps employees current with evergreen changes that static training cannot match.

Worth quoting

“The key failure isn't inattentive employees. It's the outdated system that assumes learning can succeed when stripped away from context.” – Mirko Peters (03:37)

“This is what people mean when they talk about learning in the flow of work. It's the idea that the act of learning is not a break from work, but a natural part of it.” – Mirko Peters (06:22)
