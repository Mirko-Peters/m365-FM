Many Microsoft Information Protection rollouts look secure on paper while users mislabel files or work around the controls. This episode gives security and compliance teams five quick checks covering purpose, simplicity, resistance, pilots and training. Use them to find out whether your MIP rollout will fail or fly before it goes live.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Labels with no purpose
  • 04:07 – The technical rabbit hole
  • 07:23 – The human resistance factor
  • 11:07 – The pilot that predicts success
  • 14:49 – Training that sticks
  • 18:18 – Five-point checklist

Key takeaways

  • Every label must map to a clearly stated business risk, starting with a small core set such as internal only, personal data, confidential and public.
  • Over-engineered label hierarchies and poorly prepared users create workarounds, so simplicity and clear communication drive adoption.
  • Pilot with real business users under real deadlines and reinforce training through microlearning, contextual nudges and manager follow-up.

Worth quoting

“Here's the test you can run right now before you roll out labels, answer in one sentence, what specific business risk will these labels reduce.” – Mirko Peters (02:04)

“If it takes more than three clicks, or if users have to dig through a sub menu to label a file, your taxonomy is too complex.” – Mirko Peters (05:55)
