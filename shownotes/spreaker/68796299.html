Most organizations waste AI on chat while incidents and tickets pile up in IT operations. This episode gives IT ops and SRE teams three blueprints for agents built with MCP, Semantic Kernel, Azure OpenAI tool calling and managed identity that plan, act, verify and stay inside strict guardrails, cutting MTTR and ticket load.

Key takeaways

  • MCP exposes tools in a standard shape, Semantic Kernel plans and shapes the calls, and managed identity contains every action.
  • Encode safety at the tool boundary with schemas, range clamps and signed approval tokens instead of relying on prompt text.
  • Every remediation must be verified against objective metrics and logged in an immutable audit envelope, with rollback or escalation when it fails.
