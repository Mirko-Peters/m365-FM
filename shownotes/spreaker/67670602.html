Copilot can surface sensitive contracts or forecasts to anyone who technically has access. This episode is for Microsoft 365 security, compliance and IT leaders preparing a broad Copilot rollout. You will learn how Microsoft Purview labels, auditing and insider risk management give you oversight in the right order.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden risks of Copilot
  • 03:52 – Guardrails with Purview
  • 07:41 – Shining a light: auditing and tracking AI
  • 11:52 – From reactive to proactive: insider risk management
  • 16:15 – The roadmap from insight to oversight
  • 20:10 – What the real risk of turning Copilot on is

Key takeaways

  • Copilot works within existing permissions but blends content from across the tenant, so permissions and DLP alone do not prevent sensitive data from surfacing.
  • Microsoft Purview adds content-level control: sensitivity labels travel with the data, while auditing and eDiscovery reconstruct how content reached AI outputs.
  • Roll out governance in order: sensitivity labels first, then auditing with sufficient retention, then insider risk management for high-severity signals.

Worth quoting

“The challenge is that Copilot doesn't break permission rules on paper. Instead, it operates within those permissions, but changes the way the information is consumed, effectively flattening separation lines that used to exist.” – Mirko Peters (02:23)

“The bottom line is this copilot without oversight doesn't just open risk, it hides risk. When you can't measure or see what's happening, you can't mitigate it.” – Mirko Peters (03:34)
