Power Apps and Power Automate flows that shine in testing often stall, time out or get throttled once real volume arrives. This episode shows makers and architects how to spot those breaking points early and use small Azure Functions and AI services to extend, not replace, their low-code solutions.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Where Power Platform runs out of steam
  • 04:21 – Spotting the breaking point early
  • 07:44 – Azure Functions as the invisible bridge
  • 11:43 – Smarter workflows with AI Builder and Azure AI
  • 16:04 – Future-proofing with best practices

Key takeaways

  • Growing run durations, frequent retries or throttling errors and patchwork nested flows are early signs that a Power Platform solution is hitting its limits.
  • Small, single-purpose Azure Functions can take over heavy compute and API integrations while the app and flows keep their familiar low-code surface.
  • Extend deliberately: store secrets in Key Vault, add monitoring, batch work instead of looping, design for compliance early, and pick AI Builder or Azure AI services by scope.

Worth quoting
