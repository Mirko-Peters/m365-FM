Copilot is not a helpful feature layer but a decision engine on top of Microsoft Graph that amplifies every gap in your permissions and governance. This masterclass gives security, legal and IT leaders ten enforceable design mandates, a reusable reason-plan-gate-execute pattern and a red flag test to prevent data leakage, confident fiction and irreversible automation in Microsoft 365.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot inherits your permissions and oversharing through Microsoft Graph, so default-deny scopes and enforced sensitivity labels are the only real boundaries.
  • Separate reasoning from execution with the reason, plan, gate, execute pattern, demanding structured plans, citations and rollback before any change runs.
  • Treat identity, explicit session state, observability and budgets as controls, with dedicated agent identities, least privilege and drift alerts.
