Your SharePoint list tracker has outgrown its duct tape, but Dataverse brings licensing, storage and skills costs. This episode is for Power Platform makers and IT leads deciding between lists, Dataverse and SQL, with a simple gut check, a budgeting checklist and a migration plan that avoids broken flows and security surprises.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Dataverse is more than a licensing headache
  • 00:42 – What Dataverse is and why it isn't just another list
  • 03:34 – Lists vs Dataverse vs SQL: the good, the bad and the ugly
  • 06:51 – The cost nobody puts in the demo slide
  • 10:45 – Makers beware: skills you actually need
  • 14:25 – Migration reality check
  • 17:56 – Recap

Key takeaways

  • Use SharePoint lists for small, short-term needs and Dataverse when you need relationships, record-level security, auditing and scale.
  • Budget Dataverse like infrastructure: validate licensing and premium features with procurement and plan storage, schema, roles and skills upfront.
  • Treat a list-to-Dataverse migration as re-architecture with an inventory, data cleanup, rebuilt security roles and flows, a pilot and a rollback plan.
