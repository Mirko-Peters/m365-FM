Most organizations treat Power Automate as a simple workflow tool until outages, audit questions and runaway API costs prove otherwise. This episode is for architects, platform teams and IT leaders who run automation at enterprise scale. It gives you a control plane mental model and concrete patterns, from direct path flows and child flows as execution units to KPIs that make automation explainable.

In this episode

  • 04:38 – What the automation control plane really is
  • 08:53 – Excellence as an architectural property
  • 13:04 – Anti-patterns: Christmas tree flows, API exhaustion, shadow automation
  • 24:20 – Intent, decision, execution
  • 28:28 – Direct path, design discipline and transaction-driven design
  • 50:04 – Child flows, early termination, expressions and flat nesting
  • 67:05 – KPIs, reference architecture and case study

Key takeaways

  • Power Automate should be governed as an automation control plane, because the platform enforces configuration, not intent.
  • Separate probabilistic decisions from deterministic execution: keep orchestration thin and put every side effect into idempotent child flows with receipts and bounded retries.
  • Measure explainability and ownership, such as mean time to explain, ownership coverage and API budget adherence, instead of counting flows and runs.
