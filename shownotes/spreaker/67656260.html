Microsoft 365 BizChat often disappoints because it cannot see the CRM, ERP and custom apps where business answers live. This episode is for makers and developers who want BizChat to act as a real copilot, explaining how to choose the right systems, what Copilot connectors are and how to build and secure a scoped first connector.

  • 07:19 – Finding the right integration points
  • 10:55 – What Copilot connectors really are
  • 14:38 – Building and securing your first connector
  • 18:17 – Your next step: one scoped connector

Key takeaways

  • Out of the box, BizChat only sees Microsoft 365 content, so questions about CRM, ERP or project data return vague, half-formed answers that erode trust.
  • Prioritise connectors by how often the data is needed, how much context switching they remove and how sensitive the data is, rather than by what is easiest to connect.
  • Build connectors with Copilot Studio or Teams Toolkit around a narrowly scoped use case, minimal fields, identity-based authentication and role-based permissions, tested jointly by IT and business owners.
