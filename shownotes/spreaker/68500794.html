Copilot can summarize and suggest, but on its own it saves moments, not money. This episode is for Power Platform makers and admins who need to show real ROI from AI. You will learn five Power Automate techniques, from custom connectors and Adaptive Cards to DLP, concurrency and telemetry, that turn Copilot ideas into governed, measurable automation.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The limitations of AI assistants
  • 00:34 – Why Power Automate turns Copilot ideas into action
  • 01:24 – Custom connectors: giving Copilot real context
  • 06:00 – Adaptive Cards: turning suggestions into actions
  • 09:37 – DLP enforcement: guarding against data leaks
  • 14:23 – Parallelism and concurrency: scaling Copilot
  • 18:27 – Telemetry: measuring AI ROI
  • 21:29 – The path to AI efficiency

Key takeaways

  • Copilot only produces measurable ROI when its suggestions are wired into governed Power Automate workflows.
  • Custom connectors and Adaptive Cards give Copilot access to internal systems and let users act directly in Teams or Outlook, while DLP policies keep data inside approved connectors.
  • Concurrency control speeds up high-volume flows within API limits, and telemetry from tools like Application Insights or Purview turns time savings into evidence.

Worth quoting
