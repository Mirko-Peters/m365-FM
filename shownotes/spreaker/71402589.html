Your teams may not have a focus problem but a notification architecture problem built into Microsoft 365 defaults. This episode is for IT leaders and managers who see busy calendars, constant pings and slow delivery. Learn how an attention baseline across Teams, Outlook, Viva, SharePoint and mobile can cut noise, reduce meetings and win back focus time.

In this episode

  • 07:09 – The default settings trap in Microsoft 365
  • 11:22 – The business cost leaders feel
  • 14:42 – Case: a global services firm
  • 17:37 – Building an attention baseline
  • 21:26 – What to do this week

Key takeaways

  • Default Teams, Outlook, SharePoint and mobile notifications push people into reaction mode, slowing decisions, stretching cycle times and driving up meeting hours.
  • Notifications should be governed like a security baseline: mentions and replies only in Teams, no Outlook desktop pop-ups, aligned quiet hours and protected Viva focus time.
  • Technical settings only stick when leaders define what is truly urgent and remove the expectation of instant responses, then measure decisions, delivery and meeting load.

Worth quoting

“Because quick replies and useful output are not the same thing, a person can answer 20 messages before lunch and still move nothing important forward” – Mirko Peters (01:04)
