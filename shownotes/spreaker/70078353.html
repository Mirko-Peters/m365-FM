Gluing a chatbot to a folder of HR PDFs produces confident answers without controls, evidence or a defensible decision trail. This episode shows HR technology owners and Power Platform architects how to build governed Copilot Studio agents with Logic Apps for candidate screening, ticket triage and onboarding that hold up under audits and scale.

Key takeaways

  • Treat an HR agent as a governed decision engine: Copilot Studio captures intent, Logic Apps Standard executes tools under managed identities, Dataverse holds state and monitoring holds the evidence.
  • Use deterministic human-in-the-loop confidence and risk gates with structured approval records and reason codes, so irreversible or borderline actions always stop for an accountable person.
  • Build in order of risk, starting with ticket triage, then onboarding, then candidate screening, and measure deflection, overrides, reopen rates and fairness signals instead of vanity adoption metrics.
