Many organizations buy Microsoft 365 Copilot and expect users to figure it out on their own. In this interview, Microsoft cloud solutions architect Chris Hinch explains for IT leaders and adoption owners what separates successful rollouts from failed ones, from pain-point use cases and champions programs to prompting, governance and security.

  • 45:23 – Where Copilot is heading

Key takeaways

  • Copilot adoption succeeds when it starts from department pain points and comes with real training, not when AI is rolled out for its own sake.
  • A champions program spreads working prompts across departments and creates internal advocates for the wider rollout.
  • Copilot respects user permissions, so poor governance becomes visible; tools like SharePoint Advanced Management, Purview, Entra and Defender help close those gaps.
