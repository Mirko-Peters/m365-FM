Copilot rollouts often disappoint not because the model is weak but because enterprise context is fragmented, overshared and stale. This episode is for architects and Microsoft 365 leaders who want reliable Copilot and safe agents, and shows how to build context as architecture with Graph, Dataverse, Fabric and real guardrails.

  • 09:15 – Why agents fail first

  • 21:57 – Grounding, relevance windows, Dataverse and Fabric
  • 39:50 – The autonomy stack
  • 48:19 – Context as attack surface, guardrails and drift
  • 64:54 – Case study, leader mistakes and 30-day pilot

Key takeaways

  • Copilot is only an interaction surface; its reliability is bounded by the identity, permissions, memory and evidence in the tenant underneath it.
  • A reliable autonomy stack combines Microsoft Graph as memory, Dataverse as operational state, Fabric as the learning layer and Copilot as the interaction layer, with explicit relevance windows and refusal conditions.
  • Least privilege, continuous access evaluation and provenance are the guardrails that hold when context becomes an attack surface through prompt injection, memory poisoning and drift.
