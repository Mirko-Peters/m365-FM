Clicking 'Create an app' on a SharePoint list feels like a shortcut, but it is often the mistake that later breaks your Power App. This episode is for makers and Power Platform admins who need to know when SharePoint lists are safe, why delegation and thresholds make apps lie, and when to move to Dataverse.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The create-an-app shortcut
  • 01:22 – SharePoint's seductive simplicity
  • 04:24 – The delegation disaster
  • 07:43 – The scalability wall
  • 11:55 – Governance and the cost of free
  • 16:00 – Dataverse as the scalable alternative
  • 20:26 – Prototype vs production

Key takeaways

  • Non-delegable queries against SharePoint lists silently return incomplete data once lists grow, so apps can look correct while hiding records.
  • SharePoint lists hit the list view threshold, throttling and item-permission overhead, while Dataverse offers relational data, role-based and field-level security and full auditing.
  • Use SharePoint lists for small prototypes and move apps that span departments, hold sensitive data or drive automation to Dataverse early.

Worth quoting

“SharePoint was built for collaboration documents, wikis lists. Power apps was built for applications, structured relational data, transactional workflows, record level logic.” – Mirko Peters (00:44)

“Delegation means that the heavy lifting, the filtering, sorting, searching happens on the server close to the data.” – Mirko Peters (04:35)
