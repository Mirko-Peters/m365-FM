Your Microsoft 365 tenant keeps creating teams, sites, flows and agents faster than any review process can keep up. This episode is for admins, architects and security leads who want to understand why policies on paper do not equal control. You get the five erosion patterns of tenant decay and the control patterns — ownership, lifecycle and risk-based consequence — that make governance predictable.

Key takeaways

  • Configuration is not control: Microsoft 365 governance only exists where enforcement is continuous, cross-service and backed by consequences.
  • Enforced ownership, lifecycle and risk-based prioritization are the primitives that keep sprawl, sharing drift and ownerless resources from becoming permanent.
  • Copilot and agents do not create new risk but expose and accelerate existing permission drift, so permissions must be baselined before AI scales them.

Worth quoting
