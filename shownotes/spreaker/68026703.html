Many sales teams still treat their CRM as a digital address book and lose hours to manual logging. This episode shows sellers and sales managers how Dynamics 365 Sales with Copilot, Outlook and Teams integration, predictive forecasting and LinkedIn signals turns the CRM into a real command hub.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why this isn't just another CRM
  • 03:40 – Outlook and Teams built in
  • 06:34 – Copilot as your pipeline's dungeon master
  • 10:06 – Forecasting without spreadsheet guesswork
  • 13:35 – Relationship analytics and LinkedIn signals
  • 17:41 – A unified sales ecosystem

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Sales shifts CRM from passive record keeping to guided selling with playbooks, sequences and next best actions.
  • Outlook and Teams integration plus Copilot summaries and drafts keep sellers in context and cut manual logging.
  • Predictive forecasting, relationship analytics and LinkedIn Sales Navigator signals reveal deal risks early enough to act.

Worth quoting

“A record keeper CRM tells you what already happened. A sales HQ tells you what deserves your attention right now and with which tactic” – Mirko Peters (02:45)
