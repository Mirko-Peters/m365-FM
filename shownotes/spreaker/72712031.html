Many organizations expect Copilot Studio agents to solve everything with a few instructions. Microsoft MVP Nilüfer Doğan explains for Power Platform makers, architects and IT leaders how to combine Copilot Studio with Azure AI Search and RAG, measure business value and govern agents built by citizen developers.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Welcome and guest introduction
  • 03:15 – From economics to Power Platform
  • 08:08 – Copilot Studio misconceptions and recent changes
  • 13:36 – AI agents versus chatbots and when to use Azure
  • 16:34 – Real case: a Confluence agent
  • 21:04 – Azure AI Search, Foundry and RAG
  • 27:28 – Business value, governance and human in the loop
  • 39:08 – Citizen developers and rapid-fire round

Key takeaways

  • Good Copilot Studio agents need structured knowledge, tools and automation, not just well-written instructions, and not every problem needs an agent.
  • For large or unstructured data, combining Copilot Studio with Azure AI Search for indexing, vectorization and RAG gives better and faster answers, including permission-aware results.
  • Measuring value with tools like the Copilot Studio Kit, keeping humans in the loop for serious decisions and managing environments and ALM are essential for scaling agents and citizen development.

Worth quoting
