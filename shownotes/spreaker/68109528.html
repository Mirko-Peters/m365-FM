Legacy SharePoint 2010 and 2013 workflows and InfoPath forms are being retired, yet many organizations still depend on them. This episode is for SharePoint admins and Power Platform makers who need a practical modernization path. You learn how Power Apps, Power Automate, AI Builder and Copilot Studio upgrade your existing lists and libraries without starting from scratch.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why your SharePoint still feels stuck in 2013
  • 02:26 – The 2010/2013 workflow retirement deadline
  • 04:51 – Turning SharePoint lists into Power Apps
  • 08:30 – Replacing legacy workflows with Power Automate
  • 12:08 – AI Builder for document processing
  • 16:23 – Copilot Studio as a conversational guide
  • 20:38 – Conclusion and a three-step path

Key takeaways

  • SharePoint 2010 workflows are already retired and SharePoint 2013 workflows face full retirement in SharePoint Online, so inventory classic workflows and InfoPath forms now.
  • Power Apps can generate a canvas app directly on top of an existing SharePoint list, and Power Automate cloud flows replace brittle legacy approvals with visible, auditable automation.
  • AI Builder's classify and extract models turn document libraries into searchable metadata, and Copilot Studio lets users query SharePoint data and trigger actions in natural language.

Worth quoting

“Modernization isn't a rebuild, It's an upgrade in how you inter act with what you already have. Your lists, libraries, and store data still serve as the core.” – Mirko Peters (03:50)

“Power apps can generate a canvas app from a SharePoint list quickly. You don't need to port your data or write back end code. The canvas app points directly to the list as its data source.” – Mirko Peters (06:02)
