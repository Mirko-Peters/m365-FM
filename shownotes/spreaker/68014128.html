Finance, sales and HR all bring different numbers to the same meeting? This episode is for Microsoft 365 leaders, analysts and admins who want to end version wars and data silos. Learn how embedding Power BI in everyday apps and unifying data with Fabric and OneLake turns analytics into shared, trusted decisions.

Key takeaways

  • Data silos are mainly a cultural and governance problem: departments hoarding their own spreadsheets and dashboards create competing versions of the truth.
  • Embedding Power BI in Teams, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint and SharePoint removes context switching and brings live, shared data into the places where decisions are made.
  • Microsoft Fabric and OneLake provide one shared data layer and semantic model, while Purview and sensitivity labels keep shared datasets governed.

Worth quoting

“The problem usually isn't technology. The tools exist. The choke point is culture. Teams treat their data like personal loot instead of shared guild gear, and when that happens, silos form.” – Mirko Peters (02:06)

“In practice, a lot of the friction comes from context switching, jumping out of teams to load a dashboard somewhere else. Embedding directly in teams Outlook or Excel cuts out that friction” – Mirko Peters (04:46)
