Customers, partners, contractors and AI agents no longer stay inside your perimeter, yet most identity stacks still rebuild trust with forms, passwords and duplicate accounts. This episode helps security architects and IT leaders see where Entra External ID and Verified ID fit in a claim-centric identity model. Learn how to cut sign-up friction, reduce support load and keep governance strong.

Key takeaways

  • External identity is shifting from account-centric to claim-centric: instead of rebuilding trust in every app, organizations verify portable proof against policy.
  • Entra External ID acts as the orchestration and policy layer for customers and partners, while Entra Verified ID adds portable proof through verifiable credentials.
  • Start with one costly external journey, consolidate identity orchestration, reduce friction with passkeys and just-in-time migration, then add portable proof and lifecycle governance.
