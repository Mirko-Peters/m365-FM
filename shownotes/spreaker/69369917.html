Shared support inboxes full of dead letters burn out agents and frustrate customers. This noir-style episode walks customer service leaders through three Dynamics 365 automation operators, case scanning, unified routing and Copilot Studio drafting, and shows how they turn email chaos into structured, measurable work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The shared inbox as a crime scene
  • 01:41 – Operator one: the case scanner
  • 03:02 – Operator two: the traffic controller
  • 04:41 – Operator three: the shadow operator
  • 06:16 – Case files: insurance and HR
  • 08:05 – Humans for judgment, rollout order

Key takeaways

  • Manual triage of shared inboxes routes work by mood instead of business logic, which leaves urgent cases buried.
  • Email-to-case ingestion with attachment analysis, unified routing by rules, skills and capacity, and Copilot Studio drafted replies form a three-step cleanup crew.
  • Roll out the scanner first, then routing, then AI drafting, and keep humans as reviewers who approve every reply.
