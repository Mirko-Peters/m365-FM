Getting a solution listed on Microsoft Marketplace takes far longer than most partners plan, and many submissions are rejected for avoidable reasons. This episode is for ISVs and Microsoft partners preparing a listing, and it walks through the hidden requirements, the right storefront choice and what it takes to turn a live listing into real pipeline.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The seven listing requirements
  • 01:30 – Five errors behind most rejections
  • 03:21 – The real timeline
  • 05:13 – AppSource vs Azure Marketplace
  • 07:59 – Life after going live
  • 10:25 – The repeatable right way
  • 13:15 – The real cost of the hard way

Key takeaways

  • Most marketplace rejections come from five preventable administrative errors, such as missing terms of use, privacy policy links or reviewer testing instructions.
  • Plan for 12 to 24 weeks and build Entra ID, SaaS fulfillment, metering and compliance documentation into the product from the start.
  • Choose AppSource or Azure Marketplace by where your buyer shops, and treat going live as the start of co-sell and seller activation, not the finish line.
