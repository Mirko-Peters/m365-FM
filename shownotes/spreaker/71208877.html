Many leaders still treat governance as overhead that slows innovation, until a Copilot or Power Platform rollout stalls on messy permissions and unclear ownership. This episode is for executives and IT leaders who want to turn governance into architecture, using a real services-firm case and a 15-minute readiness test to gain speed, trust and AI value.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The old belief that keeps organizations stuck
  • 04:44 – Case: a global services firm before the redesign
  • 12:29 – Governance as architecture: data, identity, control
  • 26:30 – Delivery velocity and AI time to value
  • 37:17 – What mature organizations do differently
  • 44:03 – The four-part readiness test for Copilot and Power Platform
  • 54:58 – The governance and trust dividend
  • 63:17 – Agents, starting sequence and competitive advantage

Key takeaways

  • Immature governance, not governance itself, slows organizations down; designing a governed default path makes the safe route the fastest one.
  • A four-part readiness test of data, identity, control and outcome shows quickly whether a Copilot or Power Platform rollout is ready to scale.
  • Mature governance pays off as faster delivery, shorter AI time to value and reusable trust, which matters even more once autonomous agents take action.

Worth quoting

“What slows organizations down is rarely governance itself, but rather the presence of immature governance. It's the kind of governance that depends on memory or heroic intervention from a few key people.” – Mirko Peters (03:10)

“In the world of Microsoft 365 and Copilot, AI does not invent new permissions. It simply inherits the ones you already have.” – Mirko Peters (21:49)
