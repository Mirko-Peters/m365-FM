Many Copilot rollouts stall after a few weeks, and the cause is rarely the AI itself. This episode is for leaders and architects in Microsoft 365 who want to understand why their operating model is not structurally ready. You will get a practical frame of five elements and a 90-day measurement model to make your organization able to absorb AI safely.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The real barrier is the operating model
  • 04:17 – Why AI exposes rather than fixes
  • 07:49 – The Copilot stall pattern and the silo tax
  • 18:59 – Data is not knowledge
  • 22:07 – Permissions and ownership as business design
  • 32:21 – Redesigning decision rights around data
  • 41:27 – Zillow, shadow AI and human versus system roles
  • 62:46 – Five elements and a 90-day measurement model

Key takeaways

  • Most organizations are blocked from Copilot value not by the technology but by fragmented data, unclear ownership and broken decision flow.
  • Named owners and explicit decision rights covering input ownership, recommendation logic, approval and override authority are the first real control surface for AI.
  • Leaders should measure AI readiness through decision velocity, decision clarity, rework reduction and AI trust rather than license and login counts.
