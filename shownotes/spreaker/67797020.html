Speed is tempting, but every skipped checkpoint in a no-code app can quietly turn into a data leak or an audit finding. This episode helps IT leaders, makers and architects compare low-code and pro-code on security models, governance overhead, compliance evidence and integration, so they can pick the right model for each app.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Would you know why your app leaked data?
  • 00:39 – Speed vs security: the hidden trade-off
  • 04:00 – Security models: guardrails vs full control
  • 07:50 – Governance burden: who owns the risk?
  • 11:45 – Compliance reality check
  • 16:09 – IT integration and vendor lock-in
  • 20:01 – Matching the model to your project's risk

Key takeaways

  • No-code speed comes from skipping checkpoints such as reviews, staged testing and release gates, so you need to know which guardrails you are trading away for agility.
  • Low-code platforms give you inherited guardrails, DLP-style policies and vendor compliance attestations, while pro-code gives full control but makes you design, maintain and prove every control yourself.
  • Choose the model by risk ownership: the sensitivity of the data, who answers when something breaks, and whether you need integrations beyond your standard stack.

Worth quoting

“Low code platforms give you protections you didn't design, consistent across the environment, but hard to customize. Pro code platforms give you control for every layer, but they demand constant attention and upkeep.” – Mirko Peters (07:13)

“Governance is an internal lens, but compliance is what the outside world measures you against when the auditor shows up.” – Mirko Peters (11:57)
