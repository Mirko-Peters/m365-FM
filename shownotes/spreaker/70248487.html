Consumer AI headlines suggest ChatGPT and Gemini are winning, but enterprise AI is decided in identity, data and workflow layers. This episode is for IT leaders and decision makers who want to understand why Microsoft's integrated stack creates a structural advantage and what that means for their own architecture and investment decisions.

Key takeaways

  • The enterprise AI race is decided by control of identity, data, infrastructure and workflow, not by model benchmarks or consumer visibility.
  • Data gravity, compliance certifications and native integration across Entra, Microsoft 365, Fabric, Power Platform and Dynamics 365 create switching costs that compound over time.
  • Organizations should consolidate data first, automation second and governance third, and treat Copilot as foundational infrastructure rather than an optional feature.

Worth quoting

“Competitors are building AI in isolation. Microsoft is building AI into the existing control plane where 400 million commercial seats already operate. That distinction matters more than any temporary model performance gap.” – Mirko Peters (01:08)

“Enterprise software is not a consumer meritocracy driven by personal taste, it is driven by organizational standards that dictate which tools are used for which tasks.” – Mirko Peters (28:03)
