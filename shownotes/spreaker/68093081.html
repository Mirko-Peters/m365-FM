Copilot surfaces whatever users can access, so overshared SharePoint sites quickly become an AI governance problem. This episode is for SharePoint and Microsoft 365 admins and shows how SharePoint Advanced Management, data access governance reports, site access reviews, block download and restricted access control keep Copilot from seeing too much, plus how to budget for SharePoint Premium.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Copilot and weak governance
  • 00:39 – Why basic permissions are not enough
  • 03:35 – Site owners as guards: site access reviews
  • 07:01 – Block download vs restricted access control
  • 10:22 – AI insights as strategic advisor
  • 14:07 – Smart budgeting for SharePoint Premium
  • 17:30 – The core governance playbook

Key takeaways

  • Data access governance reports reveal overshared sites so admins know where Copilot could surface content too broadly.
  • Use block download when content must be visible but not exfiltrated, and restricted access control when even discovery by people or Copilot must be prevented.
  • License SharePoint Advanced Management first for steady governance, then add pay-as-you-go content processing only where it clearly pays off.
