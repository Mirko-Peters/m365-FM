Why do so many CRM projects still fail when the technology is better than ever? Microsoft MVP Thomas Sandsør, the CRM keeper, shares nearly 20 years of Dynamics experience on the human side of business applications. Consultants, project leads and decision makers learn how to drive adoption, involve management and prepare for an agent-driven CRM future.

Key takeaways

  • CRM implementations succeed or fail on people and organizational buy-in, not on features or buttons.
  • AI agents will change how users interact with CRM, but a solid data model and clean data input remain the consultant's core value.
  • Sales, customer service and marketing need different adoption approaches, and management must be engaged early so expectations match the data model.

Worth quoting

“The times that we fail is the times we can't get the organization invested into what they are getting, they just think they're buying a product.” – Thomas Sandsør (21:02)

“I still feel the power in let's say closing a deal is meeting a person sometimes face to face, being able to talk to them and building trust, because AI is not going to build trust.” – Thomas Sandsør (18:10)
