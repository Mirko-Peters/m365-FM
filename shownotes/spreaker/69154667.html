AI agents can wire up Microsoft Graph and Entra integrations in minutes, but they quietly ship broad scopes, client secrets and policy exclusions. This episode is for identity architects and platform engineers who want AI speed without privilege creep. Learn how Spec Kit-style constitutions, specs and CI gates turn identity intent into rules the system enforces.

Key takeaways

  • AI coding agents sample internet defaults such as client secrets, broad Graph scopes and wildcard redirects, so unstated identity decisions drift away from your policy at scale.
  • Encode non-negotiables once in a constitution and enforce them with gates at generation, build, deploy and reconcile so non-conforming shapes fail before they exist.
  • Measure governance with curves like consent velocity, blocked permissions, standing privileged roles, effective Conditional Access coverage and MTTR instead of tickets and trainings.
