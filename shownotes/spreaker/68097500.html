AI agents are arriving in Teams, SharePoint and Dynamics with real entitlements and tool access, but without understanding your boundaries. This episode is for managers and IT leaders who need to govern these digital coworkers. You get a simple framework, practical controls and first steps to keep agents useful, safe and aligned.

  • 07:39 – The three pieces every agent needs: memory, entitlements, tools
  • 11:28 – The invisible problem: information asymmetry
  • 15:25 – Preventing agent-geddon: five tactics
  • 19:54 – Your first three moves

Key takeaways

  • AI agents combine memory, entitlements and tools, and each of these is both a superpower and a failure point if left unmanaged.
  • Agents follow literal instructions rather than business intent, creating a principal-agent problem and information asymmetry that leaves managers accountable for opaque decisions.
  • Inventory and tier your agents by risk, set escalation thresholds with human-in-the-loop approvals, and red-team critical agents with RBAC, MFA, DLP and audit logs in place.

Worth quoting

“Agents don't share your business goals. They share the literal instructions you feed them. That disconnect is the principal agent problem in a tech wrapper.” – Mirko Peters (02:45)

“That oversight often means requiring human approval before an agent makes potentially risky changes like sending data externally or modifying records across core systems.” – Mirko Peters (05:40)
