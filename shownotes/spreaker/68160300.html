AI assistants can go off script in seconds through prompt injection or unpredictable outputs. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Power Platform leaders who need to deploy copilots responsibly under the EU AI Act. Learn how built-in guardrails and a cross-functional AI governance board keep your AI projects trustworthy and compliant.

In this episode

  • 04:15 – Deterministic versus nondeterministic AI
  • 08:31 – The EU AI Act and risk tiers
  • 12:20 – Shields and guardrails in the Microsoft ecosystem
  • 16:15 – Governance boards: the last defense
  • 20:40 – Your next moves

Key takeaways

  • Prompt injection and nondeterministic outputs mean an AI assistant can turn from useful to harmful instantly, so guardrails, red teaming and human-in-the-loop review are essential.
  • The EU AI Act classifies AI systems by risk and demands transparency, auditability and human oversight for high-risk uses, so compliance must be designed in from the start.
  • Microsoft 365 and Power Platform already offer governance controls such as DLP, audit logs and the Responsible AI dashboard, but a cross-functional governance board is needed to run audits and monitor drift.
