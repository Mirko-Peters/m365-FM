Is your tenant really ready for Copilot, or will AI surface everything you overshared during the pandemic? Microsoft MVP and MCT Åsne Holtklimpen explains why Copilot readiness is a data governance problem and how Purview, sensitivity labels and zero trust fit together. IT, security and business leaders get practical first steps to secure their data before switching AI on.

  • 37:01 – Hot topics and final message

Key takeaways

  • Copilot does not create new security problems; it exposes existing oversharing, outdated data and weak permissions.
  • Start with sensitive information types, sensitivity labels, DLP and conditional access, which are available even with E3 licenses, before rolling out Copilot broadly.
  • Copilot readiness is really Microsoft 365 data governance readiness and needs management ownership, clear use cases and cleanup of old Teams and sharing links.

Worth quoting

“Co-pilot and AI doesn't create new problems. It basically just shines a bright, big yellow light on them.” – Åsne Holtklimpen (05:25)

“It's not something new, it's not something that just co-pilot needs to have in place to be able to have work safely. It's something you should have done ages ago.” – Åsne Holtklimpen (16:23)
