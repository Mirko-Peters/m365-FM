OneNote, Loop, Copilot Pages, Notebooks, Teams chats: Microsoft 365 users have too many places for notes. MVP Karinne Diamond Bessette shows information workers and IT leads how she splits personal and shared notes, grounds Copilot on the right content and fights information sprawl.

Key takeaways

  • Choose a note tool by goal: OneNote for personal notes, Loop for shared live content and Copilot Notebooks for grounding Copilot on project material.
  • Outdated and redundant documents degrade AI answers, so document hygiene and expiry reviews matter.
  • Copilot already works as an effective search across Microsoft 365, and user feedback does influence how Microsoft changes its apps.
