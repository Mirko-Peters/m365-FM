Copilot gets blamed for data leaks, but it only reveals the permission sprawl, unlabeled files and abandoned sites already in your Microsoft 365 tenant. This episode walks IT, security and compliance leaders through five familiar incidents and a 30-60-90 day governance blueprint with SharePoint Advanced Management, labels, DLP and discovery controls to quiet the tenant.

  • 22:01 – Incident 1: EEEU oversharing after migration
  • 36:13 – Restricted SharePoint Search vs. restricted content discovery
  • 66:28 – Shadow AI and the shadow paste
  • 111:33 – Labels, DLP, adaptive protection and compliance
  • 141:22 – Ownerless teams and data lifecycle
  • 164:41 – 30, 60 and 90 day milestones
  • 187:08 – SAM toolkit, Teams spine and agent governance

Key takeaways

  • Copilot only surfaces content that users' existing permissions already allow, so oversharing such as broad Everyone except external users grants must be fixed at the SharePoint source.
  • Use Restricted SharePoint Search only as a temporary brace and restricted content discovery on sensitive hubs, combined with sensitivity labels that encrypt and deny extract rights.
  • Make governance a recurring rhythm with named owners, access reviews, site lifecycle policies, DLP from audit to enforce and DSPM for AI to measure progress.
