Giving AI agents a face and a voice makes them feel trustworthy, but it does nothing to stop a mis-scoped delete or an overshared answer. This episode is for Microsoft 365 architects and security leaders deploying agents with Entra Agent ID, Copilot Studio and Purview. Learn how per-tool policy gates, segmented identities and retrieval boundaries turn eloquent outages into denied decisions.

Key takeaways

  • Conditional Access and Purview audit are necessary but act at token time and after the fact, so high-risk agent tool calls need a deterministic per-call policy gate.
  • Treat events as proposals, not state: idempotency keys, an authoritative state store and segmented read, write and egress identities keep agent failures small.
  • Retrieval should be an authorization boundary with venue-aware filtering, cite-or-silent generation and persisted policy outcomes as proof.

Worth quoting

“Conditional access decides who may try. A policy gate decides what may happen. Treat one as necessary the other as non-negotiable.” – Mirko Peters (45:24)

“Audit tells you what happened. Provenance tells you why you think it happened. A policy gate decides whether it's allowed to happen at all.” – Mirko Peters (30:08)
