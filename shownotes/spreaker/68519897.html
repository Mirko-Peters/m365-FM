If your OneDrive is slow and constantly processing changes, the cause is usually full SharePoint library syncs you barely use. This episode shows Microsoft 365 users and admins why the old sync habit wastes storage, CPU and governance, and how OneDrive shortcuts fix it. You get a step-by-step setup, organization tips and a clear sync-versus-shortcut decision matrix.

  • 04:38 – OneDrive shortcuts explained

  • 11:47 – Organizing and removing shortcuts
  • 15:31 – Sync or shortcut: decision matrix
  • 18:34 – Future-proofing your cloud storage

Key takeaways

  • Syncing entire SharePoint libraries creates metadata overhead, CPU and network churn, storage creep and governance drift on every device.
  • Add shortcut to OneDrive exposes only the folders you work in, roams across devices and keeps labels, retention and version history on the governed source.
  • Use full sync only when offline access for a whole scope or local tooling truly requires it, and cache just the subfolders you need.

Worth quoting

“Traditional full library sync optimizes for familiarity, not efficiency. It makes your laptop feel busy, not productive.” – Mirko Peters (04:19)

“The reason this works is simple. Governance applies to the file, not the doorway. So you keep compliance while regaining sanity.” – Mirko Peters (06:25)
