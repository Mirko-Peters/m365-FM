Most teams build one copilot on one big model and wonder why costs rise, answers blur and governance gets messy. This episode is for architects and AI platform owners on the Microsoft stack. It shows how to replace the generalist bot with a governed mixture of experts, with small-model routing, Copilot Studio and Foundry roles, escalation logic and Agent 365 governance.

  • 18:06 – Router first and expert domains
  • 32:04 – Governance, Agent 365 and Azure Policy
  • 59:25 – Build path, maturity and operating model

Key takeaways

  • A single generalist copilot running every request on a premium model creates cost, quality and governance debt; a governed fabric of bounded experts fits enterprise work better.
  • Make a cheap, structured router the first expert, use small models for routine traffic and escalate to premium reasoning or human review only on clear confidence, ambiguity and risk triggers.
  • Let Copilot Studio own the user experience and Foundry own model control, and govern the fabric with Agent 365, Azure Policy model allow lists, traces and named owners.
