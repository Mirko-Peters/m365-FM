Many organizations move to Azure expecting speed, savings and security, only to find that migrations never end and dashboards change nothing. This episode helps cloud and IT leaders close responsibility and budget gaps so that monitoring finally leads to action and continuous improvement.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The broken cloud promise
  • 00:30 – Why cloud migrations never end
  • 03:56 – The data trap
  • 07:37 – The responsibility mirage
  • 11:30 – Budget constraints, the silent saboteur
  • 16:05 – The posture for continuous improvement

Key takeaways

  • Cloud migrations do not end at go-live; treat ongoing change as the normal state of operations.
  • Tie every monitored metric to an owner who also has the authority, budget and access to act on it.
  • Fund cloud optimization as a permanent, quarterly-reviewed budget line instead of an optional second phase.

Worth quoting

“The value of monitoring isn't in the report itself, it's in the decisions and outcomes it enables.” – Mirko Peters (06:36)

“The fix isn't complicated whenever you assign responsibility paired with three things. Authority to implement changes, budget to cover them, and a clear service level expectation on how quickly those changes should happen.” – Mirko Peters (08:54)
