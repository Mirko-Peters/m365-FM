Your Copilot Studio flow did exactly what you told it to, with bad data. This episode is for makers, Power Platform admins and compliance teams who need automations they can defend in an audit. Learn how the request for information action adds a human firewall that stops null values, phantom records and false approvals.

Key takeaways

  • AI flows fail mostly because they trust incomplete or wrong human input, not because the AI misbehaves.
  • The request for information action in Copilot Studio agent flows pauses execution and collects missing data through an Outlook actionable message, leaving a traceable record of who confirmed what.
  • Pairing AI prompt validation with RFI creates a governance loop in which AI detects gaps and humans confirm reality before data reaches Dataverse.
