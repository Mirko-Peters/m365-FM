Azure AI Foundry demos look flawless, but real enterprise data turns many copilots and agents into hallucination machines. This episode is for architects, admins and AI builders moving from prototype to production. You learn how RAG with hybrid search, evaluators, observability and governance keep your AI grounded and your tenant safe.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Azure AI Foundry is called a trap
  • 00:37 – Why multimodal apps fail in the real world
  • 04:15 – Helpful agent or expensive paperweight
  • 08:03 – The autonomous agent trap
  • 11:46 – Foundry as factory or minefield
  • 16:15 – Responsible AI or responsible headaches
  • 19:35 – Bottom line: governance first

Key takeaways

  • Multimodal and RAG apps fail on messy enterprise data unless retrieval is grounded with hybrid search and semantic reranking in Azure AI Search.
  • Agents and especially autonomous agents need tight scoping, least-privilege identities, logging, rollback and content filters before they touch production.
  • Azure AI Foundry provides evaluators, observability and content safety, but only governance-first lifecycle practices turn prototypes into reliable production systems.

Worth quoting
