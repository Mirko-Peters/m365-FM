A green Intune dashboard can still hide weak Conditional Access, drifting baselines, standing admin rights, unmanaged BYOD and reckless rollouts. This episode is for endpoint and identity admins who want to know why their Intune deployment may be a security risk. Learn the five misconfigurations attackers exploit and the concrete fixes and audits you can run in the admin center, Graph and PowerShell.

Key takeaways

  • Intune only measures device health; it protects the tenant when compliance, Conditional Access and privileged access work together, so build layered CA policies in report-only mode first and keep exclusions surgical, owned and expiring.
  • Adopt Microsoft's security baselines for Windows, Defender and Edge, align compliance policies to them and convert standing admin roles to eligible PIM assignments with MFA, justification and compliant-device activation.
  • Protect BYOD with app protection policies and approved client apps, block legacy authentication, and roll out every change through pilot, early adopter and broad rings backed by a weekly Graph or PowerShell audit.

Worth quoting
