Low-code delivers fast wins, but at scale many organizations can no longer explain the automations their decisions depend on. This episode is for executives, platform owners and architects who carry the risk of Power Platform and low-code sprawl. It shows why explainability is a leadership control and how a graduation path to Fabric notebooks restores auditability, lineage and safe change.

  • 51:33 – Leadership operating model and 30-60-90 plan

Key takeaways

  • Low-code speeds up building but moves complexity into runtime configuration, so organizations lose the ability to explain the systems they depend on.
  • Explainability is a control, not documentation: without inspectable logic, auditability, data lineage and safe change all collapse at scale.
  • Define graduation criteria so mission-critical low-code workflows move into governed execution such as Fabric notebooks with version history, review and clear ownership.

Worth quoting

“Here's the trade nobody says out loud. Abstraction compresses complexity during creation, but it expands complexity during ownership.” – Mirko Peters (02:40)

“Low code doesn't remove complexity, it relocates it, it moves complexity from the build surface where engineers and architects are trained to see it, into the runtime surface” – Mirko Peters (06:51)
