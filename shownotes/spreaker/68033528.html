Staring at a Power BI model with five hundred cryptically named measures? This episode is for Power BI developers and BI teams who need to clean up and collaborate on large models. Learn how PBIP, TMDL and Git turn painful click-by-click renaming into fast, reversible bulk edits.

In this episode

  • 03:15 – Binary PBIX versus human-readable PBIP
  • 06:31 – Why TMDL is a cheat code
  • 10:05 – From keyboard smash to crystal clarity
  • 13:20 – Collaboration level-up with source control
  • 16:00 – Your next step

Key takeaways

  • Hundreds of cryptic measure names slow analysts down, invite duplicate calculations and erode trust in reports.
  • Saving models as PBIP projects with TMDL exposes measures and formatting as plain text, so bulk renames and find-and-replace take minutes instead of days.
  • Storing the text files in Git adds branching, diffs, rollback and an audit trail, which makes bulk refactoring safe and team collaboration manageable.

Worth quoting

“Confusion spreads, collaboration stalls, and duplicated efforts sneaks in as people recreate calculations instead of trusting what's already there. Poor naming doesn't just clutter the field list, it reshapes how people work with the model.” – Mirko Peters (01:31)
