IT portals sit unused while requests arrive by chat, email and hallway conversations. This episode is for IT service desk leads and Microsoft 365 admins running Jira Service Management. You will learn how a Teams integration, careful configuration and automation make ticketing faster without breaking existing workflows.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why submitting tickets in Teams feels effortless
  • 04:18 – The hidden complexity of configuring Jira and Teams
  • 08:12 – How IT agents work fully inside Teams
  • 12:10 – When automation turns integration into acceleration
  • 16:30 – Why the integration feels native to Microsoft 365
  • 21:01 – Start simple, then automate

Key takeaways

  • Letting users raise tickets directly in Microsoft Teams with adaptive cards removes portal friction and increases adoption and ticket quality.
  • A reliable Jira Service Management and Teams integration depends on careful field mapping, Entra ID consent, scoped API tokens, single sign-on and Conditional Access.
  • Combining Power Automate with Jira automation for routing, escalation and routine requests lets agents work inside Teams and focus on complex issues.

Worth quoting
