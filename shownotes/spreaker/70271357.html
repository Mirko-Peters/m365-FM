Azure environments drift away from their intended state the moment teams start moving fast, and AI agents now accelerate that erosion. This episode is for cloud architects and engineers who want to move from clicking in the portal to designing governance that scales. It shows how landing zones, Azure Policy, Entra Agent ID, CI/CD gates and drift detection turn governance into an accelerator and a career advantage.

Key takeaways

  • Governance that depends on humans to enforce it erodes at scale, so intent must be codified and enforced by the architecture itself.
  • Effective Azure governance combines identity, policy in deny or deploy-if-not-exists mode and operational enforcement through CI/CD gates, drift detection and cost controls.
  • AI agents need their own scoped identities, pre-execution gates and cost limits, because they amplify every governance gap at machine speed.

Worth quoting
