Your AI governance is built on binary rules, Copilot reasons in probabilities, and quantum computing will add a third logic entirely. This episode shows M365 governance and Azure architecture leaders why quantum-safe cryptography, crypto agility and hybrid governance gates have to be designed now, before quantum-classical workloads go live.

Key takeaways

  • Classical M365 governance is binary, Copilot and AI agents are probabilistic, and quantum adds superposition, so governance must be redesigned to span all three.
  • Harvest-now-decrypt-later makes long-lived M365 data vulnerable today, so organizations need a cryptography inventory, confidentiality horizons and a crypto agility roadmap.
  • Hybrid quantum-classical workloads need decision gates, quantum-safe identity and key management, end-to-end audit trails and portability before any pilot touches real data.
