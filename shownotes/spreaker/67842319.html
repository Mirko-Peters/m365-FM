Leadership waves a sign-in report and concludes Copilot is useless, but that chart only counts doors opened, not work done. This episode shows Microsoft 365 admins and adoption leads which telemetry really proves Copilot adoption and how to turn prompt and department data into a defensible ROI story.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Is Copilot really underused?
  • 00:33 – The CFO's report doesn't lie
  • 03:43 – Entra AD vs. Insights: two dashboards
  • 06:44 – Where Copilot clicks actually happen
  • 10:01 – Prompts: useful work or small talk
  • 13:18 – Departments, heroes and ghost towns
  • 17:29 – Closing playbook

Key takeaways

  • Entra sign-in reports only count who opened an app, so prove Copilot adoption with Insights telemetry on prompts, app activity and departmental usage.
  • Copilot usage concentrates in Outlook, Teams and Word for small repetitive tasks, so focus training and success stories on the top apps by prompt count.
  • Separate recurring prompt categories from novelty spikes and give each low-adoption department a tailored remediation plan instead of generic training.

Worth quoting

“Sign In counts show who opened the apps. Copilot adoption means showing who actually used prompts and actions.” – Mirko Peters (02:32)

“Copilot adoption grows fastest where the tool reduces friction in small, repetitive tasks, think cleaning up an email, fixing awkward grammar, or smoothing a team's message before you hit send.” – Mirko Peters (07:29)
