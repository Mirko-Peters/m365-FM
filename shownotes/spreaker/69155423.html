Row-level security works in the demo, yet your Fabric model can still leak through measures, calculated tables and shared datasets. This episode is for Power BI and Fabric architects who need governance that holds under deadline pressure. You get five failure modes and a deterministic playbook built on Entra, Purview, DLP and capacity isolation.

  • 59:02 – Why training fails without architecture

Key takeaways

  • DAX should only express analytics; any logic that changes who can see or infer data belongs to identity in Microsoft Entra, not to measures.
  • Business definitions, keys and cross-boundary benchmarks must live upstream with Purview labels and DLP that can actually block exports, blends and notebook writes.
  • Lineage and training are evidence and explanation, while enforced vetoes, enumerated Entra groups and capacity isolation are what keep Fabric models from drifting.

Worth quoting

“If a measure changes visibility, rows, columns, or classification, it is acting as an access control that belongs to identity, not DAX. If you remember nothing else, remember this. DAX must never decide who can see data identity must.” – Mirko Peters (02:53)

“Lineage is evidence, not assurance. It's post facto storytelling. It can tell you where data moved. It cannot tell you whether your policies were enforced when it moved” – Mirko Peters (11:34)
