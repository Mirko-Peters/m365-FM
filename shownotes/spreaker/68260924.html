Starting every Power App on SharePoint lists feels free, but it quietly creates sprawl, broken lookups and audit headaches. This episode helps Power Platform makers and IT decision-makers see when lists stop scaling and why Dataverse is the governed, often cheaper foundation for business-critical apps.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The convenience trap of SharePoint lists
  • 03:51 – Governance collapse: how data sprawl begins
  • 07:29 – Dataverse architecture: from data storage to governance engine
  • 13:31 – The cost illusion: why Dataverse saves money
  • 17:15 – Decision rules: when to use which and why
  • 20:50 – Conclusion: architecture is destiny

Key takeaways

  • SharePoint lists are fine for prototypes and static reference data but break down with relationships, delegation limits, view thresholds and fragmented ownership.
  • Dataverse brings relational integrity, environment separation, role-based and field-level security, and Purview DLP integration that make governance part of the platform.
  • Choose by user volume, data sensitivity, relational complexity and lifecycle: if the data outlives the project or is owned by a role, build it in Dataverse.

Worth quoting

“SharePoint lists were never designed to function as centralized business databases. They were meant for content tracking, not relationship enforcement or role based security” – Mirko Peters (02:26)

“In practice, data verse reduces total cost of ownership because governance is part of the product, not an afterthought.” – Mirko Peters (12:27)
