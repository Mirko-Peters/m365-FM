Many Microsoft 365 admins either lock down the tenant so tightly that users revolt or leave key protections switched off for fear of disruption. This episode is for admins and security leads who want strong security without a flood of Monday tickets. You will learn which Conditional Access, sharing, DLP, guest and audit settings protect your tenant while users barely notice.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The security setting everyone forgets: MFA through Conditional Access
  • 04:07 – Collaboration without leaks: sharing, sensitivity labels and DLP
  • 08:07 – The 80 percent fix: blocking legacy authentication
  • 12:03 – Guest access without regret
  • 16:30 – Visibility that actually matters: audit logs and alerts
  • 20:06 – Smart defaults that protect without friction

Key takeaways

  • Complex passwords are not enough; enforcing MFA through Conditional Access and blocking legacy authentication stops most credential-based attacks with little user friction.
  • Smart sharing defaults, sensitivity labels and clearly worded DLP policies prevent accidental oversharing in Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive without blocking work.
  • Expiration policies for guest accounts and a unified audit log with focused alert policies close long-term exposure and make real incidents visible.

Worth quoting

“Numbers vary, but the pattern is crystal clear. Password only defenses eventually fail no matter how strict the characters and symbols are.” – Mirko Peters (01:48)
