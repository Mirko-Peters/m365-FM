Teams keep migrating working SharePoint lists into Dataverse or Fabric just because Copilot is involved, paying for duplicated data and broken flows. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Power Platform makers who want to connect lists to Copilot Studio as live, permission-aware knowledge and know when migration is actually justified.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Modernization does not mean migration
  • 01:13 – The migration mirage
  • 04:41 – The SharePoint list connector in Copilot Studio
  • 08:49 – Governance without pain
  • 12:39 – Performance, scale and limits
  • 16:36 – Lists as living knowledge
  • 19:48 – The new data commandment

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Studio can now use SharePoint lists directly as knowledge sources, querying live data in the user's own security context without copying it into Dataverse or Fabric.
  • Because existing permissions, authentication and auditing are inherited, connecting lists avoids the extra governance surface, licensing and broken flows that migrations create.
  • Indexed, well-designed lists handle tens of thousands of items for conversational use; migrate only when you need high-frequency transactions, relational integrity or heavy analytics.
