Copilot Notebooks make it easy to summarize content from across Microsoft 365, but the output loses its labels, retention and lineage. This episode is for compliance, security and Microsoft 365 admins who need to keep AI-generated summaries governable. It explains where governance breaks and which policies and controls restore it.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The productivity savior that becomes a compliance risk
  • 01:16 – What Copilot Notebooks actually are
  • 03:19 – The moment governance breaks
  • 06:12 – The data lineage and compliance crisis
  • 10:02 – How to regain control
  • 16:02 – The future of AI governance in Microsoft 365
  • 20:20 – Complacency is the real risk

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Notebooks generate derivative content that does not inherit sensitivity labels, retention rules or lineage from its source documents.
  • Purview and DLP largely scan files rather than conversational output, so AI summaries can quietly form a shadow data lake outside compliance boundaries.
  • Default labeling, audit log monitoring, restricted sharing, expiration dates and derived-data policies bring notebook output back under governance.

Worth quoting

“It's not the AI response that's dangerous. It's the data trail that leaves behind the breadcrumb network no one is indexing.” – Mirko Peters (01:04)

“What breaks is inheritance. Governance assumes content lineage, AI assumes conceptual relevance. Those two logics are incompatible, So while your structure remains technically secure, it becomes legally incoherent” – Mirko Peters (05:41)
