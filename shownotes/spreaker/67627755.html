Many IT leaders still remember SharePoint as a confusing document dump with broken permissions. This episode shows admins and decision makers how modern SharePoint quietly powers Teams, OneDrive and intranets in Microsoft 365, and why its new governance model makes it manageable at scale.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Is SharePoint still a document dump?
  • 00:26 – The ghosts of old SharePoint
  • 04:34 – SharePoint's role in Microsoft 365
  • 09:02 – From document dump to productivity engine
  • 13:21 – Real-world wins with modern SharePoint
  • 17:17 – Governance and usability made manageable
  • 21:27 – SharePoint as the backbone

Key takeaways

  • Every team in Microsoft Teams is backed by a SharePoint site that stores its files, permissions and version history.
  • Modern SharePoint ties access to Microsoft 365 groups and enforces sensitivity labels, DLP and conditional access instead of fragile broken-inheritance permissions.
  • Organizations use modern SharePoint for onboarding portals, reporting hubs with embedded Power BI and controlled external sharing.

Worth quoting

“Teams provides the interface, the conversation and collaboration layer, SharePoint provides the structured, governed, and scalable content layer underneath.” – Mirko Peters (07:15)
