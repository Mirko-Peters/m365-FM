Deploying to two Azure regions does not make a system resilient if failover depends on a meeting, a healthy control plane or capacity you never reserved. This episode walks cloud architects through edge, DNS, control plane, data and governance failures in a regional outage and shows how to design systems that keep running.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Multi-region is not resilience
  • 00:36 – Edge failure when the front door goes dark
  • 03:36 – The DNS death spiral
  • 06:30 – Control plane degradation and the redeploy fallacy
  • 09:31 – State strategy
  • 12:27 – Governance and decision rights
  • 15:20 – Testing like you expect it to break
  • 17:38 – The level of preparation

Key takeaways

  • Global edge services and DNS are dependencies that need regional bypass paths, not invisible utilities.
  • Recovery plans that depend on redeploying through the control plane fail when everyone hits the same management APIs and capacity runs out, so pre-provision warm standbys.
  • Resilience comes from a deliberate state strategy, automated failover governance and regular chaos testing.

Worth quoting

“If your failover requires a manual meeting or a functioning control plane during a blackout, you do not have a plan. You have hope.” – Mirko Peters (00:16)
