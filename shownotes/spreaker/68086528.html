Your Copilot Studio agent looks perfect in the test pane, then fumbles in Teams and starts inventing policy answers. This episode is for makers building their first agents and shows how grounding, a defined persona, a tight debug loop and channel-by-channel testing turn a confident guesser into a bot that cites its sources.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – From natural one to natural twenty
  • 00:38 – Why your perfect test bot collapses in the wild
  • 03:43 – The rookie mistake: leaving your bot ungrounded
  • 07:20 – Teaching your bot its personality
  • 11:40 – Debugging the fumbles in real time
  • 14:58 – Publishing and channel surprises
  • 18:28 – Recap: ground, persona, pilot

Key takeaways

  • Test every topic with clean, casual and typo-ridden variations and broaden trigger phrases, because the Copilot Studio test pane is tidier than real users.
  • Ground the agent in trusted knowledge sources such as an uploaded policy file, wait for indexing and limit critical topics to those sources so conversational boosting cannot fill gaps with guesses.
  • Treat publishing as another test phase: retest in Teams, SharePoint and the demo website, run a closed pilot and watch analytics before a broad rollout.
