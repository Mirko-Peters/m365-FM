Most Microsoft 365 tenants already contain more identity, governance and automation capability than the third-party tools organizations keep paying for. This episode helps IT leaders and architects find that overlap across Entra ID, Purview, Power Platform and Copilot, and turn the savings into a deliberate capital reallocation instead of another budget cut.

  • 12:57 – Purview as risk and liability reducer
  • 20:12 – Power Platform and workflow debt
  • 28:10 – The Copilot efficiency gap
  • 35:59 – Identity governance maturity, Purview roadmap and CoE
  • 49:32 – Shadow IT, non-human identities and architectural erosion
  • 61:50 – Audit compression, capital reallocation and 2026

Key takeaways

  • Many organizations pay twice by licensing E5 capabilities like Entra ID, Purview and Power Automate while also paying third-party vendors for the same functions.
  • Copilot only delivers returns on top of consolidated identity, clean data and consistent DLP; otherwise it exposes architectural debt.
  • Quarterly policy reviews, automated drift detection and a Center of Excellence prevent exceptions from eroding the control plane, while freed budget should be reinvested deliberately.
