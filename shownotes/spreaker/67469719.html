Compliance checklists go stale the moment a regulation changes. This episode shows IT and compliance teams how to use Power Automate recurrence triggers, feedback loops and governance to build a living compliance system that keeps itself current.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Compliance as a never-ending checklist
  • 00:25 – Why static checklists fail
  • 04:22 – Recurrence triggers as the compliance engine
  • 08:18 – Feedback loops for adaptive compliance
  • 12:14 – Scaling without drowning in flows
  • 16:39 – Future-proofing with modular, metadata-driven design
  • 21:15 – Closing thoughts

Key takeaways

  • Static compliance checklists break as regulations change, so automation built around them only hardcodes the rigidity.
  • Recurrence triggers and logged results in SharePoint, Dataverse or Power BI turn compliance checks into a feedback loop that improves each cycle.
  • Naming conventions, centralized logging, templates and modular, metadata-driven flows keep compliance automation manageable as it scales.

Worth quoting

“Compliance can't be treated like a linear to do list with a set endpoint. Regulations form moving targets and addressing them requires movement in return.” – Mirko Peters (02:48)

“Risk doesn't appear only when an event happens. Sometimes the problem is in what didn't happen, like a policy review that never got done. If you wait for someone to act, compliance fails by default.” – Mirko Peters (05:04)
