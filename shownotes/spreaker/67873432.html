Model-driven apps are often dismissed as boring grey forms, yet they quietly win when you need a secure app in production fast. This episode is for Power Platform makers and admins weighing canvas against model-driven. You see how a single Dataverse table becomes a themed, role-secured and automated business app.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why everyone thinks model-driven apps are boring
  • 03:42 – Building from zero: tables, fields and relationships
  • 06:35 – From skeleton to app: forms, views and theming
  • 10:40 – The secret weapon: security at enterprise scale
  • 13:50 – Extending with Power Platform components
  • 17:10 – Boring by design

Key takeaways

  • Model-driven apps keep business logic in Dataverse, so they keep pace with schema changes instead of requiring constant patching like many canvas builds.
  • Security roles, field-level restrictions and Entra ID groups let you define access once in the data layer and have it respected everywhere.
  • Power Automate, Power BI and AI components extend a model-driven app cleanly, but test automations with scoped roles so they do not leak data.
