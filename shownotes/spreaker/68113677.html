Active Directory still holds the keys to logins, files and email in most organizations, and attackers know it. This episode is for IT admins and security teams running on-premises or hybrid identity. It walks through the attack paths from one stolen account to domain control and the three areas to fix first: admin blast radius, PKI templates and hybrid connector hygiene.

  • 10:51 – PKI and certificate templates
  • 15:46 – Hybrid identity as a ghost bridge
  • 19:57 – Three steps to start

Key takeaways

  • A single compromised user account can escalate to domain admin through lateral movement, cached credentials and pass-the-hash if Active Directory is left on defaults.
  • Reducing domain admins, enforcing a tiered least-privilege model and restricting service accounts removes the most common escalation paths.
  • Certificate templates and hybrid sync connectors need regular audits, logging and consistent MFA and Conditional Access, or they become hidden paths into both on-premises and cloud.
