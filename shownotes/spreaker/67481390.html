Teams holds your meetings, chats and files, yet decisions still get lost and people spend hours hunting for who committed to what. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins and team leads who want Copilot in Teams set up correctly, from licensing and transcription to pilot testing, and used to turn meetings into clear, actionable outcomes.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Copilot in Teams actually matters
  • 04:22 – What Copilot in Teams really is
  • 08:18 – Setting up Copilot in Teams without breaking workflows
  • 12:19 – How Copilot works once it is enabled
  • 16:19 – Going deeper: Planner, Power BI and compliance documentation
  • 20:36 – Decision support, not decision authority

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Teams only works when licensing, up-to-date clients, admin permissions and meeting transcription are all in place before launch.
  • Used during meetings with direct prompts, Copilot turns live conversations into structured summaries, action items and links to the relevant files.
  • Copilot should feed decisions and tools like Planner or Power BI, but people remain responsible for verifying commitments and making the final call.

Worth quoting
