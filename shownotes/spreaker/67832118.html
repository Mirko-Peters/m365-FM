Picking the wrong Teams channel type quietly exposes confidential files to the wrong people. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins, project leads and anyone who creates Teams channels. You learn when to use standard, private or shared channels and which governance guardrails keep collaboration safe.

In this episode

  • 09:10 – From guests to B2B: Microsoft's external collaboration makeover
  • 12:45 – Data governance without killing collaboration
  • 16:38 – Bottom line: channels are fences

Key takeaways

  • Standard channels expose files to every team member, private channels limit access to a selected subset, and shared channels let internal and external participants into one channel without joining the whole team.
  • Decide the channel type at project kickoff, restrict who can create channels and run regular access reviews to avoid sprawl and lingering access.
  • For external collaboration, configure cross-tenant access settings, conditional access and DLP, and protect content with sensitivity labels so security follows the file.
