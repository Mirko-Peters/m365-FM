One wrong Purview retention rule can lock files, bloat storage and flood your helpdesk with tickets. This episode is for information architects and Microsoft 365 admins who need compliance without chaos. Learn the IA guardrails and pilot plan that make Purview and Copilot work for you.

  • 08:20 – Search is only as smart as your IA
  • 12:00 – IA guardrails before you touch Purview
  • 16:09 – Pitfalls and what-if scenarios
  • 19:40 – Purview enforces the structure you give it

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Purview enforces retention and classification rules exactly as configured, so weak information architecture turns it into a blunt instrument.
  • Broad retention policies lock files, preserve junk and inflate storage, and some label settings are hard or impossible to roll back, so pilot in a small scope first.
  • Mapping content, enforcing naming and a few required metadata fields, cleaning up before lockdown and piloting make both Purview and Copilot more precise.
