Your Power Automate flows run green, yet the business still reacts too slowly. This episode is for Power Platform architects and automation owners who keep piling logic into master flows. Learn why event-first orchestration with Dataverse business events and API connectors cuts decision latency and makes automation easier to govern.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Automation debt and the model problem
  • 00:47 – Why the workflow model is breaking
  • 04:11 – Events as the API layer for the business
  • 07:28 – How Power Platform API connectors change the architecture
  • 10:57 – What to endorse and what to retire
  • 13:59 – Case: event-driven incident response
  • 17:09 – Starting the shift without breaking everything

Key takeaways

  • Workflows optimize individual steps, but business events optimize time, so design cross-system logic around named business moments instead of long master flows.
  • Dataverse business events and custom APIs let subscribers react in parallel to confirmed facts, which gives clearer ownership and smaller, testable handlers.
  • Start with one latency-sensitive decision path, back managed identities, event catalogs and event-based observability, and retire polling and mega-flows step by step.
