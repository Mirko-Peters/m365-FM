Many organizations bought Microsoft Fabric for AI but use OneLake as a dumping ground for CSV exports. This episode shows data and analytics teams how semantic models, Fabric data agents and built-in governance turn a passive warehouse into a system that reasons over data and flags issues before anyone opens a dashboard.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Your warehouse is a CSV graveyard
  • 00:48 – The dead data problem
  • 04:47 – Data agents: the missing intelligence layer
  • 09:29 – Resurrecting the warehouse with AI
  • 13:46 – Governance as the guardrail
  • 17:15 – The intelligent Fabric ecosystem
  • 21:34 – Fabric is an intelligence engine

Key takeaways

  • Dumping CSV snapshots into OneLake without semantic models or relationships leaves Fabric unable to support AI reasoning.
  • Fabric data agents, configured with semantic models, clear instructions and Copilot Studio integration, turn the warehouse into a system that detects trends and acts on them.
  • Purview, data loss prevention and lineage keep agents within governance boundaries so their conclusions stay traceable and auditable.

Worth quoting
