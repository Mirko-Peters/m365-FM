Power BI reports shared by link and PDF quietly create conflicting numbers, broken row-level security and stale workspaces nobody owns. This episode is for BI leads, report authors and Fabric admins who want trustworthy dashboards. It lays out a governance doctrine built on certified datasets, org apps, sensitivity labels, deployment pipelines and a 30-day migration plan.

  • 21:12 – Deployment pipelines as release liturgy
  • 25:04 – The 30-day migration from chaos to canon
  • 29:28 – The charge and the call

Key takeaways

  • Ad hoc share links and emailed PDFs break lineage, hide endorsements and spread conflicting numbers, so org apps should be the single doorway for report distribution.
  • Row-level and object-level security belong in the certified dataset, tested per persona in the service and kept identical across development, test and production.
  • Deployment pipelines with source control, automated checks, version notes and rollback, plus weekly stewardship of stale workspaces, keep dashboards truthful over time.

Worth quoting

“Hold this moral frame, data without stewardship becomes rumour, rumour erodes, trust and community. We do not gather to trade rumours.” – Mirko Peters (02:18)

“So we honor them at the source. We model roles at the data set. We do not patch filters in a report.” – Mirko Peters (06:50)
