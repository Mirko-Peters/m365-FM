Most Azure security failures start in identity, where Conditional Access exclusions, synced admins, overscoped service principals and forgotten guests quietly pile up as identity debt. This episode is for identity architects and security leaders who want to measure that drift in their own logs and replace it with a predictable loop. You get a minimal enforceable baseline and a 90-day remediation cadence.

Key takeaways

  • Identity debt is standing privilege plus ungoverned exceptions plus unowned identities, and Conditional Access will faithfully enforce whatever remains.
  • A minimal baseline of three policies (block legacy authentication, MFA for all, phishing-resistant strength for admins) plus PIM and tested break-glass accounts reduces blast radius this week.
  • Workload identities and guests need owners, scoped permissions, expiring credentials or access packages and access reviews, measured weekly through sign-in logs and Graph queries.
