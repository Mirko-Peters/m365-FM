Opened Microsoft Teams and your Teams tab is gone? This episode is for everyday users and IT admins confused by the new chat-first navigation. You learn where your teams and channels now live and how to build a structure that survives the next redesign.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The familiar Teams tab that vanished
  • 03:31 – Why Microsoft changed the system
  • 07:36 – Finding your teams in the new layout
  • 11:40 – Adapting workflows: pinning, tabs, notifications and shortcuts
  • 15:45 – Building a resilient structure for the future
  • 19:53 – Wrap-up: design for change, not for buttons

Key takeaways

  • Your teams and channels were not deleted in the new Teams interface; they were folded into a chat-first navigation and are reached through the Teams icon and a few extra clicks.
  • Pinning priority teams and channels, pinning tabs such as Planner or OneNote, tuning notifications and learning keyboard shortcuts restores a fast daily workflow.
  • Consistent naming conventions, clear channel usage rules, archiving inactive teams and pinned priorities make your environment resilient to future redesigns and ready for Copilot and Loop.

Worth quoting

“But here's the crucial point. Nothing actually disappeared. Your teams weren't deleted, your channels weren't archived, and none of the shared files vanished. They've simply been reorganized under a new framework.” – Mirko Peters (03:18)

“Instead of chasing interface changes, shift to what you might call a system's thinking mindset. The aim is to design your team's environment around principles resilient enough to outlast cosmetic updates.” – Mirko Peters (17:07)
