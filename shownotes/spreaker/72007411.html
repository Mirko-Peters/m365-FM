Copilot, agents, MCP and Copilot Cowork are changing how finance teams work in Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations faster than most can keep up. MVP and MCT Billur Samdancioglu explains for finance leaders and ERP consultants which AI tool fits which job and how to govern it responsibly. Learn why vibe coding is not vibe engineering and why humans still drive.

Key takeaways

  • In Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations, built-in agents, Copilot Studio agents and Copilot Cowork serve different purposes, so teams must know which tool fits which task.
  • AI in finance needs governance: environment strategy, correct context and knowledge sources, split agent workflows and continuous monitoring against data leakage.
  • AI supports but does not replace human decisions; finance professionals stay in the driver's seat and evolve their roles rather than lose them.
