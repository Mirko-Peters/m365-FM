Many Power BI rollouts produce colourful dashboards that change no decisions. This episode shows BI leads, analysts and project owners the three non-negotiable steps of scope, data quality and governance that turn Power BI projects into reliable decision tools.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The illusion of successful failure
  • 01:32 – Step one: Define and contain scope
  • 06:20 – Step two: Secure data quality and consistency
  • 11:10 – Step three: Implement governance from day one
  • 16:58 – The integrated blueprint
  • 20:38 – Common mistakes and quick wins
  • 21:58 – The non-negotiable mindset

Key takeaways

  • Most Power BI projects fail because of missing planning, not because of DAX or visuals: scope, data quality and governance must come first.
  • Contain scope creep with a decision-focused requirements workshop, a written scoping template and formal change requests for every new wish.
  • Treat data quality as infrastructure and establish governance with roles, row-level security, life cycle policies and documentation from day one.

Worth quoting

“It comes from planning discipline, specifically, three non negotiable steps define and contain scope, secure data quality, and implement governance from day one.” – Mirko Peters (01:19)

“Start by asking one deceptively simple question, what decisions should this report enable? Not what data you have, but what business question needs answering. Every metric should trace back to that question.” – Mirko Peters (02:57)
