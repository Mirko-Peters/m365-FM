The Cloud Adoption Framework looks like six clean stages, yet most cloud journeys stall at strategy and planning. This episode shows cloud architects, IT leaders and finance stakeholders how to write measurable strategies, build CFO-ready business cases and avoid the scope creep and ownership gaps that create hidden governance debt.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Six clean CAF stages, real-world breaks
  • 00:35 – The strategy stage nobody reads twice
  • 04:22 – A business case CFOs believe
  • 08:01 – Strategy that dies in SharePoint
  • 11:17 – Scope creep and world hunger plans
  • 14:57 – Why people, not tech, derail CAF
  • 19:11 – Takeaways and one next action

Key takeaways

  • Make cloud strategy measurable by tying every outcome to a named workload, an Azure service, a metric and a review cadence.
  • A business case finance will sign needs real numbers from Azure Migrate and the TCO calculator, cost levers like reserved instances and Azure Hybrid Benefit, a break-even date and a named owner for overruns.
  • Keep scope narrow with phased pilots and fix ownership early through a cloud center of excellence, because people and turf wars derail CAF more often than technology.

Worth quoting
