Most organizations think AWS is winning the cloud war, but they are looking at the wrong battlefield. This episode is for CIOs and cloud architects running hybrid and multi-cloud estates who need to decide where identity, policy and compliance should live. You learn why the enterprise control plane has shifted to Microsoft and what that means for your architecture and licensing choices.

  • 35:48 – Hybrid, licensing, regulated industries and sovereign cloud
  • 50:56 – CIO's dilemma, AWS's role and the 2028 prediction

Key takeaways

  • AWS still leads in raw infrastructure, but enterprise control has moved to identity, policy and governance, where Entra ID and Conditional Access give Microsoft the control plane.
  • Defender for Cloud, Sentinel, Purview and Azure Arc project one governance layer across AWS, Azure and on-premises, so hybrid estates are governed from Microsoft regardless of where workloads run.
  • Copilot, Entra Kerberos, enterprise agreements and regulated or sovereign requirements deepen this dependence, making a unified control plane the business choice over best-of-breed tools.

Worth quoting
