Your Fabric dataflow refreshed this morning, but will it survive the next schema change? This episode is for data engineers and Fabric admins tired of fragile, hardcoded pipelines. Learn how modular design, parameters, Delta Lake, partitioning and incremental refresh turn disposable dataflows into durable assets.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – From lakehouse to swamp
  • 00:40 – Why your working dataflow is a time bomb
  • 04:18 – The three deadly sins of dataflow design
  • 07:45 – Modularity and parameterization
  • 11:15 – Taming the lakehouse with Delta Lake
  • 15:35 – Partitioning and incremental refresh done right
  • 18:40 – Three habits for resilient dataflows

Key takeaways

  • A dataflow that refreshes today is not necessarily reusable; schema drift, hardcoded values and spaghetti logic make it fragile.
  • Modular Power Query M functions and centralized parameters turn one-off dataflows into reusable building blocks, while heavy shared logic belongs in the lakehouse or notebooks.
  • Landing data in Delta Lake and applying sensible partitioning and incremental refresh keep Fabric pipelines trustworthy, auditable and fast.

Worth quoting

“Your data flow can look fine today and still be hanging by a thread. Most people assume that if it refreshes without error, it's done.” – Mirko Peters (00:44)

“Every complex transform should be a named function with a clear input output signature and a single parameter back. If you can't explain it in one sentence, split it.” – Mirko Peters (08:43)
