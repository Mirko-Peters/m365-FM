Shared support mailboxes let cases go cold, get misrouted and depend on a few heroic agents. This episode is for Dynamics 365 Customer Service teams and admins who want to replace inbox triage with email to case, unified routing and Copilot Studio. You get real case examples, a demo walkthrough and an eight-step blueprint to make your inbox boring again.

  • 05:17 – Three operators: scanner, controller, shadow operator
  • 10:30 – Case files: retail, insurance, HR
  • 17:00 – The noir demo
  • 22:25 – The eight-step blueprint
  • 27:43 – Under the hood: the district map

Key takeaways

  • Email to case turns incoming support emails into structured case records automatically, so nothing waits in a shared mailbox for a human to pick it up.
  • Unified routing assigns cases by rules, skills, capacity and SLA urgency instead of personal preference, and routing diagnostics show why each case landed where it did.
  • Copilot Studio wired into the case form drafts sourced replies from a curated knowledge base, while a human still reviews and sends.
