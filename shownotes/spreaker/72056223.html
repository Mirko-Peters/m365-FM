Many companies jump on the AI hype train without a real use case, a security foundation or a plan for governance. Security and AI MVP Jannik Reinhard shows developers and IT architects how he builds enterprise AI apps and agents with Microsoft Foundry, App Service and AI Search. Learn practical architecture, orchestration and governance patterns you can try today.

  • 40:00 – The future of developers and final advice

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Foundry lets enterprises deploy models from multiple vendors inside Microsoft's security boundary and add agents, search and content filtering with little effort.
  • A simple architecture of a containerized app on App Service connected to Foundry, plus AI Search for retrieval, covers most enterprise AI app scenarios.
  • Good orchestration that feeds models only the context they need improves quality and lowers token costs more than adding ever more MCP tools.
