Many Microsoft 365 rollouts in critical infrastructure and government are non-compliant with BSI requirements before the first user signs in. This episode is for IT leaders and compliance teams in regulated organizations and shows how planning, identity architecture, data governance and the right extensions turn M365 into an audit-ready platform.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why regulated M365 rollouts fail
  • 04:29 – Three planning phases of a compliant rollout
  • 08:43 – Identity as the first real test
  • 13:01 – Data governance and security baselines
  • 17:44 – Closing the gaps with external tooling
  • 22:08 – Compliance as an ongoing discipline

Key takeaways

  • Platform certifications from Microsoft do not make a customer tenant compliant; under the shared responsibility model, configuration, governance and documentation for BSI stay with the organization.
  • Strategy, architecture and governance must be planned before the first license is assigned, because audit failures in KRITIS environments typically surface within the first ninety days.
  • Identity with full MFA and risk-based conditional access is the compliance foundation, and certified third-party tools are often needed to close logging, residency and reporting gaps that Microsoft 365 does not cover natively.
