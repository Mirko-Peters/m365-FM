Many teams expect Microsoft 365 Copilot to know policies and SOPs it cannot see, and then pay in rework and lost trust. This episode helps IT and AI leads decide when Copilot is enough and when they need their own retrieval-augmented generation pipeline. It walks through how RAG works in Azure and a manufacturing case study that cut service desk load.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot is the right tool for drafts, summaries and quick lookups inside Microsoft 365, within the user's existing permissions.
  • When answers depend on proprietary data outside Microsoft 365 or must be cited and repeatable, a RAG pipeline with chunking, embeddings and Azure AI Search is needed.
  • RAG quality comes from data discipline - metadata, re-indexing, mandatory citations, don't-know behavior and continuous evaluation - not from retraining the model.
