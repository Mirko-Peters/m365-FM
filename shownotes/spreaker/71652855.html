Many Power Platform Centers of Excellence promise agility but deliver months-long review backlogs, environment sprawl and orphaned flows. Based on audits of ten CoEs, this episode shows platform owners and IT leaders the five failure patterns and how to move to an automated control plane with policy as code.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The CoE as a nightclub
  • 00:43 – Governance as documentation
  • 03:41 – The manual review bottleneck
  • 06:41 – Environment sprawl and the dev in prod myth
  • 09:54 – Orphaned flows and ownership decay
  • 12:49 – Vanity metrics vs. business impact
  • 16:00 – From gatekeeper to control plane

Key takeaways

  • Governance that lives only in documents fails; enforce rules in the platform through DLP policies and policy as code.
  • Replace manual approval boards and manual environment requests with automated triage, environment zones and enforced ownership tied to Entra ID.
  • Measure the CoE by business impact such as manual hours saved, not by the number of makers or flows.
