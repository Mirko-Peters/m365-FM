Every Microsoft 365 compliance dashboard is green, yet the data you think you retain and can discover may already be gone. This episode is for records managers, eDiscovery leads and compliance owners who want to see how versioning, cleanup and discovery scope quietly drift behind enabled retention policies. You get a playbook of variance signals and alerts that show whether governance still matches reality.

Key takeaways

  • Intelligent versioning with co-authoring and autosave preserves fewer near-term versions than edits, so retention keeps versions, not every edit.
  • Short-lived content such as meeting recordings, temp exports and OneDrive spillover is often deleted before retention labels arrive, so governance succeeds on a reduced set.
  • Track three variance signals (creation ratio, survival hit rate, discovery coverage ratio) and treat flat dashboards in a growing tenant as incidents until explained.
