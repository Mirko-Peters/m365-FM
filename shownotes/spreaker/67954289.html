Copilot in Business Central is more than the canned demos, but extending it badly can break a tenant or leak API keys. This episode walks Business Central developers and admins through registering capabilities, writing meta prompts, securing Azure OpenAI keys and piloting features safely. You get a five-step checklist to try in a sandbox.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden Copilot menu
  • 04:08 – Registering capabilities safely
  • 08:10 – Meta prompts: teaching your AI manners
  • 12:07 – Guarding the keys
  • 16:18 – Wiring the workflow
  • 20:16 – Wrap-up

Key takeaways

  • Business Central exposes Copilot extension points in the System AI namespace, so developers can register their own capabilities that admins control on the Copilot and Agent Capabilities page.
  • Meta prompts act as standing instructions that define tone, format and guardrails, combined with low temperatures for deterministic finance outputs.
  • Never hard-code Azure OpenAI keys: use isolated storage and SecretText for development and Azure Key Vault in production.

Worth quoting
