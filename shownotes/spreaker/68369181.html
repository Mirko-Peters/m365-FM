Microsoft's SharePoint Knowledge Agent promises to organize content, build rules and answer questions, but it cannot replace the judgment of a SharePoint admin. This episode shows admins and governance leads where the agent helps, where it creates duplicate columns, fragile rules and risky answers, and how to contain it.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Can AI run SharePoint?
  • 01:12 – Sales pitch vs reality
  • 03:12 – Auto-tagging and metadata suggestions
  • 08:10 – Natural language rules
  • 12:06 – Asking questions of your data
  • 15:57 – Metadata inflation and AI drift
  • 19:04 – Verdict and survival guide

Key takeaways

  • The SharePoint Knowledge Agent can suggest metadata, create simple rules and answer questions, but every change still needs human review.
  • Natural-language rules and auto-created columns let users extend the schema without oversight, which leads to metadata inflation and AI drift that degrade Copilot results.
  • Confine the agent to pilot libraries, monitor column and rule creation centrally, and train users to treat every saved change as a schema decision.

Worth quoting
