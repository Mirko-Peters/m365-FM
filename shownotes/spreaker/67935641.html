Messy sensor, ERP and HR data have made digital twins brittle and expensive. This episode is for Fabric admins and data teams and explains how Microsoft Fabric's Digital Twin Builder uses an ontology, OneLake and Real-Time Intelligence to turn data chaos into trusted, real-time insights.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Digital Twin Builder: answer or data swamp?
  • 00:37 – What a digital twin is and why it matters
  • 04:44 – Low code: the promise and the catch
  • 08:11 – Ontology and the semantic canvas
  • 11:29 – Mapping data chaos into the twin
  • 14:51 – Dashboards, real-time streams and AI
  • 18:29 – Bottom line for admins

Key takeaways

  • Digital Twin Builder in Microsoft Fabric replaces fragile ETL pipelines with an ontology layered over data stored in OneLake.
  • The semantic canvas lets subject matter experts model namespaces, types and instances with low code, but clean sources, mapping discipline and governance are still required.
  • Because twin data lives in OneLake, it feeds Power BI and Real-Time Intelligence directly, enabling live alerts via Eventstream, KQL and Activator plus predictive models on top.
