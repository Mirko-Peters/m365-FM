Data engineers and Fabric admins watching simple queries crawl while cloud costs climb will learn the three settings behind most slow Lakehouses. The episode shows how smarter partitioning, targeted caching and Delta Lake compaction turn slow dashboards into fast, predictable analytics.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Lakehouse dashboards crawl
  • 04:59 – Partitioning pitfalls: the cost of one size fits all
  • 09:59 – Cache or crash: why memory matters
  • 15:20 – Delta Lake compaction as the secret weapon
  • 19:57 – Linking all three levers together

Key takeaways

  • Partition keys in a Fabric Lakehouse should follow real query filters, because both under- and over-partitioning multiply scan cost and query time.
  • Default caching treats all data equally, so reviewing query history and pinning hot tables keeps critical dashboards fast and compute costs stable.
  • Frequent small loads fragment Delta Lake tables into thousands of files, and condition-based compaction restores read performance without new hardware.

Worth quoting
