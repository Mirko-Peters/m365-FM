Are your Power Apps a patchwork of misaligned buttons and layouts that break on mobile? This episode is for Power Apps makers and Power Platform governance teams who want professional, consistent apps. Learn how containers and component libraries deliver responsive, on-brand design without positioning formulas.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why most Power Apps look amateur
  • 00:42 – The cost of layout chaos
  • 02:51 – Containers: the physics of layout
  • 08:22 – Component libraries: branding on autopilot
  • 13:22 – Building a responsive, brand-perfect app
  • 19:38 – Scaling citizen developers without chaos
  • 22:10 – The elegant discipline

Key takeaways

  • Horizontal and vertical containers replace fragile X and Y formulas, so layouts adapt automatically across desktop, tablet and phone.
  • A shared component library centralizes headers, buttons and branding, so one update propagates consistent design and accessibility across every app.
  • Containers, component libraries and a Center of Excellence turn citizen development into governed creativity that scales without rework.

Worth quoting

“Without containers, power apps devolve into art projects. Makers align controls by eye and then glue them in place with fragile X and Y formulas” – Mirko Peters (01:10)

“You position objects through intent, start, middle end, rather than by chance. You don't drag things, you define relationships.” – Mirko Peters (04:56)
