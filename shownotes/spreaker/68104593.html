Attackers no longer break through firewalls; they log in with stolen credentials. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Entra ID administrators who want to secure the identity perimeter. It explains how MFA, Conditional Access, PIM and SSO work together and which first steps to take today.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The unprotected identity perimeter
  • 00:36 – The new castle walls
  • 03:45 – Why passwords alone fail
  • 06:53 – Conditional Access as the smart bouncer
  • 10:05 – Admin privileges and PIM
  • 13:53 – Convenience without cracks: SSO
  • 18:02 – Practical first steps

Key takeaways

  • In the Microsoft cloud, identity in Entra ID has replaced the firewall as the primary security perimeter.
  • MFA blocks most attacks based on stolen passwords, and Conditional Access adds context such as device compliance, location and risk to every sign-in.
  • Privileged Identity Management with just-in-time activation and access reviews removes standing admin rights, while SSO with MFA reduces password fatigue.
