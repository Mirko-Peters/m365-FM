Data scientists and analysts whose AI experiments have outgrown laptops and CSV exports will learn how to train models directly on Lakehouse data in Microsoft Fabric notebooks. The episode covers direct data access, feature engineering at scale and monitoring long training runs.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – When big data outgrows your laptop
  • 04:03 – Direct Lakehouse access: no more CSV chaos
  • 07:38 – Feature engineering and model selection at scale
  • 11:31 – Monitoring and evaluating cloud-scale training
  • 15:25 – Training right next to your data

Key takeaways

  • Local laptops and CSV extracts break down on multi-terabyte datasets, while Fabric notebooks run training right next to the Lakehouse data.
  • Spark-based processing in Fabric lets you aggregate in place and engineer lean, well-chosen features instead of feeding hundreds of raw columns into a model.
  • Real-time job monitoring, checkpoints, model versioning and distributed evaluation keep long cloud training runs visible, recoverable and cost-efficient.

Worth quoting
