Microsoft 365 Copilot cannot see your CRM pipeline, your ticket queue or your internal APIs, so it guesses. This episode is for makers, developers and admins who want to build a custom agent grounded in Salesforce, ServiceNow and their own REST API. You learn when to use Copilot Studio, when to use Teams Toolkit, and how to keep identity, DLP and audit under control.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Copilot is blind to your systems
  • 00:35 – How Copilot sees: knowledge and tools
  • 03:13 – Path one: Copilot Studio
  • 08:26 – Path two: Teams Toolkit for VS Code
  • 14:24 – Head-to-head demo
  • 17:59 – Enterprise constraints
  • 21:56 – Build-along in Copilot Studio
  • 23:57 – Decision framework

Key takeaways

  • Copilot only sees what it is grounded on, so data in Salesforce, ServiceNow or internal APIs stays invisible until you add knowledge sources and tools with a governed access path.
  • Copilot Studio gives makers fast, governed orchestration with connectors and citations, while Teams Toolkit gives pro developers strict input validation, minimal response schemas and policy in code.
  • Licensing, admin approvals, DLP environments, conditional access, least-privilege scopes and logging decide whether an enterprise agent survives beyond the demo.

Worth quoting

“The truth is simpler and harsher. Copilot sees what it's grounded on. Nothing more, nothing less. Out of the box, its field of view is your Microsoft 365 work graph” – Mirko Peters (00:41)
