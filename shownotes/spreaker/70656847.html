Your audit found hundreds of orphaned SharePoint sites and unclassified data, and now the Copilot rollout is on hold. This episode is for IT leaders, architects and governance teams who need to move from not ready to ready enough. Learn how parallel-track governance with SharePoint Advanced Management and Purview lets you deploy Copilot while your governance improves.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Governance as a gate versus a track
  • 03:59 – How 847 sites went unmanaged
  • 10:52 – Track one: rapid triage with Purview and SAM
  • 18:40 – Track two: scoped deployment in clean zones
  • 30:06 – SharePoint Advanced Management as automation layer
  • 38:47 – Copilot does not bypass permissions
  • 50:59 – Deterministic versus probabilistic governance
  • 67:18 – Common objections and how to answer them

Key takeaways

  • Pausing a Copilot rollout to fix governance first defers value while orphaned sites, unclassified data and permission drift keep growing.
  • Automated controls such as SharePoint Advanced Management ownership policies and Purview auto-labeling make governance deterministic instead of dependent on user behavior.
  • Run remediation and a scoped Copilot deployment in parallel tracks, advancing phases only when predefined adoption, labeling and remediation metrics are met.

Worth quoting

“This is a critical distinction. Co-pilot does not create risk. It reveals existing risk posture.” – Mirko Peters (40:20)
