Executives keep asking for every KPI on one page, and teams keep building dashboards that get ignored two weeks later. This episode is for BI leads, architects and finance or IT leaders who want to turn KPIs into enforced decision rules on the Microsoft stack. You will learn how to cut decision latency with triggers, owners, actions, time windows and a decision ledger.

  • 47:11 – Scenario: revenue forecast variance
  • 56:03 – Scenario: IT incident SLA
  • 64:56 – Where AI belongs and Monday-morning principles

Key takeaways

  • A KPI only earns leadership attention when it carries an obligation: a precise trigger, one accountable owner, a pre-committed action, a time window and a feedback loop.
  • Build the decision stack in layers: converged data in Fabric and OneLake, governed meaning in the semantic model, decision state in Dataverse, enforcement with Power Automate and access through Copilot Studio.
  • Keep the decision core deterministic and use AI only for explanation, summarization and option generation, never for triggering escalations or changing operational state.
