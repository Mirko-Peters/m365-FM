Leaders keep asking whether Microsoft 365 Copilot is worth the license cost. This episode is for decision makers and adoption leads who need a realistic ROI picture. It walks through Forrester's Total Economic Impact model across sales, operations, people and payback, and shows how to test the numbers in your own organization.

  • 04:02 – Go-to-market: the sales engine upgrade

  • 11:07 – People and culture: ROI through humans
  • 15:11 – The payback clock
  • 18:34 – Test it yourself

Key takeaways

  • Forrester's risk-adjusted composite model shows Microsoft 365 Copilot returns through reclaimed hours, higher sales win rates and faster onboarding, but the figures are averages, not guarantees.
  • Total cost includes far more than licenses: implementation, IT staff and especially training drive the budget, while phased, role-based rollouts and good data governance speed up payback.
  • Measure your own baseline for one repetitive task per role for two weeks, then use Copilot consistently on it and compare the hours to build a local ROI case.
