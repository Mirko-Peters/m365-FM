Users type the exact document title into your intranet search and still find nothing? This episode is for SharePoint intranet owners, communicators and admins who want content people and Copilot can actually find. Learn how navigation, metadata, flat hub-based sites and audience targeting fix findability.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why intranet search fails
  • 00:36 – The six core elements of information architecture
  • 03:51 – Global, hub and local navigation
  • 07:33 – Metadata: the magic runes of search
  • 10:55 – Flat sites and hubs beat nested subsites
  • 14:14 – Personalization and audience targeting
  • 17:42 – Wrap-up: three checks to run

Key takeaways

  • Intranet search fails because of weak information architecture: global, hub and local navigation, metadata, search and personalization must work together.
  • Consistent metadata through columns, content types and the term store, plus a flat site structure connected by hubs, is what makes search and Copilot find the right content.
  • Audience targeting and Viva Connections deliver the right content to the right people, and IA should be tested and refined continuously with card sorting, tree testing and search logs.
