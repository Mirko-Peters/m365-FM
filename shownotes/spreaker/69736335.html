Green dashboards and full MFA coverage still do not stop identity-driven breaches. This episode is for security and IT leaders who need to move from buying controls to engineering trust, and shows how Entra governance, continuous access evaluation and Defender-to-ServiceNow automation cut the time attackers get to operate.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why well-secured organizations still get breached
  • 04:15 – From prevention fantasy to resilience
  • 08:01 – Identity is the control plane
  • 20:31 – Scenario 1: Entra governance plus ITDR
  • 32:55 – Scenario 3: Continuous access evaluation
  • 53:54 – Scenario 2: Defender and ServiceNow automation
  • 62:26 – The leadership metric: MTTR
  • 75:05 – Executive checklist

Key takeaways

  • Security controls create coverage, not resilience; breaches happen when trust models drift and authorization, not authentication, fails.
  • Identity is the control plane: Entra governance, ITDR and continuous access evaluation make access time-bound, owned and revocable inside the session.
  • Leaders should measure and reduce MTTR for identity-driven incidents by pre-approving reversible containment actions and connecting Defender signals to ServiceNow workflows.

Worth quoting

“Prevention matters. Nobody is arguing for neglect. But prevention is probability management. It reduces the likelihood of an incident. Resilience is impact management.” – Mirko Peters (04:28)

“People think governance is about saying no. It isn't. Governance is about making yes, safe. It creates predictable pathways for access, so the business doesn't need informal workarounds, shared accounts, or permanent exceptions.” – Mirko Peters (17:14)
