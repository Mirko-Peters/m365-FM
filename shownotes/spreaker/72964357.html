Old desk phone systems are expensive, rigid and tied to one location. This episode explains Teams Telephony in plain English for small businesses and IT decision-makers: what it is, how it connects to the phone network, what it really costs and which features replace a traditional PBX.

In this episode

  • 02:06 – Replacing the traditional PBX
  • 04:05 – Three ways to connect
  • 06:43 – Licensing and costs
  • 08:51 – Features that matter
  • 11:55 – Security and reliability
  • 14:05 – How it connects and next steps

Key takeaways

  • Teams Telephony is a cloud PBX built into Microsoft Teams that ties your phone number to you instead of a desk.
  • You need a Teams Phone license plus one of three connection options: Microsoft Calling Plans, Operator Connect or Direct Routing.
  • Check your existing license, get a full quote including calling minutes and support, and start with a small pilot group.

Worth quoting

“Teams Telephony is that same switchboard, but it lives in Microsoft's cloud and follows you everywhere. Your phone number is no longer tied to a physical location. It's tied to you.” – Mirko Peters (00:57)
