Copilot training decks and webinars go stale while users keep filing the same tickets and copying unofficial guides. This episode shows Microsoft 365 adoption and governance teams how to build a governed Copilot Learning Center on SharePoint and Teams that answers questions where they occur and proves its value with KPIs.

  • 17:56 – KPI playbook

Key takeaways

  • Copilot changes weekly, so one-off webinars and decks decay into shadow training; adoption needs an evergreen, governed learning center with owners, lifecycle and telemetry.
  • Build it as a SharePoint hub with SPFx web parts, PnP Modern Search, a role-based prompt library, an agent directory and policy guidance surfaced in Teams through Viva Connections.
  • Make the hub the single authoritative entry point with redirects, warning banners and auto-archive rules, and track ticket deflection, search success, prompt reuse and stale content rate.
