Copilot may be surfacing board minutes and financial data to users who should never see them, and nobody notices. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins and compliance teams rolling out Copilot. Learn how to scope Graph permissions, extend DLP and sensitivity labels to AI output and use Purview to prove your Copilot deployment is under control.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden risk of over-permissioned Copilot
  • 00:34 – When Copilot knows too much: Graph permissions and scope drift
  • 04:47 – Finding leaks with Purview audit and access reviews
  • 08:57 – Guardrails that work: DLP and sensitivity labels for AI output
  • 13:12 – Role-based Copilot access groups with Entra ID
  • 17:41 – Proving compliance with Purview reporting
  • 21:54 – Why governed AI is smarter business

Key takeaways

  • Copilot's reach is defined by the Graph permissions it has been granted, and broad application permissions can expose data far beyond what the signed-in user should see.
  • DLP policies and sensitivity labels must explicitly cover Copilot prompts and responses, otherwise AI-generated content slips past traditional rules.
  • Role-based access groups in Entra ID combined with Purview auditing and reporting let you limit Copilot per role and prove compliance to auditors.

Worth quoting
