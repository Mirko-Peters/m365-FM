AI agents can build and refactor your Power BI and Fabric models at machine speed, but the numbers may quietly start answering different questions. This episode is for BI architects, data platform owners and governance leads who want agent speed without losing semantic truth. You learn where drift starts and a four-gate governance model that keeps meaning owned, verified and auditable.

  • 24:01 – Auditability and permission drift
  • 31:38 – The four governance gates
  • 51:57 – Where agents belong

Key takeaways

  • AI agents editing Power BI and Fabric models produce architectural drift: definitions change quietly while reports keep working.
  • Measures, relationships, PBIR report files and MCP tool access are the main drift vectors, and logs alone cannot prove the intent behind a change.
  • Four gates — intent mapping, containment, semantic verification and attested release — let agents automate repetition without redefining business meaning.

Worth quoting

“A measure is a contract. It defines a business concept under filter context when that definition changes even by optimization, even by refactoring, even by helpful AI cleanup you've changed the contract.” – Mirko Peters (01:21)

“This is what hallucination looks like in BI. Not making up a number out of thin air. Making up the definition that produces the number.” – Mirko Peters (06:23)
