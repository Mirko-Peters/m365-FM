If your Power BI dashboards still wait for overnight refreshes, your skills may no longer cover what leaders expect. This episode shows BI professionals how Microsoft Fabric, OneLake and KQL databases widen their role, and how to start with a sandbox tenant and small experiments.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The ground shifted for Power BI pros
  • 00:34 – Hidden limits of traditional Power BI
  • 03:54 – Why Fabric isn't just another tool
  • 07:31 – Provisioning a Fabric tenant
  • 11:17 – OneLake: one shared data foundation
  • 15:56 – Real-time analytics with KQL databases

Key takeaways

  • Scheduled-refresh Power BI reporting no longer meets expectations for live operational analytics.
  • Microsoft Fabric puts Power BI on a unified platform with OneLake, pipelines, warehouses and lakehouses, so BI pros can shape data upstream.
  • A developer or trial tenant lets you test OneLake datasets, Direct Lake and KQL queries against streams in a safe sandbox.
