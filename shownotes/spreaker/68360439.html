Analysts lose hours every week fixing dates, trailing spaces and mismatched labels before any real analysis can start. This episode is for Excel users and data teams who feed Power BI and Power Automate. It shows how Copilot in Excel can normalize, validate and transform data with a few prompts so cleanup stops being manual work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Analysis or janitorial work?
  • 01:14 – Why Excel is a chaos factory
  • 04:33 – Copilot as Excel's AI janitor
  • 07:50 – Three commands that end manual cleanup
  • 13:52 – From clean data to smart data
  • 18:17 – The end of the manual phase
  • 21:41 – Efficiency by design

Key takeaways

  • Excel's flexibility without schema or validation is the root of inconsistent data that breaks Power BI, Power Automate and Fabric downstream.
  • Copilot in Excel only edits the sheet when you move beyond chat and give it clear commands to normalize, validate and transform data.
  • Embedding Copilot prompts at the start of every dataset's lifecycle turns cleanup into an automatic preflight check and frees analysts for real analysis.

Worth quoting
