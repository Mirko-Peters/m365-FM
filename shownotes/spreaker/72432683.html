Copilot and AI agents can reach any data your permissions allow, which turns weak data governance into a real exposure risk. In this interview, Microsoft partner solution architect Peter Rising explains to IT, security and compliance leaders how Purview, Zero Trust and defense in depth make AI adoption safe. You get a practical starting point covering sensitivity labels, DLP, retention and oversharing controls.

In this episode

  • 07:14 – How AI changed the security conversation
  • 09:36 – Zero Trust principles in practice
  • 18:28 – Purview core features and evolution
  • 28:41 – Copilot governance and oversharing
  • 40:37 – Agents and the future of security operations
  • 53:47 – Quick-fire round and closing

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft provides the security platform, but configuring Purview, Defender and Entra correctly remains the customer's responsibility, ideally with an experienced partner.
  • Sensitivity labels, DLP and data lifecycle management are the three Purview basics every organization should implement before rolling out Copilot.
  • Copilot and Agent 365 agents are only as safe as the data and identities behind them, so tackle oversharing first and apply Zero Trust and defense in depth.
