Conditional Access policies quietly leak trust through permanent exclusions, fuzzy device signals and long-lived tokens that attackers can reuse. This episode is for Microsoft Entra and Intune admins who want a calmer baseline: fewer inclusive policies, authentication contexts instead of exclusions, a safe report-only rollout and monitoring that catches bypasses early.

In this episode

  • 12:54 – A calming baseline of five policies

  • 20:29 – Monitoring, KPIs and alerts
  • 25:05 – Takeaway

Key takeaways

  • Permanent exclusions for VIPs, partner domains or trusted locations become invisible bypasses; replace them with inclusive policies and a time-bound, approved authentication context for emergencies.
  • Treat device states as distinct tiers, use filters for devices with authentication strengths as a fallback, and shorten token exposure with sign-in frequency, continuous access evaluation and phishing-resistant MFA for sensitive apps.
  • Roll changes out in report-only waves with a rollback plan, then monitor coverage and strength KPIs plus alerts for coverage drops, exclusion changes and high-risk sign-ins without a Conditional Access decision.

Worth quoting

“Move from exclude VIPs to include all and authorize exceptions through time-bound authentication context. That shift sets healthy boundaries.” – Mirko Peters (01:45)
