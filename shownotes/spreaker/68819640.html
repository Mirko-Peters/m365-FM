Many organizations run memberships, committees and partner programs in spreadsheets and shadow databases while already paying for Dynamics 365 Sales. This episode shows Power Platform and Dynamics makers how to remodel the CRM into a membership hub with a clean data model, a membership lifecycle and simplified forms, without building a custom system.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Dynamics 365 Sales as a membership platform
  • 01:16 – The platform advantage over custom solutions
  • 04:12 – Membership, committee and partner scenarios without pipelines
  • 08:25 – Data modeling: from CRM to a membership graph
  • 13:34 – From pipeline stages to the membership lifecycle
  • 18:19 – UI and form remodeling
  • 26:54 – Governance and scalability
  • 30:23 – Pitfalls to avoid and the real value

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Sales runs on Dataverse, so accounts, contacts, timelines, security and integrations can be renamed and reshaped into organizations, members and memberships without custom software.
  • Model memberships, committee assignments and partner participations as bridge tables and keep identity in the contact table instead of creating a duplicate member table.
  • Replace sales stages with a business process flow for registration, validation, active, renewal and archive, automate renewals with Power Automate, and reserve plug-ins and JavaScript for true edge cases.

Worth quoting

“Sales runs on dataverse. It's not just a database, it's the spine of the platform. Relationships, security, timelines, activities, search and automation baked in. You don't need a new app for memberships. You need a new mental model.” – Mirko Peters (01:22)

“You create a membership table that sits between member and organization. One member can have many memberships over time, one organization can have many members.” – Mirko Peters (09:14)
