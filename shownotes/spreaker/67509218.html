Outlook triage, Word cleanup and Excel-to-PowerPoint busywork quietly eat hours every week. This episode is for Microsoft 365 users and leaders who want to know what GPT-5 actually changes inside Copilot. You learn where the upgrade removes friction and how to reclaim that time for work that matters.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why everyday Microsoft 365 tools still feel slow
  • 04:11 – From instructions to true understanding
  • 07:48 – Speed you can feel in daily workflows
  • 11:35 – The quiet fix to email overload in Outlook
  • 15:26 – From Excel data to PowerPoint stories
  • 19:17 – Reclaiming hours every week

Key takeaways

  • Earlier Copilot versions followed instructions but missed intent, so users often traded manual typing for manual fixing.
  • GPT-5 in Copilot carries tone, audience and purpose across Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint and responds fast enough to keep users in their flow.
  • The biggest gains come from removing repeated micro tasks such as email triage, formatting and turning spreadsheet data into a presentation story.

Worth quoting

“Copilot interprets commands, but it doesn't yet understand the context that shapes why you're doing the task or what outcome would save you the next three steps. That gap is where friction survives.” – Mirko Peters (03:07)

“When Copilot produces a draft that needs heavy rewriting or a summary that strips out the detail you actually needed, you don't save time. You just trade manual typing for manual fixing.” – Mirko Peters (03:23)
