Microsoft Teams channels are not secure by default: guests linger, private channels spawn hidden SharePoint sites and pasted PII travels far beyond the chat. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins and security teams who need a concrete hardening plan for hybrid, guest-heavy tenants. Walk away with a five-layer playbook covering Conditional Access, Purview DLP, guest lifecycle governance, audit and retention that you can test in your own tenant.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Teams channels are not secure by default
  • 00:43 – Two incidents: the lingering guest and the PII paste
  • 04:05 – Layer 1: Conditional Access baseline that bites
  • 09:05 – Layer 2: Purview DLP for Teams chat and channels
  • 14:25 – Layer 3: Guest guardrails with Entra ID Governance
  • 19:50 – Layer 4: Audit, forensics and automated reporting
  • 24:38 – Layer 5: Retention and legal hold

Key takeaways

  • Teams is only the front end; real protection lives in Conditional Access, Purview DLP, Entra ID Governance and SharePoint sharing settings, and private channels add separate SharePoint sites that need their own governance.
  • Enforce MFA for everyone including guests, block legacy authentication, require compliant devices or app protection, and put DLP with policy tips and override justification on Teams chat and channels.
  • Guests must expire through access reviews and time-bound access packages, and the unified audit log, retention policies and legal hold provide the evidence trail you need when something goes wrong.
