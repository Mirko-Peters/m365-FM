Green dashboards often hide frustrated teams, stalled Copilot rollouts and quiet resistance. This episode shows executives how sentiment analysis of unstructured Microsoft 365 data, built on a credible privacy model, reveals what activity metrics miss. You will learn five executive signals that turn digital noise into leadership clarity.

  • 02:16 – The structural flaw in traditional reporting
  • 04:56 – Decoding unstructured data
  • 07:48 – Trust and the privacy model
  • 10:27 – Case study: the Copilot adoption trap
  • 13:34 – Five executive signals
  • 17:04 – Leading by pulse

Key takeaways

  • Activity metrics such as logins and training completion hide the behavioral gap between tool usage and real confidence.
  • Sentiment analysis of Microsoft 365 collaboration data only works when it is aggregated, anonymized and focused on processes rather than individuals.
  • Replace the 40-chart deck with five executive signals, from decision confidence to strategic alignment, that reveal organizational health.
