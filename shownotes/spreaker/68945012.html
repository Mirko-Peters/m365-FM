A single phishing click can hand an attacker a session token, and a single consent click can hand a malicious app your mailbox, often without any alarm firing. This episode walks security analysts and Microsoft 365 administrators through two reconstructed SOC investigations, token theft and an OAuth consent grant attack, and shows which Entra, Defender, Sentinel and Purview guardrails decide the outcome.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The silent crime scene and the anatomy of a breach
  • 04:09 – Three guardrails: Zero Trust, shared responsibility, defense in depth
  • 08:07 – Identity as first victim and witness
  • 12:45 – Case file one: phishing, token theft and impossible travel
  • 19:13 – Case file two: the OAuth consent grant attack
  • 22:33 – Compliance as the evidence locker
  • 24:52 – Training digital detectives

Key takeaways

  • Modern cloud attackers rarely break in; they log in with stolen session tokens or with OAuth consent a user granted, so identity signals are the first evidence to read.
  • Conditional Access with risk-based policies, phishing-resistant authentication strength, blocked legacy authentication and device code flow, and browser-only sessions for unmanaged devices stops a replayed token mid-flight.
  • Admin consent workflow, consent policies and Defender for Cloud Apps contain consent attacks, while sensitivity labels, eDiscovery, insider risk and records management turn logs into a defensible chain of custody.
