Many full-stack developers assume Teams development requires a whole new stack and give up at the setup screens. This episode is for React and Node developers who want their existing web apps inside Microsoft Teams. You will learn how to ship a personal tab, handle auth and routing quirks and design APIs ready for Copilot.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why full-stack skills don't seem to fit Teams
  • 03:27 – Turning familiar tools into a Teams app
  • 07:10 – Making your first personal tab
  • 10:55 – The hidden differences of state and routing
  • 14:31 – From tab to Copilot: building towards the future
  • 18:35 – Recap and a task for this week

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Teams is a host for your existing React, Node and Express app, not a new stack; the manifest, Teams Toolkit and Developer Portal handle the setup.
  • A personal tab runs your web app inside an iframe, so authentication needs the SDK-supported pop-up flow and navigation should stay client-side.
  • Designing message extension APIs with structured, versioned and presentation-free payloads prepares them for reuse in other Microsoft 365 surfaces such as Copilot.

Worth quoting

“Teams is not an alternative stack. It's not demanding you replace the way you've always shipped code. It's simply another host for the app you've already built.” – Mirko Peters (01:56)

“If the myth is that you need to learn a new stack, the reality is you need only to adjust your setup, and that's a much smaller gap to cross” – Mirko Peters (03:06)
