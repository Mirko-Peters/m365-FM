Cost dashboards make organizations feel in control while cloud and Microsoft 365 spend keeps drifting. This episode shows FinOps, platform and finance leaders why showback is not accountability and how guardrails, budget escalation, workflow automation and expiring exceptions turn visibility into real cost ownership.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Showback, chargeback and accountability
  • 04:25 – Why showback fails at scale
  • 08:28 – Cost entropy
  • 12:35 – Failure modes: dashboards, exceptions, shadow subscriptions
  • 27:58 – Three systems of action and ownership
  • 39:46 – Azure Policy, budgets, workflows and quarantine
  • 60:26 – Microsoft 365 shelfware and AI spend
  • 72:33 – A pragmatic maturity path

Key takeaways

  • Showback creates visibility but no accountability; governance needs owners with authority, enforced constraints and an audit trail.
  • Combine guardrails (Azure Policy with mandatory owner and cost center tags), alarms (budgets as escalation contracts) and actuation (workflows that force a recorded decision).
  • Make exceptions expire by default and quarantine unowned resources, then measure unowned inventory, exception age and time to decision instead of savings.

Worth quoting

“If the system cannot refuse a bad decision, you are not running governance. You are running persuasion, and persuasion does not scale.” – Mirko Peters (01:59)

“The missing object is always the same. A decision owner with authority and obligation. Awareness without obligation is trivia. Obligation without authority is cruelty.” – Mirko Peters (13:33)
