Many organizations pay people to reconcile data between disconnected systems instead of fixing the architecture. This episode is for IT architects and business leaders who want to see how Entra ID, Dataverse, Power Automate and Copilot Studio can form an autonomous tenant that runs processes like onboarding with minimal manual work.

  • 30:41 – The hard problems
  • 37:17 – Building the autonomous tenant

Key takeaways

  • Best-of-breed SaaS stacks create human middleware; treating Microsoft 365 as a unified control plane with Entra ID, Dataverse, Power Automate and Copilot Studio removes manual reconciliation.
  • Keep Copilot for generative tasks like drafting and analysis, and let policy engines and human-in-the-loop approvals decide deterministic tasks such as access and financial calculations.
  • Start with one high-impact, low-risk process such as new hire onboarding, build it in a 90-day discovery, design and test cycle, and back it with cost governance and a data governance council.

Worth quoting
