Contact center teams lose customers and staff when every interaction starts from scratch and urgent cases wait in line. This episode shows service leaders how sentiment-based routing, AI agents and Copilot in Dynamics 365 move a contact center from firefighting to proactive, context-rich engagement.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Spotting frustrated customers early
  • 00:38 – Why contact centers feel like firefighting
  • 03:30 – How sentiment AI detects frustration
  • 07:01 – Autonomous agents as support interns
  • 10:43 – Copilot conversation superpowers
  • 14:08 – From queues to proactive engagement
  • 17:50 – AI buffs agents, not replaces them

Key takeaways

  • Sentiment analysis in Dynamics 365 Contact Center reads tone, phrasing and pacing to route frustrated or high-value customers to the right agent sooner.
  • Case management, customer intent and knowledge management agents take over repetitive casework, ideally starting in supervised assist mode.
  • Copilot summaries, Ask a question and drafted replies keep context intact so customers do not have to repeat themselves.
