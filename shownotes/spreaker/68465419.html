Inventory that lives in spreadsheets loses assets through typos, duplicates and missing audit trails. This episode is for Power Platform makers and IT teams responsible for assets and compliance. It shows how barcode scanning in Power Apps, Dataverse, Power Automate and Power BI form a verifiable pipeline from scan to audit.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Spreadsheets pretending to be inventory systems
  • 01:21 – Inventory entropy and the cost of typos
  • 04:32 – Power Apps, Dataverse and the ingestion spine
  • 08:50 – Automation and validation with Power Automate
  • 13:16 – Power BI as the lens on order
  • 18:08 – Compliance, governance and risk containment
  • 20:33 – Verifiable truth by design

Key takeaways

  • Manual entry makes inventory errors inevitable, while barcode scanning in Power Apps captures each asset event exactly once and in a structured way.
  • Dataverse enforces referential integrity and auditing, and Power Automate flows validate barcode format, existence, location and approvals after every scan.
  • Power BI on top of Dataverse, combined with Purview, Intune and Conditional Access, turns scan events into traceable reporting and audit-ready compliance.
