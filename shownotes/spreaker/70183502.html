Organizations keep building more apps and flows, but every app adds connectors, owners, permissions and tickets that human review boards cannot keep up with. This episode is for Microsoft 365, Power Platform and Azure architects and shows how to engineer control planes from Entra, Microsoft Graph and Purview DLP that make governance automatic and cut governance-related tickets.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – More apps, more governance surface area
  • 04:46 – App sprawl symptoms and the human-review root cause
  • 12:54 – The hidden bill: support, audit and incident economics
  • 16:57 – Control plane 101 and Microsoft's built-in control planes
  • 25:07 – Three components: identity, Graph provisioning and DLP
  • 38:36 – Case studies: Power Apps explosion and Azure policy chaos
  • 63:40 – Case study: Copilot, shadow AI and agent governance
  • 76:11 – Operating model, metrics and failure modes

Key takeaways

  • Apps are only the visible layer; the real system is identity, connectors, environments, DLP and lifecycle, so app-first governance by human review cannot scale.
  • A control plane encodes intent upstream with an identity policy engine, Graph-based provisioning and DLP enforcement, so ownership, zoning and retirement happen by default.
  • Measured by governance-related support tickets, the shift cut tickets by about 40 percent and made Power Platform, Azure and Copilot rollouts governable without slowing innovation.
