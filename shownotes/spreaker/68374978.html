Copilot with GPT-5 writes polished strategies and policies in seconds, which tempts teams to skip the slower Researcher agent. This episode shows Microsoft 365 architects, compliance owners and power users when the agent is mandatory, why fluency is not provenance, and how a hybrid workflow keeps both speed and auditability.

  • 18:34 – When convenience becomes contamination
  • 22:43 – The rule of separation

Key takeaways

  • GPT-5 in Copilot optimizes for fluency and speed, but it gives no retrieval log or citation trail, so its output is a draft, not evidence.
  • Governance documentation, financial reporting, knowledge articles, Entra identity audits and executive market analysis require the Researcher agent's traceable citations and lineage.
  • A hybrid workflow lets Copilot generate the scaffolding, the Researcher agent verify critical claims and Copilot polish the result, with unverified output kept out of dashboards and regulated reporting.
