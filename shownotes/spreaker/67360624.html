Many Viva Connections dashboards launch with fanfare and are ignored within weeks. This episode is for intranet owners, Microsoft 365 admins and SPFx developers who want a homepage people actually open. You will learn how to fix the foundation and use SPFx web parts and Adaptive Card Extensions to surface the data teams need every day.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why most Viva Connections dashboards fall flat
  • 04:43 – Building a foundation that fits your ecosystem
  • 09:24 – Custom SPFx web parts for data and interaction
  • 13:50 – Adaptive Card Extensions and external integrations
  • 17:58 – Bringing it all together in a living dashboard

Key takeaways

  • Viva Connections dashboards fail when they offer generic links instead of content tied to how each team actually works.
  • Clean site structure, audience targeting, sound permissions and mobile testing form the foundation before any customization.
  • SPFx web parts and Adaptive Card Extensions bring live data and external systems into the dashboard, but need careful authentication, performance and error handling.

Worth quoting

“The trick isn't swapping out tiles or adding a chatbot. The real solution is to treat Viva connections like a system, not just another SharePoint, landing page.” – Mirko Peters (04:01)

“Don't leave governance to the last minute. It's cheaper to get policies right now than to rebuild things at scale after six months of confused access requests.” – Mirko Peters (08:02)
