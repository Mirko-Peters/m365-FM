Six dashboards and three vendors still leave four blind spots that attackers live in: email, identities, endpoints and cloud apps. This episode shows security teams in hybrid Microsoft environments how Defender XDR turns scattered alerts into one incident story with cross-domain response, and why consolidation pays back.

  • 10:33 – Blind spot 2: Identities

  • 19:04 – Blind spot 4: Cloud apps and shadow IT
  • 22:58 – The ROI equation
  • 24:29 – Handling objections

Key takeaways

  • Siloed email, identity, endpoint and cloud app tools each see only part of an attack, so the correlation work lands on analysts and dwell time grows.
  • Defender XDR fuses these signals into one incident timeline and can revoke tokens, kill sessions, remove OAuth consents, roll back mailbox rules and isolate devices from one place.
  • Response order matters: cut identity and SaaS persistence before or alongside device cleanup, or reinfection loops continue.

Worth quoting

“Silo tools are great at telling you something happened. They're terrible at telling you what happened in which order across domains. Sequence is the detection.” – Mirko Peters (02:10)
