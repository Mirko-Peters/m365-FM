Most security teams skim Microsoft Defender threat analytics reports and miss the parts that actually close attack paths. This episode gives SOC analysts and security admins a repeatable one-hour routine: read, test, act, verify. You learn how to link reports to hunting queries, incidents and Secure Score controls and to measure time to detect.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Unused, not useless
  • 00:42 – What threat analytics is and isn't
  • 05:08 – Three oversights that make it useless
  • 10:04 – From report to action in one hour
  • 16:54 – Two live detection-gap scenarios
  • 23:19 – Measurement and governance
  • 27:02 – The vow and next steps

Key takeaways

  • Read threat analytics reports down to the MITRE ATT&CK mapping and the organization-specific exposure panel instead of skimming the overview.
  • Turn each report recommendation into a Secure Score control with an owner, an SLA and evidence of effectiveness.
  • Hunt the listed techniques, join findings to incidents, verify controls by rerunning the queries and track time to detect and closed attack paths.
