When your BI bill starts rivaling your ERP and nobody can explain why, the cause is usually self-service sprawl rather than a single bad licensing decision. This episode shows BI leaders and Fabric admins how domains, certified semantic models and a Center of Excellence keep self-service fast while making licensing costs predictable.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Licensing: the horror twist in your BI strategy
  • 00:37 – The Wild West of self-service BI
  • 03:44 – Data mesh and Fabric domains as fencing
  • 07:28 – The Center of Excellence as licensing SWAT team
  • 11:30 – Semantic models: where costs hide
  • 14:57 – Licensing math gone wrong: PPU vs capacity
  • 18:41 – Fixes you can start this week

Key takeaways

  • Fabric and Power BI costs usually explode because uncontrolled self-service creates duplicate workspaces, cloned semantic models and extra refreshes, not because of one wrong purchase.
  • Fabric domains, endorsed semantic models in the OneLake catalog and build permissions let teams keep self-service freedom while reusing one certified model instead of cloning it.
  • A BI Center of Excellence should plan licensing early, using Premium Per User for pilots and premium capacity for scale, and back it with workspace policies, training and a community of practice.
