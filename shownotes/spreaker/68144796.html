IT backlogs grow because every automation request funnels through a few developers. This episode helps IT and business leaders see how governed citizen development on Microsoft Power Platform clears the queue, frees developers for architecture and delivers measurable ROI.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Developers drowning in requests
  • 00:37 – The hidden bottleneck
  • 03:42 – The everyone else problem
  • 07:23 – Developers as assembly line workers
  • 11:02 – Citizen developers: the secret weapon
  • 14:23 – Stacking the numbers
  • 17:58 – The path forward

Key takeaways

  • Developer-only automation creates backlogs and burns out engineers on micro-tasks, while the people who know the work best are locked out.
  • Citizen developers using low-code Power Platform tools inside guardrails such as a Center of Excellence, admin controls and data loss policies extend IT's reach without losing control.
  • Forrester's Total Economic Impact research on Power Platform points to strong ROI and fast payback, and Power Automate lets you track the hours each flow saves.

Worth quoting
