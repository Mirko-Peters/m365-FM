Many organizations move their servers to Azure and assume they are ready for AI, only to find their data, permissions and governance cannot support it. This episode is for IT leaders and cloud architects planning or finishing a migration. It explains why lift and shift fails AI and lays out a three-step strategy to unify, fortify and automate your cloud.

In this episode

  • 01:22 – Why lift and shift fails AI
  • 04:05 – Pillar 1: Data readiness
  • 07:51 – Pillar 2: Infrastructure and MLOps
  • 11:23 – Pillar 3: Talent and governance gap
  • 14:35 – Case study: premature cloud adoption
  • 17:04 – Unify, fortify, automate
  • 21:26 – Stop migrating, start architecting

Key takeaways

  • Lift and shift preserves old permissions, untagged data and silos, which makes a migrated estate cloud ready but AI hostile.
  • AI readiness rests on data structure, lineage and governance, mature MLOps and people who own data and AI risk.
  • Unify the data estate, enforce governance as code and automate monitoring so policies keep pace with AI workloads.
