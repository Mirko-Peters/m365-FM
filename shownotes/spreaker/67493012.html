Guest access in Microsoft 365 looks safe in a five-minute test, but external users can quietly reach entire SharePoint libraries and linger in your directory long after projects end. This episode helps admins and compliance teams understand the three identity layers behind every guest and build lifecycle policies that scale and survive an audit.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why guest access looks easy until it isn't
  • 04:10 – The three identity layers no one talks about
  • 08:19 – When Teams, SharePoint and Purview disagree
  • 12:31 – The compliance maze behind guest access
  • 16:48 – Designing scalable guest policies

Key takeaways

  • A guest is three things at once: the invitation and authentication method, the directory object in Azure AD, and the app-level permissions, and all three must be governed together.
  • Teams, SharePoint inheritance, OneDrive and Purview each interpret guest permissions differently, so a correctly configured tenant can still expose far more than intended.
  • Compliance depends on documented approvals, lawful purpose and verifiable offboarding, which scale only through scenario-based tiers, expiration rules, access reviews and automation.

Worth quoting
