Tech leaders often focus so hard on technology that they neglect empathy, rapport and their own mental health. Microsoft MVP Dan Barber shares how he learned leadership, why change management decides AI success, and practical ways to tame imposter syndrome and burnout. A must for consultants and tech leads who want to grow as people, not just as technologists.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Meet Dan Barber
  • 04:24 – Good vs. inspiring tech leaders
  • 08:28 – Trust, rapport and empathy
  • 16:46 – Remote work and human interaction
  • 20:46 – AI: opportunity, worries and skills
  • 29:35 – Certifications vs. experience
  • 33:49 – Imposter syndrome
  • 44:37 – Mental health, mentoring and community

Key takeaways

  • Technology is only a tool: understand the business first, then apply the tech and invest in the consultant side, not just the technical side.
  • AI programs need change management and humans in the loop to validate output and supply context.
  • Imposter syndrome affects most tech professionals; filtering information, using AI as an enabler, mentoring and a strong network help manage it.

Worth quoting

“technology is the tool. It's something that will enable you to do something. But you've truly got to understand the business first in order to apply the tech in the right way.” – Daniel Barber (02:39)

“I think AI is a little bit different in the fact that if you don't introduce change management with your AI strategy, then you're on a route to failure.” – Daniel Barber (27:40)
