ESG numbers that live in edited spreadsheets and DAX measures will not survive an audit. This episode gives data architects and sustainability teams a replicable Microsoft Cloud blueprint, from immutable ADLS storage and versioned emissions factors to Purview lineage and Entra role separation, so every KPI can be proven end to end.

  • 43:31 – Failure modes: CSV overrides, DAX drift, floating factors
  • 56:09 – Purview lineage, Entra roles and reporting
  • 77:56 – The minimal viable auditable blueprint

Key takeaways

  • Auditable ESG rests on four system properties: immutability, reproducibility, lineage and separation of duties.
  • Calculations belong in a governed Fabric or Synapse zone with versioned emissions factors bound to each closed period, while Power BI stays a thin presentation layer.
  • Corrections must be approved, additive adjustments rather than overrides, with Entra roles and Purview lineage providing the evidence auditors ask for.

Worth quoting
