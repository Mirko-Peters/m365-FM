Turning off Power Platform feels like the safe choice, but it often creates shadow IT, data leakage and compliance blind spots. This episode is for IT leaders and admins who are tempted to block the platform. You will learn why license removal backfires and how visibility, policy and enablement with the CoE Starter Kit build secure governance.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The false sense of security
  • 03:20 – Real risks lurking without governance
  • 07:00 – Why license removal backfires
  • 10:17 – The CoE Starter Kit as a governance game changer
  • 13:50 – Three pillars of safe innovation
  • 16:45 – Disabling hides risk instead of removing it

Key takeaways

  • Disabling Power Platform or pulling licenses does not stop business demand; it pushes data and workflows into unmanaged consumer tools outside retention, DLP and Conditional Access.
  • The CoE Starter Kit gives admins visibility into apps, flows, environments and connectors so they can guide usage instead of blocking it.
  • Sustainable governance rests on three pillars that reinforce each other: visibility, policy such as environment-level connector rules, and enablement through training and templates.
