Power Apps makers often struggle to turn polished Figma designs into consistent canvas apps. Microsoft MVP Lukas Pavelka shows makers and designers how his Figma plugins bridge design and Power Platform, and shares hard-won lessons on vibe coding, AI agents and token costs. You leave with practical habits for building better-looking apps faster without losing control of your code.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – How Lukas got from Java to Figma and Power Apps
  • 01:41 – Power Apps for Figma and Power BI for Figma
  • 06:20 – Free vs. pro features and design systems
  • 10:12 – Vibe coding in practice
  • 13:44 – MCP, models and token costs
  • 21:45 – Agent skills, security and developer background
  • 28:59 – UX, Microsoft Clarity and what comes next

Key takeaways

  • Figma plugins such as Power Apps for Figma let teams design components and whole design systems in Figma and export them to Power Apps canvas apps.
  • Vibe coding with GitHub Copilot or other AI agents works best with strict, single-purpose prompts, version control in GitHub and a developer who still understands the code.
  • Model choice and token costs matter, and agent skills plus MCP servers are becoming the way AI agents learn security, testing and tool-specific workflows.
