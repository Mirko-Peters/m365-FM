Intune alone cannot keep thousands of endpoints honest: drift, ghost devices and overpowered admins pile up. This episode shows endpoint and identity admins how to combine Intune with Azure Automation, Functions, managed identities, Graph and Log Analytics into a self-healing, least-privilege device platform.

  • 12:50 – Real enterprise scenarios

  • 23:15 – Common mistakes that break scaling
  • 26:55 – How to start: minimal working setup

Key takeaways

  • Intune declares policy, but at tens of thousands of endpoints it needs Azure Automation and Functions to reconcile drift, clean up stale devices and repair posture.
  • Managed identities with narrow Graph permissions, PIM and least privilege replace stored secrets and standing global admin rights.
  • Log Analytics and KQL turn device management into measurable metrics such as drift variance, cleanup rate and mean time to remediate.

Worth quoting

“Do not ask Intune to remember, reconcile, and repair without Azure at its side. Intune declares, Azure enforces, the graph binds, managed identity limits, log analytics witnesses, and your realm moves from managed to self-healing.” – Mirko Peters (05:41)

“Scale happens not with more hands, but with fewer keys. A runbook that disables devices after 60 days of silence does more for truth than a weekly export ever will.” – Mirko Peters (05:15)
