Every contractor or partner you ever invited into Microsoft 365 may still have access today. This episode helps admins and security leads uncover forgotten guest accounts, understand the security and compliance risk, and set up a guest lifecycle with expiration and access reviews.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Forgotten guests in your tenant
  • 00:25 – The silent guest pile-up
  • 04:33 – When guests keep the keys
  • 08:24 – Compliance nightmares: ISO, GDPR, least privilege
  • 12:06 – Mapping your guest landscape
  • 16:32 – The guest lifecycle blueprint

Key takeaways

  • Guest accounts in Microsoft 365 are easy to create but rarely removed, so most tenants hold far more forgotten external identities than admins expect.
  • Stale guests keep trusted access to Teams and SharePoint and break least-privilege requirements in frameworks like ISO 27001 and GDPR.
  • Automated expiration, recertification through access reviews and Conditional Access turn guest sprawl into a predictable, auditable lifecycle.
