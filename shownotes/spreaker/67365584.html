Your SharePoint permission reviews are outdated the moment they are finished, and guests hide in nested groups no one checks. This episode is for SharePoint and Microsoft 365 admins responsible for access governance in large tenants. Learn how to combine PnP PowerShell, Microsoft Graph and Azure Automation into a tenant-wide, automated permission audit.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Who has access to what?
  • 00:31 – Why traditional permission reviews break at enterprise scale
  • 03:50 – Building the foundation with PnP PowerShell
  • 07:38 – Mining permission data with Microsoft Graph API
  • 11:38 – Automating the audit with Azure Automation
  • 15:23 – From firefighting to continuous monitoring

Key takeaways

  • Manual, spreadsheet-based SharePoint permission reviews cannot keep up with thousands of changing sites, inheritance and nested groups.
  • PnP PowerShell with certificate-based app-only authentication and Microsoft Graph lets you enumerate every site and resolve effective permissions down to users and guests.
  • Azure Automation runbooks with Key Vault secrets turn the audit into a scheduled, logged process that can alert on risky changes such as new guests.

Worth quoting

“Permissions are living data. Treating them like a static list means you're always in the past, never in the live state of your environment.” – Mirko Peters (03:24)

“The solution isn't throwing more people at the review. It's building a way to query and consolidate this data automatically, so the moment something changes, your reports reflect that.” – Mirko Peters (03:32)
