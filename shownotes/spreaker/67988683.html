When Copilot touches a confidential file, is it productivity or a data leak in disguise? This episode shows SOC teams and security leaders how Purview DSPM for AI, sensitivity labels, DLP and Defender XDR turn confusing AI alerts into evidence and stop AI-driven data leaks before they happen.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Copilot vs the SOC
  • 00:33 – The first Copilot alert
  • 03:39 – The insider who rewrites the labels
  • 07:35 – Copilot or cover story
  • 11:04 – Forensics in the age of prompts
  • 14:50 – Winning the fatality combo

Key takeaways

  • Copilot only surfaces what users can already reach, so overshared or poorly labeled files become the real exposure.
  • Purview DSPM for AI, Insider Risk Management and Defender XDR together turn ambiguous Copilot alerts into traceable timelines such as label downgrade followed by AI access and exfiltration.
  • Proactive controls - oversharing assessments, label-based DLP exclusions, anomaly thresholds and prompt auditing with retention - let the SOC act before data leaks.
