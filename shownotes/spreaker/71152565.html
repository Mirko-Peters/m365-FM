Many Microsoft 365 governance programs look mature on paper while oversharing quietly spreads sensitive files, and Copilot now surfaces that exposure at machine speed. This episode gives CIOs, CISOs and Microsoft 365 owners one framework, one executive metric and three concrete moves with Purview, DLP and Entra to make governance automatic instead of optional.

  • 04:06 – Why oversharing beats every policy deck
  • 08:00 – The 10-minute breach
  • 15:41 – Framework and the one metric
  • 26:46 – Move one: auto-labeling and mandatory protection
  • 34:14 – Move two: DLP as an active control plane
  • 44:59 – Move three: temporary privileged access with Entra
  • 62:13 – What to mandate in 30 days and what not to do

Key takeaways

  • Governance only works when it is embedded in the flow of work, enforced by the platform and measurable through the share of sensitive data that is correctly labeled and protected.
  • Auto-labeling with mandatory protection and real-time DLP that warns, blocks or requires justification replace manual policing and awareness campaigns.
  • Purview and Entra form one control model, so privileged admin roles should be temporary and managed through PIM with approval and expiry.
