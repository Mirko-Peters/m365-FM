Many Microsoft 365 consultants are highly certified yet stuck negotiating rates and chasing projects. This episode is for consultants and partners who want to turn technical expertise into services clients compete to hire. Learn the five-element framework, productization and outcome pricing that replace hourly billing with certainty.

In this episode

  • 03:54 – What clients actually buy
  • 06:56 – The five-element framework for high-value services
  • 30:39 – Specialization creates demand
  • 33:37 – Productizing services into programs
  • 38:45 – From hourly pricing to outcome pricing
  • 65:53 – The 2026 market context
  • 73:21 – Applying the playbook in 90 days

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft 365 consultants who sell activities like workshops and deployments get compared on hourly rates, while those who sell measurable outcomes command premium prices.
  • A high-value service combines a painful problem, a clear measurable outcome, a repeatable methodology, packaged deliverables and reduced risk through fixed scope, timelines and guarantees.
  • Specializing in one problem such as Copilot readiness, license optimization or governance, and productizing it with a fixed price, is the way out of commoditization.
