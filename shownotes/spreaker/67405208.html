Field technicians often spend twenty minutes per job typing notes, and the resulting data is late and error-prone. This episode is for Dynamics 365 Field Service leaders and service managers, showing how Copilot turns spoken updates into accurate work orders, in-the-moment troubleshooting and proactive parts planning.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The paperwork bottleneck in field service
  • 03:51 – When your notes write themselves
  • 07:41 – Real-time troubleshooting support
  • 11:32 – Parts planning before the ask
  • 15:22 – From van notes to executive dashboards
  • 19:51 – Doing and documenting become one action

Key takeaways

  • Manual note-taking after each job costs technicians time and degrades data accuracy, which ripples into scheduling, billing, inventory and warranty claims.
  • Copilot in Dynamics 365 turns a short voice update into structured work order fields and a readable service history, using job, asset and parts context from the CRM.
  • Connected, real-time capture lets Copilot surface past fixes, stage parts mid-job and feed billing, scheduling and Power BI dashboards from a single source of truth.

Worth quoting

“It's not a tech being slow, it's the way the system forces the admin work to be done disconnected from the actual task.” – Mirko Peters (03:28)

“By normalizing entries into a consistent format, Copilot makes trend analysis far more reliable. Abbreviations, misspellings, or different technicians naming the same part in different ways are flattened into a single accurate reference.” – Mirko Peters (06:49)
