Buying, imaging and shipping laptops for contractors, remote workers and seasonal staff is slow, costly and risky for your data. This episode explains Windows 365 in plain English for business owners and IT decision makers: what a Cloud PC is, how Business and Enterprise differ, what it costs and how it compares to laptops and Azure Virtual Desktop.

Key takeaways

  • Windows 365 streams a full Windows desktop from Microsoft's cloud, so the device in your hands is only a window into your Cloud PC.
  • Because data stays in the Cloud PC, redirections are off by default and Conditional Access can enforce access only through the Cloud PC, contractors and BYOD users can work without company data landing on personal devices.
  • Windows 365 offers predictable per-user pricing that pays off most for contractors, seasonal staff and mixed workforces, while Azure Virtual Desktop can be cheaper for large, variable deployments but is more complex to run.

Worth quoting
