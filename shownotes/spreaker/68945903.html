AI in Microsoft 365 fails when the SharePoint, Power Apps and Power Automate layers underneath are undisciplined. This episode is for makers, architects and governance teams who want reliable apps, flows and agents. It lays out six pillars, from schema discipline to the governance spine, that stop quiet entropy before it becomes an incident.

Key takeaways

  • Stable SharePoint schemas with fixed internal names, few lookups, indexed filter columns and site-level permissions are the foundation for reliable apps and AI.
  • Power Apps and Power Automate become predictable through delegable queries, explicit concurrency handling, scoped triggers, try-catch scopes and correlation IDs.
  • AI Builder and Copilot Studio stay safe when they are fenced by human review, whitelisted knowledge sources, citations, DLP, environment strategy and solution-based ALM.
