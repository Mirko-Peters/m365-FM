A Dynamics 365 solution that is green in dev can still break the moment it lands in test or production. This episode shows Dynamics and Power Platform teams how dependency mapping, the right managed and unmanaged layering, and careful handling of environment variables and reference data turn fragile deployments into repeatable releases.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why solution packaging breaks even when it looks right
  • 05:30 – Mapping dependencies before you migrate
  • 10:25 – Managed vs. unmanaged: getting the layers right
  • 15:30 – Environment variables, data migration and automation
  • 20:40 – Treating deployment as a sequence

Key takeaways

  • Most failed Dynamics 365 imports come from hidden dependencies, so map every field, table, flow and script a solution relies on before you export it.
  • Use unmanaged solutions for development only and always ship managed solutions to production, tested in a sandbox, to keep layers clean and rollbacks possible.
  • Environment variables, connection references and reference data are not carried by the package, so document them per environment and follow the order import, configure, migrate, validate.
