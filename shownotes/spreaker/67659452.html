Many organizations switch on Microsoft 365 Copilot, expect instant productivity and then quietly decide it does not work. This episode is for IT leaders and adoption owners who need measurable results instead of a flashy demo. You learn how data, use cases, enablement and workflow integration turn Copilot into real ROI.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Copilot rollouts stall
  • 00:37 – The instant productivity myth
  • 03:40 – Data: the forgotten prerequisite
  • 07:21 – When use cases miss the mark
  • 10:24 – Why employees push back
  • 13:52 – Making Copilot stick in workflows
  • 17:03 – Three steps to prove ROI this week

Key takeaways

  • Copilot does not deliver instant productivity; it stalls when organizations roll it out without adoption planning, clear tasks and trusted data.
  • Clean data with one source of truth per process, consistent taxonomy and lifecycle policies is the prerequisite for answers people trust.
  • Real ROI comes from high-impact, repeatable use cases embedded in existing workflows, backed by role-based enablement, local champions and process metrics.
