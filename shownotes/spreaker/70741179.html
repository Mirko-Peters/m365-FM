After 500 episodes, Mirko Peters explains why consistency alone did not deliver what he built the podcast for, and what actually created leverage. This episode is for technical professionals, consultants and leaders who produce a lot but see little conversion. You will learn why distribution, positioning, execution and trusted relationships matter more than daily output, and how the same logic applies to shadow IT and Copilot inside organizations.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The original design: a portfolio machine
  • 03:58 – Failure one: content as a job portfolio
  • 11:01 – Failure two: the certification trap
  • 14:05 – The skill shift: script writing
  • 17:04 – Failure three: pure consistency versus leverage
  • 23:30 – What worked: distribution leverage
  • 29:03 – Event execution, network density and people
  • 47:58 – Executive angles: shadow IT, decision flow and Copilot

Key takeaways

  • Consistency keeps a system running, but without distribution, positioning and execution it rarely produces the outcome it was built for.
  • Owned distribution channels, live event execution and a dense network of trusted operators created more leverage than publishing volume alone.
  • Inside companies the same pattern applies: shadow IT, slow decisions and a thin Copilot return are design outcomes that need clearer decision flow and ownership, not more tools.

Worth quoting
