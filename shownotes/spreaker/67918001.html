Billions of new documents land in Microsoft 365 every day, and most of them end up untagged, overshared and invisible to Copilot. This episode explains to SharePoint admins and content owners how SharePoint Premium (formerly Syntex) automates document processing and governance so files become Copilot-ready knowledge instead of clutter.

  • 12:07 – Content governance: the bouncer
  • 16:21 – Why Copilot finally becomes useful
  • 19:54 – Where to start

Key takeaways

  • SharePoint Premium unifies the former Syntex services and SharePoint Advanced Management into one platform for content experiences, AI content processing and governance.
  • AI models for classification, metadata extraction, OCR and content assembly turn unstructured files into structured, searchable knowledge, while pay-as-you-go processing costs need pilots and cost controls.
  • Copilot only gives reliable answers when content is classified and governed, so start with prebuilt models on one high-value case like invoices and add DAG reports and site access reviews before scaling.

Worth quoting
