Most enterprise bots sound smart but hallucinate when asked about your real policies and data. This episode is for architects and IT leaders choosing between Copilot Studio and Azure AI Foundry. You learn how RAG works, where each platform shines and a simple framework for when to migrate.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why most bots are just fancy parrots: RAG and access control
  • 03:38 – Copilot Studio: quick wins with training wheels
  • 08:07 – Azure AI Foundry: the enterprise AI factory
  • 12:01 – The three-tier framework: explore, scale, govern
  • 16:05 – The hidden gotchas Microsoft glosses over
  • 19:15 – Pick your poison wisely

Key takeaways

  • Retrieval-augmented generation with identity-aware access control is what turns a hallucinating chatbot into an assistant grounded in your tenant data.
  • Copilot Studio delivers fast low-code pilots but hides model controls, while Azure AI Foundry exposes models, evaluation, MLOps and audit trails at the cost of time and skills.
  • Use an explore, scale, govern progression: prototype in Studio, move to Foundry when non-Microsoft 365 data, hallucination risk or compliance requirements appear, and budget early for tokens, licensing and connector work.

Worth quoting

“Bottom line, RAG is what turns a parrot into a real assistant. Done right, you get answers drawn from your tenant, filtered through your permissions, and grounded in sources that exist.” – Mirko Peters (03:07)

“Don't treat studio connectors as plug and play for critical systems, roadmap time and resources for extending them, or pivot the workload to foundry, where custom APIs and pipelines are first class citizens.” – Mirko Peters (18:14)
