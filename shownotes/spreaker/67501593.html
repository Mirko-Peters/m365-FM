Duplicate files, outdated policies and search results nobody trusts make SharePoint feel broken. This episode shows IT leaders and knowledge owners why the real problem is missing structure, ownership and lifecycle discipline, and how to turn SharePoint into a single source of truth that is ready for Copilot.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why SharePoint knowledge fails
  • 00:27 – SharePoint as a digital junkyard
  • 04:39 – Core ingredients: navigation, lifecycle, metadata
  • 09:00 – Making the fix stick: governance and training
  • 12:50 – One point of truth and Copilot readiness
  • 16:55 – SharePoint as a living system

Key takeaways

  • SharePoint becomes a digital junkyard when organizations treat it as storage without structure, ownership or lifecycle rules.
  • Clear navigation, content lifecycle practices and consistent metadata turn scattered files into a searchable, trusted knowledge base that Copilot can use.
  • Lasting success needs governance, automation, champions and continuous measurement, running SharePoint like a product rather than a one-time project.

Worth quoting

“SharePoint isn't failing anyone. It's the lack of intentional design that fails SharePoint. The platform offers powerful tools for content structure, governance, and life cycle management.” – Mirko Peters (03:48)

“If content lacks clear structure or metadata, AI systems can only return inconsistent results. They might surface outdated policies or irrelevant files, which undermines trust in the entire platform.” – Mirko Peters (07:29)
