Most Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts look busy on dashboards but deliver no measurable return. This episode shows IT and business leaders which adoption metrics actually predict success and how to turn usage data into a feedback loop that proves ROI.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Hype versus reality of Copilot rollouts
  • 04:47 – Hidden metrics that predict failure
  • 08:41 – Turning raw data into a feedback loop
  • 12:42 – Scaling best practices across the organization
  • 17:07 – From tool to strategic asset

Key takeaways

  • License activations and logins prove reach, not impact, so Copilot success needs baselines and task-level usage metrics.
  • Usage data only creates value when it loops back into targeted training, communication and process changes.
  • Codified prompt libraries and playbooks let one team's Copilot wins scale across the whole organization.

Worth quoting

“The problem is that rolling out technology doesn't equal transformation. Without structure, without strategy, and without monitoring, Copilot becomes just another tool among dozens already available in the productivity stack.” – Mirko Peters (01:18)

“A dashboard shows you what happened. A feedback loop says, now, here's what we'll do about it.” – Mirko Peters (09:02)
