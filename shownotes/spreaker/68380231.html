Governance teams still spend weeks reconciling Purview classifications, Power BI security and Fabric lineage in spreadsheets. This episode explains to data governance and compliance leads how GPT-5 reasoning in Copilot and Copilot Studio can automate those cross-service audits while keeping a verifiable audit trail.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Manual Fabric audits are a waste
  • 00:58 – Why governance breaks in Microsoft Fabric
  • 03:13 – GPT-5 reasoning as the missing link
  • 07:14 – The old audit vs. the GPT-5 audit
  • 11:57 – Business impact: predictive compliance
  • 16:22 – Setting up the workflow in Copilot Studio
  • 20:56 – Governance without sweat

Key takeaways

  • Fabric, Purview and Power BI each hold part of the governance picture, which makes manual cross-checks slow and error-prone.
  • GPT-5 reasoning in Copilot correlates lineage, classifications and row-level security across services in a single query and documents its reasoning steps.
  • In Copilot Studio, enable the GPT-5 model, connect Fabric, Power BI and Purview via connectors, build reusable audit prompts, test with staged anomalies and keep validating results.
