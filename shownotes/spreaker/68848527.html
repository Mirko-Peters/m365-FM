Many Dynamics 365 implementations end up as digital filing cabinets that store activity instead of driving progress. This episode shows business application owners and CRM teams how small changes to stages, fields and handoffs, plus a product mindset with a steady release rhythm, collapse cycle time and lift every downstream metric.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Filing cabinet or growth engine
  • 04:08 – Micro demo: three-stage process flow
  • 06:29 – Align Dynamics to business goals
  • 10:50 – Three levers: process, data, people
  • 15:27 – Product mindset, nearshore and real agile
  • 20:09 – Classic failure patterns and fixes
  • 22:42 – Tools for rhythm: RACI, backlog, 30-day release
  • 24:21 – What 90 days of scaling looks like

Key takeaways

  • Go-live is only a milestone; the real outcome of Dynamics 365 is how fast the system turns work into progress.
  • Trim business process flows to a few stages with verb-based exit criteria and a handful of required fields, and automate handoffs on save.
  • Run Dynamics like a product with a single accountable product owner, a friction-focused backlog and a monthly release rhythm tied to metrics.

Worth quoting

“The thing most people miss, scale is a behavior, not a feature. If the system requires meetings to interpret every status, you've built ceremony, not acceleration.” – Mirko Peters (01:56)
