Power Apps hides two very different app types under one name, and picking the wrong one can cost weeks of rework. This episode helps makers and IT decision-makers understand canvas versus model-driven apps through use cases, licensing and a four-question decision framework.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: two app types, one name
  • 01:41 – What is a canvas app?
  • 03:03 – What is a model-driven app?
  • 04:32 – Design first vs. data first
  • 06:04 – Real use cases for each
  • 10:47 – The licensing reality
  • 12:26 – The hybrid approach
  • 13:36 – A simple decision framework

Key takeaways

  • Canvas apps start from the screen and connect to almost any data source, while model-driven apps start from a Dataverse data model and generate the UI.
  • Model-driven apps bring business process flows, security roles and auditing out of the box but always require premium licensing.
  • Most strong solutions combine both: a model-driven core for the system of record and canvas apps for specialized or mobile front-line screens.

Worth quoting

“The real difference isn't about looks at all. It's about where you start. Canvas app start with a blank screen. You design every pixel, every button, every color.” – Mirko Peters (01:04)

“Model driven is not the right tool when you need pixel-perfect design. It's the right tool when you need data integrity and process consistency.” – Mirko Peters (10:36)
