Your Power App works until it doesn't, and then it fails in silence. This episode is for makers and platform owners who want to find hidden dependencies, refactor fragile formulas with With(), components and UDFs, and ship through real ALM. You leave with a refactor plan and a governance template that make failure rare.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why low-code apps fail silently
  • 00:33 – The anatomy of fragility
  • 04:48 – Forensics: mapping hidden dependencies
  • 08:50 – The With() pattern as local guardrail
  • 13:36 – Components, UDFs and enhanced component properties
  • 18:20 – Real ALM: solutions, branches and safe releases
  • 22:12 – Tests, monitoring and the governance template

Key takeaways

  • Power Apps fail where ambiguity is allowed: duplicated formulas, hidden globals, personal connections and unmanaged delegation create silent drift.
  • The With() function, pure user-defined functions and contract-based components with enhanced component properties keep logic local, named and reusable.
  • Solutions, connection references, environment variables, PR checks, pipelines and health thresholds turn low-code apps into governed, testable releases.

Worth quoting

“With is how you stop the bleed. Local scope, named intent, one entry, one exit, no global, no drift.” – Mirko Peters (08:53)

“Components are contracts, inputs, outputs, no hidden state, if you need state, you pass it in, you receive it out, you never smuggle it through, set.” – Mirko Peters (13:45)
