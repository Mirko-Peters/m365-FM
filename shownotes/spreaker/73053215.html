Many engineering leaders treat monorepo versus multi-repo as a Git question, yet their real problem is organizational fragmentation. This episode is for technical leaders and architects who want to understand why repository strategy mirrors team communication, how organizational debt and AI-generated code amplify coordination overhead, and how to choose a model that fits how their teams actually work.

  • 10:31 – How multi-repos work and their hidden tax
  • 21:31 – The infrastructure trap and repository-level fragmentation
  • 29:27 – Conway's law and the communication architecture
  • 42:45 – AI code generation and the verification tax
  • 52:02 – Trunk-based development and release flow
  • 58:47 – Measuring fragmentation and the decision framework

Key takeaways

  • Choosing a monorepo or multi-repo is really choosing where coordination happens: visibly in code review and CI, or invisibly in APIs, contracts and meetings.
  • Fragmentation comes from organizational debt such as unclear ownership, slow decision paths and inconsistent processes, and no repository strategy fixes it on its own.
  • Match your repository model to how your teams are actually coupled, measure coordination overhead and flow efficiency, and commit to the infrastructure or governance your choice requires.
