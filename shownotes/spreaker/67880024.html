Tired of scrolling endless SharePoint lists and exporting to Excel just to answer a simple question? This episode is for Microsoft 365 users and admins who track work in SharePoint lists. Learn how to connect, clean, publish and embed Power BI reports so the data finally tells a story where your team already works.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The SharePoint list problem
  • 00:36 – Why SharePoint lists are great until they aren't
  • 03:43 – Power BI and the licensing maze
  • 07:03 – Connecting SharePoint lists the right way
  • 10:18 – Cleaning messy columns with Power Query
  • 14:06 – Publishing and embedding back into SharePoint
  • 17:50 – Wrap-up: from static lists to answers

Key takeaways

  • SharePoint lists are built for storing data, not analysis, which is why filtering, trust and scale break down and users fall back to exporting to Excel.
  • Connect lists with the SharePoint Online list connector in Power BI Desktop using only the site URL, then clean person, choice, lookup and UTC date fields in Power Query.
  • Publish to a shared workspace, grant access through groups, check Pro or Premium licensing and row-level security, and embed the report on the SharePoint page your team already uses.
