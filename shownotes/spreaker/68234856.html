Your multi-cloud diagram looks resilient, but ExpressRoute, Direct Connect and Cloud Interconnect quietly bill you for every byte and millisecond that crosses a cloud boundary. This episode is for cloud architects and IT leaders who want to see where the network tax hides in egress, cross-connects and latency. You will leave with three practical strategies to stop overpaying: a primary cloud, shared services instead of transfers, and smart colocation.

Key takeaways

  • Every byte that leaves one cloud for another pays egress fees, cross-connect port charges at the point of presence and a latency penalty that physics will not negotiate away.
  • The real multi-cloud tax also hides in duplicated gateways, monitoring, DNS and engineer time, plus redundant circuits that keep billing even when idle.
  • Pick a primary cloud where your data lives, consume APIs instead of copying data sets, and if you must connect clouds, colocate them in the same metro facility.

Worth quoting

“Redundancy is free inside a single cloud region across availability zones. The moment you cross clouds, redundancy becomes replication, and replication becomes debt paid in dollars and milliseconds.” – Mirko Peters (04:08)

“The only guaranteed way to avoid the hidden friction is to keep the data where it naturally belongs, close to its compute and away from corporate tourism roads.” – Mirko Peters (09:42)
