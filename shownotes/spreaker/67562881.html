To Do, Planner, Lists and Loop often overlap until tasks get duplicated and deadlines slip. This episode helps Microsoft 365 users and team leads assign each tool a clear role so personal focus, team visibility and structured workflows finally fit together.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why M365 task management feels like survival
  • 05:03 – When To Do stops helping
  • 08:51 – The hidden strengths of Planner
  • 12:35 – When Lists outperforms To Do and Planner
  • 16:58 – Where Loop fits
  • 21:02 – Making the tools complement each other

Key takeaways

  • Use To Do for personal prioritization, not as a hidden shadow system for team work.
  • Planner becomes the shared source of truth for team deliverables when everyone actually works from the same board.
  • Lists handles structured, recurring and compliance-heavy work, while Loop captures tasks during collaboration and connects the other tools.

Worth quoting

“Task chaos isn't born because the apps are weak. It's born because apps overlap in ways they weren't meant to cover. A single tool rarely fails. It's the interaction between them that creates noise.” – Mirko Peters (04:33)

“The strength of to do is individual prioritization. The strength of a team tool is shared visibility and accountability. Mix them up and you end up with projects that look fine until you realize half the work was invisible.” – Mirko Peters (08:14)
