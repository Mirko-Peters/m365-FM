Many enterprises move to Azure and Microsoft 365 and then face cloud bills nobody can explain. This episode is for architects, IT leaders and finance stakeholders and shows, through five case studies, how governance, FinOps discipline and consolidation turn an unmanaged cloud into a secure, cost-efficient environment.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The $2.8 million surprise
  • 04:00 – Anatomy of waste: idle infrastructure, SaaS sprawl and shadow AI
  • 15:08 – The governance reckoning
  • 19:10 – Case studies: reserved instances, Power Platform and license right-sizing
  • 30:28 – Copilot governance and landing zone consolidation
  • 39:50 – Designing determinism: governance as control plane and FinOps metrics
  • 64:58 – The Monday morning playbook
  • 71:07 – Why governance becomes a competitive advantage

Key takeaways

  • Cloud waste is the default outcome of frictionless provisioning: idle resources, Power Platform and license sprawl, and ungoverned AI endpoints quietly consume budget.
  • Organizations that saved millions enforced governance first with Azure Policy, mandatory tagging, managed environments, landing zones and workload classification for reservations and savings plans.
  • Treat FinOps as a permanent discipline with the right metrics, right-size Microsoft 365 licenses by role, and clean up data before scaling Copilot with pilots and token quotas.
