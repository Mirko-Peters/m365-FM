Building a Copilot agent that works in a demo is easy; keeping it reliable, compliant and affordable in production is not. This episode is for makers, developers and admins deciding between Copilot Studio and the Teams Toolkit. It covers architecture, grounding, governance and licensing so your agents survive real-world load.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why the demo is not the boss fight
  • 02:11 – Copilot architecture layers
  • 04:15 – Copilot Studio vs Teams Toolkit
  • 08:08 – Grounding agents in enterprise data
  • 11:36 – Governance against agent sprawl
  • 15:05 – Licensing and cost traps
  • 19:11 – Bottom line

Key takeaways

  • Choose Copilot Studio for fast low-code agents within Microsoft's guardrails and the Teams Toolkit (Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit) for custom orchestration and deep integration.
  • Agents need grounding in enterprise sources with sensitivity labels and Purview classification, otherwise they either invent answers or expose data.
  • Governance and cost control, from admin roles and diagnostic logs to Copilot credit monitoring and budget alerts, must be in place before an agent goes to production.
