Custom connectors in Power Automate feel like a quick win until they become ungoverned, costly and insecure. This episode shows integration architects and Power Platform admins why Logic Apps Standard, with VNet integration, Bicep deployments and fixed-cost hosting, is the better foundation for enterprise integrations and how to plan the migration.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The ticking time bomb
  • 00:49 – The custom connector trap
  • 03:42 – The math of inefficiency
  • 06:53 – VNet integration as a governance mandate
  • 10:02 – Production-grade infrastructure vs. citizen chaos
  • 13:06 – The architect model for 2026
  • 16:06 – The migration roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Custom connectors owned by individual makers create security gaps, maintenance work and hidden per-call costs at scale.
  • Logic Apps Standard runs custom connectors as built-in extensions in a VNet-integrated, single-tenant runtime with fixed hosting costs and infrastructure-as-code deployment.
  • Keep Power Automate for human-centric, team-level tasks and move mission-critical, cross-departmental integrations to Logic Apps Standard.

Worth quoting

“The easy to build promise has become an impossible to govern reality. The model is broken because it's centered on the individual maker rather than the enterprise architecture.” – Mirko Peters (02:05)

“You're creating a world where an employee leaves the company and three mission critical integration stopped working because the custom connector was tied to their identity or their personal environment.” – Mirko Peters (03:03)
