Most Microsoft 365 Copilot users barely scratch its potential and lose hours to inbox triage, meeting notes and spreadsheet wrestling. In this episode Mirko Peters shares the prompts he uses every day, for knowledge workers and managers who want Copilot to pay for its license and turn routine work into clear outcomes.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden cost of daily productivity roadblocks
  • 04:17 – Turning email chaos into a five-minute task
  • 08:06 – Fixing meetings without the hangover
  • 11:43 – From spreadsheet wrestling to instant insights
  • 15:48 – Workflows that pay for themselves
  • 19:39 – Measuring when Copilot has paid for itself

Key takeaways

  • Copilot's ROI comes from plugging the small daily leaks of email triage, meeting follow-ups and spreadsheet preparation.
  • Specific prompts that ask for decisions, owners, risks or patterns turn raw emails, meetings and data into outcomes you can act on.
  • Chaining prompts across Excel, Word, Outlook and Teams removes context switches and compounds the value beyond minutes saved.

Worth quoting

“A well phrased request to Copilot can strip an hour long grind down to a few focused minutes for email meetings or document prep.” – Mirko Peters (03:32)

“One simple but powerful prompt I often suggest is this list the five key decisions, five action items with owners and outstanding risks from the meeting.” – Mirko Peters (10:07)
