Many Power BI teams hesitate to switch on Microsoft Fabric because they fear breaking existing reports. This episode is for Power BI admins and data teams who want to understand OneLake and Direct Lake. It walks through enabling Fabric safely, feeding the lakehouse with Dataflow Gen2, automating pipeline alerts and modeling for Direct Lake.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – OneLake and Direct Lake in a nutshell
  • 00:32 – OneLake: one governed data vault
  • 04:22 – Switching on Fabric safely
  • 07:57 – Dataflow Gen2 feeding the lakehouse
  • 11:15 – Pipeline automation and failure alerts
  • 15:00 – Semantic models and Direct Lake
  • 18:44 – Conclusion and next steps

Key takeaways

  • OneLake gives Power BI, Synapse and Data Factory one governed store with Purview lineage and labels, so data is placed once and consumed without copies.
  • Enabling Fabric in the Power BI admin portal is a non-destructive unlock; test with a pilot group, a trial capacity and a sandbox workspace before production.
  • Reliable Dataflow Gen2 ingestion, pipelines with on-fail email alerts, and a clean star-schema semantic model in Direct Lake mode deliver fast, fresh and trustworthy reports.
