Many Power Apps still run on Excel files that silently lose data, overwrite concurrent edits and leave auditors without a trail. This episode is for makers, Power Platform architects and IT leaders who need to move operational data off spreadsheets. You get the reasons Dataverse wins, how it fits with Fabric and SQL, and a concrete 10-step migration plan.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Excel is not a database
  • 04:18 – Three fatal failures: data loss, concurrency, governance
  • 08:14 – Enter Dataverse: what it is and why it wins
  • 13:00 – Migration strategy from Excel to Dataverse
  • 17:08 – When Dataverse isn't enough: Fabric lakehouse and SQL
  • 20:34 – Your 10-step migration plan
  • 26:34 – Conclusion: the spine, not the spreadsheet

Key takeaways

  • Excel is a great tool for analysis and prototyping, but without schema, transactions, audit and role-based security it fails as the data backbone of a multi-user Power App.
  • Dataverse enforces data types, relationships, business rules, security roles and auditing at the platform level, so data quality no longer depends on careful humans.
  • Migrate in phases (inventory, model, import pipeline, security, validation, freeze and cutover with rollback criteria) and keep analytics in Fabric while Dataverse governs operations.
