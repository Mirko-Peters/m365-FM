Many admins still see Intune as a tool to push policies to laptops. This episode is for IT and security teams in Microsoft 365 who want to understand how Intune, Entra ID, Conditional Access, Defender and Purview work together. You learn how to protect identities, apps and data on any device and prove compliance.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intune is more than pushing policies
  • 00:33 – Why Intune is more than device management
  • 04:05 – The identity connection: Intune plus Azure AD
  • 08:10 – Securing the apps that power the business
  • 12:20 – Compliance with Intune and Purview
  • 16:10 – Intune as connective tissue of the security stack

Key takeaways

  • Intune has moved beyond classic GPO-style device management to an identity-first model where access follows the user and device health rather than the machine.
  • Together with Azure AD (Entra ID) and Conditional Access, Intune enables contextual access decisions, and Mobile Application Management protects corporate data inside apps on BYOD devices.
  • Feeding Intune compliance posture into Microsoft Purview turns security controls into continuous, auditable evidence for regulations such as GDPR and HIPAA.
