Microsoft 365 platforms rarely fail loudly; they decay through test lists, permission drift and silently failing flows. This episode is for SharePoint, Power Platform and governance owners who want to spot that entropy early. You get concrete protocols for schemas, permissions, apps, flows and AI that turn quiet failures into visible, contained ones.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Quiet failures and entropy
  • 01:21 – SharePoint schema discipline
  • 03:13 – Permissions and inheritance
  • 03:56 – Deterministic Power Apps
  • 05:39 – Defensive Power Automate flows
  • 07:24 – AI as librarian, not oracle
  • 08:55 – The governance spine

Key takeaways

  • Treat SharePoint as a collaboration substrate with stable internal column names and few lookups, and move relational workloads to Dataverse or SQL.
  • Secure data at site or list level with groups instead of breaking inheritance per item, and design flows with trigger conditions, concurrency limits and try-catch scopes.
  • Govern AI and Power Platform with citations, human review for low-confidence results, DLP policies, separated environments, managed solutions and service principals.
