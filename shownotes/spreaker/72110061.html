Microsoft 365 keeps getting more complex, and many Copilot rollouts stall after the first workshop. Microsoft MVP Evi van der Velden explains why simplicity, habits and functional governance beat custom solutions for admins, adoption leads and decision makers. You get practical ideas for making Copilot stick and keeping your tenant easy to use.

  • 40:06 – Teams, metadata and rapid fire

Key takeaways

  • Copilot succeeds only when organizations build lasting habits with business-specific examples instead of a one-off workshop.
  • Copilot acts as a mirror that exposes old data, wrong permissions and missing archival policies, so functional governance matters more than ever.
  • Simple Microsoft 365 environments rely on standard features such as Microsoft 365 groups with accountable owners and metadata instead of custom builds.
