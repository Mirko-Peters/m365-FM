In the cloud nobody checks your work before a resource is created, so mistakes like public storage or expensive VMs slip through. This episode explains Azure Policy in plain English for anyone responsible for an Azure environment. You learn how definitions, assignments, effects and initiatives enforce your rules automatically on every deployment.

  • 06:28 – Effects: deny, audit, modify, deploy if not exists
  • 08:18 – Initiatives
  • 09:43 – Real-world examples
  • 12:08 – The big picture of Azure governance

Key takeaways

  • Azure Policy controls which resource configurations are allowed, while RBAC controls who may act, and you need both.
  • Policies are JSON if-then definitions with parameters, assigned at management group, subscription or resource group scope and inherited downward, with exemptions for edge cases.
  • Start new rules in audit mode, move to deny once you are confident, and bundle related policies into initiatives for a single compliance view.
