Many Microsoft 365 and Copilot programs show high adoption while the business still feels slow and political. This episode is for leaders and architects who want to understand why, and introduces the power architect as the missing responsibility for redesigning how authority, access and decisions flow.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why transformation fails at the power layer
  • 08:21 – Formal structure versus real power
  • 22:29 – AI does not fix power structures
  • 29:02 – The power architect defined
  • 40:05 – Mapping power, access and decision flow
  • 57:41 – Metrics that prove the redesign works
  • 64:15 – A practical redesign sequence for leaders
  • 71:48 – The leadership shift and the payoff

Key takeaways

  • Transformations stall when tools change but decision rights, ownership and access paths stay the same.
  • Copilot, Teams, SharePoint and Power Platform act as structural tests that expose weak ownership, misaligned permissions and hidden bottlenecks.
  • Start with one value stream, map decisions, dependencies, ownership and access, redesign approvals and escalation, and measure decision latency and cycle time before scaling.

Worth quoting

“Transformation is not the installation of a new capability. It is the redistribution of who gets to act, who gets to decide, and who gets to access the information they need.” – Mirko Peters (03:33)

“Policy without adherence is not control, it is just documentation, and documentation can create a false sense of safety.” – Mirko Peters (21:08)
