Many Microsoft 365 environments pay for storage and licenses that sit idle because of static quotas and oversized safety buffers. This episode shows architects and admins how to move to an elastic, pooled reservoir model with automated scaling, governance and tenant isolation. You learn how to cut waste while preparing your data for Copilot.

In this episode

  • 01:31 – Architecting the elastic reservoir
  • 06:32 – Automation and dynamic scaling
  • 12:29 – Governance as a precision instrument
  • 17:45 – Tenant isolation
  • 22:05 – Fiscal impact and ROI
  • 25:25 – First steps

Key takeaways

  • Static quotas and safety buffers create idle capacity and dark data; pooling storage into an elastic reservoir aligns cost with real demand.
  • Elastic scaling needs automated orchestration that respects throttling limits plus a governance backbone such as policy as code and lifecycle automation.
  • In shared architectures identity, sensitivity labels and tenant isolation are the real perimeter, especially before a Copilot rollout.

Worth quoting

“You add a 30% buffer to every single account because you think you are playing it safe, but in reality, you are just paying for empty space that nobody is using.” – Mirko Peters (01:04)

“If you allow the reservoir to scale infinitely without oversight, you aren't being efficient. You're just being fast at wasting money.” – Mirko Peters (11:15)
