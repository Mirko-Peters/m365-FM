Compliance teams still lose weeks stitching GRC reports together from spreadsheets, SharePoint folders and incident logs, and the result is often stale or wrong. This episode is for compliance, risk and Power Platform professionals who want reliable, auditable reporting. You will learn how to build a Power Automate pipeline that collects, standardizes and reports GRC data automatically.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why manual GRC reports are a bigger risk than you think
  • 04:40 – What really goes into a GRC report
  • 08:44 – Turning complexity into flow with Power Automate
  • 12:45 – Scaling automation from reports to strategy
  • 16:55 – The big picture: why this matters beyond reporting
  • 20:55 – Start with your first flow

Key takeaways

  • Manually assembled GRC reports are slow, error-prone and outdated by the time auditors or leadership see them.
  • Power Automate connects SharePoint evidence, Excel risk registers and Dataverse incident logs, standardizes the data and builds draft reports automatically.
  • Once the flows run, conditional reports, automated escalations and live Power BI dashboards turn compliance reporting into continuous, strategic governance.

Worth quoting

“Every manual step in the process, whether it's retyping a number or emailing a draft back and forth, creates another chance for inconsistency.” – Mirko Peters (01:19)

“It's not replacing SharePoint, Excel or data Verse. Instead, it acts as the orchestrator that listens to each of those systems, grabs the right piece in real time, and organizes them in a consistent way.” – Mirko Peters (07:56)
