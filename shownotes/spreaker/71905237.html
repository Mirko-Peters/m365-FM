Building an AI agent is easy; running one in production without exploding costs or leaking data is not. Microsoft MVP Edgar McOchieng shares how his team builds and deploys agents with Microsoft Foundry, from hybrid model routing and per-user budgets to evaluations, MCP and RAG. Developers and architects get practical patterns for production-grade AI.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: Edgar McOchieng and his labs
  • 05:16 – Mela AI, hybrid models and cost control
  • 14:08 – Microsoft Foundry fundamentals
  • 19:24 – Copilot, Copilot Studio or Foundry
  • 24:25 – Prompt flow, model catalog and evaluations
  • 31:02 – Lifecycle of an AI application
  • 41:12 – Integrations, MCP and RAG
  • 56:08 – Hot takes and priorities

Key takeaways

  • A hybrid setup that routes simple requests to local models and complex ones to cloud models, combined with per-user budgets, keeps AI costs predictable.
  • Copilot, Copilot Studio and Microsoft Foundry form three levels of agent building, with Foundry offering full control beyond the confined Copilot Studio space.
  • Monitoring, governance and evaluations are the foundation for running production-grade AI agents in the enterprise.
