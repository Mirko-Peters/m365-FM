Still managing Microsoft 365 Copilot by clicking through the admin center? This episode is for administrators who need to govern Copilot at scale and prove to auditors who changed what and when. Learn why PowerShell automation and audit exports give you control, consistency and real evidence.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The admin center is a toy steering wheel
  • 01:30 – The toy versus the tool
  • 05:34 – The governance gap in Copilot
  • 10:12 – Automation is law
  • 14:52 – Auditing: receipts instead of trends
  • 19:45 – The admin's manifesto
  • 22:20 – Control or convenience

Key takeaways

  • The Microsoft 365 admin center is built for visibility, not governance, and cannot handle bulk Copilot changes across many users and tenants.
  • PowerShell scripts make Copilot policy enforcement repeatable, logged and versioned, turning governance rules into enforceable code.
  • Auditing with PowerShell and Graph-based audit records produces timestamped, exportable evidence that regulators and auditors actually accept.

Worth quoting

“The admin center can't help you. It shows adoption metrics and usage trends, but not the evidence chain you need. Governance without traceability is theater.” – Mirko Peters (06:27)

“When you define governance rules in code, those rules execute exactly the same way every time on schedule under audit. That's compliance through reproducibility, governance not as advice, but as enforcement.” – Mirko Peters (10:50)
