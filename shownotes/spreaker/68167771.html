Many AI pilots look great in the lab and then collapse under real data, users and compliance requirements. This episode is for CIOs, architects and IT leaders who need to decide when AI deserves enterprise-scale treatment and how an AI factory with governance, MLOps and orchestrated hardware gets pilots safely into production.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Is AI just another workload?
  • 00:30 – Why AI is a different class of workload
  • 04:43 – Five questions that separate pilots from production
  • 07:45 – The pilot-to-production death zone
  • 12:16 – The AI factory as command deck
  • 16:24 – The engine room: hardware, data and algorithms
  • 19:48 – Conclusion: orchestration over raw hardware

Key takeaways

  • Check scalability, hardware, data intensity, algorithmic complexity and integration to decide whether an AI project needs enterprise-scale treatment.
  • AI pilots stall in production because of data silos, governance gaps, missing accelerators and the lack of MLOps automation.
  • An AI factory approach with templates, role-based access, private networking and orchestrated GPUs turns scattered experiments into repeatable, governed delivery.

Worth quoting
