Duplicate files, abandoned sites and stale sharing links make SharePoint search and Copilot unreliable. This episode gives Microsoft 365 admins and governance leads a playbook built on E3 and E5 features: lifecycle enforcement, standardized provisioning and retention labels, with metrics to prove the cleanup works.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Your SharePoint is a landfill
  • 00:34 – Diagnosing sprawl and its root causes
  • 02:37 – Metrics that expose the landfill
  • 04:03 – Enforced lifecycle with E3 and E5
  • 08:52 – Provisioning: the building code
  • 13:46 – Retention labels that actually work
  • 19:08 – Human roles and accountability
  • 21:24 – Measuring outcomes and the review loop

Key takeaways

  • SharePoint sprawl means duplicated files, abandoned sites, orphaned permissions and stale sharing links, and it degrades search, compliance and Copilot answers.
  • Enforce lifecycle with two named owners, inactivity checks, attestation and archiving, using Power Automate in E3 or inactive site policies in SharePoint Advanced Management with E5.
  • Standardize provisioning with request forms and templates, apply retention labels at item level, and track inactive site rate, duplicate ratio and search precision monthly.

Worth quoting
