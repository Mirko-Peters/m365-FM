Your Power Automate approvals may look documented until an auditor asks for last year's records and the run history is empty. This episode shows makers and admins how to log approvals durably, use dynamic approvers, restart flows by stage and design reminders and escalations that don't spam users.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Run history is not an audit trail
  • 00:42 – Why flow history vanishes
  • 04:11 – Approval flows that don't collapse
  • 07:28 – Escalations and reminders without spam
  • 11:10 – The logging lie
  • 14:43 – Future-proofing audit-ready approvals

Key takeaways

  • Power Automate run history is retained only for a limited time and is a debugging aid, not a compliance archive.
  • Resilient approval flows resolve approvers dynamically from groups or lists and store a persistent stage so they can restart at the right step.
  • Write every approval, reminder and escalation as structured records to a durable store such as a SharePoint list, Dataverse or SQL.

Worth quoting

“power Automate run history is a debugging aid, not a record keeper. It's disposable by design, not permanent or audit ready.” – Mirko Peters (03:47)

“Store role membership, not emails. Use object IDs when possible, so changes don't break flows.” – Mirko Peters (05:11)
