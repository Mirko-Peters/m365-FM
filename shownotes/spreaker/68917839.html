Copilot will not fix SharePoint sprawl; it echoes stale files, broken permissions and orphaned teams back to your users. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins and governance leads preparing their tenant for AI. Learn the five binds of information architecture, lifecycle, labels, DLP and retention that make Copilot answers trustworthy.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot grounds on what it can reach, so permission drift, orphaned teams, ROT data and shadow sites turn directly into stale or wrong answers.
  • Lean information architecture with hubs, lifecycle with owner attestation, sensitivity labels, DLP and retention work as one bound governance ritual.
  • Prove the cleanup with paired before-and-after prompts that track citation precision, answer variance and access mismatches.

Worth quoting
