SharePoint document libraries often turn into dumping grounds where files vanish and views lie. This episode shows SharePoint owners and Microsoft 365 admins how the new library UX, Forms, autofill columns and Copilot turn a file pile into a governed intake-to-publish pipeline that people actually use.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why doc libs became dumping grounds
  • 00:40 – The new UX: breadcrumbs, filter pills, options hub and board view
  • 03:29 – Forms for document libraries: controlling the front door
  • 07:47 – Column autofill: stop typing metadata
  • 12:22 – Copilot in doc libs: compare files, summaries, audio overviews and Q&A
  • 17:20 – A document operating system: views, rules and quick steps
  • 20:45 – Common pitfalls, pro fixes and the rapid checklist

Key takeaways

  • Control intake with Forms for document libraries and only ask users for decisions humans must make, letting autofill infer the rest.
  • Write precise, format-locked autofill prompts, calibrate them on a small batch of files and monitor the activity panel before scaling.
  • Combine published views, conditional formatting, quick steps and Copilot compare files into one intake-to-publish pipeline with a daily triage habit.

Worth quoting

“Now for the decision that separates pros from amateurs, choose what users must provide versus what autofill handles. If AI can infer it, don't ask for it.” – Mirko Peters (04:48)

“The thing most people miss is you control the rules. It's not guessing, it's following your instructions.” – Mirko Peters (08:38)
