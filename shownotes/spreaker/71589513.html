Your teams use Copilot Cowork every day, yet productivity is not improving. This episode is for managers and IT leaders who need to move from supervising people to architecting the systems, data and guardrails that agentic AI runs on. Learn the rituals and metrics that show whether your AI transition actually scales.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The myth of instant Copilot productivity
  • 03:21 – From supervision to system architecture
  • 06:33 – Case 1: The pilot that scaled nothing
  • 09:15 – Case 2: Power Platform sprawl chaos
  • 11:59 – Case 3: Governance vacuum versus gatekeeper
  • 14:34 – New rituals and metrics for architects

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Cowork stalls when managers supervise it like a human intern instead of redesigning the workflows, data and intent around it.
  • Clean grounding data, versioned prompt patterns and governance embedded into the data flow turn AI usage into reliable output.
  • Replace status meetings and vanity metrics with weekly system reviews tracking cycle time, rework rate, decision latency and incident rate.

Worth quoting
