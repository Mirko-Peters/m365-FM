Power Platform apps that work in dev often break the moment they move to test or production. This episode is for makers, IT pros and Power Platform teams who are tired of manual exports and patch jobs. You will learn how PAC CLI brings solution packaging, plug-in deployment and pipeline automation into a repeatable DevOps workflow.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why PAC CLI feels different and actually matters
  • 06:03 – Setting up PAC CLI without breaking what works
  • 11:13 – Solution packaging: from component chaos to real portability
  • 16:24 – Automating plug-in deployment as the missing DevOps link
  • 21:27 – Building a Power Platform environment that scales

Key takeaways

  • Moving apps through the maker portal alone leads to missing dependencies, manual rework and drift between dev, test and production.
  • Isolating PAC CLI with sandboxes and authentication profiles lets you adopt scripted deployments without risking production.
  • Packaging solutions and registering plug-ins with PAC CLI turns releases into versioned, traceable steps that plug into Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions pipelines.
