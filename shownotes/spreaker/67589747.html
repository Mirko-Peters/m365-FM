Endless task lists keep professionals busy without real progress. This episode is for Microsoft 365 users who juggle To Do, Planner, Outlook and Teams and shows how the Eisenhower Matrix combined with Copilot prompts and Power Automate turns busywork into clear priorities.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Busy but not productive

  • 04:13 – The Eisenhower Matrix in the modern office
  • 07:50 – Underused Microsoft 365 task tools
  • 11:33 – When Copilot meets Eisenhower
  • 15:24 – Practical prompts and automation
  • 19:23 – Fewer, stronger decisions

Key takeaways

  • Task lists treat every item as equally important, so people feel busy while their most important work stalls.
  • The Eisenhower Matrix separates urgency from importance, and Microsoft 365 tools like To Do, Planner, Lists, Outlook and Teams work best as one connected system instead of silos.
  • Explicit prompts let Copilot sort tasks into Eisenhower quadrants, and Power Automate can route each category to Planner, the calendar or an archive automatically.

Worth quoting
