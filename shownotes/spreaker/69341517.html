Azure bills keep growing because the platform allows spend without owners, limits or consequences. This episode is for cloud platform, FinOps and finance leaders who want to move from dashboards to enforcement with subscription design, Azure Policy, tagging, budgets and accountability models. You get a 90-day rollout plan to stop unowned spend.

Key takeaways

  • Azure cost is an authorization outcome: spend happens because identities, policies and subscriptions allow it, not because engineers are careless.
  • Subscriptions with named owners, enforced tagging, SKU and region constraints and time-boxed exceptions turn cost governance into an enforceable boundary.
  • Budgets should fire early and route to accountable owners, with showback and chargeback closing the feedback loop and deployment stamps providing cost-bounded defaults.

Worth quoting
