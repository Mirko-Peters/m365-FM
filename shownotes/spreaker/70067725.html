SharePoint quick steps, approvals, flows and agents make automation feel small, until it quietly becomes an enterprise control plane that moves data and changes permissions. This episode gives architects and Microsoft 365 admins a blueprint for identity, labels, DLP, orchestration and observability that keeps SharePoint automation governable at scale.

Key takeaways

  • Treat SharePoint as a workflow surface and event source, and separate event, reasoning, orchestration and enforcement so automation stays testable and auditable at scale.
  • Standardize one path per class of work: Power Automate for bounded team workflows, Graph webhooks for high-volume eventing, Logic Apps and Functions for orchestration and compute, and agents only as the intake layer.
  • Make governance real through Entra group-first access, label-first Purview classification, DLP as a runtime gate and centralized observability that proves what the system did.
