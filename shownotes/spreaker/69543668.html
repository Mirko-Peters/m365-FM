Many organizations still treat ServiceNow as a ticketing tool and Teams chats as their process, so approvals, access and changes vanish into side channels. This episode is for enterprise architects and IT leaders who run Microsoft 365 alongside ServiceNow and want a clear split between engagement and execution, with Copilot, Now Assist and connectors governed so AI produces auditable outcomes instead of noise.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Beyond ticketing: the enterprise workflow problem
  • 04:32 – Tickets track pain, workflows control outcomes
  • 08:01 – Systems of record versus systems of action
  • 18:27 – Scenarios: onboarding, incident response, finance and major incidents
  • 36:41 – Connectors give answers, orchestration gets outcomes
  • 40:39 – Copilot and Now Assist: two brains, two jurisdictions
  • 48:32 – Failure modes: workflow entropy, shadow automation, permission drift
  • 52:18 – The workflow-first operating model in phases

Key takeaways

  • Tickets only describe problems; workflows are state machines that enforce sequence, ownership and evidence, which is the operating layer enterprises actually lack.
  • Microsoft 365 and Copilot should capture intent where people work, while ServiceNow and Now Assist own authoritative workflow state, approvals and audit trails.
  • Start with read-only integration, then move to governed write actions with least privilege and rollback, and only then to agentic execution, while watching for shadow automation and permission drift.

Worth quoting
