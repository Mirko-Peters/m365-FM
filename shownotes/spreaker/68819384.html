Overflowing support inboxes, late SLA clocks and misrouted tickets are a design problem, not a staffing problem. This episode is for customer service leaders and Dynamics 365 teams who want to fix intake without burning out their agents. You will learn how autonomous agents, unified routing and Copilot drafts work together under governance, plus the pitfalls and a 30-60-90 rollout plan.

  • 18:10 – Real-world results, pitfalls and a 30-60-90 plan

Key takeaways

  • Customer service chaos comes from an ungoverned email-to-case intake path, not from email volume itself.
  • Autonomous agents in Dynamics 365 read, classify and create cases, route them with unified routing by skills, capacity and SLA, and let Copilot draft responses for human review.
  • Clean taxonomy, identity binding, audit logs, PII redaction and DLP must be in place first, otherwise automation only speeds up existing mistakes.
