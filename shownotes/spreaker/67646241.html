Defender protects you in the moment, but can you prove what happened six months ago? This episode helps security and IT admins decide when Microsoft Defender XDR is enough and when Microsoft Sentinel is worth adding for retention, compliance and cross-cloud investigations, without blowing the Azure budget.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Defender vs. Sentinel: the real question
  • 00:24 – The Defender comfort zone and its short memory
  • 04:25 – Compliance and log retention gaps
  • 08:26 – Where Sentinel shines
  • 12:52 – Adding Sentinel without blowing the budget
  • 16:56 – Making the call for your organization
  • 20:55 – Closing: shield and memory

Key takeaways

  • Defender XDR correlates signals well inside Microsoft 365, but its 30 to 90 day retention leaves long-dwelling attacks and audits uncovered.
  • Sentinel adds long-term retention, cross-cloud correlation and Logic Apps playbooks, turning Defender alerts into a complete investigation narrative.
  • Keep Sentinel costs down by streaming Defender data via connectors and ingesting only high-value logs such as identity, privileged access and endpoint alerts.
