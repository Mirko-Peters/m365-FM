AI agents rarely fail loudly; they multiply quietly without owners, version history or audit trails. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins and IT governance teams who want to enable Copilot agents without losing oversight. You will learn how the Admin Center, Copilot Studio controls and monitoring work together as a practical governance framework.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why AI agents scare so many organizations
  • 04:29 – The control panel you didn't know you had
  • 08:41 – Copilot Studio: innovation without chaos
  • 12:33 – The watchtower: monitoring and tracking agent activity
  • 16:52 – Avoiding the classic governance mistakes
  • 21:51 – Governance as the foundation for growth

Key takeaways

  • The guardrails for Copilot agents already live in the Microsoft 365 Admin Center, where admins can see which agents exist, what connectors they use and who can publish them.
  • Copilot Studio lets employees experiment safely when admins restrict connectors, actions and publishing rights to designated roles.
  • Permissions alone are not governance: audit logs, usage monitoring and consistent policies aligned with security and compliance teams are what keep agents under control.

Worth quoting

“Companies aren't afraid of copilot agents themselves. They're afraid of losing sight of them. They're afraid of forgetting who built which agent, when it was last reviewed, or why it's pulling information from sensitive systems.” – Mirko Peters (03:40)

“A better model is to allow broader participation in building ideas while limiting deployment authority to designated roles.” – Mirko Peters (07:19)
