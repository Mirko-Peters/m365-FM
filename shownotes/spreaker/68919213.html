Unstructured documents and images sit in SharePoint without meaning, and Copilot can only reason over what is governed and tagged. This episode shows content and governance teams how to use SharePoint Premium models, SharePoint Advanced Management and Purview to extract, classify and protect content step by step. You get concrete scenarios for invoices, contracts and images plus the metrics to prove ROI.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – From documents to a knowledge engine
  • 00:30 – SharePoint Premium foundations and guardrails
  • 03:22 – Invoice and receipt processing
  • 08:10 – Contract classification and taxonomy
  • 13:20 – Image tagging and content assembly
  • 17:14 – SharePoint Advanced Management for Copilot readiness
  • 22:24 – Rollout blueprint and adoption

Key takeaways

  • Lock exposure first with restricted access control, restricted content discovery, sensitivity labels and DLP before scaling SharePoint Premium models.
  • Unstructured, freeform and image models with confidence thresholds and human review turn invoices, contracts and images into governed, searchable metadata.
  • Measure deltas such as overshared sites, label coverage, anonymous links and time to classify to prove ROI and Copilot readiness.
