Many organizations pay for Microsoft 365 E5 and Copilot yet leak money through unused licenses, orphaned apps, permission sprawl and governance that exists only on paper. This episode is for CIOs, CFOs and Microsoft 365 architects who need to justify their Microsoft spend. Learn the seven architecture sins, how to calculate your own leakage and a 90-day path to a governed control plane.

Key takeaways

  • Licensing, permission sprawl, manual governance, app sprawl, ungoverned AI agents, builder bias and blanket E5 licensing compound into invisible waste in a Microsoft 365 tenant.
  • The root cause is a missing control plane: Entra ID should act as the backbone from which SharePoint, Teams, Purview, Defender and Copilot agents inherit policy.
  • A 90-day recovery of audit, governance automation and control plane design, sustained by clear ownership, decision rights and cadence, recovers license waste before renewals and price increases.

Worth quoting
