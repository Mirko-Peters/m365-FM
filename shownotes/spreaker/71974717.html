Managed connectors feel like the easy button, but under load they become a brittle bottleneck of throttling, latency and retry storms. This episode shows integration architects why the fix is a protocol-level shift to gRPC, persistent streams, queue-fronted resilience and built-in Logic Apps connectors.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The managed connector illusion
  • 00:47 – The latency tax of middleware
  • 04:00 – gRPC and the binary revolution
  • 07:38 – Persistent streams beyond polling
  • 11:06 – Asynchronous resilience patterns
  • 12:23 – Built-in versus managed logic
  • 13:40 – AI orchestration at protocol speed

Key takeaways

  • Managed connectors add hidden latency, throttling limits and retry storms because data passes through a shared middleware layer you do not control.
  • gRPC with Protocol Buffers and persistent streams such as WebSockets reduce payload size, CPU overhead and empty polling traffic compared with REST and JSON.
  • Queue-front high-speed flows with a durable buffer like Azure Service Bus and move to Logic Apps Standard with built-in connectors to keep data inside your virtual network.
