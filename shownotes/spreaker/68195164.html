Think enabling Microsoft 365 Copilot is just a switch in the admin center? This episode is for IT admins, security and compliance leads who need to understand what contracts, licenses, RBAC and data controls actually govern before rollout. You will learn which Purview, Defender and licensing steps to take so Copilot becomes a disciplined enabler instead of an oversharing risk.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What really flips when you turn on Copilot
  • 00:40 – Contracts: Product Terms and the DPA
  • 05:07 – Licenses and RBAC: the locks on every door
  • 09:32 – Data exposure: Copilot mirrors your permissions
  • 13:58 – Technical controls: Admin Center, Purview and Defender
  • 18:09 – Governance in practice: system-enforced rules
  • 22:49 – Wrap-up: three checks for your tenant

Key takeaways

  • Copilot is governed by the Microsoft Product Terms and the Data Protection Addendum before any toggle is flipped, so legal review and residency mapping come first.
  • A Copilot license grants features, not permissions: Copilot only surfaces what a user can already access through Microsoft Graph, so RBAC and permission hygiene define the real exposure.
  • Effective Copilot governance is system-enforced: Purview labels, DLP, DSPM for AI and Defender endpoint DLP turn policies into rules users cannot bypass.

Worth quoting
