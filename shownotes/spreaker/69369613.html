Most AI pilots impress in the demo and then stall on untrusted answers, cost spikes and compliance panic. This episode is for CIOs, CDOs and platform leaders who need AI to survive production in the Microsoft ecosystem. You get a three-to-five-year operating model built on decision rights, guardrails, semantic contracts and unit economics.

Key takeaways

  • AI amplifies the existing operating model, so ownership of truth, access approvals and cost must be decided before a pilot scales.
  • Identity, classification and lineage, and semantic contracts must be deterministic layers so probabilistic AI components stay within a bounded blast radius.
  • Steer AI with unit economics such as cost per decision, each with a named business owner, plus time-bound, recorded exception pathways.

Worth quoting

“AI doesn't leak data accidentally. It leaks it correctly under bad identity design. AI doesn't create wrong answers randomly. It produces wrong answers deterministically when your enterprise cannot agree on truth.” – Mirko Peters (02:21)
