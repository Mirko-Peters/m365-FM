Your Power BI reports may work, but a messy data model can quietly cost thousands every year in capacity and waiting time. This episode is for Power BI developers and analysts who want faster refreshes and lower costs. Learn how a proper star schema, disciplined DAX and clean relationships eliminate the performance tax.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Your Power BI dashboard is lying about the cost
  • 00:50 – The inefficiency tax of messy models
  • 02:20 – The fix: dimensional modeling with a star schema
  • 05:45 – DAX discipline and relationship hygiene
  • 09:25 – The $10,000 ROI of a clean model
  • 12:32 – Reset your model and stop paying the tax

Key takeaways

  • Bloated Power BI models with unused columns, calculated columns and tangled relationships quietly cost money through capacity usage and the time people spend waiting for refreshes.
  • A star schema with a lean fact table, descriptive dimension tables, one-to-many single-direction relationships and surrogate keys lets the VertiPaq engine compress data and DAX evaluate predictably.
  • Move data shaping into Power Query, keep DAX measures simple and layered, test with DAX Studio, and start by fixing your single biggest offender in an existing model.

Worth quoting
