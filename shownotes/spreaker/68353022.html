Manual typing into rigid Power Apps forms is slow, error-prone and quietly corrupts Dataverse and Power BI reports. This episode shows Power Platform makers and admins how the AI data entry agent with smart paste and file upload turns messy emails and screenshots into clean, validated records, and what to check before enabling it.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The pain of manual data entry in Power Apps forms
  • 00:47 – Why traditional Power Apps forms fail
  • 02:58 – Meet the AI data entry agent: smart paste and file upload
  • 06:01 – Prerequisites: enabling AI form fill
  • 08:05 – Demo: effortless record creation
  • 11:46 – Demo: complex data and validation
  • 15:45 – Architecture: how the agent interprets data
  • 18:45 – Limitations, licensing and the future of Power Apps UX

Key takeaways

  • The AI data entry agent lets users paste emails or upload images into model-driven Power Apps forms and maps the content to Dataverse columns for review.
  • It is enabled per environment in the Power Platform admin center and respects existing validation rules, data types and Dataverse security roles.
  • Plan for premium licensing, AI capacity consumption, language limits and a mature schema, and roll it out gradually starting with controlled departments.
