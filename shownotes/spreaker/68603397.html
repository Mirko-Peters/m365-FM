Many Copilot rollouts light up licenses and then stall with zero behaviour change. This episode is for IT, change and business leaders responsible for Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption. Learn the five failure modes and a practical 90-day plan that turns licenses into measurable everyday habits.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Copilot rollouts fail
  • 00:35 – Tools versus habits
  • 04:53 – Failure mode 1: vague use cases
  • 08:38 – Failure mode 2: data access and trust
  • 12:02 – Failure mode 3: no change engine
  • 15:36 – Failure mode 4: license sprawl
  • 17:23 – Failure mode 5: skills and workflow embedding
  • 19:33 – The 90-day adoption plan

Key takeaways

  • Copilot adoption is a behaviour-change project: design role-specific, recurring tasks instead of generic productivity promises.
  • Leaders who demo their real work live with Copilot, plus a steady comms cadence and champions, create the visible permission that drives MAU.
  • Fix labels and DLP, embed prompts and Copilot Studio micro-agents in daily workflows, and measure MAU, time to draft and ticket trends.

Worth quoting

“Wasted budget, annoyed executives, zero behavior change. The truth, deployment isn't adoption, behavior change is the product.” – Mirko Peters (00:08)

“Adoption happens when a specific person does a specific task a new way because it's faster, safer, or clearer.” – Mirko Peters (01:00)
