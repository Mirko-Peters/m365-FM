Many organizations rush into AI agents and end up with tools nobody uses. This episode helps business and IT leaders decide whether they really need a Copilot Studio agent by starting from personas and real use cases, and shows how grounding, skills and a structured rollout drive adoption.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Do you need your own agent?
  • 00:25 – The false promise of a quick fix
  • 04:16 – Personas: who is the agent for?
  • 08:18 – Use cases that actually matter
  • 12:15 – Teaching skills and knowledge
  • 16:13 – Avoiding rollout pitfalls
  • 20:09 – Choosing wisely at the first decision

Key takeaways

  • Generic, quickly deployed copilots often stall because they lack role-specific context and grounding in company data.
  • Clear personas and a short list of use cases scored by value, frequency and feasibility decide whether a dedicated Copilot Studio agent is worth building.
  • Knowledge grounding plus action-taking skills, a phased pilot rollout and internal champions turn an agent into a trusted everyday tool.

Worth quoting

“Efficiency doesn't come from hitting enabled. It comes from asking the harder question right at the beginning. Do we need a copilot studio agent that reflects our processes or language and our data.” – Mirko Peters (03:34)
