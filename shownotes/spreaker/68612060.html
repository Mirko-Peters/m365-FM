Microsoft's new App Builder looks like a comeback for Canvas Apps, but it is really a personal automation lane built on SharePoint lists. This episode helps Power Platform owners and Microsoft 365 admins draw the line between personal and enterprise apps, plan the move to Dataverse and put the governance guardrails in place before toy apps become critical systems.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The truth about Canvas Apps and App Builder
  • 00:27 – The facade: why App Builder feels like Power Apps
  • 04:16 – The split: personal Copilot layer vs enterprise Power Platform
  • 08:41 – The migration cliff: from toy app to critical system
  • 12:42 – Governance that works: DLP, permissions and restricted search
  • 16:52 – Agents as the new runtime
  • 19:10 – Rapid implementation checklist and micro stories

Key takeaways

  • App Builder is a personal, SharePoint-first automation lane that looks like Power Apps but lacks Dataverse, ALM and enterprise-grade security.
  • Define and publish Dataverse thresholds such as relationships, record volume, audit needs and external APIs so promotion to the enterprise lane is not a debate.
  • Govern the edges with Copilot DLP and sensitivity labels, access reviews, Restricted SharePoint Search and connector approvals instead of micromanaging builders.

Worth quoting
