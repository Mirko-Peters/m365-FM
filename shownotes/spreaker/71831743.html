Most dashboards die quietly because they depend on someone remembering to open them at the right time. This episode shows BI leads, Power Platform architects and executives how to shift from static reporting to a proactive notification blueprint with Power BI, Power Automate, Dataverse and AI that pushes action when it matters.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why dashboards decay
  • 00:41 – The data graveyard
  • 03:09 – From dashboards to event thinking
  • 06:19 – Why most alert strategies fail
  • 09:05 – The proactive notification blueprint
  • 12:44 – High-value use cases
  • 15:22 – Governance, limits and cost control

Key takeaways

  • Dashboards support exploration, but high-value decisions need event-driven notifications that push context to an owner when a business condition changes.
  • A notification architecture uses Power BI as a sensor, AI for anomaly detection and summaries, Power Automate for orchestration, Dataverse for state and feedback loops to tune the rules.
  • Treat notifications as products with owners, life cycle reviews and cost monitoring, and watch Teams payload limits, throttling and metered AI credits as you scale.
