Power BI dashboards built on Bing Maps visuals are about to break as the service is retired in favor of Azure Maps. This episode walks Power BI admins and analysts through the tenant settings, the pitfalls of the one-click upgrade and a careful manual migration, plus the often-overlooked shape map visual.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Bing Maps visuals are on borrowed time
  • 01:17 – Why Bing Maps had to go
  • 04:22 – Tenant settings before migration
  • 08:16 – The one-click auto-upgrade mirage
  • 12:34 – Manual migration step by step
  • 17:07 – Bonus: shape maps
  • 20:30 – Modernize before it breaks

Key takeaways

  • Bing Maps-based visuals in Power BI are deprecated, and reports that still use them will show broken placeholders unless they are migrated to Azure Maps.
  • A Power BI admin must first enable the Azure Maps visual and the related data-processing settings in the admin portal, otherwise converted maps silently fail to render.
  • The automatic upgrade handles simple maps, but bubble sizes, zoom, colors, layers and drill-through need manual rebuilding and verification; shape maps add a client-side option for region visuals.
