Microsoft's FY26 partner restructuring changes how partners are evaluated, funded and surfaced to field sellers, and many are still on the old playbook. This episode is for Microsoft partners, MSPs, ISVs and distributors, and it explains the new solution areas, designations and incentives plus a sequencing strategy to protect co-sell access and funding.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why partners aren't ready
  • 00:49 – The old model and why it broke
  • 01:48 – Six solution areas become three
  • 03:57 – New designations and badges
  • 06:37 – Incentive implications
  • 09:29 – Sequencing strategy
  • 12:53 – Aligning go-to-market
  • 15:17 – What each partner type should do now

Key takeaways

  • In FY26 Microsoft consolidates six solution areas into three: AI Business Solutions, Cloud and AI Platforms, and Security.
  • Designations and especially advanced specializations gate co-sell visibility and the largest incentive programs, so partners must sequence them deliberately.
  • Start by checking your Partner Capability Score in Partner Center, anchor on your strongest area, set up attribution such as partner admin link and CPOR, and align your messaging to the new solution areas.

Worth quoting

“This isn't just a rename, it's a re-architecture of how Microsoft invests in its partner ecosystem. And the partners who treat it like one will be the ones who capture the growth.” – Mirko Peters (00:40)

“The problem is, this model created too much fragmentation. Partners stacked badges across multiple areas without building real capability in any single one.” – Mirko Peters (01:11)
