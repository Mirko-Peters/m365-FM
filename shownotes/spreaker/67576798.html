A SharePoint form customized with Power Apps can look perfect in the designer and still fail once real users and real data arrive. This episode is for makers and admins who build request forms on SharePoint lists. You will learn why performance and validation break at scale and which practices keep a form reliable over time.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why easy starts turn into hard problems
  • 04:10 – Hidden performance traps after rollout
  • 08:00 – Validation nightmares you do not see coming
  • 12:20 – Native SharePoint forms versus Power Apps
  • 16:41 – Practical strategies for survivable Power Apps forms
  • 20:16 – Reliability over polish

Key takeaways

  • Power Apps forms that work in preview often slow down in production, so test with production-scale lists and keep formulas inside the form lightweight.
  • Validation must be layered: Power Apps gives users immediate feedback, while SharePoint column settings enforce data integrity however the record is saved.
  • Customize a SharePoint form only when the business logic needs it, offload heavy work to Power Automate, document formulas and roll out in phases.
