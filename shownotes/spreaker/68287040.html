Approval chains stall because people are on vacation or miss emails, and classic Power Automate approvals become fragile once you add thresholds and specialist reviews. This episode is for Power Platform and Copilot Studio makers who want to build AI-driven multistage approvals in agent flows that decide routine cases instantly, escalate exceptions to humans and stay auditable.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Approvals stuck in corporate purgatory
  • 01:18 – The problem with traditional approvals
  • 03:40 – Building the AI stage
  • 07:21 – Human oversight and conditional stages
  • 11:22 – Dynamic inputs and receipt validation
  • 15:07 – Testing, versioning and publishing
  • 18:17 – Real-world payoff

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Studio agent flows let an AI stage evaluate each request against precise, testable policy instructions before any human sees it, so only borderline or high-value cases reach managers.
  • Manual stages, manager lookup via the user connector and conditions such as amount thresholds turn a single approval into a multi-tier process, with every decision logged in Dataverse.
  • Document inputs let the AI cross-check receipts against claim data, and disciplined testing, naming conventions, versioning and status updates make the flow reliable and auditable.

Worth quoting

“Every claim that meets objective policy is approved instantly. Every edge case lands neatly in a manager's queue with the context already attached. Humans make value judgments, not clerical confirmations.” – Mirko Peters (18:22)
