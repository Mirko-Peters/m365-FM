Still hiding passwords and connection strings in config files, Key Vault or Git history? This episode is for Azure developers and admins who want to replace secrets with Entra ID tokens and managed identities. You will learn how app registrations, claims checks and the On-Behalf-Of flow keep real-world apps secure without credential sprawl.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The doormat key problem: hard-coded credentials
  • 03:35 – Why tokens beat passwords
  • 07:35 – Managed identities: service identities without secrets
  • 11:15 – App registrations, scopes, roles and consent
  • 15:15 – Forwarding tokens with the On-Behalf-Of flow
  • 19:10 – Wrap-up: the practical gain

Key takeaways

  • Hard-coded secrets spread across configs, repos and backups, so the fix is to stop using them rather than hide them better.
  • Entra ID issues short-lived, scoped access tokens, and managed identities let Azure resources obtain them without any stored credentials.
  • Careful app registrations, claims checks in your APIs and the On-Behalf-Of flow keep permissions tight and the chain of trust intact across services.

Worth quoting

“The cure for password sprawl isn't to hide the passwords better, it's to replace them with something that self destructs when it's misused.” – Mirko Peters (03:31)

“Passwords are static. Tokens are temporary and scoped, which means if they leak, the blast radius is tiny and the clock is already ticking before they expire.” – Mirko Peters (04:06)
