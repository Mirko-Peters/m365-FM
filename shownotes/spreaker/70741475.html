Your Microsoft 365 tenant may be technically flawless and still be heading for a silent collapse. This episode is for architects, IT leaders and governance owners who need systems their organization can still operate in five years. Learn the failure patterns behind automation sprawl, security friction, Copilot stalls and identity collapse, and how intent-based governance prevents them.

Key takeaways

  • Technically perfect Microsoft 365 tenants often collapse silently because they are designed for capability instead of long-term operational durability.
  • Intent-based governance with three zones for personal, collaborative and enterprise-record work survives change better than rigid configuration thinking.
  • Audit governance debt such as global admin counts, unowned teams and flows, unlabeled data and permissions sprawl before scaling Copilot.
