One click on Describe your page in Power Apps can quietly move an app onto Dataverse and premium licensing. This episode helps Power Platform makers, admins and IT finance teams understand what Generative Pages really deploy, why virtual tables do not avoid the cost, and how to use the feature without surprise invoices.

Key takeaways

  • Generative Pages in Power Apps build React-based pages inside model-driven apps on Dataverse, so every user of the result needs a premium Power Apps license.
  • Connecting SharePoint lists as Dataverse virtual tables does not avoid premium licensing, because Dataverse still handles metadata, permissions and CRUD operations.
  • Use Dataverse only when needed, isolate premium components in dedicated environments, restrict premium connectors, review the Power Platform admin center monthly and plan licensing with finance.
