Copilot in Dynamics 365 sounds smart but gives average answers because it does not know how your business sells, orders and renews. This episode is for CRM and ERP teams who want domain-specific AI without compliance risks. Learn how to map data flows into Dataverse, secure the bridge and structure data so Copilot truly understands it.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Copilot in Dynamics 365 feels generic
  • 00:30 – Why Copilot needs more than default data
  • 04:06 – Mapping the data flow from your systems to Copilot
  • 07:46 – Building a secure data bridge without breaking compliance
  • 11:28 – Structuring external data sets for AI understanding
  • 15:11 – Teach Copilot your business language

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Dynamics 365 starts with general-purpose knowledge, so forecasts and recommendations stay generic until it is fed your own CRM and ERP data.
  • Data should reach Dataverse through deliberate paths such as APIs, Dataverse connectors or Azure Data Lake, with ingestion filters, role-based access, Conditional Access, encryption and audit logging.
  • Clear metadata, taxonomies and schema mapping matter as much as the data itself, because Copilot can only use fields it can interpret.
