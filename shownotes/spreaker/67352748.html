Fetching a sales record or ticket during a Teams chat often means five clicks and a lost conversation. This episode is for Teams developers and admins who want custom apps people actually use. You will learn when to use bots, tabs or message extensions, how to build an action-based extension with Adaptive Cards, and how to deploy it safely.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why message extensions are the most overlooked productivity power-up
  • 04:53 – Search, action and link unfurling extensions compared
  • 09:36 – Building an action-based message extension
  • 14:07 – Packaging, deploying and scaling your extension
  • 19:02 – Letting Teams do more of the work

Key takeaways

  • Bots and tabs get most of the attention, but message extensions remove context switching by bringing live data straight into Teams conversations.
  • Search extensions surface records, action extensions collect input and trigger back-end processes, and link unfurling turns pasted URLs into rich previews.
  • A precise app manifest, tightly scoped permissions, staged rollouts and compliance checks decide whether an extension scales across the organization.
