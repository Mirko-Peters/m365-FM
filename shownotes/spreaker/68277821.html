Model-driven Power Apps sound enterprise-ready, but for many teams they turn a simple tracker into a Dataverse project full of security roles, environments and change requests. This episode shows makers and IT leads when to skip Dataverse and build faster with a fusion team using Teams, SharePoint lists and Power Automate.

  • 18:33 – The real lesson

Key takeaways

  • Model-driven Power Apps depend on Dataverse, which brings licensing, environments, security roles and governance overhead that many simple business apps never need.
  • A fusion team can deliver most of the value with SharePoint lists for data, Power Automate for approvals and notifications, and Teams as the interface.
  • Start light, prove value quickly and move to Dataverse only when real requirements such as complex integrations or regulated data demand it.
