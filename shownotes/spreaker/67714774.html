When a Power App stops saving on the last day of the month or an Intune policy locks users out, your cloud contract will not pay for the lost time. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins and IT leaders who depend on cloud services for critical work. You will learn how SLAs shift risk to you and how systems engineering practices help you build real resilience.

  • 14:42 – From risk to resilience
  • 18:01 – Three actions to build reliability

Key takeaways

  • Cloud SLAs and the shared responsibility model limit provider liability, so the lost time and revenue from an outage remain your problem.
  • Microsoft 365 services share dependencies such as identity, DNS and network routing, so one failure can cascade across Teams, Outlook, SharePoint and Power Apps.
  • Borrowing from systems engineering, build redundancy, monitoring and tested fallback procedures so outages become manageable speed bumps instead of crises.

Worth quoting

“The provider maintains uptime most of the time, but you carry the consequences when it doesn't. That isn't malicious, it's simply the shared responsibility model at the heart of cloud computing.” – Mirko Peters (02:52)

“If multiple Microsoft apps suddenly fail together, your first diagnostic step should be to ask is this identity, is this DNS? Or is a local network appliance getting in the way?” – Mirko Peters (08:28)
