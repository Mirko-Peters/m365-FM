Power Platform is switched on for every user by default, and with agents arriving, ungoverned tenants are becoming a real data risk. Double MVP Craig White shares how admins, CoE leads and IT decision makers can put tenant settings, DLP and environment strategy in place without slowing makers down. Learn how to turn governance into enablement and scale citizen development safely.

Key takeaways

  • Because Power Platform is on by default, the first governance steps are locking down tenant settings and applying data loss prevention policies before activity grows.
  • Shadow IT in Power Platform can reveal motivated makers and future champions, so the goal is to harness it rather than try to stop it.
  • AI amplifies years of oversharing, so admins must revisit sensitivity labels, zero trust and least privilege before rolling out agents broadly.
