FAQ
Does Expert Connect cost anything?

No. It is free for publishers and experts.

Can I contact every expert?

No. Requests go only to experts who opted in and match. Contact details are shared after they accept.

Can I stay anonymous as an outlet?

You can hide your outlet until an expert accepts. Your account stays verified for us.

Which formats are supported?

Quote, interview, guest article, podcast appearance and talk.
